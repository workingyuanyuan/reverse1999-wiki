# Rules for agents maintaining this site

This site is an unofficial wiki for the game Reverse: 1999 (重返未來：1999 / 重返未来：1999; developer and global publisher Bluepoch 深藍互動). Its first readers are AI agents; humans come second. The rules in `../AGENTS.md` apply; this file holds what is specific to this site.

State on 2026-11-23: a page for every released arcanist (138; Evelyn not yet), six Afflatus index pages, a page for every Psychube (116, 109 of them with art) with five index pages, six system pages (Summoning and pity, Combat basics, Statuses, Insight and levels, Resonance, Release dates), the story (6 index pages, 136 chapters, 1,499 stages, with the full text in each language) and the home page, in English and translated. Published 2026-11-23 at https://yuanaruru.com/reverse1999-wiki/: the arcanist pages first, the Psychube and four newer system pages the same day, then Resonance and the Psychube art, the Resonance set on every arcanist page, and the story. Not yet published (2026-11-24): the kit of every arcanist page, the effect of every Psychube page and the effect column of the Statuses page in the game's own text, with edition differences; a profile page for each of the 134 arcanists out on the global service; Schneider's page and profile; no page opens with a paragraph (story chapters, five system pages); the prose ledger (`reviews/prose-ledger.json`).

## Decisions by the owner (2026-11-22)

- Service: the mainland China service is the main source. It runs about two versions ahead of the other services (mainland 4.1, global and Taiwan 3.9 on 2026-11-22). Readers on the global, Taiwan, Japanese and Korean services look up which arcanists the coming versions bring, to plan their pulls, so arcanists that are out only on the mainland service are in scope. Every arcanist record carries the mainland release version and date and, where it exists, the release on the other services; a page states that an arcanist is not yet out on the global service as a fact of the record, never a predicted date. This replaces, for this site only, the rule of the other game wikis that the next version stays out.
- Languages: English at the root, Traditional Chinese (`/zh-tw/`), Simplified Chinese (`/zh-cn/`), Japanese (`/ja/`) and Korean (`/ko/`). Names in each language follow that language's official strings: Simplified Chinese from the mainland service, Traditional Chinese from the Taiwan service, English from the global service, Japanese and Korean from the global client's Japanese and Korean text. A name that has no official string in a language yet (an arcanist, skill or status out only on the mainland service) is translated (owner, 2026-11-22): English takes the community translation of the Fandom wiki, Japanese and Korean are translated by us, Traditional Chinese converts the Simplified name. The page's caveat says these names are not yet official, and they are replaced by the official strings when the global client has them.
- Arcanists in the mainland data but not yet released there (Evelyn, mainland release 2026-11-26) are included when an official announcement gives the date (owner, 2026-11-22): the Afflatus index lists them with that date. Their page waits until the client data holds their kit (2026-11-23: Evelyn's skills are placeholders).
- Schneider (owner, 2026-11-24: she gets a page; what it holds and the rain are the agent's default) is in the character tables of both clients with her kit, profile and voice lines, but `isOnline` is 0 in both: she was never playable. She has an arcanist page and a profile page like the others, written from the same tables, whose release cells say she is not playable (`unplayable` in `scaffold.py`); the Beast index lists her, and she stays out of the counts of the release, Insight and Resonance pages (`SKIP` in `pipeline/write_indexes.py`). Her page ends with the stages of the main story in which she speaks and her last two lines of chapter 2 as the game gives them (2ND-15, as the Storm comes), written by hand from the story pages. On her pages the margin rain is the Storm (`src/styles/reverse.css`): ten layers of one streak each, every layer a tile of its own size rising at its own speed, with gusts, because one tile moving as a whole shows its grid; only where motion is allowed. Her art is taken by the Storm and comes back, every 14 s: it fades from the feet up as it drifts up and blurs, then returns head first (owner decision, 2026-11-24: an exception to the root rule that images do not move, for this one image).
- Release cells give dates only, no version numbers (2026-11-22): the version an arcanist came with is not in the client data, and a guessed version would be wrong for one service or the other. Drake and Glyndŵr have no date in the records yet and carry the mainland version 4.0 instead.
- Art (revised 2026-11-22): the full-length art of each arcanist from the Reverse: 1999 Wiki (Fandom), file `<English name>.png`, a transparent figure shown whole and unaltered. The official sites carry full-length art for the ten launch arcanists only (Taiwan site), so a link to an official original is not required. The page notes credit Bluepoch, the Fandom file page and the image file at the revision used (`pipeline/fetch_art.py` records it in `records/art.json`). Arcanists with no file on Fandom (in 2026-11: Evelyn, not yet released) have no art. A Psychube shows its illustration from the same wiki (2026-11-23), file `<English name> Psychube.png`: the picture of the Psychube's own screen with its lettering, on a transparent ground with a brushed edge (`pipeline/fetch_art.py --psychubes`, `records/art-psychubes.json`); Fandom has none in 2026-11 for Dressing Up the Truth and the six Psychubes out only on the mainland service, and those pages have no art. Facts are never taken from `github.com/myssal/Reverse-1999-CN-Asset` alone: its `mappings/ArcanistMap.json` (ids, names, birthdays, skins) may serve only when a second source agrees.
- Scope of the first release: every released arcanist, after three sample pages of different rarities were approved (2026-11-22).
- Second scope (2026-11-23): Combat basics, Statuses, Insight and levels, every Psychube, and the release dates of both services. Resonance waits for a later batch.
- Third batch (2026-11-23): Resonance, and the Psychube art; each arcanist page names its Resonance set and patterns in the profile table.
- Sources: look for wikis in every language of the site, Simplified Chinese first. Survey below.
- Story text (2026-11-23): the full story text of every chapter, as the game has it, in the language of each page, from the Reverse: 1999 Story Compendium (`github.com/VioletWilde/Reverse1999-Story-Compendium`, pinned in `records/story.json`). The English wiki on Fandom and BWIKI also carry the full text of each stage; the Compendium has all five languages of the site: English, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese from the global client 3.9, Simplified Chinese from the mainland client 4.1. The Compendium has no licence file and its README reserves the rights of the game's text to their holders; the page notes cite it at its commit and name Bluepoch as the owner of the text. Its second copy of each stage (the folded client transcript) is left out. The earlier default of no story chapter list is replaced. Layout approved on a sample (chapter 1ST, 2026-11-23): an index page per kind of story (main story, events, character stories, anecdotes, other stories, with the two chapters of standalone Trails), a page per chapter with its stages, a page per stage with the story, then the Trails it opens. Chapters out on the mainland service only are listed by their Simplified Chinese name, without a page; a chapter in the English folder only (900024) is left out.
- Game data (owner, 2026-11-22): the decoded client tables in `github.com/St-Pavlov-Foundation/re1999-data` (mainland client) and `github.com/St-Pavlov-Foundation/re1999-data-global` (global client) are the sources of in-game text and numbers: names, rarity, afflatus, damage type, skill names, effects and multipliers, Psychubes, materials. The mainland repository gives the Simplified Chinese text and every arcanist; the global repository gives the official English, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese strings of what is out on the global service. Each page cites the file at a fixed commit and records the client build from `data/meta.json` in `appliesTo`. Wikis (Fandom, Huiji, BWIKI) are second witnesses, read for samples.
- Recommended Psychubes on arcanist pages (2026-11-23): the profile table of each arcanist page lists, in the game's order and linked, the Psychubes that `character_recommend.json` (`equipRec`) recommends for it; it is the same record as the Recommended row of the Psychube pages, read from the other side, and is written by `scaffold.py` (`profile` rewrites finished pages). An arcanist with no recommendation has no row. `check_pairs.py` fails when a page on either side lacks the link back.
- Huiji (`res1999.huijiwiki.com`) may be read in the owner's own Chrome (the `claude-in-chrome` tools), one named page at a time at a human pace, for a bounded list of pages; facts only, restated, and no copy of its text committed. Not with the headless Playwright browser, and never by working around its Cloudflare challenge with a script: the challenge is the site's refusal of automated reading.
- Text of the game on every page (owner, 2026-11-24): the descriptions of incantations, Ultimates, Insight passives, Portray levels, Euphoria, status terms and Psychube effects are shown as the game gives them, in each language's edition (English, Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese from the global client, Simplified Chinese from the mainland client). They replace the text the site had written in its own words, which was a default and never the owner's choice; a paraphrase drifts (the site's 繳械 dropped the game's 普通). Tables of numbers compiled from the game stay as our facts. No copy of the decoded tables is committed (they carry no licence).
- Arcanist profiles (owner, 2026-11-24): each arcanist out on the global service has a profile page with the game's file text (cover, items, culture, skins, voice lines), written by `pipeline/profile.py` from `character_data`, `skin` and `character_voice`. Only entries the global client has are written, in every language, so the languages hold the same set. `pipeline/check_profile.py` reads the tables on its own and fails for any text on a profile page that the game does not have, besides the two headings of our own.
- Arcanists and Psychubes out on the mainland service only (owner, 2026-11-24): their kit or effect text exists in Simplified Chinese alone, so only the zh-cn page shows it. The English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean pages leave it out and keep the facts (profile table, Insight materials, names).

## Defaults taken from the advisors (2026-11-22; the owner may change them)

Fable, Astra and Neon were asked (`~/.local/state/delegation-log/docs-sites/20261122-r1999-plan*`). All three proposed the global service; the owner chose the mainland service for the reason above.

- Arcanist index split into six pages by afflatus: one index of about 140 links exceeds 5,000 characters.
- Answers across entries come first: summoning carry-over by banner type (counter, featured guarantee, destination banner as separate columns), Psychube to arcanist and back by trigger condition, Euphoria, a five-language name table, and what differs between services.
- No tier lists.
- No answer line (root rule, 2026-11-24): a profile page and a story stage show no answer line under the title; their title and first lines already say what the page holds, and the hidden note at the top of the article carries the description (`hidesAnswer` in `src/lib/site.mjs`, checked by `check-dist.mjs`).
- Differences between the editions of the game's text (agent's default, 2026-11-24): each page shows one edition, so where the editions say different things on a value or condition, the last section of the page (`## Edition differences`) says so in our own words, from `records/edition-notes.json`, plus Epiphanies out on the mainland only, written by `pipeline/edition_notes.py`.
- Short sidebar on profile pages (agent's default, 2026-11-24): a profile page shows its arcanist's Afflatus group whole and only the first link of every other group, as story pages do (`src/routeData.ts`). With the whole sidebar the 670 profile pages took the build to 946 MB, 54 MB under the 1 GB GitHub Pages serves.
- Wikis that refuse automated reading (a Cloudflare challenge) are not read by script.
- Who lets a change go live: the owner confirms every time. Look: taken from an official site, with continuous ambient motion behind the content (root rules). Address: `https://yuanaruru.com/reverse1999-wiki/`.

## Content rules

- Arcanist pages: profile table (rarity, Afflatus, damage type, tags, Resonance set with its five patterns besides the default, the Psychubes the game recommends, mainland and global release), then the kit in the game's own text (`pipeline/kit_text.py`): Incantations (each card by rank), Ultimate, Inheritance by Insight with the materials table, Portrait, Euphoria, and the statuses the kit names; then a five-language names table, and the edition differences where there are any. Only labels and headings are ours. The kit is written from `cache/source/<slug>.json`; zh-cn pages take the mainland client's text, cut to the entries the global client has. Nautika's Insight II kit is swapped in by the battle logic, not a table: `AFTER_INSIGHT` in `kit_text.py` lists its skill ids (matched to the Fandom Insight II tab). Brume's character record lists her cast forms in one run: they are grouped by the rank digit of their ids.
- Psychube pages: profile table, the effect in the game's own text for Amplification 1 to 5 (`baseDesc` of `equip_skill.json`), names table. `baseDesc2`, filled for five Psychubes, is not shown: the game does not show it (Fandom lists `baseDesc` alone for Her Second Life) and `skill2` is 0 in every row.
- Statuses page: the effect column is the game's text of each status id (`skill_eff_desc.json`); the lead and the section intros are facts in our own words.
- Data codes, checked against Fandom on 2026-11-22: `rare` + 1 = stars; `career` 1 Mineral, 2 Star, 3 Plant, 4 Beast, 5 Spirit, 6 Intellect; `dmgType` 1 Reality, 2 Mental. Insight level caps in `character_rank` are cumulative (30, 70, 120, 180): the in-game cap of Insight n is the difference. In `skill_ex_level` descriptions, `▩n%s` names the skill a Portrait level changes: 0 the Insight passive set, 1 and 2 the incantations, 3 the Ultimate.
- Official terms per language: `cache/glossary.json`. Insight 洞悉 / 洞察 / 통찰, Portrait 塑造 / 塑造 / 형상, Euphoria 狂想 / 狂想 / 광상, Inheritance 傳承 / 伝承 / 계승, Incantation 咒語 / スペル / 주문, Ultimate 至終的儀式 / アルティメット / 최종 술식, Moxie 激情 / モキシー / 열정, Genesis DMG 本源創傷 / ジェネシスダメージ / 고정 피해, Precognition 先覺 / 予察 / 선각, Epiphany 閃念 / 瞬想 / 발상 (the global client's Euphoria screen; in Traditional Chinese 啟示 is Eureka, a team mechanic).
- Never edit `src/content/docs/zh-cn/` by hand.
- Summon rules: the rule text of each banner type is in `const.json`, row id = `info` of `summon_pool_detail.json` (`value2` holds the Chinese text in the mainland data and a language key in the global data); rates and pity are `initWeight` and `awardTime` of `summon_pool.json`. The global English text also keeps older rule texts under other keys (`language_10000347` to `language_10000363`) whose wording differs: read the key that `const.json` names. A banner's type is the one its rule text names in brackets (【限时角色征集】), not its title: some banners titled 限时活动征集 are of the Time-limited type.
- Euphoria opens at Insight III level 30 for a 6-star arcanist and at Insight III level 1 for a 5-star (Fandom, page Euphoria, revision 91875); the client data does not hold the condition.
- Where the two clients disagree on a value or a target, the page gives the mainland reading and says in one clause what the global client has.
- Psychube pages: profile table (rarity, tags, effect name, max level, stats at max level, the extra stat, the game's own recommendations from `character_recommend.json`, whether the global client has it), the effect with every changing value written for Amplification 1 to 5 as 16/20/24/28/32%, and a five-language names table. Stats at max level are the base values of `equip_strengthen.json` times `attributeRate` / 1000 at that level; the extra stat is the highest break level of `equip_break_attr.json`, in tenths of a percent (checked against Fandom on three Psychubes, 2026-11-23). The index pages split the Psychubes by the stat they add (Critical, 6-star damage, 5-star damage, Survival and healing): one index of 116 links passes the first 5,000 characters.
- Combat rules come from the game's Field Manual tips (`loading_text.json`) and the Afflatus relation texts of the global client (`language_10030417` to `language_10030420`); Fandom is the second witness for the 30% bonus. That Spirit and Intellect each have the advantage over the other is not stated in either, so the page does not say it.
- Statuses page: the shared statuses, chosen as those that at least three arcanists' own kit text names in brackets (a term named only inside another status's description does not count). The column lists those arcanists; it does not claim they apply the status.
- Resonance page: every value comes from the mainland tables and is checked to agree within a rarity (2 and 3 stars together), or `write_systems.py` stops. The four sets of Resonance Patterns have no name in the game; the page names them by the shape of their main Idea. What a pattern raises or lowers is the sign of the change of its main Idea against Placidity at the top level; the values of Ideas are left out, as no second source gives their unit. Which arcanists the game shows as Reasoning instead of Resonance is not in the tables, so the page names no arcanist for it. BWIKI's Sonetto page (revision 26647) agrees with the tables on every Resonance and pattern material.
- Story pages (`pipeline/story.py`): written from the Compendium and never edited by hand. The answer line is a chapter's only opening: the body starts with its table of stages, and no description says the page holds the text. The Compendium's bold marks are made to parse (`fix_marks`: a space after a speaker label, punctuation moved outside a span, a span over a blank line closed in each paragraph, unpaired asterisks shown as characters) without changing a word; `check-dist.mjs` fails for a stage that shows an asterisk its twin does not escape. Names of chapters and stages are the Compendium's headings in each language; main-story stage codes (1ST-01) are the chapter's `chapterIndex` and the stage's place in the chapter (agreeing with Fandom on chapter 1ST), and in-game dates are `year` and `time` of the global `episode.json`. Arcanists who speak are found by their name in each language and merged, so every language links the same pages. A stage title that another page also has gets its chapter in brackets. Links between the Compendium's files point at our stage pages; a link to another site (pictures of in-game items) keeps its text only. Only the six index pages are in the sidebar (the character stories take two, `characters` and `characters-2`, because an index row with its full address is about 140 characters and the first 5,000 hold about 28 rows): each link there sits in every page, ahead of the content. Chapter and stage pages show the Story group and only the first link of every other group (`src/routeData.ts`): the whole sidebar is about 60 KB of HTML, and on 7,500 story pages it took the build to 1.4 GB, past the 1 GB GitHub Pages serves (836 MB with the short sidebar, 2026-11-23). Stage pages are left out of `llms.txt` and the bundles; `llms.txt` says where they are. `pnpm verify` runs `check-dist.mjs` in eight processes (`scripts/check-dist-parallel.mjs`). A stage is three steps from the home page (home, story index, chapter page); `check-dist.mjs` allows that step for story pages only. `check_prose.py` skips chapter and stage pages, which are game text. `to-zh-cn.py` skips `story/`.
- Level costs (Dust and Sharpodonty per level, `character_cosume.json`) are left out: the rows run one longer per Insight stage than its levels, and no second source gives the totals.
- Section headings carry no quotation marks: Starlight curls them in the HTML and the heading check against the twin fails.

## Look (approved 2026-11-22)

Taken from the mainland and Taiwan official sites: parchment text `#eee4d5`, tan `#bba893`, one orange-brown accent family (`#a95d35` rules and table head rule, `#b55829` marks, `#db6f39` hover only); serif headings (the sites use Source Han Serif; the site serves Noto Serif TC, SC, JP and KR at weight 600 from the `@fontsource` packages, the same design under SIL OFL, split by Unicode range so a page loads only the slices it shows; each language's cut draws its Latin letters too, so a heading never mixes two serifs); a copper rhombus before section headings and the site name. Body text stays sans-serif: the serif fallbacks of Windows read poorly at body size.

The site opens in the dark theme (2026-11-22): with nothing stored it is dark; a reader's choice of light, dark or auto is kept (`src/components/ThemeProvider.astro`, `ThemeSelect.astro`). Dark theme (revised 2026-11-22; follows in part a dark, analogue, editorial design language in the game's manner): black painted ground; warm light leaking in from outside the window (right edge, low; bottom left corner), fixed to the window, so the middle where the text column sits stays darkest; a static grey grain tile over everything (`src/assets/grain.png`, alpha 0.10 baked in, normal blending, never animated); bars that cover scrolled content (header, phone menu, table of contents bar) carry the same grain; wide-window sidebars are clear. Square corners (1 px), including the phone menu button and the table of contents toggle; no shadows; weights 400 and 600 only; tables have no frame, rules across only and a copper rule over the head row, tabular figures; links and current items stay parchment with a copper line, because copper small text falls short of contrast on black; the chosen theme button is underlined, not filled; prose keeps about 42em a line in Chinese and Japanese, 46em in Korean and 75ch in English, while tables use the whole column. Not adopted, as too heavy for a reference site: narrative decoration (floating photos, background big type, catalogue micro text, mirrored titles), paper surfaces, serif body text. Checks on background-only screenshots at 390, 1440, 1920 and 2560 px: centre darker than edges, maximum luma under 40, grain mean 13 and spread 7.6 with equal channels, the text column's 16 px blocks at most 20 luma with a range under 8.

Light theme: parchment paper `#f4ede2` with brown ink, same tables and marks. Motion (`src/styles/reverse.css`, end): entrances measured from the official site's CSS (fade in while rising 2rem, 0.5 s, default ease; 0.3 s pointer responses), and rain in the margins that keeps rising (2026-11-22): the key visual of the official site is rain on a window, and in the game's story the Storm makes the rain go up from the ground to the sky, so short streaks drift straight up one tile in 4 s and drops creep up the glass in 90 s; a mask keeps it to the margins between the text column and the two sidebars, and to the bands outside the centred frame on a very wide window. Favicon: a copper rhombus with rain lines, drawn for this site. Character art: the full-length figure, without frame or shadow, floats right of the profile table (two columns: field and value) and the opening paragraph on a wide window, at most 26rem high; on a phone it stands above the table, at most 20rem high; the first section starts below it.
