## Story

Marsha presses a piece of gauze to the man's cheek. A cut from the rough wall she pushed him into.

Adelheid holsters her pistol and folds her arms, listening to the other man.

**Mikhail:** My name is Mikhail, and the man you're pinning against the wall is my comrade, Alexei.

**Mikhail:** We're from nearby—Dorovka.

**Marsha:** Are you soldiers?

**Mikhail:** Yes. We were in Brest when the armistice was signed. At first, they sent us back to recover at the hospital in town, but then things started to fall apart.

**Mikhail:** We've been looking through these old buildings for medical supplies—anything we could use to help the wounded there.

**Mikhail:** We thought maybe we could bring them back something before we got out of here. The hospital is really desperate for supplies.

**Marsha:** What happened to the hospital?

Mikhail glances between Marsha and Adelheid, and his eyes brighten when he notices the badge on Marsha's chest.

**Mikhail:** A few days ago, there was chatter that Manus Vindictae was launching an attack. But no one took it seriously; we thought they were on the run.

**Mikhail:** Then they hit us in force. In just a few days they overran what was left of the army and seized control of all the supply routes. Now they're squeezing the life out of every last pocket of resistance.

**Mikhail:** We know they'll hit Dorovka any day now. But there's no way to evacuate the wounded safely.

**Mikhail:** Ma'am, you're from the St. Pavlov Foundation, aren't you? I recognized your badge.

Mikhail's pleading gaze on Marsha says what he cannot.

**Marsha:** That's correct. Mikhail, do you know how many people are still in the hospital?

**Mikhail:** About a hundred. Mostly the wounded that are too weak to move, along with the doctors and nurses.

**Mikhail:** What will happen if Manus Vindictae surrounds the hospital ...?

The scenario plays out in Marsha's mind to its inevitable end.

**Marsha:** I will contact the Foundation in Sen, explain the situation, and request immediate support.

**Marsha:** But they won't have time to get here before the Manus. Before then ...

Marsha looks at Adelheid. Her head has dipped, heavy with thoughts of her own.

**Marsha:** Mikhail, I need to discuss this with my comrade.

**Marsha:** If possible, I will join you at the hospital and do what I can to assist you until the Foundation arrives.

**Mikhail:** Thank you!

Behind them, Adelheid has opened the door and briskly stepped outside.

Outside the house, Adelheid removes the little shield hanging at her waist.

Thin sheet metal with a rough weld. Never properly polished, its uneven edges sting her palms as she turns it in her hand.

**Adelheid:** By this time tomorrow, we may have reclaimed our stolen relics. All the wealth and history of our Order lies just out of grasp.

The thin morning light turns the distant horizon pale.

**Marsha:** ... By this time tomorrow, that hospital, and all of the people inside, may be nothing more than a charred ruin.

**Marsha:** These people need our help.

Adelheid frowns, yet her eyes can't help tracing the simple strokes painted across the shield.

Staś had never seen their home. He had no idea what Hohes Schloss actually looked like.

But through Adelheid's words, it seems he imagined it as an unyielding tower that stood tall atop a mountain for a thousand years.

A castle where generations of knights lived, fighting for justice in service of the weak and the needy.

The place she had left before coming to Sen, where she had met a boy in need of help. The boy who painted this tall and noble tower.

**Adelheid:** Losing those relics has laid our shame bare to the whole world.

**Adelheid:** As Acting Grand Master, I failed to stop them; I was derelict in my most sacred duty.

**Adelheid:** Now I have a chance to right this injustice. I cannot allow them to escape.

**Adelheid:** If the Order is to survive, we must reclaim those priceless treasures.

Adelheid looks down the road toward the town center, conflict tightening her expression.

**Marsha:** "You shall be the shield that protects the weak."

Adelheid replies with a dry, bitter laugh.

**Adelheid:** We always recite the words from The Code of Chivalry and the Knight's Vows before we march.

**Adelheid:** Whether to defend a knight's honor or save precious lives ... Such things ought not to be weighed against one another.

**Adelheid:** Yet, here we are, forced to decide.

Marsha walks up to her mentor and comrade-in-arms.

**Marsha:** Remind me, Sister Adelheid, why did you receive this shield?

She replies with a sharp glance deep with turmoil.

**Adelheid:** A boy lingered in death's hand, sick with a deadly fever. But I cured him. In turn, I suppose he wanted to protect me.

**Marsha:** It may not be the equal of our greatest relics. But it was given with noble intention.

Neither need say the obvious. A bullet would pass through that thin sheet metal like it was paper.

But Adelheid planned to carry it into battle all the same.

**Marsha:** Our vows have always been in service to people like him. Without expectation of reward.

**Marsha:** That single act of mercy is more worthy of our vows than a thousand gilded relics and chests of looted gold. That shield is proof of your honor as a knight.

**Marsha:** I don't see a dilemma at all.

Marsha's voice is as soft as rustling grass. She knows she's challenging everything her mentor has ever held dear, but she will not let it go unsaid.

**Adelheid:** ... If we miss this opportunity, there may never be another chance to reclaim what we've lost.

**Marsha:** But the Order has a long way ahead—

**Marsha:** Long enough for us to restore its glory and fill that emptied castle once more.

**Marsha:** Let it begin with that rusty shield. Let it be the sign of what it is to be a "true knight."

Marsha stands firm. She can sense her old master hesitating; all she needs is a gentle push.

Yet for as much as it might herald a new beginning, that tower on her shield is also a reminder of their roots.

**Adelheid:** ... I remember when you were the one that couldn't wait to face the enemy. Leaping to your death instead of falling back to care for the wounded.

Marsha smiles softly.

**Marsha:** War changes us. It changes everything. In ways that cannot be undone. But I must believe the changes I went through have a purpose.

**Adelheid:** ... With two Hochland Knights in their defense, the hospital may just hold out until the Foundation's support arrives.

Marsha exhales in relief.

**Marsha:** Thank you, Adelheid.

She smiles and then takes out her communicator.

**Marsha:** Mr. Razor, we have an emergency ...

Adelheid gives the shield one last glance before fastening it back to her waist.

Combat Hospital, Dorovka

The four of them waste no time and reach Dorovka by noon. They can spy the hospital from the end of the road.

But something else comes into view as well—rising smoke.

**Alexei:** Damn it! They're already here—

**Marsha:** Wait, Alexei!

Marsha grabs the soldier just before he begins to charge. She fixes her focus on the distant scene.

**Marsha:** The smoke isn't coming from the hospital. It's ringing outside. They're still fighting.

**Marsha:** Hmm? What's that ...

In the bright flare of exploding grenades, hardened steel flashes silver, and horses pant and whinny as they pound across the concrete.

**Adelheid:** That can only be the Order. They're here, and they're fighting Manus Vindictae.

Adelheid's voice lifts with each word.

**Marsha:** Then there were other knights nearby ...

After the initial shock, Marsha nearly cries out in joy and relief.

**Marsha:** Hurry—let's move!

She releases Alexei as they all begin to dash forward toward the battle.

Adelheid follows a few steps behind. She can see Marsha from the back.

All of a sudden, it seems as if she were that foolhardy girl once more.

But then the wind lifts the edge of her grayish green coat, revealing a glimpse of the nurse's uniform beneath.

The unexpected arrival of a dozen knights of the Hochland Order catches the splinter force of Manus Vindictae completely off-guard.

Outside the hospital, the knights mop up, ensuring the threat is completely neutralized.

**Marsha:** The hospital is safe; we've run the Manus off for now, thanks to the Hochland Order.

**Marsha:** I need to speak with the head of the hospital and assess our situation.

**"Razor" Captain:** Your requested reinforcements are still enroute, even if Manus aren't a threat for now, that hospital needs to be evacuated.

**Marsha:** Understood.

Just as Marsha finishes her report, she is suddenly pulled into a warm, excited embrace.

**Hildegard:** My dear Marsha, you're alive!

A familiar face brightens on her like a golden sun; her childhood sparring partner leaps toward her with a beaming smile.

Marsha hugs her back just as tightly.

**Marsha:** Yes, Hilde, I'm alive.

**Marsha:** I'm so glad we found you here! But why are you here?

**Hildegard:** We weren't supposed to come this way, but we heard from some of the evacuees that the hospital was in trouble. We rode as fast as we could; God be good, we made it in time.

**Hildegard:** And on top of that, to find you and Master Adelheid together. It is truly a blessed day, sister.

**Adelheid:** ...

Adelheid does not interrupt the reunion between comrades.

She listens to Hildegard as the victorious warrior beams with yet more words of joy and knightly elan, drifting deep into thought.
