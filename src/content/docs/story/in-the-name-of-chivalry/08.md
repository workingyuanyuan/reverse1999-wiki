## Story

Outside, Combat Hospital

Marsha finds the knight a short walk from the hospital, sitting in quiet contemplation at the edge of a field. She sits down near her without intruding.

The knight sits among the bending tall grass like an old stone. As if she had always been there.

**Adelheid:** To sit in silence and set the mind to quiet is an essential virtue for a knight. In contemplation we may search our resolve and explore the depths of our belief.

It was a lecture she had heard before, and the message was clear. So, for some time longer they sit together in silence, accompanied only by the rustle of the autumn wind.

**Adelheid:** With our training, we can sometimes forget that we do need to use arcane skills to quiet our emotion. Sometimes, it is better to do battle with the trouble in our hearts instead of pushing it away.

**Marsha:** What is it that you're fighting, Adelheid?

**Adelheid:** Regret.

**Adelheid:** When I saw our knights outside the hospital, I was relieved, overjoyed to have found some of our lost comrades. But though we saved these people, we left Rejowa; we let Manus slip through our hands.

**Marsha:** ...

**Adelheid:** I cannot find a center between my joy and my despair. Now the path ahead for our Order seems more unclear than ever before.

**Marsha:** You made the right decision based on what we knew.

**Adelheid:** I did. And I am greatly heartened to have found Hildegard and the others. That is itself a blessing.

**Adelheid:** But they did not come here for me. I didn't know they were here. I didn't even know they were alive. Our order has no home and no direction. It has only magnified my disgrace.

**Marsha:** I know the burdens of command weigh heavy on you, Sister. But you must not be so harsh on yourself. You were given responsibility for an organization that was already falling to ruin; its troubles are not of your making.

**Adelheid:** ... There is a little solace in that.

**Adelheid:** But though I may not have created these troubles, I have been a steward over them and stubbornly refused to fix them.

**Adelheid:** And I think I found the reason. I am in search of an answer, not just for the Order, but for my own sake.

**Adelheid:** Who is responsible for the Order's downfall?

A cruel question.

**Adelheid:** Manus Vindictae? That answer is too easy—to blame an enemy for our downfall is a hollow excuse.

**Adelheid:** Shall I blame the Kaiser? The military? The state? How long have we served the rulers of this land and never once doubted their authority?

**Adelheid:** But I fear the truth is all too simple ... The Order has grown outdated. The Age of Chivalry is long over; we've always been a mere remnant. The horrors of this war come as a long-awaited death knell.

**Marsha:** But ...

Adelheid raises her hand, stopping Marsha.

**Adelheid:** When I found you in that storage room, I was filled with guilt and regret ...

**Adelheid:** I felt the same way the day I heard of your "death."

**Adelheid:** I will never regret saving you from that snow, child. But perhaps I was wrong to bring you to the Order.

**Adelheid:** You could have lived some happy life with a normal family ... instead we forced you to suppress your nature, molded you into a warrior, and convinced you that it was your duty to give your life for the sake of your vows.

A silent grief strikes Marsha's heart. Adelheid has carried her "death" all this time.

**Marsha:** I'm sorry I didn't tell you then. I shouldn't have let you carry that guilt.

**Marsha:** I don't regret becoming a knight, Adelheid. My days with the Order have given me the most precious gift.

**Marsha:** It is the character you, Adelheid and the Order gave to me that has kept me from despair in the face of horror. It is my vows that keep me on my path when the way is narrow and the road uneasy.

Marsha turns to Adelheid, closing in toward her, meeting her face. Adelheid replies with a defeated sigh.

**Adelheid:** I can see your resolve.

**Adelheid:** Once we brought your temperature down, I left to help the others.

**Adelheid:** I feel as though I looked away only a moment before you were right back up, tending to the wounded as if you had forgotten you had nearly died.

**Adelheid:** You may only have the pauldrons now. But you are as true a knight as any of us.

**Adelheid:** A worthy sister of the Hochland Order.

Adelheid returns briefly to her contemplation, picturing the knights working tirelessly through the hospital, and Marsha stands among them.

**Adelheid:** So, I say to myself—

**Adelheid:** See, Adelheid. Chivalry has not faded after all. It can still be a shield that protects the weak and vulnerable of this age.

Adelheid turns to meet Marsha's eyes. Conviction and pride burning on her face.

**Adelheid:** As for the proposal you mentioned, this reimagining of the Order ... I will consider it.

Hallway, Combat Hospital

In the soft golden light of dawn, a transport convoy bearing the Foundation's logo arrives outside the hospital.

Marsha finishes up her rotation through the ward. But on hearing the noise, she finds an unboarded window and leans out to get a view.

**Marsha:** Mr. Razor, good to see you've finally arrived.

The squad captain offers her a wordless nod before returning to unloading the vehicle.

Behind him, people in gray-and-white checkered uniforms carry supplies into the hospital as new medics rush in beside them.

**Marsha:** Reverend Mother, this is Mr. Razor from the Foundation.

Marsha leads Irene over.

**Irene:** Mr. Razor, we appreciate the Foundation's support. Your arrival is a long-awaited godsend.

**"Razor" Captain:** Don't mention it. We've got our orders. We'll begin by transferring all the heavily wounded out to a safer location.

**"Razor" Captain:** In four days' time, there'll be a train passing through the station nearby. Everyone else is to load up there for evacuation.

**Marsha:** I've been given permission to remain with you here until that time. And once we evacuate, I will be accompanying the soldiers that wish to keep fighting in the Don region.

**Marsha:** We'll be deployed to assist the Zeno Cavalry Corps near Bereggrad.

**Irene:** Wonderful. I'll share the good news immediately.

After Irene leaves, "Razor" turns back to Marsha.

**"Razor" Captain:** I should apologize. We've arrived much later than intended. I'm not proud of that.

**Marsha:** I understand. Mr. Creius informed me of the situation; you were delayed by Manus Vindictae.

**"Razor" Captain:** We ran into one of their own convoys along the way. It was heavily defended. But what they were transporting might just be of interest to you.

Marsha's heart skips a beat.

**Marsha:** Do you mean ...?

**"Razor" Captain:** You should gather the knights first.

Marsha summons her brothers and sisters, and they follow "Razor" out to the Foundation's parked convoy. Among the familiar liveries of the Foundation, one truck stands apart.

**Marsha:** A Manus truck.

**"Razor" Captain:** Correct. We had already gotten word that the hospital was secure. So, we knew we had an opportunity.

**"Razor" Captain:** We decided to engage them, just to see what was worth all of the fuss they put into defending it.

**"Razor" Captain:** We sent them packing and managed to seize the transports they were protecting. Which led us to this one.

**"Razor" Captain:** During the inventory we noticed that many of the items bore the seal of your Order, Marsha. So, the Foundation authorized us to return these spoils to the Hochland Order.

**Adelheid:** ...!

Adelheid steps forward in disbelief as "Razor" opens the door of the truck.

Inside the dim interior lie dozens of covered paintings, stacks upon stacks of yellowed books and tomes, silverware, and a shattered, dust-covered sword.

**Adelheid:** The ledger of the Hochland Order. Candlesticks from the meeting hall, and ...

A sword forged of patterned steel rests among them. Its hilt is wrapped in black horsehide; the blade fractured into several pieces.

The familiar Latin inscription can still be made out along the broken blade.

**Adelheid:** That's Hermann's sword, the one that vanished after his death.

Adelheid gathers the broken blade into her arms. Her finger runs along the edge lightly, and a thin line of blood wells up. A dim, dark-blue shimmer ripples across the sword.

**Adelheid:** Hermann's arcane skill, bound to the blade and awakened by blood, reveals the truth.

**Adelheid:** Hermann used this sword in his fight with Manus Vindictae, leaving traces of their incantations on it.

**Adelheid:** They must have taken it to hide any evidence of their involvement.

A long-rumored secret finally lays itself bare.

Behind Adelheid, the knights drive their swords into the earth, hands resting on the hilts, heads bowed in silence.

Marsha puts her hands together and offers a quiet prayer for her old Grand Master.

**"Razor" Captain:** We didn't recognize the sword at first, but we found the Order's name on some of those books and documents. Though we knew for sure after we saw this ...

"Razor" pulls away the velvet cloth covering the paintings.

After the dust settles, the solemn and sacred past appears on display in front of them.

**Marsha:** ...!

**Hildegard:** That's Marsha's dubbing ceremony!

**Hildegard:** I remember we had a painter staying with us at the castle; he painted the ceremony to repay us for our hospitality.

**Hildegard:** That was the day Marsha took her vows as a knight.

To have such a moment memorialized in a painting is a rare honor.

Yet by coincidence, Marsha's knighting was preserved forever.

A moment that belonged not just to her but to all the knights of her Order.

Every knight once stood in that same place during the dubbing ceremony. They pledged vows before their new brothers and sisters and waited for the sword, the very symbol of knighthood, to rest on their shoulders.

**Marsha:** I vow to hold the Hochland Order in reverence, to defend it faithfully ...

**Marsha:** I vow to keep this oath without deceit, to heed the statutes of virtue, and to honor the counsel of my brethren—

**Marsha:** Until the day of my death.

Marsha murmurs her oaths beneath her breath, flooded by the memory.

**Marsha:** ... All the training I've done was in service of that day.

**Marsha:** The day I became a "knight."

In the painting, Marsha's expression is solemn. She wears her ceremonial attire. Her folded hands tell of her expectation for the glory to come.

Countless faces of knights, living and fallen, seem as though they could perfectly overlap with the girl's face.

**Hildegard:** The knights departed from Hohes Schloss after the dubbing ceremony, carrying out their duties under the Grand Master's command.

**Hildegard:** We looked ahead to future missions, eager to prove ourselves, to return with glory earned by our swords.

Until that "knightly glory" was laid to waste beneath the withering scythe of machine gun fire. Now, the ones that survive wander without purpose around its grave.

But the young woman in that painting walks a different path—a narrow one, defined by her vows but no longer constrained to them.

A path too small for steeds to charge, too tight for armor to pass. So, she releases her horse, lays down her sword, and packs away her breastplate.

Yet she keeps her pauldrons. Their silvery steel gleams as a guide, like the North Star, a reminder of what matters.

**Adelheid:** ... The Knights of Hochland must begin anew.

As the painting is uncovered, the distance between past and present clears away the dust of history. It opens a bloody wound long left untended. A rift that has kept them divided and weak.

Adelheid removes her cloak and gently covers the broken sword.

**Adelheid:** I never would have dared to dream this sword could be recovered.

Stranger still for it to have been found and returned by an organization she had long distrusted.

**Adelheid:** Though the sword is far from our Order's most precious relic. It's possible all of the items of true value are gone forever. Heh ... But it doesn't matter.

The painting before them once proudly adorned their home's stone corridor.

The candlesticks once burned in the meeting hall, witnessing the knights' worship and prayer.

Apart from that sword, the rest of the items are humble things from the castle.

Yet though they may be of little worth, they are rich in memories. Not all of them glorious, but small, tangible, and real.

**Adelheid:** Even so ... I feel our treasure has been restored.

Just like the shield Staś gave to Adelheid, the one she carries at her waist.

**Adelheid:** What defines a knight?

Adelheid asks herself.

**Adelheid:** ... To uphold our values and never bow to evil, to honor our brethren alive and dead through our words and our deeds.

**Marsha:** "It is more of a heavy burden than an honor." You told me that after the dubbing ceremony.

Marsha walks up to her mentor and comrade-in-arms.

**Adelheid:** Yes. A knight's path is not one of extravagance and luxury. We learn to endure hardship and live simply. To keep our minds fixed on justice.

Adelheid looks deep into Marsha's eyes. The future knighthood in her vision stands right beside her.

The answer is within reach.

Adelheid turns and faces the others.

**Adelheid:** Brothers and sisters. After we leave this hospital, we will search for our scattered comrades.

**Adelheid:** Along the way, I intend to follow Sister Marsha's advice. This age does not need mounted warriors, but it still needs the Order. We will find a new purpose: to help the vulnerable, to heal the sick, and to care for the wounded as we once did in days of old.

**Adelheid:** The knights will distance themselves from the wicked games of war. We shall return to where we first began.

**Adelheid:** From this day on, we fight only to save lives—from the sickle of death.

**Adelheid:** Our efforts will no longer be confined by nationality or creed.

A murmur ripples through the knights, yet not a single voice rises in opposition.

Marsha listens quietly, surprised.

**Hildegard:** We will follow wherever you lead, Adelheid, but this is a significant change ...

**Adelheid:** That may be so. I am but the acting Grand Master, though it is my vision. I will convene a meeting of the Order so that we might decide on our path together.

Adelheid looks at "Razor."

**Adelheid:** If the Order chooses this path, there may come a day when we will seek to collaborate with the Foundation—provided the Foundation remains what it is today.

The squad leader bows his head in respectful agreement.

**"Razor" Captain:** May the peace be with us.

**Adelheid:** Sister Marsha, should that day come, I hope that you will be as you have been, a bridge between our organizations.

**Marsha:** I will, Grand Master.

The simple words are said as a solemn oath.

A breeze sweeps across the plains, carrying the crisp scent of late autumn frost. As it passes through them, it seems to strip away the dust that has gathered on their Order.

The morning light shines on them, a promise of a new day, a new beginning.

Marsha raises her hand before her eyes. The brilliant dawn spills in through her fingertips.

**Marsha:** Hmm ... what a good day it is.
