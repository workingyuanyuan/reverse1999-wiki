## Story

Hallway, Combat Hospital

Although Manus Vindictae has been driven off, the battle has only worsened the hospital's condition.

Its corridors, already crowded, have received a new influx of wounded. Coughs and low groans echo out even past the doors.

What knights and soldiers can stand help move the wounded through the hallway and into the makeshift triage.

**Wounded Soldier:** We have to go! We're all going to die!

A soldier on one of the beds suddenly thrashes upright, shoving away the nurse who is trying to stymie his bleeding.

The nurse grips his shoulder to steady him.

Another doctor rushes over with several strips of cloth, trying to tie the soldier's arm to the side of the bed.

But he only struggles harder.

**Nurse:** Doctor, he needs a sedative.

**Doctor:** No! His pulse is too weak. Someone hold him down—

Just then a hand covered in armor reaches in and presses gently on the soldier's forehead.

**Nurse:** Sister Marsha!

In short order Marsha has made herself indispensable to the staff here as they treat the wounded and dying as best as they can.

**Marsha:** I've used my arcane skill to calm his delirium. You should be able to treat him now.

She moves between wards. Her pale face running damp with sweat.

**Doctor:** Phew ... Sister, you arrived just in time.

On the bed, the soldier's murmuring softens, and his movements slow. The doctor quickly binds his limbs and begins stitching the wound.

**Mikhail:** Sister Marsha!

Marsha turns and sees Mikhail at the doorway, accompanied by a woman in a nun's uniform.

Marsha checks the soldier for a moment more. When the doctor gives her a nod, she steps toward the door.

**Irene:** I am Mother Superior Irene, and you must be the woman from the Foundation.

She takes hold of Marsha's hands in a firm but gentle grasp.

**Irene:** God bless you for what you've done here!

**Marsha:** I've done very little, Reverend Mother. The knights of the Hochland Order arrived before we did.

**Marsha:** But more support from the Foundation is on the way. They should arrive before nightfall.

**Irene:** Any help they can provide is already a miracle. There are still so many wounded here; these young men need all the help we can give them.

She looks around the hospital, her expression heavy with grief.

**Irene:** We had hoped that there would finally be an end to this bloodshed after the armistice. But it seems the war continues, just wearing a different face.

**Irene:** With the rumors of bandits and violence further east, everyone is worried for their families. But they want to help; they want to bring an end to this violence.

**Marsha:** I will see about securing their passage home, Reverend Mother. I'm certain the Foundation will appreciate whatever help they can offer.

The nun thanks Marsha once again, even more effusively than the last time.

**Nurse II:** Mother Irene—

A panting nurse rushes down the hallway, holding an empty glass bottle.

**Nurse II:** We're out of ether. There's only one bottle left in the warehouse.

More bad news arrives, and the dutiful woman responds.

She must weigh the value of each resource against the lives and suffering around her.

**Irene:** Prioritize surgeries; we need to make this bottle last until the evening.

**Irene:** For the others, we'll have to rely on pressure and immobilization. Have we any alcohol left?

The nurse nods.

**Irene:** A shame it's all ethyl; more than anything, I think these boys need a stiff drink. But in lieu of that, soak some herbs with it and use them as topical salves.

She turns to Marsha.

**Irene:** Sister, would you mind relaying the urgency of our situation to the Foundation once more?

**Marsha:** Of course.

Marsha looks around the room and sees other knights in silver armor working through their injuries and pain to assist the beleaguered hospital staff.

She tightens her trembling hand, then makes a resolute decision.

**Marsha:** Reverend Mother, perhaps there's something more I can do.

**GAMEPLAY**

***

Early in the morning, Adelheid moves through the hospital, searching for someone.

**Adelheid:** Sister Hildegard, have you seen Marsha?

Hildegard pauses at the question. She's been boarding up doors and windows since the evening prior.

**Hildegard:** I passed by her about a half hour ago; she was coming out of the operating room. Why?

**Adelheid:** I haven't been able to find her.

**Adelheid:** What was she doing in an operating room?

**Nurse Ⅲ:** Oh! I can explain.

A nearby nurse pushes a medicine cabinet as a brace against the door and joins the conversation.

**Nurse Ⅲ:** After we ran out of anesthetic last night, Sister Marsha offered to use her arcane skill to help the men through their surgeries.

**Nurse Ⅲ:** Last I saw her, she was headed to the storage room.

Adelheid exchanges a knowing look with Hildegard, her eyebrows knit tightly.

**Adelheid:** Do you have any ice, nurse? We need a bucketful or more.

**Nurse Ⅲ:** Nothing we can spare here, no. But some of the soldiers found an ice room in the cellar of an old building nearby.

**Nurse Ⅲ:** It was quite large; I think perhaps it was for a restaurant. We've been sending parties to collect ice from it from time to time, but there should still be plenty left.

**Nurse Ⅲ:** But what do you need the ice for?

**Adelheid:** Worry not, we'll take care of it.

**Adelheid:** Hildegard, fetch that ice and bring it to the storage room—now.

**Adelheid:** That foolhardy girl. She's pushed herself far beyond her limits; I'm certain of it.

**Adelheid:** I doubt she can even move for all the pain she's taken in.

As Adelheid enters the storage room, her suspicions are immediately confirmed by the slow, ragged breaths that heave out from the corner.

**Marsha:** \*painful exhale\* Urgh—

In a room of empty boxes, old sheets, and broken things, she finds Marsha curled up against the wall. Her eyes are shut; tears and sweat form a confluence of rivers as they pour down her cheeks and neck.

**Adelheid:** Marsha!

Adelheid hurries to her side and eases her gently to the floor.

At just a touch, she can feel the burning heat radiating from her body.

**Marsha:** Adelheid, ugh—

**Adelheid:** Shh. Don't speak. Slow your breathing ... there's nothing for it now. You must simply endure.

**Adelheid:** Hildegard is bringing up some ice water. We'll cool you down just like we used to back home; do you remember that?

**Adelheid:** I know you've always feared the cold; you have every reason, but you won't survive this heat.

Marsha gives a small nod, forcing a faint, apologetic smile.

**Adelheid:** You just won't be content until you die a martyr, will you?

**Adelheid:** Why would you do something so reckless? Every time you take in someone else's pain, you put your own life at risk.

**Marsha:** I know ... But, I have you ...

Marsha gasps the words out between heaves, her voice weak.

Adelheid is struck in waves: concern, then fury, pity, and lastly, pride.

Had there been no other knights in the hospital, Marsha might have looked for other options.

But she knew the others would understand what she'd done and what to do.

It wasn't reckless; it was a calculated risk. An act of complete trust in her comrades-in-arms.

**Adelheid:** ... Promise me, Marsha. Swear by your oath you will never do this to me again.

**Hildegard:** Marsha!

The other knights arrive hauling a tub and buckets of ice water, barking orders back and forth as they clear a space in the crowded room.

Together they lift Marsha up and ease her into icy water.

**Marsha:** Mmph!

Burning heat hits icy cold; the clash is severe, sharp, painful, sending her into convulsing shivers as she sinks into the tub.

Words fail to breach through her now-clattering teeth. Hot and cold slam into one another like the beginning of a cyclone; the pain swirls in her, tearing her thoughts apart. Her vision darkens.

**Adelheid:** Marsha!

She falls back into the abyss.
