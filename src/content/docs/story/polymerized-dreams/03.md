Streamlined, recessed strip lights highlight the various exhibits on display. In the distance, there's some kind of interactive installation illustrating the unique properties of polymer.

**Vertin:** "The Invention of Polymer and Its Uses" ... "The Properties of Polymer" ... "The Establishment of Kollektiv" ...

**Tereshkova:** Utilizing polymer's unique electrochemical properties, Comrades Sechenov and Filimonenko invented the first Soviet polymer hydrogen cell in 1939, paving the way for tremendous advancements in the field of robotics!

**Tereshkova:** We will always remember their sacrifice! Just as we honor Dr. Sechenov for once again coming to our rescue!

**Tereshkova:** It was his polymer that formed the basis of Redemption, the vaccine that proved instrumental in stopping the transmission of the disease, saving the Soviet Union from ravages of the Plague!

**Tereshkova:** Of course, with so many having succumbed to the disease, the Soviet Union turned to further developments in robotics to meet labor demands.

**Tereshkova:** Before long, new advanced models of robots made up a significant portion of our labor force, helping to deliver a new age of prosperity.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Is that what you've been aiming at with all of your Critter experiments, X?

**Tereshkova:** In order to fully optimize our robots' production power, Dr. Sechenov led a team of scientists to develop and launch Kollektiv 1.0!

**Tereshkova:** This revolutionary system unified all of our robot assistants under a single vast neural network, ensuring maximum efficiency!

**Tereshkova:** Then in 1950, Dr. Sechenov astounded the world with yet another development: A means of incorporating polymer into the human body, a process called Polymer Assimilative Adaptation.

**Tereshkova:** It is with this assimilative power that we can now implant interface devices directly into the human body, allowing people to connect to Kollektiv 1.0 and control robots using their minds.

**Tereshkova:** This technology holds the potential to establish a shared database of human minds, granting humanity unprecedented access to knowledge.

**Tereshkova:** And we are proud to announce that this potential will soon be realized with the coming launch of Kollektiv 2.0!

**Scientist III:** Molotov's here? It's hard to believe after all the bad blood between them.

He raises his hand toward the audience, and little by little, the rippling settles.

**Sechenov:** Thank you. First off, I'm very honored to have so many preeminent experts from every scientific field in attendance today. Welcome, everyone. I am Doctor Dmitry Sergeyevich Sechenov.

**Sechenov:** We all understand that there are times when a single discovery can turn the entire scientific world upside down.

**Sechenov:** But every great discovery must be built upon the knowledge of countless predecessors that rests at its foundation, to say nothing of luck, before it can be published and preserved for all time.

**Sechenov:** My achievements have never been solely my own. Only by standing on the shoulders of our forefathers have I gotten the chance to gaze upon the limitless horizons of science.

**Sechenov:** Standing here now, I must thank them from the bottom of my heart. Thank you. Thank you.

**Sechenov:** Nineteen years ago, when my team and I invented polymer, I had already foreseen its boundless potential.

**Sechenov:** A perfect synergy of mathematics, logic, physics, chemistry, biology, and other foundational studies in equal part, constantly pushing the frontiers of humanity's scientific exploration—

**Sechenov:** —bringing us to this historic day, where I have the honor of inviting every one of you to participate in this preview of Kollektiv 2.0.

**Sechenov:** Comrades, please follow me into the bright, limitless future!

**Mesmer Jr.:** No adverse reactions either. Looks like it doesn't hurt.
