## Story

**Ms. Stranger:** That was ten years ago.

???

The scene shifts into darkness, broken only by the glow of a TV screen with static storming across it.

**Ms. Stranger:** Mhmm.

A girl wakes to find herself strapped into a tilted restraint chair. The bindings render any attempt to struggle useless.

**Ms. Stranger:** When I opened my eyes, everything around me was pitch-black. All I could see was a TV and a single light bulb hanging overhead.

**Ms. Stranger:** A device held my head in place and my eyelids open. I had no choice but to stare at the fuzzy screen.

**Ms. Stranger:** Just as I started to think I was dreaming, I heard heavy footsteps.

As her story unfolds, a pair of red lights flicker in the darkness.

**???:** The drug has taken effect. We can begin.

**???:** Are you awake? Are you comfortable?

The voices sound distant, like they're coming from deep underwater.

**Ms. Stranger:** Where am I? Who are you?

**Ms. Stranger:** I'd heard enough stories to know what was happening. I'd been abducted by aliens. I was aboard their UFO.

**???:** You won't be able to move for a while, so it's best you settle in ...

**Ms. Stranger:** I couldn't help but think of Colorado's cattle mutilation cases from the 70s. Aliens had abducted entire herds of cows, taken their body parts, and left the corpses to rot in the fields.

**Ms. Stranger:** My imagination ran wild. I saw myself drained of blood, dissected, organs removed, and then discarded as a hollow shell.

**Ms. Stranger:** But strangely, even as I thought of all that, I didn't feel afraid.

**Ms. Stranger:** Their glowing eyes in the dark, their low voices ... They made me feel calm.

**Ms. Stranger?:** I know what you are. You're aliens.

**Ms. Stranger?:** Are you going to dissect me? Am I going to die?

**???:** Yes, we're aliens. Are you not afraid of us?

**Ms. Stranger?:** Should I be?

**Ms. Stranger?:** What does fear even feel like?

**???:** You're not afraid because you know you don't need to be, and you're right.

**???:** We can be friends.

**Mesmer Jr.:** You're sure they were aliens?

**Ms. Stranger:** Absolutely, Ms. Mesmer. They exist, and my abduction proves it.

**Ms. Stranger:** They'd probably been on Earth for a long time, given how fluent their English was.

**Ms. Stranger:** Though, I was unable to understand everything they said. Their vocabulary was a bit too advanced for my age.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Aliens might well exist, and plenty of people in Laplace believe they do.

**Mesmer Jr.:** But what I'm questioning right now is your mental state.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Did you experience anything traumatic as a child? Like abuse or abduction?

**Ms. Stranger:** Abduction? Only from the aliens themselves. And even if they tried to cover their tracks ...

**Ms. Stranger:** I'm certain these memories are real.

**Mesmer Jr.:** \*sigh\*

Mesmer finds herself caught between frustration and a strange admiration for such a perfectly structured yet utterly abnormal psyche.

**Mesmer Jr.:** What happened after that? Between you and these alleged aliens?

**Ms. Stranger:** Not "alleged," Ms. Mesmer. This was not some weather balloon being mistaken for a UFO. I saw them up close. They were real.

Her sincerity takes Mesmer Jr. by surprise.

**Mesmer Jr.:** No comment. Please, continue.

**Ms. Stranger:** No. Not until you admit they were indeed aliens.

**Ms. Stranger:** Insisting Earth and its creatures are all alone in the universe is a bit sad, don't you think, Ms. Mesmer?

**Mesmer Jr.:** What I think is irrelevant. I can't submit a report saying you were abducted by aliens to the Foundation.

**Mesmer Jr.:** They'd say I've finally lost it.

**Ms. Stranger:** Mhmm.

For the first time since the session began, Mesmer Jr. sees her patient pause in contemplation. Perhaps accepting the idea of these extraterrestrial kidnappers will help stabilize her.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Well, it's not impossible that you encountered something like that.

The occasional white lie is, according to both protocol and treatment guidelines, permissible at impasses such as this.

But Ms. Stranger has taken this acceptance and run with it.

**Ms. Stranger:** How about this?

**Ms. Stranger:** We include an objective description of them in your report to make it sound more credible.

**Ms. Stranger:** For example, humanoid creatures with gray-white fur, antennae, and glowing red eyes.

**Ms. Stranger:** Something like the Mothman.

**Ms. Stranger:** Actually, that might just work.

**Ms. Stranger:** If you don't want to call them aliens, we can call them Mothmen.

**Ms. Stranger:** Would wording it like that still get you into trouble?

**Mesmer Jr.:** Fine. Let's say you encountered something resembling Mothmen.

Mesmer resists the urge to rub her temples. She has to stay professional.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Can we continue? I'm getting tired.

Silhouettes manifest around the red lights—the Mothmen.

**Ms. Stranger:** Alright, where were we?

**Mesmer Jr.:** You were saying the ali—ahem, sorry, the Mothmen wanted to be your friends.

**Ms. Stranger:** Heh. Yes. And we had a bizarre yet joyful time together.

**Mothman:** We'd like you to watch something.

**Ms. Stranger:** Cartoons?

**Mothman:** Close. But they won't be quite so colorful.

**Ms. Stranger:** True to his word, the Mothman's "cartoons" were in black and white.

**Ms. Stranger:** The contents were mostly macabre, painful imagery, oddly juxtaposed with classical music.

**Ms. Stranger:** But if you held on until the screen turned white, you'd see happy, peaceful scenes.

**Ms. Stranger:** So I lay there, still strapped in the chair, holding my breath and waiting for the dark parts to end, then laughing when the light parts finally came.

**Ms. Stranger:** It was fun.

**Mothman:** She shows no discomfort in the face of disturbing stimuli.

**Mothman:** But when she's exposed to peaceful stimuli, her positive response is stronger than expected.

**Mothman:** That's where individualized instruction comes in. She's a quick learner.

**Ms. Stranger:** This continued for about 14 days. It was difficult to tell without going outside.

**Ms. Stranger:** By the 14th day, I was good friends with the Mothmen.

**Ms. Stranger:** I still couldn't leave the room, but they no longer restrained me in the chair.

**Mothman:** Starting today, you will begin receiving formal instruction.

**Ms. Stranger:** What will I be doing?

**Mothman:** Don't worry. The lessons are simple. Just do as we tell you.

**Mothman:** What you learn here will become your strength. And when the time comes, you will help many, many people.

**Ms. Stranger:** I learned a lot from them, much of which I rely on to this day.

**Mesmer Jr.:** So this is the "training" you mentioned earlier?

**Mesmer Jr.:** I thought it was the Foundation that trained you as a spy.

**Ms. Stranger:** You're a graduate yourself, Ms. Mesmer. You know the SPDM isn't equipped for such things.

**Ms. Stranger:** To teach you to hold your breath for prolonged periods or how to function without sleep.

**Ms. Stranger:** But the Mothmen ... They are natural masters of espionage. Any Foundation official would be ecstatic to receive their "gift."

**Ms. Stranger:** I proved it the minute I joined the Foundation.
