## Story

**Ms. Stranger:** And that was the end of it. Arcana let me go because she expects me to come back and serve her regardless.

**Mesmer Jr.:** \*sigh\*

Mesmer rubs her temples, letting out the longest sigh of the day.

**Mesmer Jr.:** You've done enough. We'll end the session here.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Personally, I still have a lot of questions, but procedurally, everything checks out.

**Ms. Stranger:** Thank you for going through all of that with me, Ms. Mesmer. It feels good to have sorted out all those memories.

**Mesmer Jr.:** I'm glad to hear it. Now, I'll give you your diagnosis.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Memory disorder, mild dissociation, abnormal emotional expression ...

**Mesmer Jr.:** I'm sorry, but I can't clear you.

**Mesmer Jr.:** I think you'll need a period of observation and recovery within the Foundation.

**Ms. Stranger:** I've heard I can choose to recuperate inside the Suitcase. Is that true?

**Mesmer Jr.:** Normally, yes. But your account of Manus Vindictae is too ambiguous. The Foundation may restrict your access to the Timekeeper.

Mesmer hears a sigh, one that sounds so genuine it takes her by surprise.

**Ms. Stranger:** I suppose waiting a little longer won't hurt.

The sound of the door opening interrupts the appointment.

**Constantine:** Hello, Ms. Mesmer. I've come to show my support for a retired subordinate. I hope you don't mind the interruption.

**Mesmer Jr.:** ...

Mesmer looks up at the unexpected visitor and stands to leave.

**Ms. Stranger:** Wait! Ms. Mesmer.

Ms. Stranger stumbles out of bed as she grabs Mesmer's arm.

**Mesmer Jr.:** What is it?

**Ms. Stranger:** You see Madam Constantine too, don't you? She's real?

**Mesmer Jr.:** Yes. Don't worry. You're not hallucinating.

Seeing Mesmer nod at Constantine, Ms. Stranger relaxes and lies back down.

**Ms. Stranger:** That's a relief. Would you mind giving us a moment?

**Mesmer Jr.:** I was just about to.

**Mesmer Jr.:** You'll have around an hour. I'll wait outside.

**Mesmer Jr.:** If you need me, just press the button by the bed. I'll come in immediately.

Ms. Stranger's grip loosens, and Mesmer leaves the two of them alone, closing the door behind her.

**Constantine:** So, how was the diagnosis?

**Ms. Stranger:** Thank you for coming, Madam Constantine. I'm glad to see you in person.

**Ms. Stranger:** Unfortunately, my results weren't great. Freedom is still some way away.

**Constantine:** I'm sure Ms. Mesmer was as lenient as she could be.

**Ms. Stranger:** Yes. I can tell she's one to want to do the right thing.

**Ms. Stranger:** As for the Secondary Defense Program, the human experiments, the Phototaxis ... she knows nothing. It was all the Mothmen's doing.

**Ms. Stranger:** You don't need to worry about her turning against you.

**Ms. Stranger:** Though judging from that session, she might need rest even more than I do.

**Constantine:** That's just how she is. Anyway, I didn't come because I was worried about you leaking anything.

**Constantine:** And even if you had told her everything, she wouldn't turn against the Foundation or against me.

She sits beside the Artificial Somnambulism device, pretending not to notice the twitch in Ms. Stranger's fingers.

**Constantine:** I'm here to confirm your retirement request one last time.

**Constantine:** I can authorize a partial reopening of the lab. We can give you a peaceful new set of memories.

**Constantine:** You could be an ordinary young woman from a normal family. Loved, cared for, and living a simple, worry-free life.

**Constantine:** The Foundation and the Secondary Defense Program would have nothing to do with you.

**Constantine:** It's certainly the less taxing option, mentally.

**Constantine:** Are you sure you want to join Team Timekeeper as you are now?

**Ms. Stranger:** ...

**Ms. Stranger:** Heh. Heh-heh.

Ms. Stranger smiles as she removes the diagnostic headset.

**Constantine:** What is it?

**Ms. Stranger:** Sorry, your question took me by surprise. It's similar to what Arcana asked me.

**Ms. Stranger:** But I appreciate the chance to answer it.

The smile remains on her face as she looks at her adoptive mother.

**Ms. Stranger:** I decline, Madam Constantine.

**Ms. Stranger:** Even if I don't know who I am, even if all my memories are false ...

**Ms. Stranger:** I choose to live as I am.

**Ms. Stranger:** One day, I'll find the answer to that ultimate question of "Who am I?"

**Constantine:** I thought you would've wanted to free yourself from everything the Secondary Defense Program forced onto you.

**Ms. Stranger:** A freedom that demands I abandon my past is no freedom at all. There are still pieces of happiness in those memories, and those belong to me.

**Ms. Stranger:** And even if I accepted, the Foundation would never truly let me escape their surveillance.

**Ms. Stranger:** Living such a life, even as a "normal" young woman, would be far more suffocating.

**Ms. Stranger:** I'd much rather join Team Timekeeper. Greater freedom, less oversight. Even your political rivals would have a hard time interfering.

**Constantine:** Vertin's Suitcase is certainly a convenient place to house more complex cases.

**Constantine:** But how can you guarantee you won't divulge things she should stay ignorant of?

**Ms. Stranger:** Even if I did, I don't think it would be as bad as you imagine.

Her eyes meet Constantine's.

**Ms. Stranger:** As time goes on, more and more people will try to win Vertin over.

**Ms. Stranger:** And with that comes options. Sooner or later, you'll struggle to keep her under your thumb.

**Ms. Stranger:** As will the Foundation as a whole.

**Ms. Stranger:** That's why you need to befriend Vertin. And the most important thing in a friendship is sincerity, isn't it?

**Ms. Stranger:** Let me go to Vertin as that show of sincerity, just like the day I was brought to you.

**Constantine:** I didn't expect the Secondary Defense Program would turn you into a politician.

**Ms. Stranger:** They implanted loyalty and morality into me, but politics was never part of it.

**Ms. Stranger:** Most of the people there are tired of those hollow games.

**Ms. Stranger:** This is something I picked up on my own by observing one very capable politician.

**Constantine:** Your proposal does indeed benefit me, assuming your loyalty doesn't waver.

**Ms. Stranger:** The Phototaxis is gone. I can't promise unwavering anything anymore.

**Ms. Stranger:** But you can still trust me.

**Ms. Stranger:** I'm sure you of all people know how to keep a former subordinate on your side.

**Constantine:** Then I have no reason to refuse.

Constantine stands, cutting their allotted hour alone short.

**Constantine:** I'll personally approve your transfer to Team Timekeeper. Consider it a parting gift.

**Constantine:** You'll enter the Suitcase free of any burdens.

**Ms. Stranger:** I felt the same way when I hid in it back in Antarctica—burdenless.

**Ms. Stranger:** It's spacious, you know, on the inside. Might be just the place to retire.

**Constantine:** I wish you all the best. Er, should I call you Ms. Stranger now? I heard that's the name you chose.

**Ms. Stranger:** Call me whatever you like.

**Constantine:** Then I sincerely hope you enjoy your life inside the Suitcase, Pupa.

A keyword. A name reserved for a mother addressing her adopted daughter. The first name she'd received and, at one point, an anchor for her sanity.

But now, it's just a name. Her mind registers the syllables, but no conditioned response follows.

She lets the word linger in the air. Then, she speaks of her own volition.

**Ms. Stranger:** Thank you for your blessing. Goodbye, Mother.

An unexpected form of address.

**Constantine:** Which identity is speaking right now?

**Ms. Stranger:** There's only one person standing in front of you. She's called "Stranger."
