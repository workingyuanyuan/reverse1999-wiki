## Story

**Forget Me Not:** Are you ready, Ms. Grace?

**Forget Me Not:** Don't be afraid when you put it on.

**Forget Me Not:** So long as you truly believe in the Guiding One, so long as you truly reject the world as it is, you needn't feel any fear.

**Forget Me Not:** Though you now walk blind, the mask will be your cure. It will open your eyes to what truly matters.

**Ms. Grace:** Indeed. I don't feel fear. I simply want to treat this honor with the respect it deserves.

She stands in a dimly lit room under the watchful eyes of followers eager to see if she will join their ranks.

The gentleman stands beside her like a priest witnessing a baptism. With careful hands, she lifts the mask.

**Forget Me Not:** If you cannot let go of your terror, then let your hatred guide you.

**Forget Me Not:** Let your murdered parents—let the honor the humans stole from you—make the choice for you.

**Ms. Grace:** Phew.

She steels herself with a deep breath. Then, as if submerging herself into the baptismal water, she puts on the mask.

What comes next is calm. She does not suffocate. Her body and mind do not reject it. Her breathing remains steady.

The room is so quiet she can hear her own heartbeat. Then, it is broken by a lone burst of applause.

**Forget Me Not:** Congratulations, Ms. Grace. You're one of us now.

**Forget Me Not:** The Guiding One has accepted you.

**Ms. Grace:** Thank you very much, Mr. Forget Me Not.

**Forget Me Not:** Don't thank me. Thank yourself for accepting the trial. I merely offered the choice.

**Forget Me Not:** What fortune that my mercy has at last found the right recipient, that one more will stand with us as we witness the birth of a new order!

**Ms. Grace:** What fortune indeed! May the Guiding One lead us to the true future.

**Ms. Stranger:** As the last survivor of an arcanist family persecuted by humans, "Ms. Grace" was able to get close to Forget Me Not with ease.

**Ms. Stranger:** She gained even more of their trust after putting on the mask.

**Ms. Stranger:** Its trial would expose any traitors or nonbelievers or so they thought. They never doubted me for a second.

**Mesmer Jr.:** And the Foundation sees anyone who's worn it as a lost cause.

**Mesmer Jr.:** So far, you're the only exception. How did you manage that?

**Ms. Stranger:** Because I am a faithful believer in Arcana?

**Mesmer Jr.:** Repeat that joke again, and I'll have to call security.

**Ms. Stranger:** If humor is off the table, then I suppose I have no choice but to tell you the dreadful truth about the Mothmen.

**Ms. Stranger:** Their "training" extended beyond mere mental conditioning.

**Mothman:** How long did you sleep yesterday?

**Ms. Stranger:** Two hours.

**Mothman:** There's no clock in your room. How did you measure time?

**Ms. Stranger:** The sound of the tape. One loop takes 15 seconds. It played 480 times.

The Mothman doesn't respond. With the flip of a switch, the sole light illuminating the room goes out.

**Mothman:** Do you remember its contents?

She searches her memory. After a prolonged silence, she forces out an answer.

**Ms. Stranger:** I'm sorry. I can't remember.

Another flip of the switch. The light, dim though it is, frees her from the pitch-black abyss.

**Mothman:** Think again.

**Ms. Stranger:** Yes, I remember now.

She looks up at the lightbulb—the same as it was when she first arrived—and her throat, tongue, and lips produce words of their own accord.

**Ms. Stranger:** "All for the peace and safety of mankind."

**Ms. Stranger:** The Mothmen used specially designed pre-sleep audio and their arcane skills to mold my mind into something more resembling their own.

**Ms. Stranger:** As long as a "light" was switched on, I would be drawn to it.

**Ms. Stranger:** One light that never went out was that of the Foundation. It kept me acting as their agent.

**Ms. Grace:** And when I began my final mission, another light was switched on: Manus Vindictae.

**Ms. Stranger:** That is what turned me into Ms. Grace, a collector of corpses and despiser of humanity.

**Mesmer Jr.:** So you used self-hypnosis to become Ms. Grace, which fooled the mask into seeing you as a devoted follower.

**Ms. Stranger:** No, nothing like that. And before you speculate, it wasn't a split personality, either.

**Ms. Stranger:** It was merely an instinctive adaptation.

**Ms. Stranger:** Just as a moth is drawn to light, just as a dog salivates at the ring of a bell, I produced the thoughts and memories needed to fulfill my duty when the cue was given.

**Ms. Stranger:** Such conditioning meant there was no disloyalty for the mask to detect.

**Mesmer Jr.:** You're dismissing your own free will.

**Ms. Stranger:** A reasonable conclusion. I know it's difficult to wrap your head around, but the concept isn't a philosophical one.

**Ms. Stranger:** Perhaps a simpler explanation would be better. You can think of it as a clockwork mechanism inside my head.

**Ms. Stranger:** It ticked over, shaping my memories and generating my thoughts.

**Ms. Stranger:** It made me think and remember in ways that aligned with Manus Vindictae, leaving nothing for the mask's arcane power to detect.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Is it still controlling you now? Am I talking to the mechanism?

**Ms. Stranger:** It's gone.

**Ms. Stranger:** If it were still there, the Foundation would never have doubted my loyalty. And you wouldn't be interrogating me right now.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Again, this isn't an interrogation.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Anyway. If it's gone, then I'll assume I'm talking to the real you.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Tell me, how do you see Manus Vindictae now?

**Ms. Stranger:** Evil, repulsive black gunk.

**Ms. Stranger:** Though, to be completely honest, if not for all that darkness, they might have been more appealing than the Foundation.

**Ms. Stranger:** But let's continue. I can show you Manus Vindictae from the inside, along with how I really felt about them.

**Ms. Stranger:** Following the mask trial, things went pretty much as expected. Ms. Grace became a favorite of Forget Me Not, and I was quickly promoted to Apostle.

Basement, Unknown Location

**Follower:** Ah, there you are, my lady.

**Ms. Grace:** I hear you have a rather troublesome newcomer on your hands.

**Follower:** That's putting it lightly. She's dangerous, and she hasn't put on the mask yet. Be careful, my lady.

Several Manus followers surround a young woman, all gawking at her as if she were a wild beast in a zoo.

**Ms. Kimberly:** I'm not putting on that hideous mask! And I have no intention of joining you!

**Ms. Grace:** Is that so? I thought you'd find the mask quite appealing, given your unique taste in toys.

**Ms. Kimberly:** ...!

**Ms. Grace:** The follower who brought you here had a little accident. Unfortunately, this was all I could recover.

**Ms. Grace:** Given that it doesn't seem like his style, I'd guess it belongs to you, is that right?

**Ms. Kimberly:** Yes.

**Ms. Grace:** Good to know. Now then, what do you say to a calm little chat, hmm?

**Ms. Grace:** I'm Grace. What's your name?

**Ms. Kimberly:** Kimberly.

**Ms. Grace:** Heh. From your looks to your name, you seem like you walked straight out of a spy novel.

**Ms. Kimberly:** Wh-Where I come from is none of your business!

**Ms. Grace:** Oh, but it is. You've piqued my interest, Ms. Kimberly. Why not come to Texas with me? There's no better way to get to know each other than on a little road trip, after all.

Even with the rebellion of a stallion burning in Kimberly's eyes, the moment Grace pulls out the wind-up toy, she is reduced to a docile lamb.

**Ms. Kimberly:** As you wish.

**Ms. Stranger:** I recovered the contract item sealing Anjo Nala, or "Ms. Kimberly," as she called herself at the time, before the Manus followers realized what they'd stumbled upon.

**Ms. Stranger:** Thanks to the Foundation restricting the dissemination of succubus intel, Manus Vindictae's rank and file never understood the importance of that oddly cute wind-up toy. Securing it for myself was quite the achievement.

**Ms. Stranger:** She's much sweeter than she looks as long as she doesn't have her claws at your throat.

**Ms. Stranger:** Anyway, I ended up taking her to Route 77 to help in the preparations for the upcoming ritual.

**Ms. Stranger:** And that's where we ran into a series of small unfortunate events.

Hallway, Tuesday's Motel

**Follower:** H-Here we are, my lady. The others should already be waiting for you.

**Kayla:** I told you, you got the wrong girl! I ain't this "Grace" or whoever. I don't even know you!

**Kayla:** Where are you taking me?! Please, let me go!

**Follower:** Don't make this harder than it has to be.

A girl and a brutish Manus follower are locked in a standoff in the hallway.

**Ms. Grace:** Would you quiet down? The whole motel can hear ... Hmm?

Another woman, so alike she could pass for the girl's twin, emerges at the far end of the hallway. The arguing pair stares at her, stunned.

**Kayla:** Emm?

**Follower:** A-Another Ms. Grace?

**Ms. Grace:** Well now, I've read stories of doppelgängers, but ...

**Ms. Grace:** Never in my life did I think something so exciting could happen to boring old me.

Ms. Grace steps closer, carefully looking her "doppelgänger" over.

**Ms. Grace:** Hmm. Not exactly the same. But I'd wager most people wouldn't be able to tell the difference.

**Ms. Grace:** With a bit of makeup ... Yes, yes. I think this could work just perfectly.

**Ms. Grace:** I'm terribly sorry. My attendant must have mistaken you for me. It seems someone's due for an eye exam.

**Kayla:** So, it was all just a mix-up? Whew. Well, that's a relief.

Her shoulders relax, her panicked expression giving way to one of genuine surprise.

**Kayla:** My name's Kayla. Good you showed up when you did. Things might've gotten real ugly. Was just about to give this friend of yours a good clobbering.

**Kayla:** But who could've predicted something like this happening, right?

**Ms. Grace:** Yes. For a moment, I'd thought the motel's proprietor had put a mirror at the end of the hall to prank me.

**Ms. Grace:** Well, this makes for a memorable stay, at least. You can call me Grace.

**Ms. Grace:** Sorry for all the trouble. I'd invite you for some tea or coffee to make up for it, but I'm terribly busy today. I assume you'll be able to make it back on your own?

**Kayla:** It's okay! I'll be in town for a few days. Maybe we can grab a bite together when you're free.

**Kayla:** We look so alike. Who knows what else we might have in common, right? I'd bet we could be the best of friends!

**Ms. Grace:** Of course. I'll be sure to pay you a visit when I get the chance.

**Collaborator:** Why not invite her to stay for dinner, Ms. Grace?

Another figure peeks out from a doorway further down the hallway.

**Collaborator:** We just got some fresh ingredients—should be good.

**Ms. Grace:** Hmm. A lovely idea, but shouldn't we keep a low profile? And we ought to respect her wishes, especially given how rudely she was dragged here.

**Collaborator:** No use now. She's already seen our faces.

**Collaborator:** I'll ask her. Kayla, right?

**Collaborator:** We've got some time before dinner. Want to help us prep the stuffing?

**Kayla:** You bet I would! Count me in!

**Ms. Grace:** ...

**Kayla:** We can see what else we've got in common, Grace!

**Kayla:** Y'know, I always wondered what my life would've been like if I hadn't grown up on a farm.

**Kayla:** Seeing you now, it's like seeing another version of myself, raised somewhere else. I'm just bursting with questions. I don't even know where to start!

**Ms. Grace:** I'm afraid you might be disappointed. I can't say I grew up anywhere particularly interesting.

She falls silent, her eyes shifting from her "doppelgänger" to the hallway as she walks further.

**Collaborator:** From now on, don't send followers or believers to gather materials. They're too clumsy, too obvious.

**Collaborator:** Have the Order of Enlightenment handle it. They're better suited for this kind of work.

**Collaborator:** By the way, Ms. Grace, have you seen Kimberly? We've lost some materials. She might have taken them.

**Collaborator:** You need to bring her back before she eats them.

**Ms. Grace:** Yes. Kimberly is rather impulsive when she doesn't have orders, isn't she?

**Ms. Stranger:** After that, I had no choice but to assume Kayla's identity when going about town, to avoid raising suspicion over her disappearance.

**Ms. Stranger:** I thought it was some twisted joke fate had played on us both, but just like that, it was over.

**Ms. Stranger:** It was all beyond anything I'd anticipated. Truth be told, it was rather painful.

**Mesmer Jr.:** I take it you felt empathy for her? Given she looked just like you.

**Ms. Stranger:** I felt sadness and guilt, just as the Mothmen's training dictated.

Based on her brain activity, she's fully grasped morality and loyalty.

**Mothman:** Based on her brain activity, she's fully grasped morality and loyalty.

And if the two contradict each other?

**Mothman:** And if the two contradict each other?

We'll deal with that when the time comes.

**Mothman:** We'll deal with that when the time comes.

**Ms. Stranger:** But they also taught me that I had to complete my mission. Having two conflicting "lights" simultaneously switched on ... It hurt.

**Mesmer Jr.:** How do you feel looking back on it now that you're free?

**Ms. Stranger:** Now, I think there were plenty of other ways I could've handled that situation.

**Ms. Stranger:** I could've said she left or shifted the blame onto someone else. Anything would've been safer and more efficient than pretending to be Kayla long-term.

**Ms. Stranger:** Honestly, the motel owner likely saw through me from the start. Whenever she talked to me, she'd bring up legends about doppelgängers.

**Ms. Stranger:** As for why I made the choice I did, I think the real reason was Ms. Grace's penchant for "collecting."

**Ms. Stranger:** Or maybe, just like Kayla, I wanted to see what could've been.

**Ms. Stranger:** Meeting someone who looks so similar—it's like meeting another version of yourself.

**Ms. Stranger:** Looking at them, you can't help but imagine what life would be like in their shoes. And, naturally, you end up feeling envy, jealousy, and even pity.

**Mesmer Jr.:** Then let me ask: how did it feel "becoming" Kayla?

**Ms. Stranger:** She was different. At first, I was certain she was real. Her memories all held together. She gave me a chance to be a genuine person.

**Ms. Stranger:** But once I became her, I realized it was no different from all the other fabricated identities.

**Ms. Stranger:** They might have been real to others, but not to me.

**Ms. Stranger:** They weren't mine to have.
