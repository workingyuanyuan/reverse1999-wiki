## Story

Carlota Bar

Sometime during the next day, a strange atmosphere hangs over the tavern where the gamblers usually gather.

**Gang Grunt I:** Mr. Tlazo ...

**Gang Capo:** Yeah?

**Gang Grunt I:** That lucky-ass girl's been counting her winnings for a while now, along with some other girl.

**Gang Grunt I:** They planning something?

**Gang Grunt II:** Sir, you think it's time we—

**Gang Capo:** Cram it!

The rebuke comes like the snap of a frightened dog.

**Gang Capo:** We can't afford to mess with arcanists.

**Gang Capo:** Hers ain't no ordinary luck, I tell ya. Must've used some kinda witchcraft or somethin'.

**Gang Capo:** You heard about those people who drowned on dry land, or just up and disappeared from locked rooms? That's just the start of the weird s\*\*t they get up to.

**Gang Capo:** One of our associates in the police told me that was all because of some arcane skill.

**Gang Capo:** So, unless you're lookin' to join those stiffs, you stay away from these outsiders.

As they trade wild theories about witchcraft and arcanum, the two figures in the corner exchange a glance over the opened letter in their hands.

**Centurion:** "When the sacred calendar and solar calendar realign, we celebrate the delaying of doomsday."

**Centurion:** "We climb up the Hill of the Star and watch the stars reach their zenith."

**Centurion:** "The annual art auction will commence soon. The Turquoise Serpent Club sincerely looks forward to your arrival."

**Centurion:** Pft. Trying a little hard.

**Centurion:** An art auction, huh? Ah, an invitation with no time or location!

**Centurion:** So this is the letter of supposedly "incredible value"?

**Centurion:** What's your excuse this time, Tlazo?

**Centurion:** It's gibberish. Do I have to remind you how much you owe me?

The eavesdropping capo straightens in his seat.

**Gang Capo:** I can't tell you nothin'. I mean it. I don't got a clue neither!

**Gang Capo:** I got the thing off some desperate gambler. Claimed it holds the biggest secret in Mexico City.

**Gang Capo:** But before I could make him, ahem, I mean, ask him anythin' more, he skipped the border.

**Gang Capo:** As for this club, all I know is that it's hidden away in some plantation outside of the city. But I ain't never heard of no auction.

**Gang Capo:** No human's ever set foot in that place—arcanist territory.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** Great. A conversation utterly devoid of useful information.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** This line on the invitation, "when the sacred and solar calendars realign," it's referring to an Aztec myth about the coming of the end of the world.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** So, once every 52 years, at the full cycle of the Aztec calendar, the Aztecs held their New Fire Ceremony to celebrate that they survived another cycle.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** Before the new sun rose, they climbed up the Hill of the Star and watched the Pleiades star cluster reach its zenith.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** Look at these symbols.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** These represent the 20 days in the Tonalpohualli.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** Their order guides the flow of energy. It's an incantation to activate a ritual.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** The key to its activation is the flint knife symbol, called the Tecpatl. It represents the date of the New Fire Ceremony.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** The question is, where will the ritual take place?

A large-scale artificial supernatural phenomenon is likely to occur in

Mexico.

Furthermore—and with no thanks to you brainless dolts—I was able

to confirm my leads that pointed to Mexico City itself.

Imagine what you overboiled eggheads could do if you spent even a

fraction of your time on something useful.

Lorentz Butterfly

**Lorentz Butterfly:** According to the changes observed in the Chaotic Energy within the area—

**Ulrich:** "A large-scale artificial supernatural phenomenon is likely to occur in Mexico."

**Ulrich:** "Furthermore—and with no thanks to you brainless dolts—I was able to confirm my leads that pointed to Mexico City itself."

**Ulrich:** "Imagine what you overboiled eggheads could do if you spent even a fraction of your time on something useful."

Conference Room, Laplace

In the meeting room, Ulrich tucks the letter away, his ferrofluid resting languid in his tank.

**Ulrich:** This is the latest update from Lorentz Butterfly.

**Ulrich:** She's named the plan "The Nine Trials of Death."

**Enigma:** Utterly ridiculous!

**Enigma:** This is already the third letter from her this month! Are you seriously planning a joint paper with this Butterfly?

**Ulrich:** Naturally, and don't worry. I'll be sure to include your name in the acknowledgements.

**Enigma:** ...

**Enigma:** Her first letter claimed that Lorentz's informants discovered a new type of arcane material, one that has been circulating across the globe since our reversal to 1920.

**Enigma:** The Manus has been using it to revive the dead, smuggling the material under the guise of a common mineral pigment.

**Enigma:** In her second letter, she tracked down its source to Mexico City.

**Enigma:** And now this! Some prophecy about a supernatural phenomenon!

**Enigma:** The Butterfly's trying to provoke us, mocking us for always being a step behind Lorentz.

**Ulrich:** Do I have to remind you that she did the same thing after the third, sixth, and the seventh "Storms," Substitute Director Adler?

**Pointer:** Pardon my interruption, but you didn't call this meeting just for us to be an audience to your squabbling, did you?

Hearing the argument go on long enough, the focused researcher finally speaks up.

**Ulrich:** Certainly not.

**Ulrich:** Which reminds me, Pointer, I have something to tell you.

**Ulrich:** In accordance with Plesetsk branch tradition, we have decided to hold a party to celebrate your and Hissabeth's successful completion of your field mission here at headquarters.

**Ulrich:** Though I personally disagree with the principle.

**Ulrich:** In order to spare time for the party, I've informed the "Storm" Research Group to extend their workday to 12 hours for the week.

**Ulrich:** I do believe I've truly cracked this "humanitarian management" thing, though Researcher Regulus may disagree.

**Pointer:** I would be inclined to disagree as well.

**Pointer:** Besides, we've already been back for three weeks now.

Their chatter is cut short as the meeting room door opens. An assistant walks in along with another not-unfamiliar face.

**Enigma:** Enough about all that. Settle in, everyone. And would you get down from that table!? The meeting's about to begin.

**Enigma:** Ahem. Considering some of you never have, or have never, cared to understand Laplace's structure, I think it's necessary I fill you in.

**Enigma:** The Funding Committee and the Staff Committee are responsible for evaluating the risk of each project. Put simply, they are the ones who approve or fail your budget applications.

**Enigma:** With that said, allow me to introduce Mr. Ludwig, the new representative of the Staff Committee.

**Ludwig:** Greetings, everyone.

**Pointer:** I wasn't aware you'd recovered. Glad to see you're well, Mr. Ludwig.

The bird-headed man offers a gentle, appreciative nod.

**Ludwig:** Thank you. I will be assisting you in your research from now on.

**Ludwig:** In fact, the topic of this meeting is related to your prior discussion.

**Pointer:** Wait, don't tell me you're going to the party?

**Ludwig:** Ahaha, no, no. Though, if you think I wouldn't spoil the mood, I'd be more than happy to.

The raven sweeps his gaze across the assembled researchers.

**Ludwig:** I've read the letter from Lorentz Butterfly.

**Ludwig:** Over the years, Manus Vindictae has never ceased its pursuit of a resurrection ritual.

**Ludwig:** Many of their actions have caught our attention: searching for the scripts of Renaissance alchemists, taking control of the Fountain of Youth in Bimini—all done after a certain Cavendish descendant joined their ranks.

**Enigma:** Tsk. Sounds just like what a mad alchemist would do.

**Pointer:** But, given what we know now, they've succeeded at least once.

**Enigma:** You're speaking of Antarctica.

The mention of that event draws a long, poignant silence.

**Ludwig:** If Lorentz Butterfly can bring us some of this Manus pigment, then there's a chance we can figure out how it works.

**Ludwig:** Their ritual has brought back a person who doesn't belong to this era. Most would call it a miracle to brave the "Storm" in such a way.

**Ludwig:** Have we considered that this may also be a means to truly understanding the "Storm"?

The room erupts again.

**Pointer:** Brave the "Storm" ...

**Pointer:** Mr. Ludwig, have you considered the hypothesis that the "Storm" doesn't cause physical death?

**Ludwig:** I have. It suggests that those who disappear in the "Storm" are not technically dead.

**Enigma:** Then where do they go?

**Ludwig:** Over the years, Foundation branches around the world have reported on the reappearance of friends and family, though changed in some way and firmly of the current "Storm" era. It seems these anomalies have become increasingly frequent.

**Ludwig:** The Staff Committee is also monitoring these anomalies. We can't afford to overlook any details, no matter how insignificant they may seem.

**Enigma:** Then we can't just sit here waiting for someone to send a letter when they're in the mood for it.

**Enigma:** That is, if the Staff Committee allows us to act ...

He lets the words rest before turning to a new topic.

**Enigma:** As it happens, after the second letter, I took the initiative to commission someone to investigate.

**Enigma:** Though she's a Foundation employee, her body was made by Laplace. So, she volunteered to do some fieldwork for us.

**Enigma:** There may be the occasional "minor incident," but she's in the Timekeeper's unit, so she can be trusted.

**Ludwig:** An excellent choice. With the Timekeeper's help, she can operate without the Foundation's surveillance.

**Enigma:** Exactly. And she's particularly adept at keeping secrets.

**Ludwig:** I hope this won't involve any inhumane methods, Adler.

**Enigma:** No need. Her memory will take care of it for us, or should I say, her lack thereof.

Suburb, Mexico City

In the scorching wilderness, a glow of arcane skill flickers.

Enigma's trusted investigator teleports into the place where the flickering light had been.

**Loggerhead:** Uhh, I better double-check the destination. Mexico City, Mexico.

**Loggerhead:** Huh, but this doesn't look like a city.

**Loggerhead:** Was there something wrong with the teleport disk? Defective, I guess? I did hear that Laplace is working on a reusable version.

As she looks out with confusion at the strangeness of her point of arrival, her communicator rings.

**Enigma:** Is everything alright, Ms. Latham?

**Loggerhead:** All good, Mr. Adler. I've arrived in Mexico City.

**Loggerhead:** Please let Madam Lucy know that I'm more than happy to be helping Laplace.

**Enigma:** Madam Lucy is no longer at Laplace.

**Enigma:** Forget it. We've just received the latest letter.

On the other end, the substitute director drones through its contents.

**Enigma:** These are the possible starting points.

**Enigma:** If you encounter Manus Vindictae, avoid confrontation and prioritize your own safety, Ms. Latham.

**Loggerhead:** Don't worry about me. I've borrowed the Rumbling Roaring Box from Mr. Ulrich.

**Loggerhead:** All I have to do is turn it on, and it'll show me all the places nearby with supernatural stuff going on.

**Loggerhead:** Wait, where's my luggage?

Not far off, she spies a Desert Devil Lizard hauling her belongings away.

**Loggerhead:** ...

**Desert Devil Lizard:** ...

**Loggerhead:** ...

**Loggerhead:** Stop right there!
