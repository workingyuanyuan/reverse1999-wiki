## Story

Hallway, Cambridge University

**Annie:** Marguerite! Marguerite!

The shouts draw nearer, accompanied by rapid footsteps.

**Marguerite:** Is something the matter?

**Annie:** You left so quickly after class. I've been chasing you halfway across campus.

**Annie:** I just wanted to say this is amazing! Our project got the highest mark in the class! I know it's mostly because of you.

**Marguerite:** No. The credit belongs to you, Annie.

**Marguerite:** You designed the proposal and handled the data analysis. I merely organized the results.

The indifferent transfer student observes the girl with clinical focus.

She calibrates the muscles of her own face accordingly.

At last, she produces a smile identical to Annie's. A flawless imitation.

**Annie:** You're smiling, Marguerite. I haven't seen you smile like that before.

**Marguerite:** Why wouldn't I smile? I'm thrilled.

**Marguerite:** There's really so much that I can learn from you.

**Annie:** Me? What could you possibly learn from me?

**Marguerite:** You possess a certain gentleness.

An overabundance of empathy.

An overabundance of empathy.

**Marguerite:** And such selflessness.

A kindness indiscriminate in its application.

A kindness indiscriminate in its application.

**Marguerite:** You think about others constantly.

And therefore, easily overlooked and undervalued.

And therefore, easily overlooked and undervalued.

**Marguerite:** I've learned a great deal from our time together.

**Marguerite:** I've reached a conclusion: I intend to integrate into this university environment the way you do.

**Annie:** That was surprisingly direct.

**Annie:** To tell the truth, you're the first real friend I've made here.

**Annie:** Thank you. Thank you, Marguerite.

**Rachel:** Thank you, Marguerite!!!

**Rachel:** It's only been a month since we were approved, and we've already finished our updated fieldwork proposal.

**Marguerite:** Coalwall stayed up all night refining the first draft of the safety regulations.

**Marguerite:** Her caution and precision are the real reasons we finally passed review. Thanks to that meticulous framework, we can now set our evacuation to just ninety minutes before the "Storm."

**Rachel:** Oh, of course, she deserves equal credit. I should formally thank both of you!

**Coalwall:** You're far too kind.

**Coalwall:** I'm just as interested as you are to see where this research leads.

**Coalwall:** There are so few projects here at Laplace that have nothing to do with arcanum.

Within the genuine warmth of this interaction, the pale researcher blends in without the slightest awkwardness.

**Marguerite:** After the "Storm," most purely scientific teams disbanded. Rachel here might be the last researcher that has maintained a strictly materialist analysis of the phenomenon.

**Marguerite:** Most have already given up, as a physical explanation seemed impossible.

**Rachel:** Well, I'm glad to know there's at least someone who appreciates my vision.

**Marguerite:** Every hypothesis has its reason for existing, and if it bears the slightest chance of being true, it's worth testing.

**Marguerite:** Newton and his apple began their journey with a flash of insight. Well, perhaps several.

**Marguerite:** From watching an apple fall to deriving the law of universal gravitation, then reconstructing it under general relativity.

**Rachel:** Exactly. Proving that science can still explain the post-"Storm" world is my top priority.

**Marguerite:** Do you feel the same, Researcher Coalwall? A desire to use scientific order to understand apparent chaos?

Her near-perfect imitation of a gentle look sends shivers down Coalwall's spine.

**Coalwall:** Oh, you shouldn't ask me. My opinion isn't really relevant.

**Coalwall:** All theoretical research, to me, is just speculation from an ivory tower. I prefer more practical work.

A sudden chill falls over their conversation.

For a moment, only the scratch of pens and the tapping of keys remain.

**Rachel:** Ah!

Rachel jumps to her feet and pulls the beeping communicator from the top shelf.

**Marguerite:** This is ...

**Marguerite:** The Timekeeper just issued the 24-hour countdown to the "Storm."

**Marguerite:** This is our golden opportunity to observe circulation changes prior to the "Storm."
