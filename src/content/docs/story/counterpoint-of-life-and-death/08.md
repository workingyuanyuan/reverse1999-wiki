## Story

The "Storm" descends once more, eroding the remnants of 1987.

Hallway, Laplace

Inside the Laplace Scientific Computing Center, some researchers pause their work and stare at the absurd, distorted world outside. Others move faster, seizing this rare window to conduct experiments.

**Marguerite:** ...

**Marguerite:** The "Storm."

She casts a detached glance at the others and continues, not once breaking her stride.

"Unnecessary Information" Room

Only a few weeks have passed since she joined Laplace. She's still considered a newcomer.

But recently, something has changed. She uncovered a secret hidden within Laplace.

**Marguerite:** Here it is, archives related to Chaos Theory. The contents are riddled with errors, almost comically deliberate and unlikely to draw anyone's attention.

**Marguerite:** Yet why hide an elegant riddle here? It stands in stark contrast to this file's mediocrity.

**Marguerite:** Who could possibly decipher such a riddle, especially without any real grounding in Chaos Theory?

Only someone utterly bored with everything Laplace has to offer.

It does not take her long to filter out the interference and reach the answer.

**Marguerite:** Are these coordinates?

She invokes an arcane skill, attempting to contact a faraway place.

**Marguerite:** Whisperedwords,speak!

The first thing to emerge from the other end is a fragrance steeped in the scent of tears.

**???:** Oh! So someone found the coordinates at last.

**???:** You're the very first to decipher the puzzle I concealed within Laplace.

**Marguerite:** It wasn't much of a challenge.

**???:** Hah! Perhaps so, but once you consider the surrounding challenges, it becomes significantly more difficult.

**???:** For instance, it was hidden in a neglected file, one riddled with errors and liable to deletion at any time, and Chaos Theory stands in direct opposition to Laplace's doctrine.

**Marguerite:** So? Who exactly are you, madam?

**???:** My name and identity are irrelevant. I have lived a very long time, and such labels no longer matter to me.

**???:** I am no longer within the Laplace Scientific Computing Center, nor am I under the Foundation's jurisdiction.

**???:** I left those coordinates to invite you into a secret society hidden within Laplace—Institutum Lorentz.

**???:** Tell me, dear, would you like to find the very first butterfly that caused the "Storm" to fall?

Laplace Rehabilitation Center

The "Storm" and the first circulation observation end abruptly.

Beyond the ICU doors, Coalwall and Ulrich stand vigil over a sleeping figure.

**Coalwall:** Will she recover?

**Ulrich:** I'm afraid I have no more insight on that account than you do.

**Ulrich:** The medical personnel have relayed to me that Rachel was suffering from severe oxygen toxicity, resulting in severe respiratory distress and retinal damage. She hasn't been responsive to any attempt to wake her. In short, she's in a coma.

**Ulrich:** Her monitoring equipment indicates that during the probe's recovery, she experienced some kind of unforeseen underwater accident.

**Ulrich:** Something severed the instrument's cable, causing it to float several hundred meters away from its original coordinates. It greatly delayed our recovery time. There's no explaining how. We might as well conclude a shark decided to take it as a chew toy.

**Ulrich:** The truth of the matter will have to wait until she regains consciousness.

**Coalwall:** ...

**Ulrich:** She is also exhibiting mild symptoms of Storm Syndrome. Fortunately, the observation point was far from the epicenter of the "Storm," though thankfully her distance from the epicenter greatly reduced its effects. She handled it well.

**Coalwall:** Fortunate indeed.

**Coalwall:** Perhaps that, too, was part of the design.

The ferrofluid does not grasp her meaning.

**Ulrich:** This kind of work is simply unsuitable to fragile humans.

**Coalwall:** Mr. Ulrich, I would not wish for even you, an Awakened, to violate protocol by returning to safety at the very last moment before the "Storm" falls.

**Coalwall:** All for a single set of data.

The ferrofluid rests placidly at the center of his helmet.

**Ulrich:** But there are certain dangerous tasks we have no choice but to undertake, and we Awakened are simply better equipped.

**Ulrich:** Let's hope Researcher Rachel wakes up soon. Otherwise, our natural physics department may be reduced to just Adler. One may as well just declare the whole thing defunct.

**Ulrich:** Now then, I'm off to read Lorentz Butterfly's latest paper.

**Ulrich:** She's calculated several coordinates where supernatural phenomena may occur, suggesting a connection to the core mechanism of the "Storm."

**Ulrich:** Though I really do wonder how these provocative letters always manage to bypass security and end up on my desk.

**Coalwall:** Supernatural phenomena. That is indeed a compelling direction.

Ulrich turns toward her in a sideways glance.

**Ulrich:** You surprise me, Coalwall; I thought you had little interest in exploring arcanum theory.

**Coalwall:** Ah, well, I've simply come to understand something.

**Coalwall:** Her true objective is surprisingly simple.

The words bounce off of his glass exterior without understanding.

**Ulrich:** Who do you mean? Lorentz Butterfly?

**Coalwall:** Forget it.

**Coalwall:** Mr. Ulrich, if I had no interest in arcanum theory whatsoever, I would never have joined Laplace.

**Coalwall:** After all, the "Storm" is unquestionably an arcane phenomenon.

**Coalwall:** But I don't believe science has become obsolete in the face of that fact. The rules of the universe have simply changed beyond recognition.

**Coalwall:** It's time for science to embark on a new path.

**Ulrich:** By integrating arcanum?

**Coalwall:** Exactly.

**Ulrich:** In that much we are agreed. At Laplace, most consider Institutum Lorentz an affront to science. I, however, remain undecided.

**Ulrich:** As Lorentz Butterfly wrote in her letter:

**Only through pneuma can we understand Chaotic Energy. Only by monitoring the ebb and flow of Chaotic Energy can we "measure" the "Storm."**

**Lorentz Butterfly:** **Only through pneuma can we understand Chaotic Energy. Only by monitoring the ebb and flow of Chaotic Energy can we "measure" the "Storm."**

**Ulrich:** I don't entirely understand her methodology, but it may yet reveal the truth of our world.

**Ulrich:** Hmm. I'm curious about this Lorentz Butterfly. What sort of person do you think she is?

Coalwall gazes at her sleeping companion beyond the glass in quiet contemplation.

**Coalwall:** A genius, one unconstrained by rules, norms, maybe even basic manners. But a genius—that much no one can deny.
