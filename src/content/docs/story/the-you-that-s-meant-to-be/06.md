## Story

**Mrs. Owen:** Ah, yes, that's the Dobharchú I know.

**Mrs. Owen:** Tender yet heartless. A witch among witches. The most mysterious of all arcanists.

Rhiannon scratches her cheek, at a loss.

As a daughter, she ought to defend her mother. But she knows Mrs. Owen is right.

**Mrs. Owen:** And then? I heard you fought with Dobharchú the eve of your sixteenth birthday and stormed off.

**Rhiannon:** No. I mean, I wouldn't call that a fight, exactly.

**Rhiannon:** Mam was only trying to comfort me. I just didn't want to listen, so I left.

**Hotelier:** Oh! Ran away from home, did you?

**Rhiannon:** N-No, nothing like that!

**Rhiannon:** I wanted to see home again. Our old home. Just for a visit to pay my respects to Tad and the others.

Trouble always comes without warning.

**Aderyn:**ŜvebiStay!

The leaf-covered ground gives way, sending her plummeting into a pit. Only a quick burst of arcane energy keeps her from hitting the bottom.

**Glyndŵr:** Coo?

The Adar Llwch Gwin, far too large to fall in, crouches at the edge. He peers down and calls out, worried.

**Aderyn:** I'm fine.

The pit reeks of rot and metal. The sight of a rope net rigged at the bottom sends a bead of cold dripping down her back.

**Aderyn:** What is that?

**???:** Think we got something! Go on, check if it's a big one!

Before she can process what's happening, the sound of footsteps and clanging metal closes in from outside the trees.

**Aderyn:** Glyndŵr! Take them away!

She grabs a fledgling from her shoulder and holds it up, trying to get it to fly, but the malicious voices boom too close for escape.

**Aderyn:** Quick! Turn invisible!

Glyndŵr has already concealed himself within the light, but the two fledglings clinging to Aderyn have yet to master that trick.

Several rough, avaricious faces crowd around the pit's edge.

**Poacher I:** Oi! It's a girl, and she's got some chicks!

**Poacher II:** That tip-off was money well-spent, it was. Young Adar Llwch Gwins ...

**Poacher II:** Just imagine the Sharpodonties we can get for those on the black market!

A hand snatches at her shoulder. The frightened fledglings crouch and puff their feathers.

Aderyn's body reacts before her mind can.

She throws herself to the far side of the pit, but she's not fast enough to escape the greedy, calloused fingertips that graze her collar.

**Poacher I:** Tsk!

As she sees the glint of drawn weapons above her, a bold idea takes shape.

**Aderyn:** W-Wait! Don't ... Don't hurt them or me.

She tilts her head back. Moonlight filters through the canopy above to highlight her mud-smeared face and the helpless, panicked expression she puts on display.

Aderyn tightens her hold on the fledglings. Perhaps it's her imagination, but the little birds seem to understand.

**Aderyn:** The ... The bigger ones sell for more, right? That's what you're after—full-grown birds?

She gulps to sell the idea that she's steeling herself to divulge a secret.

**Aderyn:** I know a place. There's a nest, more than one bird. If you let me and the fledglings go ...

The poachers look at each other with narrowed eyes, weighing her words.

**Poacher I:** Lying to us won't end well for you.

**Aderyn:** Of course! I'm a hunter, so I know these woods. I came across the nest by accident. It's well hidden. In ... In a ruin, to the east.

**Aderyn:** I-It's where I found these ones! Look!

Convinced by the fledglings in her arms, they drop a rope into the pit.

**Poacher II:** Climb up.

Aderyn settles the fledglings back onto her wand, grabs the rope, and pulls herself out.

She may have made it out of the trap and back into the moonlight, but she can't help feeling as though she's stepped into a far more dangerous cage.

**Aderyn:** Th-This way. Let's go.

She points to what she remembers to be east, keeping just the right amount of fear in her voice.

**Aderyn:** It's a bit of a walk.

**Poacher I:** Don't try nothing.

The lead poacher pats his holstered weapon, letting its presence finish the threat.

**Aderyn:** Of course, as long as you're willing to spare me and my birds.

**Aderyn:** A-And if you could deal with the large one at the nest, I'd be even more grateful.

**Poacher II:** Got in over your head with the big one, did you?

**Aderyn:** Yes. I mean, I stole these two from the nest, so ...

**Poacher I:** Oh? They say Adar Llwch Gwins are known to hold grudges.

**Aderyn:** Yes, exactly. That's why the big one won't leave me alone.

**Aderyn:** But after hatching them, caring for them, I can't bring myself to give them back.

**Poacher II:** Ha! You've got nerve, I'll give you that.

The poachers shove her forward, and they set off.

Aderyn leads them with a limp and a nervous smile on her face. Every step she takes weaves another thread into a dangerous lie.

Her palms are clammy, but the chilly wind dries them somewhat. She wants to believe it's Glyndŵr, silently watching from above.

**Aderyn:** I have to do this.

She has to keep up the act, either until an opportunity presents itself or she's backed into a corner.
