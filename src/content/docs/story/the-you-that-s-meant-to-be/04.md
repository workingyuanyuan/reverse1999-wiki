## Story

Tywyll Estate

Her father used to say with a smile that fire was the greediest child of all.

Aderyn never agreed, for she had the biggest appetite, the most strength, and the sturdiest body. How could a few little flames rival the greatest Tywyll of them all?

**Aderyn:** Tad ... Mam! Where are you?!

The doorframe etched with the heights of generations of Tywyll children, the birdhouse built to mark their first Adar Llwch Gwin's hatch day, the oak tree her mother had brought all the way from her hometown when it was just a sapling ...

The heat and smoke force tears from her eyes.

**Aderyn:** You were right, Tad. It really is the greediest.

She staggers toward the blaze. The arcane barrier she conjures barely reaches her skin before it's seared away.

**Aderyn:** Ow!

The flames devouring the manor taunt her with the crackling and groans of long-dried wood.

**Aderyn:** Where is everyone?

**Aderyn:** Glyndŵr!

The bird's cry cuts through the heavy smoke, letting a sliver of pale moonlight fall on the center of the fire.

**Aderyn:** ...!

**Aderyn:** Tad ... Tad!

She tears away from her original path and veers toward the heart of the flames.

**Aderyn's Father:** Stay back!

She halts. There's something in his shout, something ragged and broken that she's never heard from him before.

**Aderyn:** ...

His contorted face is covered in sweat and blackened by smoke.

She can't help but take two steps forward. Her eyes strain to see past the flames.

**Aderyn:** Hmm?

Her foot catches something soft.

**Aderyn:** Gwen?

Her cousin lies still on the scorched earth. Her lovely red locks are now charred and brittle.

She's curled up as if slumbering. No, she's clutching something, something small and gray.

Its young feathers—feathers that once cut through wind and caught the light—are now limp and dull.

**Aderyn:** Gwen? Gwen? Get up. You have to run!

Aderyn's voice scrapes against her throat like a rusted blade.

She rubs her eyes with shaky hands, trying to see through the wall of fire.

**Aderyn's Father:** Aderyn, run!

But she pushes through the flames and sees everything.

Every shred of hope turns to ash and blows away on the wind.

She can make out the dark silhouettes of some other Tywylls amid the flames. Some are standing, while others, like Gwen, have fallen.

**Aderyn:** ...

**Aderyn:** Ah!

Her mouth gapes as if to scream, but nothing comes out.

**Outsider:** Grab her! She's an arcanist!

Aderyn spins around. The slumber incantation she casts is clumsy and unpracticed; they slump to the ground, but their eyes close for only a moment.

They stagger back up and raise their axes toward her. Their smoke-reddened eyes blaze out of their ash-covered faces like something from a nightmare.

**Poacher:** Are you gonna let a little brat get the best of you?!

The outsiders burst into laughter, as if her burning home was nothing more than a bonfire to them.

**Aderyn's Father:** Don't touch her!

Her father struggles against his restraints, but the enchanted rope holds him fast.

Aderyn raises her wand breathless, only to find that sparks have already reduced her birch branch to charcoal.

**Aderyn:** T-Tad ...

**Poacher:** How touching. We could let her go, of course.

A sinister grin spreads across his face.

**Poacher:** You call yourselves protectors. But just how far do those ideals extend, I wonder.

**Poacher:** Quite the flock you've got here. Invisibility, speed, loyalty—ancient arcanum's gift to the modern world. Worth a whole air fleet, the lot of 'em are.

**Poacher:** We'll let one of you survive for each one of the birds you give up.

**Aderyn's Father:** ...

**Aderyn:** The-The birds?

Her gaze shifts toward Glyndŵr.

The strongest adult Adar Llwch Gwin of the Tywyll family.

Her father's friend and partner. Her guardian and playmate.

**Aderyn's Father:** Aderyn ...

His voice is helpless, resigned.

It's the same sort of half-scolding, half-indulgent sigh she'd hear whenever she misbehaved.

**Aderyn's Father:** Glyndŵr! Get her out of here!

The giant bird surges upward in one sweep of his wings. Before Aderyn can even react, her collar is yanked tight from behind, and her feet leave the ground.

**Aderyn:** No, don't! Stop it!

**Glyndŵr:** Coo ...

Her struggling only makes the bird beat his wings harder.

**Aderyn:** Glyndŵr! Put me down! I'm not leaving!

She knows it's no use. A full-grown Adar Llwch Gwin can fly faster than even the strongest of gales. Through teary eyes, Aderyn watches her home, once lush green turned burning red, disappear from view.

**Aderyn:** \*whimper\*

**Aderyn:** Ioffermyfleshaspledge:grantmesightofthisworldandtheworldsbeyond!

**Glyndŵr:** ...?!

Glyndŵr opens his talons as he comes down for a landing, letting Aderyn tumble into the soft grass.

He folds his wings and stands over her like a proud gray oak. His golden eyes are looking down on the weeping girl.

**Aderyn:** Go. Just-Just go!

She pushes herself up from the grass and shoves at the giant bird with all her strength, but Aderyn can't move him an inch.

**Aderyn:** I'm fine; save THEM! Please! Go!

Her broken cries scatter in the wind. Glyndŵr's golden pupils narrow into pinpoints.

He opens his wings just a little and moves instinctively toward the trembling girl.

**Aderyn:** Please ...

Aderyn reaches her tear-streaked hands up to lay them against his beak.

**Glyndŵr:** Coo.

He gives Aderyn a long, sympathetic look.

Then he spreads his wings wide. The resulting gust rolls across the sea of grass like a tide as he takes to the now moonlit sky and flies back toward the rising smoke and orange-glowing flames.

Aderyn crumples onto the ground. She's left with only the crisp midnight wind to comfort her; it tears her heart open, and through it, they wail together.
