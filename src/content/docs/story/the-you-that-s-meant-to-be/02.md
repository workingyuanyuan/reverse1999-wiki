## Story

**Aderyn:**HereIhumblystandatthecleftoftheearth,beseechingAnnwn'smercy.

**Aderyn:**Ioffermyfleshaspledge:grantmesightofthisworldandtheworldsbeyond!

Her eyes meet another's in the darkness, flashing with a golden hue.

**Aderyn:** Mhmm.

Nothing. Not so much as a breeze.

By the Mirror Lake

Light shimmers across the water. The giant bird's half-translucent tail feathers catch the color of it and quiver.

**Glyndŵr:** ...

**Aderyn:** Glyndŵr, did it work? Did the bond form?

The bird stares back at her without response.

**Aderyn:** Again? Hmm. Did I mess the incantation up somehow? Where'd I go wrong?

She draws a deep breath and points her wand at the lake.

**Aderyn:** Water ... Change the water.

The calm lake ripples as her incantation spreads across it. Then the water churns, and what rises from its surface is no longer mist.

**Aderyn:** ...

Colorful crystalline bubbles float upward, and the light they refract dances with the shadows on her face.

\*pop\*

The Adar Llwch Gwin tracks the rising bubbles with wide eyes. With but a flap of its wings, the bubbles burst one by one, falling back into the lake and taking the dreamy scene with them.

**Adar Llwch Gwin Fledgling:** Coo!

The little bird beats its wings to take off from the wand and settle atop Aderyn's head.

**Aderyn:** Stop it.

She lifts the fledgling from her hair and holds it close.

**Aderyn:** I still can't bond with Glyndŵr.

**Aderyn:** "A Tywyll of sixteen must journey far."

**Aderyn:** "With blessings and a dear companion's sight ..."

**Aderyn:** How am I to carry on the tradition if I can't so much as ...

With a sigh, she returns the little bird to her wand.

Then she turns to face the still-silent Glyndŵr.

**Aderyn:** You still won't accept me. Am I ... Am I still not worthy, Glyndŵr? Is that it?

Aderyn murmurs her worries as she strokes the Adar Llwch Gwin's smooth breast feathers.

Just as she's done after each of her many failed attempts before.

**Glyndŵr:** ...

Glyndŵr looks down at the dejected little witch and lets out a rumble unbefitting of a bird.

**Aderyn:** Nothing for it but to keep trying, I suppose.

But that inner door, as ever, stays shut.

**Aderyn:** Let's go home, Glyndŵr.

**Glyndŵr:** Coo.

Glyndŵr stoops obediently onto the ground, letting Aderyn climb onto his back.

Tylwyth Teg Berry Garden

\*bang\*

A sudden thudding against the wooden door startles the drowsy birds sheltering under the eaves of an otherwise quiet orchard.

The vine-choked latch rattles under the impact, no longer strong enough to contain the fearsome creature within—

Which promptly rolls its small and rotund form out.

**???:** Coo ...

Though freshly hatched, its feathered wings still wet and sticky, even, the hatchling waddles out for an adventure.

**Adar Llwch Gwin Fledgling:** Coo?

As if sensing trouble on the breeze, it rolls its round little body to one side, only to bump straight into the path of the very trouble it was avoiding.

**Adar Llwch Gwin Fledgling:** Coo!

The hatchling lets out a shrill chirp as the gust from Glyndŵr's wings sends it tumbling toward the well.

Before it can fall into the ice-cold water, though, a hand catches it.

**Aderyn:** You ...

She cups the tiny creature in her palms and looks it over, incredulous.

Unlike Glyndŵr's solid beak, this one is half-translucent, like a horse chestnut bud catching the early spring light—pale, curved, and new.

**Aderyn:** A newborn Adar Llwch Gwin?

**Adar Llwch Gwin Fledgling:** Gah!

**Aderyn:** But the nesting box's been empty for years. What winds brought you here, little lad?

It wriggles furiously, but there's no escaping the practiced bird trainer's hands.

**Aderyn:** Don't care for being called "little lad," do you?

Aderyn, unbothered by the sticky membrane coating the plump little bird, pinches both wings delicately and flips it over.

**Aderyn:** Oh. A girl! Do pardon me, miss.

**Aderyn:** Hmm. How about "Bechan"?

A hatchling this young has no understanding of her language. So, the gesture is just a silly trifle.

But the little creature struggles again, and tender as it looks, her beak is already sharp enough to draw faint lines across her palm.

Aderyn pulls her hand back. The hatchling seizes the moment and lifts her wings wide—

—only to crash helplessly to the ground.

**Aderyn:** Pfff.

Bechan rolls across the ground without any coordination, causing itself to pick up a solid coating of dust and debris.

**Bechan:** Coo ...

Aderyn scoops up the pitiful creature and brushes her off.

**Aderyn:** Not so fun being covered in dirt, is it?

She looks at Bechan with a bemused, almost maternal smile. The damp membrane and dust have mixed into clumps of mud caking her wings.

**Aderyn:** Don't you worry, little Bechan. I'll get you tidied right up.

She cleans the hatchling using some well water warmed with "Drako Drought," then dries her delicate down with "Flamo Flurry."

Three years without a hatchling to tend to, yet her hands still remember every step.

**Bechan:** Coo ...

The little bird sleepily sunbathing atop her wand is proof enough of that.

**???:** It's sunny in London, for a change.

**Dobharchú:** You should come to the witches' fair sometime, Aderyn. I'm certain you'd enjoy it.

A voice, attempting to speak with warmth yet unable to conceal the stiffness beneath, comes through the oil-and-grit-clouded water.

**Dobharchú:** Oh, this water is positively filthy. What have you been up to?

**Aderyn:** Mam.

Aderyn leans over the basin and stirs the water through which her mother is speaking, then carries the sleepy little Bechan over to it.

**Aderyn:** I, uh, I found her today, right outside the coop.

**Dobharchú:** Today? But whose could she be?

Dobharchú touches her chin as she looks past her daughter to the giant bird standing silent behind her.

**Dobharchú:** Glyndŵr?

**Glyndŵr:** ...

He replies with a huff.

**Dobharchú:** I'll get to the bottom of this. Remember to keep her warm.

**Aderyn:** Got it. So, uhm, when will you return?

The subject change is far from graceful, but when it comes to her mother, nothing ever is.

**Dobharchú:** Soon.

A shop bell chimes through the water. Dobharchú has stepped into a quiet store.

**Dobharchú:** I'm picking out some things for you to take on your journey. It's almost time, after all.

**Aderyn:** My journey ...

**Dobharchú:** A new wand, a tent enchanted with space-expansion, or you could pick your own. Whatever you'd like.

**Aderyn:** I ... I'd like ... No, I couldn't possibly ask for anything.

**Aderyn:** I don't deserve to go, Mam.

**Aderyn:** I failed. Again.

**Dobharchú:** ...

Aderyn's mouth opens and shuts as she struggles to find the words she wants to say.

**Aderyn:** Could you tell me ...

**Aderyn:** Was I too cowardly? You know that night? Is ... Is that why Glyndŵr still won't bond with me?
