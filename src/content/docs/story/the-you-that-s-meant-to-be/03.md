## Story

**Mrs. Owen:** Poor thing.

Mrs. Owen dabs the corner of her eye with a handkerchief.

**Mrs. Owen:** I remember that night, the night the great Tywyll family scattered to the winds.

Like leaves fallen from an oak tree.

**Mrs. Owen:** I helped the surviving Tywylls move after the poachers had been driven off, you know.

**Mrs. Owen:** Some stayed in this very hotel until their new homes were built.

**Rhiannon:** Others traveled further. Cardiff, Dublin, London ... Some even crossed the pond.

**Mrs. Owen:** It was just Dobharchú that hid away with the birds, and you.

Mrs. Owen picks a bilberry from the fruit dish and holds it up to Bechan's beak.

**Mrs. Owen:** I know just how hard it is for a teenager to live the same day over and over. My own daughter could hardly stay put for two days without getting restless.

**Mrs. Owen:** Your mother said you were only a child then, and you did everything you could.

**Mrs. Owen:** Why would you think that you weren't brave enough?

**Dobharchú:** Why would you think that you weren't brave enough?

**Aderyn:** If only I'd been braver, if I hadn't frozen, if I hadn't been thrown from the fight ...

**Aderyn:** Could I have saved them? Would Tad still be ...

**Dobharchú:** Aderyn!

Dobharchú's smile fades, and she says her name with an almost scolding tone.

**Dobharchú:** You were twelve years old.

**Dobharchú:** This should never have been your burden to bear.

**Aderyn:** ...

**Aderyn:** You always shut me out like this, like I wasn't even part of it!

**Aderyn:** What if—even if there's only one chance in a thousand, even ten thousand—

**Aderyn:** What if I could've defeated those poachers?!

**Dobharchú:** Aderyn ...

The water sloshes out of the basin, and her words dissolve into the spillage.

**Aderyn:** ...

Aderyn purses her lips and steps back, pulling her feet away from the dirty water creeping toward her.

**Aderyn:** I ...

In the end, nothing comes out.

After a moment, she turns toward the fading sunlight, pushes open the orchard gate, and walks into the woods without looking back.

Deep in the Forest

Years of neglect have seen nature reclaim the path to the place she once called home.

Now she struggles through the waist-high grass, guided only by moonlight.

**Aderyn:** ...

She pushes away the wild overgrowth. The rustling sound rolls outward in waves all the way to the forest's edge.

**Aderyn:** Glyndŵr? Are you there?

A cold gleam of moonlight sweeps across the empty corner behind her and disappears.

**Aderyn:** Just like back then ...

**Aderyn:** A moonless night with a chilly breeze.

**Aderyn:** I would've been bumbling around in the dark if it weren't for my arcane skill's glow.

**Aderyn:** I offer my flesh ... as pledge: grant me sight ... of ...

**Aderyn:** ... this world and ... the worlds beyond?

Aderyn stumbles through the incantation, and energy pours out from the tip of the birch branch she's holding.

The arcane skill forms a string of incomprehensible symbols, then sinks into Glyndŵr's feathers.

**Aderyn:** Well? Did it work, Glyndŵr?!

For a moment, her eager face is illuminated not by moonlight, but by an arcane glow.

**Aderyn:** The bonding—it worked, right? Please tell me it worked!

**Glyndŵr:** Coo.

He raises his wings and preens his feathers ruffled by arcane energy.

**Aderyn:** Mhmm.

Aderyn closes her eyes. She tries to shut out the world, tries to perceive with her heart, and yet ...

There's nothing to perceive.

Her elders had always said the incantation flows between witches and their bonded Adar Llwch Gwin. But where there should be a stream, she can feel only the void of space between them.

**Aderyn:** I ... I see.

Glyndŵr's silence drives her mad. Her cheeks puff, and her arms fling outward—an attempt at some kind of bird body language.

**Aderyn:** You're real stingy, you know that?

**Aderyn:** I thought we were friends! But you—you won't even let me borrow your sight! Is a bird's-eye view too much to ask for?!

**Glyndŵr:** Coo?

**Aderyn:** Don't you "coo" me! I'm not forgiving you, you-you big birdbrain!

She slumps to the ground with a huff. Glyndŵr looks down at her, then settles to give her a soft wall of feathers to rest against.

And just like that, all is forgiven.

**Aderyn:** Is it because you think I'm not strong enough? But I've already mastered 32 arcane skills! That's got to count for something!

Glyndŵr huffs out a warm breath. Aderyn can read the incredulous "really?" written on his face.

He had, after all, been there the day she burned off her father's eyebrows with "Drako Drought."

**Aderyn:** Alright, "mastered" might be a stretch.

**Aderyn:** But I ... I'm still really strong, aren't I? Why, one day, I'll be as strong as the goddess Rhiannon herself!

Aderyn makes a fist and swings it through the air.

\*bang\*

**Aderyn:** Huh?!

**Aderyn:** ...!

A fiery glow rises from the horizon beyond the surrounding trees.

Another explosion. Red and orange bloom across the sky.

For a moment, her mind goes blank, then adrenaline yanks her back.

**Aderyn:** That's ... No!

**Aderyn:** Glyndŵr! Quick, fly back! Go without me!

Without a moment's hesitation, he spreads his wings and vanishes into the night.

Aderyn's little legs fight the knee-high grass as she sprints toward the blazing sky.

She still clings to a shred of hope.

But the fire swallowing her home threatens to burn that shred of hope away.

**Aderyn:** Fire ...

**Aderyn:** Tad! Mam! Gwen!

The sight of strangers stops her from calling out any more loved ones' names.

**Outsider:** A straggler, is it? Don't let her get away!

She tightens her grip on her birch branch.
