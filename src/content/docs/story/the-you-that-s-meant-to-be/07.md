## Story

Darkness gives way to dawn.

The still-sleepy sunrise bathes everything in a pale glow.

And as they reach the path's end, in that radiance capable of bringing every secret to light, a scorched ruin looms ahead.

**Aderyn:** Here, here it is.

The breeze carries a familiar scent.

No longer the sweetness of the orchard, it's all ash and grief, soaked so deep into the soil and rubble that no amount of rain could hope to wash it away.

A few burned and broken walls—skeletal remains poking out from a grave—are all that remain of her home.

**Aderyn:** ...

Sounds she had hoped to forget come back to her: the crackling of burning wood, her own panicked cries, and the Adar Llwch Gwins' mournful calls.

Whatever joy her sixteenth birthday should have brought is replaced by something else—a marrow-deep guilt she'd never been able to shake.

Now, in this charred, dead ruin, it wakes again, far more terrible than before.

**Aderyn:** If only I'd been braver, if I hadn't frozen ...

**Aderyn:** If Glyndŵr hadn't needed to rescue me, if he'd just stayed with Tad, he might've ... might've ...

Again and again, she's punished herself for this sin for not being "enough."

**???:** Leave, Aderyn.

A childlike voice in the back of her mind, caught between calm and crying, commands her.

**???:** Turn invisible. Turn back.

**???:** Go back to the orchard. Reinforce the rituals. You can't afford another mistake, so leave it to someone who won't make one. Wait for Mam. She'll handle it.

There's the voice again, timid yet tempting. She can't deny that its logic is sound.

**Aderyn:** Invisibility ...

She finds safety in the idea; like a siren call, it feels dark and yet almost irresistible.

She can feel the arcane energy she inherited from her mother stirring. It's desperate to conceal her, desperate to wipe away every trace of her presence.

One incantation—one of the few she knows by heart—and she would vanish. She could leave these poachers to a stronger, more reliable witch.

**Aderyn:** I ...

**???:** Now, Aderyn, go.

Her steps slow, coming just shy of a complete stop.

Her grip on her wand tightens, and a faint glow manifests around her trembling hand. Just as she's about to release the arcane energy, though, she catches the glint of her little companion's eyes.

**Bechan:** Coo!

Bechan's wide, bright eyes reflect her own hesitant face. Layered on top of it is another pair of eyes, looking right back at her.

They're the same eyes that watched her from the scorched ground, right until the bitter end. They're eyes filled with both despair and resolve—eyes that, despite it all, never backed down.

**???:** Leave. You have to leave before it's too late.

**Aderyn:** No.

She grips her wand. The arcane glow has expanded to engulf her white knuckles.

**Aderyn:** I could make a mess of everything again.

**Aderyn:** And maybe I'm not really sure what I ought to do, but ...

**Aderyn:** I won't just lead the danger elsewhere and push my responsibility onto someone else.

**???:** ...

**???:** I'm proud of you, Aderyn.

The voice fades.

**Poacher II:** What're you dragging your feet for? Come on, get moving!

She draws a deep breath. The cool air, thick with ash and memories, fills her lungs and sweeps all thoughts of retreat from her mind.

**Aderyn:** This is it, gentlemen.

**Poacher I:** Here?

The poachers whoop in triumph and begin searching the charred ruin for their quarry.

Now.

Crimson flames shoot from her wand, ensnaring the wrist of the bow-wielding poacher.

**Poacher II:** Aaaaghhhhh!!

The bow falls to the ground as he clutches his arm and begins rolling in the dirt.

Aderyn doesn't hesitate. A second Drako Drought is already charging in her wand.

**Poacher I:** Damn it. I knew you were up to something.

The poacher's hand flies to the weapon at his hip.

**Aderyn:** DrakoDrought!

\*bang\*

Her flames shatter the projectile just as it leaves the barrel. She'd never encountered a firearm before, never really understood how it worked. The shower of hot sparks and metal slag catches her off-guard.

**Aderyn:** Urgh!

The surprise sends her stumbling back.

**Poacher I:** Some guide you turned out to be.

**Poacher I:** Those two chicks of yours will more than make up for the trouble, though, wouldn't you say?

Just as the leering poacher raises his weapon again, the winds shift without warning.

**Aderyn:** Glyndŵr!

The gray silhouette plunges in a headlong dive.

**Poacher I:** The net!

A large net with an eerie shimmer drops right into Glyndŵr's path.

\*buzzzzz\*

The sound it emits makes Aderyn's skin crawl, but it is the cry of pain and fury that Glyndŵr lets out that truly breaks her heart.

**Glyndŵr:** \*shriek\*

No longer able to sustain his harmonization of light and shadow, the illusion blurring him falls, leaving Glyndŵr fully exposed.

**Poacher II:** It worked! It really worked! A full-grown Adar Llwch Gwin—a live one, at that!

**Poacher I:** So that bloody brat didn't ... No, no, she tricked us, but it don't matter one bit! We've nabbed ourselves a big one!

The poachers celebrate their catch, but Aderyn doesn't move just yet.

**Aderyn:** Phew.

She bends down.

Her fingers sink deep into the blackened earth in search of a pulse—of the last sign of life in her long-dead home.

**Aderyn:** Our defensive ritual ...

The arcane energy thins to the faintest threads, weaving through the scarred dirt and remnant ash to find a way up.

**Aderyn:** Water,returntothisearthitsstolenpeace.

A low rumble rises from below.

**Poacher I:** What's that?

He grips his gun and raises it toward Aderyn, but he's too late.

One by one, azure incantations blaze across the dead earth, and an underground river breaks through, soft yet unyielding.

**Poacher II:** Wh-What! \*gasp\*

**Poacher I:** N-No! \*gurgle\*

The water coils around their heads like a python, seizing their air with brutal tenderness.

As the poachers go down, Aderyn sprints toward her ensnared companion.

**Aderyn:** Glyndŵr!

She throws herself over the fallen bird and cuts the sinister net away with precise arcane skill, taking care not to injure Glyndŵr.

Once freed, Glyndŵr bows his head to nudge Aderyn's forehead with his large beak.

**???:** Glyndŵr? Aderyn?!
