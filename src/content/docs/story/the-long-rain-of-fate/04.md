## Story

The bluntness of the question cuts through the fragile calm.

**Beryl:** On edge?

**Félicienne:** Sulky, anxious, irritated ... pick whichever you prefer.

**Félicienne:** But that is how you're acting.

**Beryl:** ...

**Félicienne:** Oh, a rare sight indeed.

**Félicienne:** The ever-composed Beryl Bouanich—even when revealing her terrible taste, she hides it beneath a false calm.

**Félicienne:** But how to explain such an absurd phenomenon ...?

**Félicienne:** The visible disappointment, the mockery with no place to go.

**Félicienne:** You hold an answer only you know, reveal it to no one, yet expect us all to see straight through to it.

**Félicienne:** Your arrogance is well hidden, I'll give you that, yet still you long for someone who can see what you see.

**Félicienne:** Everyone else is simply too slow, aren't they?

**Félicienne:** \*cough\*

Félicienne gasps for breath.

She has done her best to conceal her weakness in this confrontation.

**Beryl:** ...

Beryl smiles again.

**Beryl:** So I am anxious, then ...

This is not the smile she uses to navigate social worlds, but a smile born from within, vivid enough to change her whole expression.

In the faint lines at the corner of her eyes, the shattered gemstone glimmers softly.

**Beryl:** None can force "me" to make a choice. All those possibilities unknown to "me" ...

**Beryl:** The only one who holds the power to decide is a "me" that has not yet come to be ...

**Beryl:** Hm. Then it is this new "self" which unsettles me.

She smiles, gathers the fragments in her palm, and hands them to Brume.

**Beryl:** Adèle, you search for meaning. Would you like to keep them?

**Brume:** ...

**Beryl:** When did I begin to no longer need "me" and the possibilities it brings?

Crystal Shop, Paris

Memories surface before her.

**Beryl:** Before I was born, the Parlor of Crystals was nothing more than an old, cramped shop.

**Beryl:** My mother, facing the difficulties of survival, took the box of crystals passed down from her mother.

**Beryl:** She had no carefully laid business plan, no network built over generations ...

**Beryl:** But she knew the crystals were of good quality and would fetch a fair price.

**Beryl:** Just as they had for those distant relatives.

**Beryl:** And with the addition of a few small gifts, many would gladly help her sustain her life.

**Clairine:** I saw a window set into a stone wall.

**Clairine:** It was damp, cold, but ...

**Clairine:** Outside the window was a clear blue sky, where all light gathered.

The woman quickly sorts through the tangled thoughts in her mind as she describes what the orbuculum revealed.

**Beryl:** She attempted to push beyond her limits to reach the truth.

**Guest:** What does that mean?

**Clairine:** It's best not to rush. Understanding fate's hidden language requires patience and insight.

**Clairine:** An ill-matched scene. It means your life may need a turning point, or that you may soon fall into hardship.

**Beryl:** Fate was stingy with her—the image it provided ever unclear. She had to choose her words carefully.

**Beryl:** —Gently, leaving room for retreat.

**Guest:** Madame Clairine, do you mean ...

**Clairine:** Your life is indeed improving. The turning point has already arrived. You must be cautious moving forward.

**Clairine:** It will determine whether you live outside the window, or inside it.

**Guest:** Oh! I knew it! Those greedy parasites ... Madame Clairine, thank you for the warning!

**Clairine:** No need to thank me. This is but a small repayment for your generosity.

The client leaves, taking the yellow crystals he purchased with him.

**Beryl:** This was her tactic. After the sale, she would offer a complimentary divination to draw in the curious.

**Beryl:** She interpreted vague symbols, disguising fate's turn as gentle care.

**Beryl:** She even led them to believe that if they followed her guidance, what the pendulum showed was only a possibility, not destiny.

**Beryl:** Just like the window my mother saw.

**Beryl:** It was a window of a prison cell, and the sky outside was the last view that client would see at the end of his life.

**Beryl:** For he would soon face the guillotine.

**Beryl:** The "parasites" he spoke of would later join with the police to steal the key to the treasury he guarded.

**Beryl:** How do I know this? Let me tell you.

Bouanich Residence

**Clairine:** Everything's fine now.

The woman gently pats the wailing infant in her arms, pacing the room with a furrowed brow.

**Beryl:** The crystal business had finally stabilized, and my mother, no longer needing to worry about survival, started a family.

**Beryl:** But for a long time after my birth, my crying never ceased.

**Beryl:** My mother lost sleep, grew thinner day by day, and the business, which had just begun to flourish, nearly died back to a stump.

**Beryl:** Her modest divination revealed nothing to her, so she turned her hopes to a doctor.

The doctor examined the infant carefully. After a long silence, he removed his stethoscope and shook his head.

**Doctor:** ... I'm sorry, madame. There is only so much medicine can do. I see nothing wrong with the child.

**Beryl:** Fate has no pity. It had forced endless histories into a mind not yet ready to hold them.

**Beryl:** Heh ... crying was inevitable.

**Beryl:** When I began to speak, to walk, to step beyond the estate—when I saw the very same people I had seen in those visions ...

**Beryl:** Only then did I understand what I saw was a glimpse of the future.

**Beryl:** A law that transcends time and space, reenacting the past, present, and future in an endless cacophony.

**Beryl:** Among its noise, the history of the family called "Bouanich" unfolded before me.

**Beryl:** My gift led my mother to believe I was not suited for public education.

**Beryl:** So she kept me at home and taught me everything she herself had learned.

The cover of The Revelations of Lenormand hits the table with a thud, breathing out a puff of golden dust into the air.

**Clairine:** "A voice declared to me that the moment of judgment had come for the crowned one."

**Clairine:** That was Napoleon's defeat in the Lenormand prophecy ...

**Beryl:** I saw something too, Maman.

**Beryl:** Brilliant crystals will glitter in our parlor, and customers will come one after another, drawn by our name.

**Beryl:** Our storefront will be elegant and luxurious, and raw minerals from all over the world will all be within our reach.

**Clairine:** You mean to say ...

**Beryl:** Maman, shall we bring my vision to reality?
