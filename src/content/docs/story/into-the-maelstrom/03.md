## Story

Field, Steppe

Igor takes the reins, his eyes narrowing as he stares up toward the North Star through reddened and weary eyes.

Since arriving on the steppes—no, since leaving São Paulo—he has not slept a full night.

The black-violet night surrounds him. He leads no army now and follows no orders. He simply rides toward the North Star.

**Villager:** ♫ My little horse and I, we ride through the golden fields beneath the sky. How sorrowfully my heart sighs. ♫

A song drifts along from the river, giving the night a sudden peace and life.

**Grigory:** ♫ I hear the wind whispering on and on. ♫

A low, murmured hum finds the quiet soldier. Heartened by the singing across the water, it grows clearer.

**Grigory:** ♫ I hear the river singing me a song, as if trying to say ... ♫

His face may be overcast, but his singing is as bright and clear as the moon.

**Igor:** ♫ The river is trying to say, "The boy will never come back home from far away." ♫

**Igor:** Who taught you that song?

**Grigory:** My grandfather taught my father, sir, and my father taught me.

**Grigory:** Though he only managed to teach me this one song.

**Igor:** You're a man of many talents, Grigory.

**Grigory:** Thank you, sir. I never would've expected that you sing as well.

The soldier's surprise is plain. His face softens, just as his voice did.

**Igor:** There is no Cossack who cannot sing.

**Igor:** In the old days, after a hard-won victory, we would sing that very song. Who knows how old it truly is?

**Igor:** ♫ The Black Sea's waves rise high. Warships light up the night. ♫

**Igor:** ♫ Douse the lamps, sink the boats. Cossack honor will never die! ♫

The rhythmic tune taps a rhythm against the air.

**Igor:** That song reminds me of the prisiadka dance. Do you know the steps?

**Grigory:** Put a gun to my head, sir, and I still wouldn't know my left foot from my right. At least when it comes to dancing.

**Igor:** Then it's not just your father's nose that scares off the ladies, is it?

Igor laughs, catching the poor soldier off-guard, but soon after, the laughter is returned, only ended by a sudden chill in the night air.

Cold as the night before a battle.

**Igor:** A true Cossack doesn't hold back his thoughts. Listen to his song, and you know his heart.

**Igor:** If the next day's fight looks grim, we drink ourselves into a stupor and sing that song.

The admiral breathes the crisp air deep into his chest.

**Igor:** ♫ Cold wind blows beyond the hill. So sharp it silenced the birds' trill. Trees shiver in the winter's chill. ♫

**Igor:** ♫ Through the winter night snow falls low, where lies a thick shroud made of snow. A Cossack's bones lie deep below. ♫

Al Muntar

**Officers:** ♫ ... made of snow. A Cossack's bones lie deep below. With his last breath, the Cossack pleads. Build him a high grave mound where he can be laid. ♫

**Officers:** ♫ Like the soil of the homeland he used to know, where bright red hydrangeas shall grow. ♫

**Andrea:** Care to make a wager, Arseny?

**Arseny:** There is no roulette wheel here to bet on. Except the cylinder of your handgun.

**Andrea:** Bah, I gave up on revolvers ages ago. I won't be picking a number between one and six.

**Andrea:** Here. Before a fight, I always flip a coin.

Arseny takes the coin, its back showing a woman bearing a sword with arms wide, and its front showing its value.

A one-ruble commemorative coin, proudly stamped with an image of the Great Patriotic War's most famous monument.

**Andrea:** The old flags are gone. Yet here we are, fighting over new ones.

Arseny holds the coin up to the dark sky and blows on it.

**Arseny:** You'd let a ruble decide your fate? Go home, old man. You're better off farming than fighting.

**Andrea:** I have long wished to trade in this gun for a scythe.

Arseny smiles, tosses the coin high, catches it, and covers it on the back of his hand.

**Andrea:** Let me see. Heads. You lucky dog.

**Andrea:** The coin says you keep your head tomorrow.

**Arseny:** And you? Heads or tails?

**Andrea:** I always got heads before. This fearsome nose of mine wards off bad luck.

**Andrea:** Unlike poor Igor. Always tails.

**Igor:** Death follows me the way fleas follow you.

**Igor:** But at a distance.

They rarely drink before battle now. Tonight is an exception. They know what they face tomorrow.

The vice admiral's cup is filled and emptied again and again. He drinks with every man more than once.

But his mind stays clear, his sight as keen as an eagle's.

**Igor:** Every man still here has a stiff neck and a thick skull. Thankfully, Death's scythe is dull. It will break before it cuts through even one of you.

Igor's gaze, hot from the fire, passes over each officer.

**Igor:** Hold your ground, obey your orders. I give you my word, victory will be ours.

**Igor:** As always.

**Officer I:** Comrades! To Victory!

**Officer II:** Let's beat those monsters back to hell!

**Officer III:** See the bank in the middle of the street? Tomorrow I'll hold that door myself. Not a soul gets through. Safe enough to take a hot bath!

Each officer stakes a claim over their sectors, boasting over who might spill the most enemy blood.

**Arseny:** And the high ground?

Arseny points toward the raised overlook at the center of town, like the exposed spine of a slumbering beast.

The commanding view over the city is an obvious prize and likely to be the measure of victory or defeat.

By tomorrow's end, that hilltop would climb higher still, raised on the corpses of the fallen.

**Arseny:** We'll need an entire regiment up there if we have any hope of holding it.

The talk and laughter stop. The fire seems to quiet, too.

**Igor:** No need for a regiment. It will only take one man.

Arseny pauses, the drink flushing from his red face to his eyes. He pats Igor on his shoulder.

**Arseny:** I should have known it would be you.

He raises his cup high.

**Arseny:** To Igor!

**Officers:** To Igor!

His saber catches the firelight, making its edge resemble a burning crescent moon.

Igor wipes his shashka, from the eagle-headed brass hilt to the elegant, fierce sweep of the blade.

Golden liquor splashes onto the steel.

**Andrea:** Hey! To Igor!

Igor takes the cup from Andrea's hand.

The singing is done, and the cups are empty. Around them, men begin to snore.

**Igor:** You hold your drink better than you used to.

**Andrea:** Old Andrea gets drunk only when he wants.

**Andrea:** But I say better to ride into battle fuzzy than clear.

**Andrea:** Better to forget the danger, to forget that I am only flesh and blood, not a blade.

**Andrea:** Yet, tomorrow I wish to have a clear mind. The clearer, the better.

**Igor:** Why?

**Andrea:** You should know why. You never get drunk before a fight.

**Igor:** A commander needs to think and see clearly.

**Andrea:** Yes. See how the men mount up, how they form their line, and how they cut the enemy down like beets.

**Andrea:** See how they march and ride toward their deaths.

**Igor:** This is war.

Andrea matches Igor's eyes for a second, locked in certain shared knowledge.

**Andrea:** But this is no ordinary war. Our enemies are different. We have more to worry about than tanks and machine guns.

**Andrea:** Arcane weapons we could never have imagined. You saw them in action. It was worse than a bomb. Men turned to vapor, not even a body left to bury.

**Igor:** From the day we chose this path, we knew the stakes. Men die in war. In the end, we must accept our own deaths too.

**Andrea:** And what are we dying for? What if all of this is for nothing?

**Andrea:** Look at this city. A soldier may live only a day here, an officer three. Why do we ask these boys to die for it? We're only here for one reason.

**Andrea:** Because we believe in you, Igor.

**Andrea:** Even among the officers, we cannot help but believe. They think you can take the very scythe from Death's hands.

**Andrea:** That you will pluck victory from the maw of hell itself.

**Igor:** Because I will. Whether the enemy be the devil or death itself, I will.

**Igor:** I will be the last defense for every one of you.

**Andrea:** I have no doubt, commander. I have never doubted you, not since the first day we bled together.

**Andrea:** But in all of this fighting, all of this blood, we're only the blade, not the hand that wields it.

**Andrea:** That hand hides behind a communicator. They count the losses of war as numbers on a graph, not the faces of dying boys.

**Andrea:** They are more than names waiting to be carved into the Hall of Merit.

**Andrea:** What do we fight for? Whose orders do we even follow?

**Igor:** We swore an oath: Obedient, loyal, unwavering.

Andrea faces Igor eye-to-eye. The laughter is gone from his face. Every wrinkle of age holds taut like a stretched rubber band.

Every line is tight, ready to break.

**Andrea:** I won't be the only one asking these questions tonight.

**Andrea:** No man rides into the jaws of death without a reason. No man.

The old soldier says the words softly, like a silver thread the wind might blow away.

But Igor hears it as loud as thunder.

He looks down, his eyes fixed on his cup.

**Igor:** You've had too much to drink.

**Igor:** When morning comes, you will forget these words. So will I.

Andrea looks at him a moment, then the strength leaves him. His shoulders sink like earth worn down by shells.

**Andrea:** I am drunk.

Andrea picks up his empty cup and steps back into the dark beyond the fire.

**Igor:** Andrea!

**Igor:** We have barrier troops at the rear. They'll shoot anyone who runs.

**Igor:** You know this. If you run, a bullet goes right between your eyes.

**Igor:** I won't bend protocol for you.

Andrea nods, smiling as he points his finger to his temple like a gun.

**Andrea:** Bang!
