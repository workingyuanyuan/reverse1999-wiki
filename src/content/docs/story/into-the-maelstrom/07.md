## Story

The admiral and the soldier pause at the river's edge.

Winter has been late to arrive, but when it came, it spread fast and fierce, turning the river's surface into brittle ice.

**Grigory:** This won't be thick enough. A few days from now, when the ice is solid, the horses can walk right over.

**Igor:** Some ice looks as solid as concrete until a hoof goes through.

**Igor:** My father lost a sled horse that way.

**Grigory:** The elders in my village used to say the same. The roads on the river seem wide, but not every path is safe.

**Igor:** Correct.

**Igor:** In times like these, we must choose our path carefully.

The horses stamp softly by the river's edge. The vast steppe stretches out beyond its curve, many paths still open to the riders.

**Igor:** Are we still going the same way, soldier?

The soldier exhales, letting the cloud of his breath obscure his face, as if to hide his thoughts.

**Grigory:** Perhaps we are not, sir.

A river mist hangs in the night, drifting like a silver veil over the forest.

**Igor:** Where are you going? The White Army? The Red Army? Manus Vindictae?

**Grigory:** I'm not staying right here, sir. I know there isn't anywhere to run. You've already seen me.

**Igor:** A soldier's doubts always show on his face.

**Igor:** You have access to command information, so someone is always watching you.

**Grigory:** I never wanted to run to Manus Vindictae. I don't want a mask. I won't become one of those monsters.

**Igor:** I know.

**Igor:** You fought well last time. There's a ceremony before we break camp. Tomorrow, you'll receive a promotion and a medal of honor.

**Grigory:** It isn't a medal that I want, sir. I don't want to see my name carved into that wall.

**Grigory:** Once, maybe. Not anymore.

**Igor:** What do you want, soldier?

**Grigory:** I suppose ... I just ...

A faint breeze stirs, light as the brush of a nightingale's wing.

**Grigory:** I just want to go home. I want to be home.

The wind picks up, carrying the scent of dry mugwort and thyme across the steppe.

**Grigory:** At first, I thought I hadn't changed. But with every battle, I feel as though my heart freezes a little more. Like ice forming over the Don.

**Grigory:** Nothing causes a ripple in me anymore. Fear, anger, and grief—they just slide away.

**Grigory:** All I ever wanted was to be honored in that hall. I thought that's where I'd finally find my rest.

**Grigory:** But now, being here, I want something else. I don't want to be a soldier remembered on a wall.

Igor watches the lilac mist part, the wind carving a path through the fog to the white birch forest beyond.

**Igor:** This place holds all that remains of our past.

**Igor:** But the past is the past, son. This era is ending.

**Igor:** But not my army. I will lead you through the fire and the "Storm."

**Igor:** We will exist no matter what may change in the coming era. We will be an army for all of time.

**Grigory:** Do we have a choice?

Igor doesn't answer.

The steppe feels utterly silent. Golden stars, unable to bear the silence, start to fall into the dawn.

**Grigory:** I don't want to die fighting for a city of strangers, sir, or fall in some cold wasteland in Antarctica. I want to die here in the soil of my motherland.

Not everyone wishes to board this Ark.

**Grigory:** My name isn't fit for the Hall of Merit. I will dig a small grave on the steppe.

**Grigory:** And the next year, red hydrangeas will bloom where I sleep.

**Igor:** You know, I have never accepted desertion from my ranks.

**Grigory:** I know.

Grigory dismounts, stands tall, and meets Igor's gaze. His young back is straight and solid.

**Igor:** How fast can your horse run?

**Grigory:** Fast as dry lightning on the spring steppe.

**Igor:** Then mount up, son. Ride as fast as you can.

Grigory's face grows serious. He knows what is coming.

But the shadow lifts from his face, slowly replaced by a full and beaming smile.

The soldier adjusts his cap.

**Grigory:** I salute you, sir!

The wind rustles the tall feather grass as Grigory swings into the saddle.

**Grigory:** Run, old friend. Outpace the bullet this time.

The white horse races toward the line where the steppe meets the night sky. At the far edge of sight, a faint, mythical blue light shimmers.

Igor raises his rifle. His right index finger finds the trigger. His hands are steady, the pressure even.

Breathe in. Breathe out. Hold.

Bang!

The bullet scrapes over the top of the soldier's head, knocking his military cap off.

**Igor:** "Let this golden shield guard you. Your faith shall be your armor, your flesh like iron."

**Igor:** "Even bullets will not dare to scratch you."

The white horse runs on, faster and faster, into the dreamy blue light.

Igor hadn't aimed for the soldier's head.
