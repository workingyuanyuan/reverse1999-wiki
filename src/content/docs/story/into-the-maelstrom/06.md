## Story

**Igor:** I had my sights on a hare. I asked the commander, "What do I do now?"

**Igor:** He told me I was a good shot. Told me not to move; to stand my ground. And if any of our own ran back, to shoot them with my rifle.

**Igor:** Like hunting a hare.

A rough laugh escapes Igor. The soldier replies.

**Grigory:** I wanted to run away. The first time.

**Grigory:** But I knew there was no turning back. If I ran, I'd end up with a bullet through my head. But if I pushed onward, I at least had a chance.

**Grigory:** So I just kept going. That fight through that snowy mountain pass, you remember it?

**Igor:** I remember ...

**Grigory:** I got knocked off the cliff in the first few minutes. They left me hanging there until your flight unit saved me.

**Grigory:** I'm not a proper soldier, sir. I could never be like you.

**Igor:** In the last battle, I saw that you were the first to storm the enemy's position. You've done well.

Grigory looks at Igor, surprised. A genuine grin spreads across his face.

**Grigory:** Thank you, sir.

**Grigory:** I know you don't say those things lightly. I think every soldier under your command would want nothing more than to hear that we've done well.

**Grigory:** Every one of us wanted to be like you, sir. That was our second wish.

**Grigory:** The first was to have our names carved in the Hall of Merit at headquarters.

**Grigory:** But the more battles I fight, the less I understand why I'm fighting.

**Grigory:** The friends I joined up with are all gone. On that mountain, at the South Pole, in São Paulo ...

**Grigory:** Maybe their names are carved into that hall now, but I'm not sure I would have the heart to look.

His voice catches in his throat. He hesitates over his next words.

But he turns anyway and meets the admiral's gaze.

**Grigory:** What about your friends, sir?

Nikolai Ivanov. KIA, struck by artillery fire while holding position around the bank. 1995, Al Muntar.

Dmitri Smirnov. KIA, fell to arcane skill while rescuing the wounded. 1995, Al Muntar.

Andrea Petrov. KIA, shot while leading a cavalry charge. 1995, Al Muntar.

The Battle of Al Muntar in 1995. Zeno forces fought a costly yet glorious battle, withstanding powerful new arcane skills and large-scale rituals. Their sacrifices laid the foundation for the Battle of the "Storm" in 1999. Their service embodied the Zeno creed: Obedient, loyal, unwavering.

Zeno Headquarters

The losses were so great that Zeno headquarters was forced to expand the Hall of Merit in 1999.

Most of Zeno's fallen are marked by only a name on the wall.

Yet Igor sees a face behind each one.

**Administrative Staff Member:** Sorry to keep you waiting, Admiral.

**Administrative Staff Member:** But the files you requested regarding the Battle of Al Muntar in 1995 are not yet declassified. Unfortunately, they're ...

**Igor:** Above my clearance level. Correct?

The staff member gives a polite, apologetic smile, choosing the words carefully.

**Administrative Staff Member:** The thing is, there were many such smaller engagements in 1995. It can be difficult to keep track of every archive and file.

**Administrative Staff Member:** And as I'm sure you're aware, headquarters is still sorting through the aftermath of the recent battle. Things here are even more disorganized.

**Igor:** I understand.

**Administrative Staff Member:** Thank you, and thank you for your service to Zeno. Can I help you with anything else?

**Igor:** In the third row, sixth column, for 1995, you've mixed up Nikolai's and Dmitri's last names.

**Igor:** Please, have it fixed. Those two never got along.

**Administrative Staff Member:** What?

The staff member puzzles over his words for a moment before settling back into the professional and well-polished smile.

**Administrative Staff Member:** Apologies for the mistake, sir. I'll make certain it's corrected.

**Igor:** Thank you.

The admiral turns and leaves. The door closed behind him.

Hall of Merit, Zeno

He walks along the names in the Hall of Merit, from 1995 to 1999.

The losses of 1999 occupy an entire wall of their own.

A girl with black hair stands before the wall. She is still small, not yet the daring lieutenant she would one day be, and has to tilt her head back to see her parents' faces, given the rare honor of a portrait.

**Igor:** Moldir.

**Moldir:** Admir ... Father.

**Igor:** You may call me Admiral. Or Igor, if you would like.

It is his first day with his new foster daughter. He stands beside her, looking at the photo of his comrade.

**Igor:** Arseny. He fell in 1999, in Abadan.

**Igor:** It's a good picture.

**Igor:** We knew these photos would end up here one day. He made sure to dress for it.

**Igor:** I told him to shave that beard.

**Igor:** He told me, "You're one to talk."

Moldir turns, studying her new foster father's face, from his forehead to his jaw.

Then she nods.

**Igor:** You are his daughter, indeed.

**Moldir:** I never imagined there would be so many names.

**Igor:** There were fewer when we started.

**Igor:** I came here on my first day with Zeno. That day, I decided that I would not rest until my name was carved alongside the others.

**Moldir:** Did that make you proud?

Igor pauses.

**Igor:** At that time. We believed we were fighting to bring peace and order to the world.

**Igor:** We thought a soldier's courage could tame this mad world, like breaking a wild horse.

**Moldir:** So my mother and father, they were proud, too.

It isn't a question, but Igor nods anyway.

**Igor:** They earned a soldier's honor.

**Moldir:** Will my name be here one day?

Igor bends down a little, meeting Moldir's gaze.

**Igor:** You would not be the first to wish for it. But I hope not to see that day.

**Igor:** Do you want a soldier's honor, like your parents?

The girl looks up at the wall, at the thousands of names stretching out like a vast web, hanging over the shoulders of the living.

She doesn't understand war yet. She couldn't possibly answer his question.

But there is one thing she needs to know first.

**Moldir:** All these people ... Why did they die?

**Igor:** Because ...

There are many answers. They died in the Battle of the "Storm." They died by Manus Vindictae's hand. They died to push back chaos and evil. They died fighting for peace.

He lets his gaze wander across the length of the Hall, where names stretch back from 1999 to the founding of Zeno.

Countless names broken up by countless doors. Each time he comes here, he sees them open and close.

**Administrative Staff Member:** "The files you requested regarding the Battle of Al Muntar in 1995 are not yet declassified."

**Communicator:** "Believe that every drop of blood shed by your men will water a new world of peace and order."

**Administrative Staff Member:** "Sir, you don't have the clearance."

**Communicator:** "Every sacrifice is necessary, but we can't say anything more."

**Administrative Staff Member:** "Sorry."

The admiral stands before the 1999 "Storm" wall, looking at the young, familiar faces. He can't bring himself to answer Moldir's question.

**Igor:** I don't know.

**Igor:** But I will find an answer.
