## Story

The military cap falls into the river, breaking the razor-thin ice. The pieces chime as they float away, carrying the cap away.

He holds the rifle up. His hands are steady, but a hint of confusion lingers on his face. He can't recall when a scope has been mounted.

He looks through the scope.

**Officer:** Charge!

**Officer:** Cavalry, hold the line! Take the flank!

Black fire burns at the foot of Al Muntar. Arcane bullets scream ominously toward the men.

The attackers charge in waves, breaking against shell craters, tangling in wire, and splashing into red drops.

One wave falls. Another rises. Relentless as a rushing river against the last ice of spring.

**Arseny:** Andrea! What are you doing? Come on! Forward!

**Arseny:** You'll be shot!

A single drop stands still on the hilltop, neither advancing nor retreating.

Perun paces nervously amid the sounds of gunfire. His rider seems unaware. The loose reins drift in the blood-red waves.

Igor feels his finger on the trigger. A sharp, almost painful, kiss of cold steel.

**Igor:** Don't look back.

A shell bursts nearby. The horse rears up in fright.

Andrea's throat moves. He lifts his head, as if trying to locate where the fire came from.

**Igor:** Keep running. Don't look back.

Andrea tightens his legs around the horse's belly, leaning forward like a drawn bow. No one knows what he is thinking right now.

But Perun seems to hear something. The horse gives a long, glad whinny and turns, racing toward empty ground where there is no enemy and no comrades.

**Andrea:** "No man rides into the jaws of death without a reason. No man."

**Andrea:** "Bang!"

Igor's finger jerks on the trigger subconsciously.

\*clack\*

The rifle is empty. He has already fired his last shot. All he holds now is a shashka. The blade is rolled and chipped all along the damaged edge.

"In close quarters, a blade is swifter than a rifle."

**Vasily:** "In close quarters, a blade is swifter than a rifle."

**Igor:** You were correct, Father.

At his feet lie bodies in blue uniform. Beneath them, bodies in yellow.

Foe and friend lie together, layer upon layer, a hill of the fallen.

**Communicator:** \*buzz\*

**Communicator:** "Admiral Igor, the files you requested regarding the Battle of Al Muntar in 1995 are not yet declassified."

**Igor:** And the operation's objective is above my clearance level.

**Communicator:** "For now, you only need to follow the order. The order is ..."

**Igor:** Advance. Whatever it takes.

**Igor:** But not this army.

**Igor:** Requesting use of a vacuum bomb to be deployed against Manus Vindictae lines.

**Communicator:** "The Pax House has determined that this weapon cannot be authorized at this moment."

**Igor:** Then when will the "moment" come?

The silence that follows is long and heavy.

**Communicator:** "There has only ever been one order."

He sees the earth form a soft wrinkle. Soldiers' bodies are extinguished in an instant like the flame of a candle.

**Communicator:** "We will honor every fallen soldier."

**Communicator:** "To cross through the chaos of the unknown, we will require your men for further experimentation."

He sees countless soldiers enter the White Stone House. They leave their faces behind, melting into pure white.

They never return. Only their names remain, row upon row upon the wall in the Hall of Merit.

**Moldir:** All these people ... What did they fight for? Why did they die?

**Igor:** I don't know, Moldir.

In the Hall, he sees his own picture, taken in his first year with Zeno.

On that day, everyone wore their dress uniforms.

They waited in line with a sorrowful sweetness for the honor that was sure to come.

**Igor:** I once believed in it.

**Igor:** I sent you to the treacherous front. I told you to advance at any price, even if the path led to the edge of the abyss or through the flames of hell.

**Igor:** There has only ever been one order. I thought it would bring a world of peace. I thought it would give a soldier glory that wouldn't fade.

**Igor:** However ... However ...

Igor feels something sharp in his palm. He opens his hand. A die lies there.

"His heart is a hanging lute, a hanging lute ... gently plucked ... "

He feels a hollow tremor. Yes, this has happened before. It will happen again. His heart was once as hard as stone.

Fate plucks the long-rusted string.

The die falls with the rain in São Paulo.

**Igor:** We failed to achieve glory, but I see too that glory has failed us.

**Igor:** Forgive me, children.

**Igor:** My sin is not that I stained that honor. Not that I disobeyed the White Stone House. It is that I did not stand beside you to the last.

**Igor:** I couldn't bring everyone into tomorrow.

**Vasily:** Haha! Hahahahaha!

**Vasily:** So brave, so stupid! Another young man destined for death.

The old, drunk cavalry commander stands in the fallen leaves with a fierce, fiery look in his eyes.

**Vasily:** Let me teach you the secret to winning, then.

**Vasily:** You must fear death, boy.

**Vasily:** Not just your own. You must fear the death of every man.

**Igor:** Their deaths are on my shoulders.

**Igor:** My soldiers will be with me, no matter whether they live or die.

**Igor:** My sins will be with me until the darkness of the underworld devours all that I am.

The old soldier smiles faintly. His calloused hand dives into his coat but finds no flask.

**Vasily:** Tch. Hey, little Igor. Do you remember how to hold a rifle?

**Igor:** Yes, Father.

**Igor:** First, plant the stock firmly against your shoulder. Lean back a little.

Igor's right index finger finds the trigger. He sees flakes settle on the barrel. The trigger feels cold as ice.

**Igor:** Steady ...

**Igor:** Slowly ...

**Igor:** Steady.

**Igor:** Breathe in, breathe out. At your breath's end, that is the moment.

He closes his eyes, then opens them. A figure enters his scope.

**Igor:** Don't look back, son.

**Igor:** Keep running. Don't look back.

The figure grows clear, halted between the steppe and snow, neither advancing nor retreating.

He looks a long time before he knows the man before him.

**Igor:** Don't look back. You must move forward, soldier.

**Igor:** I will not look back, nor will I follow another order.

**Igor:** You betrayed those who trusted you. You betrayed yourself.

**Igor:** I've taken another path.

**Igor:** Is it the right path?

The soldier and the admiral hold their distance together against the wind and snow, as Igor lets himself think the next moment through.

There has only ever been one order.

There has only ever been one order.

**Igor:** Yes, sir.

**Igor:** Obedient.

**Igor:** Loyal.

**Igor:** Unwavering.

His finger stays upon the trigger.

Bang!
