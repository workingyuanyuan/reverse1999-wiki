## Story

**Voyager:** \*violin playing\*

**Hedone:** My dear, is it true that you come to us from the cosmos?

**Voyager:** Heh heh.

Under the sovereign's searching gaze, Voyager takes her time smoothing the hem of her dress and returns an equally curious look.

**Hedone:** In the dim memories of my former life, I recall meeting with the polis's elders as they gathered in a symposium to muse beneath the starry skies.

**Hedone:** I had just completed an escort for our merchant fleets, and I'd returned to tell them of my discovery: that on open seas, land is not seen at once but rises slowly from the horizon.

**Hedone:** Before I could share more, one of them became strangely ecstatic and dashed away. He nearly clawed his way through the door. Haha.

She raises her cup to the serene visitor from the stars.

**Hedone:** Ah, but I mustn't bore you with these long-forgotten things. It doesn't matter where you come from. Tonight we feast to celebrate your achievement.

**Voyager:** Hmm?

Voyager stares at the cup in Hedone's hand. She tilts her head, looking utterly puzzled.

**Hedone:** You don't understand?

Fulfilling her duty as host, Hedone presses the cup Voyager's been eyeing into her hand.

**Hedone:** Take it. It's little bother. I need to free my hands.

**Hedone:** Citizens, honored guests, interlopers, would you care to join me in a game?

**Hedone:** A family tradition, once used to pass the time as we journeyed the sea.

**Hedone:** I speak, of course, of kottabos.

The godlike facade of Hedone momentarily breaks, and the excited girl beneath pokes through. Beside her, the bartender sets a basin and six oversized beer mugs on the counter before anyone can blink.

**Islander I:** I'm in. I'm always up for kottabos.

**Islander II:** Really? Haha. Don't you remember how badly I beat you last time?

**Islander I:** Hahahaha. A truly great game is as fun to lose as it is to win.

**Sonetto:** Kottabos, a traditional Greek game played at social gatherings. Floating disks are placed in a basin, and players take turns tossing their wine dregs at them. The first to sink a disk wins.

**Sonetto:** It's a game of precision, aim, and forearm strength.

**Vertin:** You seem familiar with it, Sonetto. Have you played before?

**Sonetto:** Uh, no, no. I simply read about it, from a column in the newspaper about customs around the world.

Sonetto offers the explanation plainly.

**Hedone:** Whether you've played or not, my dear, you've described it perfectly.

**Hedone:** Though we've made some slight adjustments to the traditional rules.

**Hedone:** A touch of arcanum and our disks begin to show a little more enthusiasm.

The disks in the basin spin faster and faster, churning the water inside until it starts to splash over the rim.

**Hedone:** This is sure to be exciting.

**Barcarola:** Oh! I saw people playing something like this back on the "Free Breeze."

**Barcarola:** But I never had the chance to join them. I was always too busy with my shows.

**Barcarola:** I'd be eager to give it a try.

Barcarola finds herself transfixed by the spinning plates. Her eyes grow wide with joyful anticipation.

**Regulus:** You can count me in as well. What's that make us? We've got me, Barcarola, Hedone ...

**Hedone:** Hahahahaha! I will demonstrate the game, but I wouldn't dare participate. It wouldn't be sporting.

**Hedone:** After all, this feast is in your honor. I would not have you worry yourselves over besting me again.

A round of cheers is offered, and then they dive into the ancient game together.

Narcissus stands closer to victory than she's ever been in her life, unofficial as it may be.

**Narcissus:** \*panting\*

Her breath grows heavy, and without realizing it, she's clenched her jaw tight.

She tries to compensate for her clammy hands with a tighter grip, not a sensible strategy, but she pushes forward all the same.

\*bang\*

Hermes's last puck gets knocked clean off the track.

**Hermes:** Fine, I lose. You win. Hail to the champion.

Her victory, however, doesn't seem to bring any calm to her nerves.

**Narcissus:** Victory over a shuffleboard. It's nothing to be proud of.

**Narcissus:** Oh, but, would you mind putting on a grand awarding ceremony for me, Themis?

**Themis:** ...

**Apollo:** If you permit it, dear Narcissus, I could weave you a laurel instead.

**Narcissus:** A laurel?

**Narcissus:** Ahem. Wouldn't it be too much? Better to save it for tomorrow's victory.

**???:** Tomorrow?

She needs not even turn around to confirm it. The sheer sound of the person's effortless stride is enough to confirm to Narcissus just who has arrived.

**Narcissus:** Hermes! You told me You-Know-Who wouldn't be around tonight.

She speaks through gritted teeth to the messenger across the table, but all she gets back is an evasive whistle.

**Hedone:** Ah, but why wait until the morrow when the night is still young? Shall we have another game, little champion?

**Hedone:** Allow me to sweeten the prize—a reward, should you prove worthy.

Hedone beams, causing Narcissus to take a deliberate, if nervous, step away.

**Narcissus:** Al-Alright. Bring it on. I'm not afraid of you.

**Narcissus:** That's right. I'm not afraid of you.

Her body screams the opposite. As if trying to convince herself, she repeats it, trembling.

...

As it turns out, the bolder the promise, the shorter its shelf life tends to be.

**Narcissus:** ...

**Hedone:** Is something the matter, my dear little Nymph? Where has your smile gone?

Narcissus grips the puck tight in her hand, as though clutching at the hem of victory's robe as it slips away from her.

**Narcissus:** Why? I just want to win for once. Why do you always have to sabotage me?

**Narcissus:** This is just like when you took away the oracle's blessing from me.

**Hedone:** Sabotage? Is that what you think? I simply made certain the game was fair.

She tosses another puck out far too carelessly for how well it lands, and her poor opponent edges that much closer to defeat.

**Hedone:** Do you think the gods would ever truly favor a miserable little thief who cheats her way to victory?

**Narcissus:** What-What are you talking about?

**Hedone:** Tell me, have I lied?

**Hedone:** That scale of yours could still even the roughest of waters, and enhances your otherwise limited arcane skills. I would not call that fair play.

Narcissus freezes in place, her face flushed.

**Narcissus:** You ... You ...

**Hedone:** And on the topic of your much-abused oracle.

**Hedone:** It seems I must once again educate you on their nature. Did you not know that all the oracles of this island come to us through a specific ritual?

**Hedone:** We've recorded each instance of it and the oracle it brought. In total, 137 messages of wisdom have been bestowed on us from the gods. Yet, somehow, your oracle is not numbered among them.

**Narcissus:** I-It's real! I performed the ritual. I asked the gods for their wisdom. They even created a stream so that I would know it was true.

**Narcissus:** I asked them to show me the way to victory, and it flowed outward towards the sea.

Hedone replies with a scornful laugh.

**Hedone:** A fantasy. No, indeed, far worse than a fantasy. A delusion borne of arrogance and naivety.

**Hedone:** Do you really think that stream formed just for your sake?

**Hedone:** We live upon an island shaken endlessly by the volcano's fire. The shifting rock and stone creates new streams by the dozen each year.

**Hedone:** Which among them do you refer to?

Facing down the island's sovereign, Narcissus feels her argument slip away under the barrage of words.

**Narcissus:** No, no. It was special. How can you claim it has no meaning? I can't be doomed to lose to you forever.

**Hedone:** Do you really believe that were it not for that false oracle, you would have never found the strength to best me?

**Narcissus:** I ... I ...

As if trying to push away the words, she squeezes her eyes shut and shoves the puck forward with all her strength.

The puck flies out carrying a force and precision unlike any before it.

Her one remaining puck ricochets again and again, knocking every one of Hedone's pucks out of place.

Then it glides to a gentle halt.

All eyes fall on the puck, looking as if it were a proud soldier awaiting its medal for delivering victory.

Narcissus stands with her mouth hanging open, unable to truly believe her own eyes. Victory is hers at last.

**Narcissus:** I ... I won?

**Regulus:** Too right you did. You finally got her, Narcissus! Woohoo!

The shuffleboard game has drawn every eye in the bar.

**Vertin:** I think some congratulations are in order.

**Sonetto:** But is she alright?

Worried, Sonetto waves her hand in front of Narcissus.

Narcissus looks out blankly at the crowd around her, if she even sees them at all.

**Narcissus:** I ... won?

Something new rests against her neck. Narcissus touches it without thinking.

A reward she can't refuse.

A scale hangs from a delicate silver necklace, glowing softly.

**Hedone:** And for our conquering champion, her promised reward.

**Hedone:** Whether your oracle is true or false, it matters little.

**Hedone:** You may continue to believe that it brought you to inevitable victory over me, as you wish.

**Hedone:** But I would advise you to make the most of this scale, Narcissus.

**Narcissus:** I, I don't know what to say.

Even if it was only a game at a bar, it is a victory, one that she had almost given up hope of ever achieving.

But now it's all happening too fast. All she can do is clutch her recovered scale.

**Hedone:** Then say nothing. After all, it remains my right to challenge you for it at any moment.

Hedone waves her off.

**Barcarola:** Ms. Voyager?

**Voyager:** ...

**Regulus:** Get out! Did you really drink all these?!

Concerned, Barcarola takes Voyager's hand and brings her face to face. Beside them, the bar is lined with empty beer mugs.

**Sonetto:** Ms. Voyager, are you alright?

**Voyager:** Heh heh.

Serene as ever, she smiles and shakes her head.

**Hermes:** \*burp\* Anoth—another round.

**Islander I:** I won't ... lose ... to anyone.

**Islander II:** That's nonsense. You've never even beaten me.

**Regulus:** Joplin's pipes! You drank the lot of them under the table!

Regulus can only stare at the people slumped and staggering around her.

Just beyond the doors of Bar Helicon, the noise fades, and two old friends meet again.

**???:** Dear Narcissus, you look upset.

Faced with the warm greeting, Narcissus stubbornly turns her head away.

**Narcissus:** I don't want to talk to you, Apollo.

But only a moment later, she turns back.

**Narcissus:** That oracle, everything you told me ... was it really a lie?

**Apollo:** You've been ever set on following that oracle. What changed?

Apollo lets the question drift toward her.

**Apollo:** Ah. Is it because Lady Hedone spoke a truth? One that has long sat in the back of your mind?

**Narcissus:** I, I ... I'm just confused.

A flicker of disappointment crosses her face.

**Narcissus:** I want to win, and not just at some silly little game.

**Apollo:** ...

**Apollo:** \*sigh\* I cannot claim to be as just as Themis, but I should hope you know better than to call me a liar. I am an Oracle. I transmit the wisdom of the gods as a sacred duty. I would not lie, not especially to a friend.

**Narcissus:** You-You mean ...

Little by little, light creeps back into Narcissus's eyes, and her voice climbs right along with it.

**Narcissus:** The oracle was real all along? But, please, tell me that it wasn't just predicting I'd win at shuffleboard. Let it be about tomorrow's game, when my heroic team finally defeats Hedone.

**Apollo:** My dear friend, you hear "jump" and think the gods mean to leap over Olympus itself.

**Apollo:** It is not the nature of oracles to tell you the exact shape of your future. They are wisdom, possibility, warning, but never certainty.

**Apollo:** You cannot take them as divine commands or providence. They were never meant to define our lives.

**Narcissus:** I ... I still don't understand.

Apollo offers her an all too familiar shrug.

**Apollo:** Don't worry. The gods did not mean for them to be understood. At least, not in that way.

**Apollo:** Their wisdom reveals itself in its own time.
