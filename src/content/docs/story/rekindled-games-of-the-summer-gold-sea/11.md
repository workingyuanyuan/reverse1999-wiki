## Story

Ritual Plaza

The Mediterranean sun blazes high in the sky, and the islanders' love for the Aquatic Games seems to burn just as hot.

In the distance, the volcano steams. Spectators crowd around the starting point for the final event.

**Themis:** In our final competition, our contestants will undergo a perilous challenge to navigate waters riddled with obstacles and reach the mouth of the volcano.

**Themis:** The course and its obstacles are entirely the makings of nature. Each turn may help or hinder you.

**Themis:** Contestants are permitted to use any means at their disposal to reach the finish line.

**Themis:** This will be a true race. The first to arrive will be our champion.

The judge lays out the rules, allowing for momentary pauses to build the crowd's anticipation and imagination.

**Sonetto:** Let's go over what we know, Timekeeper. Each one of these events, the sea glide, the beast taming, and now this obstacle race ...

**Sonetto:** ... were a part of Hedone's original journey to the volcano's heart.

**Vertin:** Right. As she said herself, these Games are meant to relive her heroic deeds in preparation for the "hunt."

**Sonetto:** So then, this isn't just for fun or prizes. We're participating in a vital ritual for the sake of this island.

**Vertin:** There's something else, too. Behind every one of her great deeds in the fresco was cooperation.

**Vertin:** But now ...

The sea wind carries off some of the heat, and the water stays calm.

**Vertin:** It seems she prefers to act alone.

**Themis:** There has been a last-minute change in our roster.

Themis fixes her eyes on the volcano's mouth. Smoke curls out from its rim in a steady breath.

**Themis:** Several teams have withdrawn from the Games, leaving us only two remaining competitors for our final event.

**Themis:** Lady Hedone ...

The revered sovereign of Ephaistonos steps onto the ritual platform alone.

**Hedone:** Let's get this over with quickly, shall we?

She lifts her chin, eyes fixed on the finish line, her words coming out crisp and clear.

**Hedone:** Bring these Games to their proper, triumphant end.

**Themis:** And Vertin.

The outsider steps onto the platform beside her.

**Vertin:** I look forward to our final match, Lady Hedone. And may the best team win.

**Vertin:** But, after all we've witnessed here, I have a question on my mind.

**Vertin:** There's something I must know that's far more important than the results of this competition.

**Vertin:** You've talked about a connection between the Aquatic Games and your "ritual."

**Vertin:** The celebration, the prophecy, and the Games are all necessary steps, aren't they?

**Vertin:** Then—

Under the sheer weight of the sovereign's pressure, there's no room left to even flinch.

**Hedone:** This is neither the place nor the time for your idle curiosities, Vertin.

**Hedone:** You are guests here. I warn you not to meddle in our affairs.

Hedone's expression turns to stone, and she steps forward.

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper!

Sonetto steps up too, placing herself between them.

**Vertin:** No need, Sonetto.

Hedone's shadow falls over them both.

**Hedone:** Even under sacred hospitality, you would do well to fear my wrath.

**Hedone:** I must admit you have brought new joy to this island.

**Hedone:** And I've come to regard you fondly. Perhaps one day I might even call you friends, a feeling which has long been absent from my life.

All traces of warmth from the feast have drained from her face, leaving only the iron-fisted tyrant beneath.

**Hedone:** But do not mistake my hospitality for weakness. You are not one of us, and that will not change, not with banquets, not with games, not even with victory.

**Hedone:** I am fond of you, indeed.

**Hedone:** But I will not allow you to undermine me further.

**Hedone:** Do not presume to question me again.

**Hedone:** You would do well to focus on your purpose here, and that alone.

She leans in, her voice cold and sharp as a blade.

The distant volcano exhales a blast of thick smoke as if puffing with rage.

**Hedone:** Themis! The day is fleeting.

**Hedone:** To your post. Begin the game.

**Themis:** The Pact remains. Lady Hedone will compete alone.

**Themis:** And, as agreed, Vertin is permitted to use any number of teammates to aid in her victory.

**Themis:** With that said, let the game begin!

**ONiON:** Welcome back, sports fans and game fanatics alike. Are you sure they'll like this opening?

**ONiON:** I'm your sportscaster on the ground, ONiON.

**Hermes:** And I'm Hermes, Ephaistonos's very own messenger.

**ONiON & Hermes:** And we are live at the final round of the Aquatic Games.

Colorful gliders slice across the sky behind them as the two begin their coverage of the event.

**ONiON:** In order to cover every angle of this exciting finale, the two of us will each be following one of the remaining teams.

**Hermes:** Hello out there, new friends across the world. I'm Hermes. Wait, I already said that. Anyway, I'll be following the one and only champion of the island, Lady Hedone.

**ONiON:** Meanwhile, I will be tracking our dear Timekeeper as she navigates this final challenge.

The two gliders part ways, each trailing their contestant down separate lanes.

**Hermes:** Try to keep up with that camera.

**Hermes:** Try to keep up with that camera.

**Narcissus:** Wait for me, Hermes. It's too high!

Trailing after Hermes, Narcissus gets whipped around by the wind, her webbed hands holding onto his sneakers for dear life.

**Hermes:** Oh, hey, Narcissus. Did you wanna help out? Watch the shoes. They're limited edition.

**Narcissus:** Of course. I'm following this game to the bitter end. I won't miss a second.

**ONiON:** We're fresh on the trail of our courageous Timekeeper.

**ONiON:** We're fresh on the trail of our courageous Timekeeper.

**ONiON:** There she goes. Vertin has dived into the water with all her trademark "grace" and has taken the lead heading toward the first obstacle.

**ONiON:** But wait. I may have spoken too soon. Lady Hedone is gaining on her.

The two contestants hit the water with radically different speeds and grace, and the two journalists following them drift apart once more to follow them.

**Hermes:** I don't know how else to describe it. Lady Hedone is plowing through the water with all the poise and speed of a killer whale.

**Hermes:** Maybe that sounds a little disrespectful, but I promise you it's accurate. I have to have some journalistic integrity, don't I?

**Hermes:** Ah, forget it. Whatever you wanna call it, that kind of speed and ferocity is ratings gold.

**Hedone:** Vertin!

Hedone swims back to reach her opponent with total ease, cutting through the water with all the agility of a fish.

**Hedone:** I must say, seeing you swim like this has melted away all of my frustrations.

**Vertin:** ...

**Hedone:** Hahahahaha! I'll await you at the finish line, my dear. I'll be sure to have a celebratory drink prepared for you.

**Hedone:** What an honor that will be to be served by the great Hedone in her moment of triumph.

**Hedone:** Presuming you make it to the finish line at all.

The next moment, she sinks beneath the water, leaving behind a graceful trail.

**Vertin:** Hmm?

Something rises from beneath the water, providing a lifting force.

It's a Delphyne, the very same that helped them win the last round.

**Delphyne I:** \*squeak\*

**Vertin:** It's you. Would you be willing to help me again?

There's no trace of Hedone's direction from the surface. Yet with the Delphyne's help, gliding through the water is now much faster.

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper! Watch out!

**Vertin:** ...!
