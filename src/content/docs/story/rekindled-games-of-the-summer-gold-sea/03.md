## Story

**Sonetto:** Urm ...

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper ...

Ritual Plaza

As they swirl through the darkness of the endless sea, a new bright land begins to spill into view.

A time-worn plaza and altar stand overlooking the blue sea, half-swallowed in the shade of passing figures, trees, and colorful houses.

The hidden island is finally revealed.

**Regulus:** Hah! Wakey wakey, sleepyheads. Nice to have you back.

**Sonetto:** What happened?

**Regulus:** I'll spare you the blow-by-blow. Good news first: we weren't torn apart by sea monsters, and we aren't in the belly of a whale either.

**Regulus:** Far as I can tell, we've arrived at our destination, and lucky for us, they're in the middle of a big party.

**Regulus:** But here's the bad news: we're very much not on the guest list.

**Sonetto:** Huh?!

No ropes bind them, but some arcane power pins them in place, holding their bodies fast.

The plaza is overflowing with people. Waves crash against the rocks along the shore, white spray nearly splashing onto their hair and sleeves.

The "uninvited guests" find themselves bound to a pillar at the edge of the plaza, powerless to do anything but watch.

\*thud\*

The first beat of the war drum sounds, shaking the earth and sky. The crowd goes quiet.

A figure steps onto the altar at the center.

**???:** Citizens of Ephaistonos, hear me now.

**???:** Once more, the great quest is nigh; once more, we convene, united as one, to anoint me, your huntress, in our blessed ritual.

**???:** Alas, one matter stands between us and our preparations. These interlopers have set foot upon our island unbidden.

**Sonetto:** ...!

**???:** But I beseech you all: pay their transgression no mind! For in the fullness of our sacred ceremony, they will be dealt with appropriately.

Her voice is unconcerned, but her eyes hit them like something solid, freezing body and mind together.

**???:** And with that, let us return to our purpose here. For the beast stirs within its lair once more, and when its long slumber is broken, its terrible wrath will be levied upon us again.

**???:** Thousands of years ago, we threw ourselves into battle with the beast and in our victory claimed our place on this island.

**???:** In a gift of thanksgiving, we offered the beast's head to the mighty volcano, subduing its burning heart so that we may flourish.

**???:** Yet now we feel it beat anew in the tremors beneath our feet.

**???:** To safeguard our home, another bargain must be made. The hour has come. The beast awakens, and we must tear its covetous eye from its skull!

Clear. Firm. Each word rings with a confidence that tips into arrogance.

**Hedone:** Which is why I, Hedone, with the memories of my past life reclaimed, will cast our enemy back into the depths of Tartarus, leading us once again to victory!

**Hedone:** Raise your cups, good citizens, and bear witness, for all that I have said will be done! To victory!

**Islander I:** All hail Lady Hedone!

**Islander II:** Victory belongs to Lady Hedone!

The crowd cheers, raising their golden cups high. Drops of purple-red wine fly through the air, creating a hazy, heady scent.

**Hedone:** Apollo!

A young man steps forward. He whistles, and a bird descends from the sky to its nest in the tree.

**Apollo:** Lady Hedone, my divination awaits your command.

**Hedone:** Ahahahaha! I recall when we last stood here. An epoch come and gone, and yet it is as it was ever thus.

**Hedone:** How fares your preparations? I trust you know my expectations.

**Hedone:** Go on, fair and auspicious Apollo. Deliver us favorable omens as you have before.

With a satisfied smile, Hedone takes a sip of wine and pours the rest onto the altar. It runs down through the cracks in a thin stream.

A blood-red serpent slithers out of the crack and crawls up to the nest in the tree.

Branches whip and shudder as the serpent and bird tear into each other in the nest.

**Apollo:** The serpent consumes its egg.

**Hedone:** Then the serpent is wise, for its enemy is soon to arrive.

The bird lets out a piercing shriek, its beak driving hard into the serpent's weak point. The serpent goes limp.

Everyone holds their breath.

**Apollo:** ...

Suddenly, the dying serpent snaps its jaws open, sinking its fangs into the bird's throat, and the two drop from the tree.

Apollo walks up to examine the animals that fought to the death. The bloody serpent slithers up his fingers and opens its eyes weakly.

The bird lies dead.

**Apollo:** The auguries are clear, Lady Hedone.

**Hedone:** ...

**Apollo:** The beast will fall, and you shall return triumphant.

The serpent flicks its tongue and lies still on the prophet's hand.

**Apollo:** But stones sharp as dragon's teeth lie before you.

**Apollo:** And with your triumph, what you have forsaken will be restored.

**Hedone:** My dear Apollo, were I not so forgiving, I might venture you are warning me against my quest.

Hedone's brows knit together. She glares down at him with clear dissatisfaction.

**Apollo:** Omens must be read for what they are, not for what we want them to be, my lady.

**Hedone:** Hmph. Let the fates pluck their strings as they will. I care only for Typhon's eye.

**Hedone:** Whatever else may come of my quest is of little consequence to me.

**Apollo:** There is perhaps more that lies beneath the surface of these signs.

The young man's voice sinks, and he reaches for a more hopeful explanation.

**Sonetto:** Apollo, the Greek god of prophecies. His name certainly fits. I wonder if that ritual he performed is related to the "oracle" Ms. Narcissus told us about.

**Regulus:** "Ritual" this, "oracle" that. This whole island seems barmy.

**Sonetto:** Hold on. Regulus, where is everyone else? Where's the Timekeeper?

**Sonetto:** I need to get us free as soon as possible so we can search for her. A poem on liberation might do.

Sonetto rifles through her mind for a relevant verse and begins to chant softly.

**Sonetto:**"Liberty,scatteringcontagiousfireintothesky."

Shaped by her arcane skill, the imagery of breaking free takes form as an invisible pair of scissors.

**Regulus:** Mind hurrying it along, Sonetto? My arms have gone all pins and needles.

Sonetto's expression clouds as the scissors fade away.

**Sonetto:** The binding isn't especially complex, but it must have come from a powerful arcanist.

**???:** Sonetto?

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper!

A familiar, hushed voice speaks from behind her.

**Vertin:** Keep it down. Narcissus, would you please undo the incantation?

**Vertin:** Hurry, before Hedone notices us.

**Narcissus:** No trouble at all.

**Regulus:** Ow, careful! That's my finger!

**Narcissus:** Oh, sorry.

**Regulus:** "No trouble," she says. But if anything, she's made it tighter. Hey, uh, maybe you should try another way?

**Narcissus:** No, no. I'm almost there. Just a little bit.

**Narcissus:** Hmph! Come on, Narcissus. Nothing can stop you. Hedone certainly can't be that clever.

**Narcissus:** Just hold on a moment longer.

Narcissus begins murmuring to herself.

**???:** Sisyphus himself would marvel at your efforts in futility.

**Hedone:** Shall I do the rest?

**Sonetto:** —!

The proud woman standing off in the distance a moment ago closes the distance in a flash, her shadow looming over them.

**Narcissus:** Hedone! You really went out of your way with these bindings, didn't you? All to humiliate me!

**Hedone:** \*scoff\* It takes no great effort to accomplish your humiliation.

**Hedone:** But then, what does a prideful little thief know of humility to begin with?

Arms crossed, she looks down on the Nymph who stares back incredulously.

**Narcissus:** I ... I ... You! That's ... That's not ...

**Hedone:** Ah, your vain attempt to rescue these interlopers is almost enough to evoke some pity in me. Perhaps it's best you return to your pond, Nymph.

**Hedone:** While I have little taste for idleness, it may serve to still your quixotic mind.

**Narcissus:** ...

**Regulus:** So much for inevitable triumph, eh Narcissus? You sure that "oracle" of yours wasn't leading you into a trap?

**Narcissus:** It couldn't be! Apollo is ... never ... wrong ...

**Hedone:** My dear, naive little Nymph. Have you never learned the difference between a prophecy and an oracle?

She eyes the newcomers with interest, weighing her next decision carefully.

**Hedone:** Tell me, did your "oracle" warn you of this?

The next second, she turns to the spectators watching the ritual and snaps her fingers.

**Hedone:** Citizens!

**Hedone:** You have come for a celebration, have you not? What sort of host would I be were I not to provide some spectacle?

**Hedone:** And I would be all the more remiss if I did not include our new guests!

**Hedone:** Perhaps, after our festivities, we might allow them to pay homage to an ancient Roman custom: a battle betwixt human and beast!

She lifts her hands high, and her people answer with a roar.

Spurred on by their ruler, the cheering crowd bray for the battle to begin.

**Regulus:** Beasts?! Er, Your Ladyship, er, Regalness, since we're guests and all, we'd hate to interrupt your big party.

**Regulus:** Huh? I can move my hands again. The incantation's gone.

As the force binding her body fades, Regulus immediately rubs her sore wrists.

**Hedone:** Naturally. I would not have our honored guests kept bound and tied for their event.

The sovereign's moods and decisions arrive like sudden storms, violent and impossible to predict.

**Hedone:** We have many traditions on this island. Drinking, sailing, festivals ... and above all, our sacred Games.

**Hedone:** I can think of no greater welcome for our new arrivals but for them to partake in one themselves.

**Hedone:** A game to prove through strength and will that you deserve to stand among our noble citizens, or die a spectacular death in the act!

The next moment, rustling sounds rise from deep in the forest, as if plants were grinding against each other as something tears through them. The sounds swell into a thunderous cacophony of grunts and stamping feet.

The ground trembles in rapid pulses as the chaotic stampede draws closer. Then, the creatures burst through.

**Sonetto:** Gold ... Golden-fleeced rams?

**Vertin:** Watch out!

**Narcissus:** This-This is an outrage. I won't let you touch them, Hedone, not even a hair on their heads!

She spreads her arms wide, trembling as she stands before her chosen champions.

**Narcissus:** We-We did it! Unbelievable! You really must be the promised heroes!

**Narcissus:** Not so smug now, are you, Hedone?! This is only the beginning. Soon, we'll bring your tyranny crashing down, and you'll be groveling at our feet.

Narcissus puffs up as she announces her inevitable victory, to the growing discomfort of her comrades.

**Regulus:** That's the spirit, Narcissus. Stick it to the man. Mind you. All we did was chase away some flashed-up sheep.

**Regulus:** And, uhm, maybe we should "ix-nay the revolution-ay" until we're out of earshot of the lady that can tie us up with her mind?

**Hedone:** A most impressive display! Ah, I do so enjoy the rams—ever inspiring acts of violence and courage. It is little wonder the Colchian Dragon guarded them without rest.

**Hedone:** But indulge me, little Nymph. What is this scheme of yours that will bring me "groveling at your feet"?

Hedone offers a smile without warmth, bending down as though eager to listen.

**Apollo:** I believe I know what caused this confusion, Lady Hedone.

Apollo strides over, stepping in just as the scene teeters on the edge of chaos.

His interruption buys them a moment's breathing room, and in that narrow gap, the group exchanges a few hushed words.

**Vertin:** Sonetto, Regulus, does this all seem odd to you too?

**Vertin:** Whether on account of their leader or something else entirely, this island is quite different from what we expected.

**Vertin:** And if they're still hosting Games, why would they have lost contact with Ulu?

**Vertin:** Something must have changed here recently. Perhaps it's related to this "Typhon" awakening.

**Sonetto:** I believe there's more to it than that, Timekeeper. Hedone appears to be one of the most powerful arcanists we've ever encountered.

**Sonetto:** If I'm correct and she is the one who's concealing this island from outsiders, those unbreakable bindings might just be the least of her arcane skills.

A mighty volcano silently broods over the island.

**Vertin:** Well observed. Whatever the case may be, we need to investigate the island further.

**Regulus:** Far be it for me to be the voice of reason, mates, but I doubt "Lady Hedone" is going to let us gallivant about wherever we please.

**Hedone:** In that much, you are correct.

Hedone inserts herself into their conversation with dramatic flair.

**Hedone:** Done with your plotting now, interlopers?

**Hedone:** If you have so little to do besides chattering, I can always arrange another competition.

They gulp down their fears, realizing now may be their last chance to plead their case.

**Vertin:** Lady Hedone, forgive us. We were merely discussing how best to apologize for our intrusion. Allow me to explain the purpose of our visit.

**Vertin:** We've come at the request of Ulu, the sacred flame of the Uluru Games. She's been trying to make contact with your island for many years without success.

**Vertin:** She gave me this to present to you when we met.

**Hedone:** Hmm?

**Hedone:** This is indeed my token.

**Hedone:** Haha. I'd never have imagined she'd have kept it after all these years.

The token buys them a little patience from the sovereign.

**Hedone:** So then, friends of the sacred flame, have you come all this way only to return my token to me?

**Vertin:** Not just that. We've also come to request that you open the Aquatic Games up to the world once more.

**Vertin:** Ms. Ulu told us your island was once a renowned host of them.

**Vertin:** We would like to see the Games revived, and we would also ask for your permission—

**Vertin:** To compete against you.
