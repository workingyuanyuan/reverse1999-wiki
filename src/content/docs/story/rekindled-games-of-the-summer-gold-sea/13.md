## Story

The heat grows fiercer.

**Narcissus:** Vertin!

**Narcissus:** Where's Hedone?

The first group of companions arrives at the crater. Narcissus's face is tight with worry, desperate for an answer.

**Narcissus:** Did she do it? Did she slay Typhon again?

**Hermes:** I came too late. Ah, I'm a failure to my namesake. I should've been with her.

**Narcissus:** The whole island is falling apart. There have been earthquakes, and even the sea is raging.

**Regulus:** Whoaaa!!

An excited cry splits the air, and then Regulus comes soaring up from below the cliff.

**Regulus:** \*panting\* Hope you don't mind me using your spare. I thought you might need some help.

She lands steady on her feet, and Sonetto glides down shortly after.

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper! What's happening here?

**Vertin:** I'm not sure yet. Hedone demanded that she fight Typhon alone. It seems she damaged it in some way, but it retreated.

**Vertin:** She followed it into the heart of the volcano. I think she needs our help.

**Sonetto:** Understood. Reinforcements are already on the way.

**Sonetto:** Despite all the destruction, the people of the island have gathered together. They want to fight.

Looking down, the crowd moves as one, surging in a single direction like the white edge of a breaking wave.

**Regulus:** You don't say! I thought they were just a chilled-out bunch of game junkies, but they've really risen to the challenge.

**Regulus:** Guess they're not too different from their leader.

**Regulus:** Not that it changes anything. Our jetpacks are out of juice, and I'm not about to clamber back down on my own. \*sigh\* Seems like we've got no choice but to help.

The volcano rumbles beneath them.

**Vertin:** Hedone's power comes from the volcano. It stands to reason that the volcano's eruption would likewise be linked to her.

**Vertin:** Her fighting the volcano is almost like fighting herself.

**Apollo:** If there is anyone beneath Olympus who could win such a fight, it is Lady Hedone.

The judge and the prophet climb up onto the high ground to meet them.

**Themis:** She has always led us with conviction.

**Themis:** Regardless of anything else, she has earned our trust in that.

**Themis:** Come what may, we will stand beside her.

**Themis:** For Hedone! For Ephaistonos!

Deep Within the Volcano

Magma courses through the volcano's depths like blood through a body, carrying its scorching heat.

Typhon's heavy breathing fills the air around them.

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper! Watch out!

**Vertin:** ...!

There she stands, the one who once stood amid the magma and endured every searing flame.

Her breath is just as ragged, yet she holds her head high, showing no sign of defeat.

**Hedone:** ...

**Vertin:** Lady Hedone.

**Vertin:** You've slain Typhon before. You can do it again.

Typhon has retreated even deeper within the volcano, cowed by Hedone's presence.

**Hedone:** No. The beast cannot truly die.

**Hedone:** It will always return.

**Hedone:** I should have known better. The heart of the volcano was not sated by my triumph. It was merely entertained by our battle.

**Hedone:** No matter whether I offer it Typhon's eye or heart ...

**Hedone:** Even were I to break it into pieces, burn it to ash, and scatter it into the winds and sea, it would return.

**Hedone:** It, like me, is tied to the will of the volcano.

**Hedone:** The "god" that granted me power!

The fires around them surge to match her fury.

**Vertin:** That may be so. But, would you allow an interloper to offer a new perspective?

The magma around them burns hot enough to scorch skin.

**Vertin:** Your victory all those years ago was real.

**Vertin:** Just as the fresco depicts it.

**Vertin:** You overcame those challenges, just as we have now.

**Sonetto:** We've recreated every step: the celebrations, the prophecy, the Games ...

**Sonetto:** The same steps that you and your friends once took.

Sonetto steps closer to the towering figure.

**Vertin:** Lady Hedone, the one who wields the power of the volcano—

The words are swallowed up by the voices that follow.

**Hermes:** Lady Hedone! I know you can do it. I've been recording every moment of your battle for posterity. Well, actually I did miss a few bits.

**Hermes:** Forgive me. I should have gotten here sooner.

**Hermes:** Anyway, what I mean to say is that since we're all in this together, I thought maybe I could share an idea.

**Hermes:** Just a thought. Maybe I'm just daydreaming, but I said to myself:

**Hermes:** "I wish I could have been one of Hedone's companions and fought by her side."

**Hermes:** And now I have the chance to really do it.

**Themis:** I have long been the judge for all our Games. I treat that role with the utmost responsibility.

**Themis:** I believe in acting without bias towards anyone or anything.

**Themis:** But in this, I will for you, Lady Hedone.

**Themis:** Games aside. Whether as a judge or just a citizen, I will always stand by your side.

Standing out from the crowd, Apollo, so closely bound to this ritual, bends low in a deep bow.

**Apollo:** You were once hailed as "Queen" among us.

**Apollo:** But though we still look to you as our leader and guide, you refuse any title but "Lady."

**Apollo:** You have always been keenly aware of the changes of the world. The rules of the past, even the conventions of power, are insignificant to you.

**Apollo:** What matters most has always been this island and its people.

**Apollo:** We have that in common, Lady Hedone.

He steps toward her.

**Apollo:** It is not the victor of the Games who wields the power of the volcano.

**Apollo:** It is bestowed on anyone who completes the ritual and finishes the hunt.

**Vertin:** We've celebrated, we've played your games, and now we've come to the end.

**Vertin:** Lady Hedone, let's face this last challenge together.

**Vertin:** You're not alone anymore.

**Narcissus:** You heard them, Hedone!

Even after all the years of resistance, Narcissus cannot help but stand by her side in this moment.

**Narcissus:** We won't go down without a fight.

**Hedone:** ...

Renewed with her people's earnest trust and support, Hedone turns to stare deep into the churning magma.

It's as though time itself flows past behind her eyes.

**Hedone:** Before now, I had never truly feared your deaths. It is, after all, inevitable.

**Hedone:** But now, my people, my friends ...

It's the first time in a long time that she has said that word.

**Hedone:** I dare not bring you an early one, to prove the stories of my past once again true.

**Hedone:** But if you are not afraid of tales long past, if you will stand with me in defiance of them ...

**Hedone:** Destiny awaits!

**Hedone:** Vertin.

The earth shakes and the volcano groans as Typhon unleashes the last of its fury.

**Typhon:** ...!

The beast, reborn from death, tears through solid rock and lets out a deafening roar.

**Hedone:** What lies ahead is no mere game. It will be a battle to shake the heavens. But I believe that we can triumph, together.

**Hedone:** Besides, we haven't yet declared a winner of the Games. Perhaps we'll need to host a tie-breaker.

She reaches out one hand and slides her arm ring off the other.

**Vertin:** I look forward to it, Lady Hedone.
