Ritual Plaza

**Themis:** Welcome, one and all, to the final ceremony of the Aquatic Games.

**Themis:** I am certain that the outcome of this competition needs no explanation.

Themis's voice carries more feeling than usual as she declares the end of this extraordinary competition.

**Themis:** With the Games concluded, Lady Hedone's power is no longer limited by the Pact.

**Themis:** And I am pleased to announce that, this time, the winner's laurel will not sit on just one brow.

**Hedone:** But first, I feel that I am obliged to fulfill my vow to Vertin, co-victor of our Games.

Hedone raises one hand, and a brilliant light rises around her.

Gold and treasure spill across the ground, as if some vast, hidden vault has broken open.

**Hedone:** In my many voyages and adventures, I have amassed a great fortune of rare treasures.

**Hedone:** And now, I would bestow them upon my friends.

**Hedone:** These are yours, Vertin. Consider them rewards for your part in our victory and a token of my friendship and gratitude.

They stand surrounded by glittering treasure.

**Vertin:** That's very generous of you, Lady Hedone.

**Vertin:** But we didn't come here seeking any treasure or rewards.

**Regulus:** Hah. Speak for yourself, Vertin. If you're going to turn them down, at least let me have them.

**Regulus:** Think about what all that treasure could do. We'd never have to worry about the ship's maintenance budget again.

**Sonetto:** Hmm. I'm sure we can get your funding through the Foundation, Regulus.

**Regulus:** But-But treasure ...

Bright as it shines, the treasure cannot hold them.

**Vertin:** We couldn't possibly take anything from you, Hedone. Your friendship alone is enough.

**Hedone:** ...

**Hedone:** You earned that many times over, and not just my own. You already have a wealth of friends around you.

That's true, and there's something like a quiet joy in knowing it.

**Vertin:** I do.

**Hedone:** But there is one gift I will not permit you to deny: the laurel of victory is yours by right.

**Hedone:** Come! Take it as a memorial of your triumph.

**Narcissus:** Shouldn't I get a laurel too?

Narcissus slumps over the bar, sulking. She opens her palm to reveal her broken silver chain, the shimmering scale that once hung from it now conspicuously absent.

She once thought of it as a token of good luck, a miracle that all but fell from the sky.

She thinks back to how she felt in the moment she threw it away.

**Narcissus:** To create a path through the lava ... Everyone else thought it was impossible. But my scale did it.

**Regulus:** Narcissus, I've got something for you! It's not exactly a laurel, but here, take this chain I found! Not sure exactly what it's for, or where I found it for that matter.

**Narcissus:** A chain? Why?

**Regulus:** Well, we've only got the one laurel, and you've got to lift something up, don't you? That's what champions do!

**Narcissus:** Oh, I see. Thanks, Regulus. But if there is just the one laurel, Vertin deserves to have it.

Regulus doesn't push the subject but just waves the familiar empty glass at her.

**Regulus:** Suit yourself. Me? I'm taking this as my trophy. Pretty swank, I'd say. Apollo's really got great taste in glassware.

**Regulus:** Sure, it would've been fab to have my pick of Hedone's treasures. What's more rock 'n' roll than staying true to yourself, right?

**Regulus:** Oh, and don't forget what they put together for Sonetto.

Beside her, Sonetto holds up a golden playing card, looking a little uneasy.

**Sonetto:** Uh, Hermes said it was a special award for the "grand champion of pinochle."

Every contestant walks away with a keepsake of their own.

**Narcissus:** Well ... Laurel or not, at least I got to meet you all. I've got enough memories to treasure for a lifetime.

**Narcissus:** That's my prize. And that's more than enough.

She pats herself on the shoulder, cheering herself on like always.

**Vertin:** Narcissus.

Footsteps sound behind her, and she turns at once.

Something bright flickers in her eyes.

**Vertin:** Would you take this laurel?

**Narcissus:** Ver-Ver-Ver-Ver-Ver ...!

**Regulus:** Er, hello? Ground Control to Major Narcissus?

Dutiful as ever, Regulus calls her name, a smile warming her voice.

**Narcissus:** B-But this is a symbol of victory! You're the one who deserves it after all you've done!

**Vertin:** And you were the one that brought us here. Just like the oracle foretold.

**Vertin:** You were right to believe in it all along.

Narcissus can't even manage a full sentence, her heart hammering, her breath coming too fast. Every word spills out in a strange pitch.

**Narcissus:** Umm. \*whimper\*

**Narcissus:** Waaah!

She throws herself at the heroes she'd sought out from beyond the island and bursts into tears.

A triumphant cheer drowns out every laugh and every sob.

**Hedone:** A selfless deed to sweeten the mood. Nicely done, Vertin.

Cup in hand, Hedone laughs and pulls her dear friend in by the shoulder.

**Hedone:** I do believe I promised to make you a drink. Don't worry. It's non-alcoholic.

**Vertin:** Thank you, Lady Hedone.

**Narcissus:** \*cry\*

**Hedone:** Enough, Narcissus, you'll drown the island with all those tears.

**Hedone:** And they have little place in a time of joy.

**Narcissus:** But, but I can't help it.

**Narcissus:** There's no rule against crying at a party. And I thought you gave up being a tyrant.

**Sonetto:** If you need it, I have a handkerchief here.

**Hedone:** Or perhaps a drink might dry your tears? I'll whip up another just for you. Though, I think I'll hold the alcohol for yours too.

**Narcissus:** Hey, I'm old enough to drink.

**Hedone:** Hahahahaha. Right! I do believe you're older than I am, aren't you? At least in this incarnation.

**Regulus:** What?!

**Regulus:** How old are you exactly, Narcissus?

Regulus's voice seems to carry like a cannon blast.

For Hermes, this is turning out to be a spectacularly unlucky day.

He glances at Apollo and Themis across from him, then back at Hedone in front of him.

**Hermes:** Uh, hey.

**Hedone:** Hi.

Hedone only gives an idle nod. Beside her, the golden cup shining with jewels cradles wine the color of honey, its sweetness drifting soft through the air.

**Hermes:** Are you playing cards?

**Themis:** We are.

Themis lays down another card.

**Hermes:** Mind if I join?

**Hedone:** Of course not. But I can't stay long. Perhaps you can take my place.

Expressionless, Hedone tosses her hand of cards onto the table.

**Hedone:** Really, now? You can't even let me win for old time's sake?

**Apollo:** I believe that Lady Hedone herself declared that cheating in all fashions was prohibited on the island.

**Hermes:** I'll let you win, Hedone. Just as long as you keep playing.

**Hermes:** Gods, with you leaving and Narcissus already gone, I'm running out of people to play pinochle with.

**Hermes:** I'm going to be stuck with these two. They never let me win.

Ignoring Hermes's desperate pleading, the iron-hearted sovereign rises to her feet, lifts her cup, and drains it in one go.

**Hedone:** Rest assured, my friends. I will return.
