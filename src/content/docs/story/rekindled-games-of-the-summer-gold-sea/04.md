## Story

Ritual Plaza

The outsider's offer sets the whole crowd talking at once, chatter rising until it fills the entire plaza in a deafening clamor.

**???:** The Aquatic Games?! Haha! I can hardly remember when the last one was.

**???:** Must be years, mustn't it? Decades? Centuries? Whatever it is, I can't wait to get back in the water.

A young man pinches the brim of his baseball cap, grinning in anticipation.

**???:** It's been 33 years, in fact, since the last formal Aquatic Games, anyway. Though that never stopped us from having our own events.

**???:** It's hard to imagine any life here without the thrill of competition.

**???:** In fact, it's only been a few weeks since I hosted our last one, where I recall you quite enjoyed yourself, Hermes.

The composed woman wearing far more traditional clothing nods in agreement.

**Hermes:** But this must be an extra exciting one for you, dear Themis. You'll be presiding over Lady Hedone's own performance soon, I'm sure. She's never been one to turn down a challenge.

**Hermes:** Someone better warn our guests. Challenging the mighty Hedone? Now that's not something you do lightly.

Narcissus appears to be the most enthused out of everyone present.

**Narcissus:** I second it. I second the challenge.

Her arm flails upward with excitement.

**Narcissus:** It's a brilliant idea, don't you think? A wise, far-sighted proposal, like, like wisdom etched in stone.

**Narcissus:** I knew you were the ones, the exact people destined to help me.

**Narcissus:** Just as the lead oracles of Dodona shaped the paths of many before, you have come to shape mine.

Her eyes light up at the prospect of her changing fate.

**Hedone:** All this excitement, it does bring to mind my last Uluru Games, in an age before my long sleep.

**Hedone:** Of course, our mutual friend could only watch, flickering from the shoreline. How amusing that the very spirit who proposed the Games couldn't participate herself, ahahahahaha!

**Hedone:** Tell me: how does our little Ulu fare now? Does she still tremble at the sight of water?

The memory of the fire spirit seems to put her in a good mood. She rests a hand on the shoulder of the one who made the proposal.

**Vertin:** She's doing well. We were told to send you her warmest regards.

Hedone meets a steady gaze, calm and respectful.

**Hedone:** Very well, friends of the sacred flame.

**Hedone:** I am inclined to accept your challenge. However ...

The grip on the shoulder tightens.

**Hedone:** I fear you are not worthy opponents for one such as me.

**Hedone:** Why should I compete with an opponent who offers no real challenge to my supremacy over the sea?

Her words cut without mercy, yet her expression gives away her interest in the answer.

She seems to have a taste for the unexpected and the novel.

**Vertin:** We may not be a match for you. That much is true.

**Vertin:** But you said yourself that these sorts of Games are always hosted before a great quest. They're a part of your rituals, aren't they?

**Vertin:** And as we know that you compete in them yourself, we assume you have some way to give a fair chance to the other islanders.

**Vertin:** We would only ask for the same treatment. We'll compete as a team so that we have a chance of rivaling you together.

**Vertin:** What do you say? Perhaps we can breathe some new excitement into your old traditions.

**Sonetto:** It would be much like the Uluru Games, with contestants from all over the world competing in the same events.

**Sonetto:** And we would naturally abide by all your rules and stipulations, both in the Games and elsewhere on the island during our stay.

Sonetto continues, her tone serious.

**Sonetto:** We ask that you give it due consideration, as holding the Aquatic Games would be in accordance with your usual proceedings.

**Hedone:** "Stipulations"? "Due consideration"?

**Hedone:** Ahahahahahahaha!

Sonetto flinches half a step.

**Sonetto:** Forgive me, Lady Hedone, if I've said anything out of turn.

**Hedone:** No, nothing of the sort. I merely find your rigid formality rather amusing.

**Sonetto:** Amusing?

**Hedone:** We hold these sacred Games because they instill in us the resolve and passion necessary to face danger and death.

**Hedone:** Whether it's a fight to the death or a simple card game between friends, in the end we toast the glorious victors, to celebrate life and the thrill of competition.

**Hedone:** It is hardly some mere ritual through which we must "proceed."

**Hedone:** That said, you might do a passable job at embodying their spirit.

**Hedone:** Themis, attend me!

She curls her hand, and in response, Themis steps forward.

**Themis:** I am at your service.

**Hedone:** Themis will be our master of ceremonies and adjudicator. Nothing escapes her keen eyes.

**Hedone:** On this island, all stand equal before her judgment, myself included.

She smiles as she introduces the visitors to her goddess of justice.

**Hedone:** What say you, Themis? Will you do us the honor of overseeing the Aquatic Games?

Themis provides her answer after a measured pause.

**Themis:** Without question, Lady Hedone.

**Themis:** We still have time before Typhon fully awakens.

**Themis:** I can see no better occasion for the Aquatic Games than the eve of your quest.

**Hedone:** Well said. Already you demonstrate the wisdom of your judgment, dear Themis.

She turns to the contestants—her rivals, her azure cape rising like a graceful wave.

**Hedone:** Now to you, interlopers, prophesied heroes, envoys of the sacred flame. Whatever you may be, I confess a growing interest.

**Hedone:** Your request is approved. You may compete in the Games.

**Hedone:** Now, our judge has agreed, the competitors are willing, and the field is ready.

**Hedone:** However, before the event begins, I would like to make a few additions to our agreement.

**Hedone:** Firstly, if you win ...

She snaps her fingers.

**Narcissus:** Ah!

**Narcissus:** That's the "Eye of the Tides"! They say whoever wears it can find any treasure buried beneath the sea.

**Narcissus:** The craftsmanship alone is a wonder to behold. Every gem on it shines with the brilliance of a sunlit sea. No mortal hand could ever forge anything so spectacular.

**Narcissus:** Hephaestus himself would marvel at it.

**Regulus:** Blimey! Imagine what you could do with a beauty like that!

**Regulus:** Vertin, listen, you know I never ask you for anything, but we have got to win that necklace.

**Narcissus:** And that must be "The Conch of Safe Haven"! It looks like a simple shell, but when blown, it summons a magical harbor that can weather any calamity at sea.

**Narcissus:** At least that's what the old tales say.

**Narcissus:** Oh, the net beside it, I know it too. It's the "Silver Net of Kharon." If you cast it into the river Styx, you'll draw up endless coins and gems like you've never seen.

**Narcissus:** Though, it does bear a curse. Those who draw too greedily may be pulled into the Styx themselves.

**Narcissus:** We may have our differences, but her taste in treasure is immaculate.

The grandeur of the treasure before them almost sends Narcissus's head into a spin. The jewels grow ever more dazzling as she speaks.

**Hedone:** You do possess an encyclopedic knowledge of all things shiny, don't you, little Nymph? One of your few redeeming traits.

**Narcissus:** Hmph! Well, I must know them all if I'm to prove I'm worthy of having them.

**Narcissus:** And believe you me, these treasures would be far better served with me than locked away in your hoard.

Her longing stare is fixed solid on the treasures, unwilling or unable to part with them for even a blink.

**Hedone:** Admire them as you like, Narcissus. Just remember, when you do that, they are all mine.

**Hedone:** But these baubles have never been enough to satisfy me.

**Hedone:** What I crave is worthy competition, and worthy competitors demand the finest rewards.

**Hedone:** Defeat me in these Games, and the treasures before you will be yours to take.

**Hedone:** Now, let us turn to the matter of your wager.

**Hedone:** What should become of those who fail to best me?

Hedone crosses her arms and gives a small nod.

**Hedone:** Ah, I could tell you such stories. Many miserable fates have befallen those who disappoint me.

She strides before them, her eyes sweeping over her opponents one by one with an almost wicked fervor.

**Hedone:** What shall it be this time? I could always melt you in the fires of the volcano or summon a horde of Scyllas to tear you limb from limb.

She lets the words dangle over their heads like a blade.

**Narcissus:** ...!

**Regulus:** Oi, Narcissus! What're you doing cowering behind me? Didn't you say you were some kind of destined champion?

The frightened Nymph makes a show of pretending not to hear her.

**Hedone:** Oh, how silly of me. I gave up those punishments when I agreed to host the Aquatic Games. I hope I didn't alarm you.

**Hedone:** That sacred flame can be quite persuasive. She insisted that competition should be a celebration of life and vitality rather than a struggle for survival.

The blade withdraws, but its edge lingers.

**Hedone:** For her sake, then, let us be contestants, not combatants.

**Hedone:** Eureka!

She leans down toward the boldest petitioner.

**Hedone:** You will decide your own wager, so long as it satisfies me. I have every trust that you'll figure out something amusing. You did propose the Games, after all.

**Hedone:** The risk of failure is all part of the fun, until you actually fail, that is.

**Vertin:** ...

**Vertin:** We accept your terms, Lady Hedone.

The words evoke a contented smile.

**Hedone:** Very well. Now the stakes are settled. Themis!

Without another word from Hedone, the judge begins explaining.

**Themis:** The Aquatic Games, in their oldest form, consist of three events.

**Themis:** First: the Sea Gliding Competition.

**Themis:** Second: the Sea Beast Taming Exhibition.

**Themis:** And finally, the Underwater Obstacle Race.

**Regulus:** Wooo, hang about! This is all starting to sound right up my alley. You lot have got style.

**Narcissus:** Oh, we always try to keep up with the times. It hasn't been all that long since we closed ourselves off to the world. We used to be quite a busy trade port.

**Narcissus:** I remember when I took a trip to one of the great capitals of the outside, only to find that they had nothing different from our markets here.

**Narcissus:** Our home really was perfect in every aspect, until ... well.

**Themis:** I will, of course, explain the rules of all events in full for our newcomers so that they can compete fairly.

**Themis:** On that topic, before we begin, the Pact of Balance must be sealed to ensure fairness and offset any advantage gained through arcane skills.

**Themis:** This pact will constrain the use of arcanum to a sensible portion of Lady Hedone's powers for the duration of the Games.

Her eyes sweep over all present.

**Themis:** Now, if all contestants agree to what has been stated—

**Themis:** I will ask for your names.

**Vertin:** You may call me Vertin. I'll be our team leader.

**Sonetto:** Sonetto.

**Regulus:** Regulus!

**Hedone:** Hedone!

A brilliant light bursts from Themis's palm, bathing the contestants in its glow.

**Themis:** By the authority of the scale, I bind all our competitors to its sacred balance.

**Themis:** The Pact is sealed.

**Themis:** Let the Games begin.

**???:** You've reached the LSCC. Our office is temporarily closed. Working hours will resume after the break. Please leave your message after the beep. "Beep."

**???:** Oh? Is that you, Timekeeper?
