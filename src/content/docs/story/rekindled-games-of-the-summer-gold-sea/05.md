## Story

Laplace Office

**X:** Haha. Good to hear that you all made it there in one piece.

**X:** Ah, looks like an electronic communique from Mr. APPLe just came through.

**X:** Yes. He seems to be in reasonably good shape. I'll keep him posted on your ongoing expedition.

**X:** Hmm? Ah, unpowered aeronautics. Yes, gliding. Sounds like fun.

**X:** Of course we'll help. I've got just the specialist at the very front of my mind.

**X:** Hmm. Shouldn't take too long. We'll push for a special motion and sort out the paperwork later.

**X:** Anytime, Timekeeper. Talk to you soon.

**Medicine Pocket:** What's she after? And which poor schmuck did you just volunteer as a "specialist"?

**X:** Don't be so snarky. When the Timekeeper calls for aid, we answer, right? We'll figure out the rest.

**Medicine Pocket:** So, you're saying we've gotta fight over it. Awesome. \*sigh\* How're we gonna do this, then?

X throws up his hands.

**X:** I don't see any reason to come to blows. I figure we just pawn the job off on Enigma.

**X:** He's been asking to be involved in fieldwork. And he's not here to complain about it.

**Medicine Pocket:** Wait, he's out? Then who's gonna sign these forms I just filed?

**Medicine Pocket:** Nobody's sending him anywhere until I get my applications approved!

**X:** Well, who else is there? Everyone else is chronically indisposed. I can't even find any willing subjects for my latest prototype.

**Medicine Pocket:** You got approval for a new design?

They give X an overly firm pat on the back.

**X:** Medicine Pocket, do I really need to remind you again about the workplace conduct agreements we all signed?

**X:** Unfortunately, I've got too much on my plate as it is and a shift to cover. Besides, poor Mr. APPLe is drifting somewhere in the Aegean Sea waiting for an update from Team Timekeeper.

**Medicine Pocket:** Run a few errands, message that floating fruit—sounds easy enough. Why don't I take those things off your big piled plate? Anything else? No? Great!

**Medicine Pocket:** Oh, and do let me know if this latest teleport disk iteration still has that weird aftertaste. Have fun out there!

**X:** Hold on. What do you mean by that? Medi—

Beach, Ephaistonos

Hermes sizes up the two challengers with interest.

**Hermes:** So, a seafaring musician and a scientist. Quite the team!

**Regulus:** Ahem, ahem! This is a brilliant captain and her ever-clever shipwright. Just you wait. We'll build a ship that flies like nothing you've ever seen before.

**X:** \*sigh\* How have we never thought to install a return function on these teleport disks? Hmm? Yes, of course, Captain Regulus.

X maintains a professional smile without expression.

**Hermes:** A ship like nothing ever seen before? Well now ...

**Hermes:** You're not wrong to think that craftsmanship's the key to it all.

**Hermes:** It's pivotal, really. Every ship gets the same start, you see. A push and a long slide up to an inclined ramp, then, swoosh! You're off into the air, gliding over the water.

**Hermes:** It's all about getting that good glide, making sure your creation goes the farthest before touching the sea. Right on theme for the competitions on this island, because ... anyone?

He watches the "students" before him like an eager kindergarten teacher, urging them to shout out an answer.

**Regulus:** Theme? Uh, 'cause there's scores for style?

**Narcissus:** No, he's obviously talking about victory.

**Sonetto:** Hmm, aren't Games meant to be for fun?

**Regulus:** Fun? Come off it, Sonetto. What is this "Ask the Family"?

**Vertin:** The spirit of competition?

**Hermes:** So close. The answer is and always will be—the love of the game. Hahaha!

**Hermes:** Since the day we set foot on this island, we've cared for one thing above all else: the thrill of a good game! Cards, tabletop, wrestling, even hunting! As long as there's a winner, we're getting into it.

**Vertin:** But why?

The question is simple, but it seems to bring a sparkle to the young man's eye.

**Hermes:** Well, that's probably because we won this island with a game.

**Hermes:** Yes! A very dangerous one! And Lady Hedone was its victor.

**Hermes:** Since then, they've become an inseparable part of our lives—sacred rituals we perform before any challenge or danger to pump us up for what's to come!

He maintains his bright smile as he steers the lesson from ancient history to the present.

**Hermes:** Oh! I should warn you. Tomorrow's competition isn't just about going the distance. It's for the spectators too.

**Hermes:** The reaction of the crowd factors into the final ratings.

**Hermes:** That's why I said you'd make a strong team.

Eyes shining, he raises a finger and points at the team.

**Hermes:** You've got a builder, a planner, an assistant, and a captain.

**Hermes:** Quite the interesting mix, I must say.

**Hermes:** I'm sure the spectators will be hooting and hollering, just as long as you do it in style.

**Regulus:** Style? Ha, well, we've got that by the buckets. And I've already got some ideas brewing.

**Regulus:** X, old buddy, old chum! Listen up.

Regulus rushes over to X, who's already busying himself with designs and blueprints, and the two dive headfirst into their work.

A cheerful chime rings out.

**Hermes:** Oh! That'd be my cue. Have a card game to catch.

**Hermes:** See you soon. When you run around as much as I do, you learn to appreciate every moment off your feet.

He tips his cap and waves farewell to the newcomers.

**Hermes:** May the gods grant you luck, or at the very least, a good bit of fun!

**Narcissus:** Vertin!

With their pre-competition lesson wrapped up, the team dives into their work. Not long after, Narcissus arrives with an exasperated expression.

**Narcissus:** I've been all over the place trying to sneak a look at the other teams' progress. Hedone's, especially.

**Narcissus:** But Themis was quicker than I thought. She set up barriers everywhere. Anyone who tries to get close to another team's work gets bounced off like a rubber ball.

**Vertin:** We appreciate your effort nonetheless. So, are those barriers one of Themis's arcane skills?

**Narcissus:** Actually, they come from the Pact itself. Besides limiting Hedone's power, it also stops anything that might compromise the fairness of the Games.

The keyword lands, and Sonetto is the first to respond.

**Sonetto:** I've been meaning to ask: what kind of arcane skills does Lady Hedone possess?

**Narcissus:** Oh, that's simple. Her abilities are linked to the island's volcano. They're essentially one and the same.

**Narcissus:** They were granted to her after she defeated Typhon. That was before her long sleep. When Typhon began to stir again, she woke up and took control once more.

The way she explains it makes it all seem like common knowledge, though she does her best to deliver it with excitement.

**Narcissus:** You-You really don't know anything about this place, do you? If you don't know about Hedone's powers, then what about me? What about the oracle?

**Vertin:** I'm afraid we don't place quite the same faith in oracles as you do.

**Narcissus:** Ah!

She looks troubled. To get an answer, she needs the right nudge, a little more encouragement.

**Vertin:** But if you could explain a little more, it might improve our chances in the coming competition.

**Narcissus:** If you want to know more about Hedone, I know where you can go, but ...

**Narcissus:** Urgh, I loathe the very idea of going there. It's just so full of ... her!

**Narcissus:** But I'll grit my teeth and power through if it means defeating that tyrant. Come on!

**Sonetto:** Ms. Narcissus, if you don't mind me asking, there's something I've been wondering about the way your people treat games on this island.

Earnest curiosity and an urge to gather strategic intel vie for control in her voice.

**Sonetto:** When we were tied up before, heh, I noticed that the crowd didn't really respond with excitement or hostility. They seemed almost expectant.

**Narcissus:** Well, they weren't there just to watch Hedone toy with her captives. They were hoping to see our efforts too, to see if we could turn the tide.

**Narcissus:** And we didn't disappoint them, did we?

**Narcissus:** Hmm. I think they enjoyed that more than just watching us lose.

**Narcissus:** Oh, here we are.

Parting the leaves veiling the path ahead, they find their destination at last.

**Narcissus:** Ow, something just hit me.

**Sonetto:** I believe they're Eclipse Bats. They're known for being territorial.

**Narcissus:** Right, I-I forgot about them. Hedone never lets outsiders near this place.

***

COMBAT:

***

The wings stirring in the dark scatter and vanish.

**Vertin:** —!

**Narcissus:** This fresco depicts Hedone defeating Typhon and calming the volcano.

**Narcissus:** I'm certain the details have been embellished, but it should still be useful to you.

**Narcissus:** I suppose you need a guide, do you? Though I'd rather not recount her feats.

No one could look at this and deny it: this is an epic torn straight from the pages of ancient Greek myth.

Blessed by the wind ruler, she turned into a feather and soared through the sky.

**Narcissus:** The monster is Typhon. Around it, a violent storm rises like a wall while the air within remains perfectly, deadly still.

**Narcissus:** Even the most experienced seafarer couldn't hope to pass through its terrible gales unscathed.

**Narcissus:** That was until—Hedone. They say the winds themselves favored her.

Narcissus admires the fresco until unable to hold back her fascination. The story spills from her lips, vivid and alive.

**Vertin:** To get up close to a monster on windless water in those times ...

**Sonetto:** It seems all but impossible.

Blessed by the sea ruler, she once tamed sea beasts and rode the tides.

**Narcissus:** Not if you ask Hedone. After she crossed the still waters, Typhon turned the sea against her, raising towering waves, stirring up deadly whirlpools, pulling all life into the depths.

**Narcissus:** Even the mightiest of all sailors would scarcely survive the torrent that awaited her.

**Narcissus:** But a sea beast answered Hedone's summons. And on its back, she sailed forward.

**Sonetto:** A sea beast? Sounds a little like the second event of the Aquatic Games.

**Sonetto:** Could that be what it was inspired by?

Under the eyes of the gods, she once walked through a rain of fire and ascended a perilous bluff.

**Narcissus:** Her final challenge came not from the sea, but from above.

**Narcissus:** Flaming rocks rained down like a meteor shower, but that didn't slow her down. She had at last reached Typhon's lair.

In the end, she slew Typhon.

Near the Fresco

The fresco stretches far in both directions, tracing a path all the way to where the islanders live. Somewhere close, cheerful music rides the air from the houses.

**Sonetto:** Typhon is missing an eye. What happened?

**Narcissus:** It was taken from him and buried deep in the volcano.

**Narcissus:** It's said that Typhon will regain it when the volcano's wrath is reawakened.

Their eyes land on the final fresco. Behind Hedone, weapons of every kind lie scattered across the ground.

**Vertin:** She stood there before Typhon with no one by her side, all those weapons scattering about.

**Vertin:** Did they once belong to her friends?

**Narcissus:** Yes. When her ship set sail, she had many comrades. But only Hedone returned.

**Sonetto:** If she survived all those trials and defeated the monster herself, she's even more powerful than I imagined.

**Narcissus:** I don't doubt she is. She was given the power of the volcano as a reward. As long as she remains on this island, she is invincible.

**Narcissus:** Her power has almost no limits. The island itself has become her Elysium, bending itself to her will.

Narcissus paces back and forth in front of the fresco, growing more and more furious with righteous indignation.

**Narcissus:** And believe you me, she's made full use of it!

She curls her small, webbed hand into a fist and shakes it toward the sky.

**Narcissus:** Whatever she desires, she takes, even what I treasured most in this world!

**Narcissus:** My eulogia—my blessing!

**Sonetto:** She stole your blessing? How?

**Narcissus:** On an island of games, some days you win, and some days you lose.

**Narcissus:** But there are two exceptions to that rule—Hedone and I. She never loses, and I never ever win!

**Narcissus:** Not once have I won the Aquatic Games! Not once! All those years! All those attempts! How could anyone live with so much shame?

**Narcissus:** A Nymph, born of the water, yet completely incapable of winning a single Aquatic Game!

Her lips press into a thin, trembling line.

**Narcissus:** But Apollo gave me hope. He spoke to me an oracle. After that, I searched day and night for the stream that flows out of this island.

**Narcissus:** Following his words, I traveled to distant shores where I found an artifact that would guarantee my victory.

**Narcissus:** But as soon as I returned home, Hedone snatched it away from me!

**Narcissus:** And that's why we must defeat her!

She stops her steps and stares at her companions—destined heroes in her eyes.

\*creak\*

Down at the end of the fresco, a wooden door half-buried in ivy swings open.

Hermes steps out from inside, muttering into the communicator in his hand.

**Hermes:** What? But you gave your word. Same time, same place, same game—it's pinochle night. I know you're busy. You're always busy.

**Hermes:** But you're already 34 minutes late.

**Hermes:** Yeah, pinochle again. But we don't have to play cards if you're not in the mood. We can just hang out. Just us three, come on.

**Hermes:** Narcissus? What are you doing here?

**Hermes:** Oh? What do you know? Narcissus is here. That makes four if you show up. Come on. Think about it, alright? See you soon.

He clips the communicator back to his belt, braces the wooden door open with one hand, and with the other, snags Narcissus as she tries to slip away.

**Narcissus:** Four? I was just ... passing ... through!

**Hermes:** Come on, Narcissus. We could really use another player. I know you've been avoiding Apollo, but can't you just put that aside for one night?

**Narcissus:** No. And what does your card game have to do with me?

Before she can finish, Hermes all but tosses her inside.

**Hermes:** Dear Narcissus, I beg you, please, on bended knees. Just one little round of pinochle.

Relief spreads across Hermes's face. He turns to the other two guests, putting on the most earnest expression he can manage.

**Hermes:** Oh, hey! Didn't see you two there.

A warm, golden light spills from behind the door, and light, rhythmic music pulses through the air.

**Hermes:** Maybe you'd like to join us too. We've got a little hideaway nearby. What do you say?

The messenger extends a welcoming hand toward the passing heroes.

### Game Time

> **Description：** Life is a game. Come, spin the wheel of players before the final curtain falls!

> **Action：** Pick Up the Poster

Here, Life Is a Game

Citizens of Ephaistonos, the annual sacred ritual is upon us! The winners of the games will be appointed to new posts! The following games have been added this year: Wine Cellar Keeper - Big Sip, Little Sip Each contestant receives a large goblet of wine. Contestants play rock-paper-scissors, and the winner of each round takes a sip from their own goblet. The first to finish their wine wins, while the contestant in last place is appointed to the post. (Because they've proved they aren't a drinker and have dreadful luck.) Council Scribe - Brain Cells at Full Tilt A set of meeting minutes will be displayed on a whiteboard. Contestants must memorize as much important information as possible within the allotted time. Once viewing time is over, they must reproduce in writing everything they can remember. After four rounds, the contestant with the highest accuracy wins and is appointed to the post. Shell Cutter - Caveman with an Eye for Beauty The organizers provide assorted seashells and conches. Contestants must bring their own tools, bore holes in the shells, and string them into necklaces with silk thread. A panel of five master shell artisans will score the necklaces for beauty and decide the winner. Note: Power tools are not permitted. For more positions and more games, inquire in person at Bar Helicon. Come join the games and decide for yourself how you will live in the year ahead!
