## Story

Typhon is dead.

Its massive body melts back into the magma below, bubbling like a pot left to simmer.

**Hermes:** Its eye!

Hedone looses an arrow that pierces it clean through, slowing the massive body's descent.

But still, it keeps sinking.

**Hedone:** We must claim Typhon's eye to complete the ritual.

**Hedone:** Even now in its deathly slumber, it holds us from our victory.

**Regulus:** Ah! What are you doing, Narcissus?

A faint breeze stirs, barely noticeable above the rising steam.

The figure darting past is far from graceful, stumbling with every step, yet she runs toward the boiling magma with unwavering resolve.

Someone moves to stop her, but a single look from Hedone holds them back.

**Hedone:** ...

**Vertin:** I think she's ...

**Vertin:** She's trying to complete the ritual.

**Narcissus:** \*panting\*

**Narcissus:** Ouch, my feet! Hot!

Yet she keeps running, yelping now and then as the scorching rocks singe her feet. She tears the necklace from her throat and, without a moment's hesitation, hurls her most treasured blessing into the magma.

In this instance, victory is the last thing on her mind.

Narcissus: Ah ...Narcissus: So, that's what it's supposed to look like.Narcissus: "The path ahead belongs to you alone."Narcissus: "Stretching wherever you dare to go."Narcissus: "Move forward, and you shall arrive."

**Narcissus:** Ah ...

**Narcissus:** So, that's what it's supposed to look like.

**Narcissus:** "The path ahead belongs to you alone."

**Narcissus:** "Stretching wherever you dare to go."

**Narcissus:** "Move forward, and you shall arrive."

**Regulus:** You've done it!

**Regulus:** Bloody brilliant!

**Hermes:** Hail Narcissus!

**Hermes:** Hail to our conquering champion!

Their unexpected hero cradles the eye in her arms as she rushes back to her friends.

She glances back, and the path behind her fades away like a curtain falling at the end of a show.

**Narcissus:** Is this ...

**Narcissus:** ... what the oracle meant?

**Narcissus:** I regained my blessing and triumphed over my enemy with allies from afar.

**Narcissus:** I just always thought that enemy would be Hedone.

She hesitates, staring down at the eye in her arms.

**Hedone:** Ahahahahaha! You weren't wrong to think so.

**Hedone:** Was I not your greatest rival?

Hedone claps her on the shoulder, and the force of it knocks Narcissus into a stumble. Her "enemy" looks down at her like an old friend.

**Narcissus:** Ah! Apollo!

Narcissus lets out a shriek laced with the frustration of discovery.

**Apollo:** As I have said many times before.

**Apollo:** The true wisdom of the gods is often only seen in hindsight.

**Apollo:** But it inspired you nonetheless.

Apollo smiles and, together with the others, watches the magma swallow their fearsome enemy whole.

Typhon's body dissolves away into the magma, as though it had never existed at all.

**Hedone:** The beast will return no more.

After a thousand years, the journey comes to its end at last.

Climbing up from the volcano's depths, they find the whole island laid before them: a little outcropping in the wine-dark sea, alive and flourishing once more.

**Hedone:** ...

Yet the sovereign stops in her tracks.

**Vertin:** Is there something wrong, Hedone?

Her expression carries a peace they've never seen her wear before.

**Hedone:** There's something about this moment that feels familiar.

**Hedone:** After I slew Typhon, when I returned to my people, they gathered to greet me like this.

**Hedone:** I remember ...

She lets her gaze pass over everyone gathered around her.

**Hedone:** We stepped upon these shores together and started our lives anew.

**Hedone:** But this time ...

**Hedone:** I have decided I must leave them.

Hedone delivers her decision softly, as if the iron-fisted tyrant melted away with Typhon.

**Narcissus:** What?!

**Narcissus:** La ... Lady Hedone, you aren't ... Tell me you're joking?! How can you just leave?

Stunned Narcissus cannot help but momentarily forget their feud.

But when she looks to the others, she finds them calm, none of them particularly surprised by their leader's decision.

**Hedone:** Heh.

**Hedone:** I left my polis behind and battled with Typhon. I bargained with the heart of a volcano. I died and was reborn.

**Hedone:** You spoke truly before, Apollo. This island is in many ways a testament to my life.

**Hedone:** All that we have here sprang from the choices I made.

**Hedone:** "Chase renewal and embrace it."

She turns to face her guests.

**Hedone:** The world has changed. It changed long before I ever awoke.

**Hedone:** And the "Downpour" will change even more.

**Hedone:** I have a responsibility. If I do not join in the fight against it, then one day I may not be able to protect this land from its ravages.

**Hedone:** I don't want to live at the whims of this volcano anymore. And I don't want this land to wallow in idle isolation.

**Hedone:** I have lived and died and lived again. I have seen the full circle of that ancient ritual, and I see now what it has taken from me.

**Hedone:** I want to make this new life my own, no matter if success or failure comes of it.

**Apollo:** Lady Hedone ...

**Hedone:** My dear Apollo, I see now the wisdom of your reading.

**Hedone:** "What I have forsaken will be restored." So it has.

She closes her eyes and relief settles across her face. Her arm rings seem to catch fire under the soft sunlight.

**Hedone:** I have recovered friendship, a thing I fear I had too long forgotten how to hold. And now, I place my faith in you, my people, my friends, to steward this island.

**Hedone:** I do not know what that will mean for my powers. Should I leave, it is possible I will lose them forever.

**Hedone:** But I must rediscover my will and place myself back into the hands of fate. I will set forth just as I did in the very beginning.

**Hedone:** Like the day I stepped aboard that ship bound for a new home.

Her voice is certain, though her face carries a trace of nostalgia.

**Vertin:** Lady Hedone.

One of her newest friends pipes up with a question.

**Vertin:** Before you go anywhere, don't you think we should settle our score?

Hearing that, Hedone lets out a quiet laugh.

Hedone grabs her most worthy rival by the wrist, her grip so strong it nearly lifts the rival off the ground.

**Hedone:** I hereby declare that—

**Hedone:** The Winner of the Aquatic Games and Champion of the Island can be none other than ...

**Hedone:** Lady Hedone!

**Narcissus:** What? You can't be serious.

All at once, the feud seems to reignite within her, but it's just as quickly tempered by Hedone's next declaration.

**Hedone:** And her dear friend, Narcissus!

**Narcissus:** You-You had me going for a second there.

**Hedone:** And, of course—

**Hedone:** Vertin and her friends!

After millennia, the fresco, silent keeper of history, welcomes new eyes once more.

And all of them look to the blank space at its center.

**Hedone:** This is where I displayed the eye of Typhon after my first triumph. At the time, it was just a simple hole in the wall.

Hedone looks back at the long stretch of fresco behind her.

**Hedone:** The fresco was painted around it later as our island grew.

Now, both the ones depicted and the ones who painted them have long since passed into darkness, and only the reincarnated one still looks up at the memories they once shared.

She sets their prize, the eye of Typhon, back into its place.

**Hedone:** I believe that we have ended its cycle of rebirth.

**Hedone:** But should it ever return again, I know that I can trust you, my friends, to protect our home.

**Hedone:** Perhaps no longer with a great hunt. Perhaps you might find your own way—a new way.

**Hedone:** Vertin, how can I ever repay you?

Hedone stands in front of the fresco, whole again at last.

**Hedone:** For all that you have done. For standing beside me even as I bid you to leave.

**Vertin:** I can think of no greater honor than being included in your people's stories, Lady Hedone.

**Regulus:** Couldn't have said it better myself! Holiday smashed, I'd say.

**Sonetto:** Your definition of a holiday seems to have changed a lot, Regulus.

Sonetto picks up on the word and turns it over thoughtfully.

**Regulus:** Not at all! We've had loads of fun, made friends, gone to parties, done a bit of monster-slaying, but most importantly, no paperwork. Now that's a proper holiday.

**Sonetto:** I can agree with that.

She smiles.

**Sonetto:** Thank you, Lady Hedone. We've very much appreciated your hospitality.

**Hedone:** You speak as if you're ready to go. After all these trials and tribulations, surely we can prolong our celebrations a little while yet?

**Hedone:** For the rest of your stay on the island, and I believe, too, in the days and weeks to come, much merriment and mirth await you.
