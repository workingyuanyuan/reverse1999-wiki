## Story

**Are you ...**

**???:** **Are you ...**

**... looking for the island of Ephaistonos?**

**???:** **... looking for the island of Ephaistonos?**

Aboard the APPLe

Regulus jumps up at the words.

**Regulus:** Who's there?

**APPLe:** Captain—

**Regulus:** Not now, Mr. APPLe! I just heard a voice.

They end up talking right over one another.

**Regulus:** I think someone's speaking to us. Could be a chance to figure this whole thing out.

**APPLe:** A voice? This APPLe heard nothing, Captain. If I may, the ship requires some repairs.

**Regulus:** What? Are you seriously telling me I'm the only one who heard it?

**Sonetto:** I didn't hear anything either, sorry.

**Vertin:** Do you have any idea where the voice came from?

**Regulus:** From the sea! The sea is trying to tell us something!

She points out toward a sea without end.

Nothing greets them but the soft lapping of waves.

**Regulus:** Oh, that's rich. Now that everyone's listening, you decide to clam right up, don't you?

**Regulus:** Argh! How come I was the only one who heard it? Maybe those screaming Scyllas did something to my hearing.

**Vertin:** We'll keep an ear out for any sounds coming from the water. For now, we have another problem.

Their focus shifts toward more pressing matters.

**Vertin:** If we maintain our current speed and heading, then we could very well pass by the island entirely.

**Vertin:** I think we should stop for a while to investigate.

**APPLe:** This APPLe agrees. Captain, as I was saying, some of the railings appear to be wobbly. We should repair them before leaving.

**Regulus:** Wobbly? We only just spent half a bloody fortune getting the ship tuned up before we left.

**Regulus:** Now this is more like it. Salt in the air, open sea, hah, some proper holiday atmosphere at last. If only I had a sun lounger to actually enjoy it!

**Sonetto:** Regulus, please. We still have a meeting to get through.

**Regulus:** Right, right. But need I remind you the whole point of this voyage?

**Regulus:** Mystery island or not, this is still a holiday.

She reaches out for a welcoming can of soda. It pops open with a gush of fizz, having been disturbed by every rolling bump along the way.

She offers the open Dr. Papper to Sonetto.

**Sonetto:** Th-Thank you, but, uh ...

A bright yellow bag of snacks, puffed up with air, follows right after.

**Regulus:** Come on, mate, relax a little. Those poor frazzled nerves need a rest, especially after dealing with those slimy, screeching Scyllas.

**APPLe:** You've raised an excellent point, Captain!

**Regulus:** Too right I have, Mr. APPLe. Always nice to have my brilliance recognized. Uh, only, what do you mean?

**Sonetto:** I believe Mr. APPLe is referring to the monsters we fought.

**APPLe:** Indeed I am, Ms. Sonetto. In Greek mythology, Scyllas were sea-dwelling monsters that stirred waves and threatened passing sailors, pirates included.

**Vertin:** It stands to reason that an island founded by the ancient Greeks would also be home to creatures from Greek myth.

**Sonetto:** Hmm. In Homer's epic, the Scylla was a monster that prevented Odysseus from reaching home.

**APPLe:** Yes, one of the many trials he faced after the Trojan War.

**APPLe:** There was also the Cyclops Polyphemus, the wind ruler Aeolus, the master sorceress Circe ... Each added to his misery.

**APPLe:** And yet, Odysseus never abandoned his quest to return home to Ithaca.

**Regulus:** Sounds to me like getting back there was a lot more trouble than it was worth.

**APPLe:** Not everyone is as suited to a life on the open seas as you are, Captain.

**APPLe:** For Odysseus, it was never a question of whether facing those trials was logical. He was driven by his "telos," or purpose. He had no choice but to follow it.

**Sonetto:** So, he would have endured anything to reach his home again.

**APPLe:** Precisely. We each have a purpose shaped by our beliefs and desires. For Odysseus, it was returning to his family.

**Regulus:** Fair play to him, I guess. But you wouldn't catch me dodging sea monsters just for a homecoming party.

**APPLe:** In ancient myth, hardship is almost a prerequisite for anyone seeking true knowledge, heroism, or reward.

**Regulus:** But didn't he lose all his crewmates along the way? I can't even imagine how he survived all that by himself.

**APPLe:** It was a considerable loss, Captain. Some might argue too great for the reward. Though one could say that was precisely why Odysseus went to the lengths he did.

**Vertin:** I suppose it depends on what you value most.

**Vertin:** Against all the pain and suffering he went through, all the friends that he lost, it's hard not to question if returning home was worth it.

**Regulus:** Putting aside all the grim and nasty bits, you've got to admit he had one hell of a journey. Staring down a Cyclops, facing off with the Scylla, surviving a Siren's song ...

**Regulus:** If I went through all that and lived to tell the tale, I'd be a certified legend of nautical rock 'n' roll!

**Regulus:** Scyllas, Sirens, ah, what's the other one again? Right, Nymphs. Though, I'd say they're the weakest of the three.

She offers her assessment with the kind of confidence earned by having never encountered any of them.

Amid the soft churn of waves, one begins to stir out of sync, as if agitated by their conversation.

**Sonetto:** I'm not sure most arcanobiologists would agree with you. At least, not from a taxonomic standpoint.

**Regulus:** I don't give a toss about that. I'm talking battle strategy. If all three of them come at me at once, which do I do in first? The weakest one, or the one most likely to gobble me up?

Under the rippling waves, the words sink like bait.

**Vertin:** Well, Regulus? Would you prioritize the Nymph?

The waves stir restlessly.

**Regulus:** Well, a Nymph can't be as dangerous as the other two. It lacks the Siren's killer voice and the brute force of a Scylla.

**???:** You really ought to know your limits!

From somewhere behind the APPLe, a voice rings out, piqued with frustration.

**???:** But, hmm, that's the confidence you'd expect from a hero!

**Regulus:** —!

**Regulus:** You heard that this time, didn't you? What did I tell you?!

**Regulus:** It's that voice again!

**Regulus:** You were the one speaking to me earlier. Who are you?

**???:** Ahem ... By decree of the oracle, I am to wait at the boundaries of our island for a hero from afar—

**???:** Who shall bring judgment upon the capricious tyrant, Hedone!

**Vertin:** A tyrant?

**Regulus:** Oh, now that is the perfect opening to a grand adventure. Mr. APPLe, ready yourself. We are about to become legends.

**APPLe:** If circumstances permit, this APPLe might prefer a quieter approach.

Sonetto turns to look out over the water, scanning the horizon with concern.

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper, we don't even know who this voice belongs to. We should be careful.

**Vertin:** It is a convenient offer, given that our destination is the island.

**Vertin:** But it would be reckless to trust someone who won't even show their face.

The ripples begin to slow, as if hesitating to decide their next move.

But those aboard don't have to wait long.

**???:** If that's what you want, then so be it!

**Narcissus:** Lost souls on the waves, distress no more! I am Narcissus, guided by the ordained stream to point the way along your great journey!

**Sonetto:** Narcissus? The young man who turned into a daffodil while staring at his own reflection?

**Narcissus:** Hah, so you've heard of my namesake. But I am no man, nor am I particularly attached to my reflection. It is a title alone.

**Narcissus:** As it is with the tyrant Hedone, who is far from a goddess of pleasure and delight, I assure you. She has the entire island pinned beneath her heel!

**Narcissus:** Conceited! Wicked! A loathsome creature beyond all measure!

**APPLe:** That's quite the grudge this young lady holds against that sovereign.

**Vertin:** Ms. Narcissus, this tyrant that you speak of—is she the ruler of Ephaistonos?

**Narcissus:** Correct! Her tyranny stretches far, far back into ancient times. Our people have suffered too long beneath her iron grip.

**Vertin:** Does she wield a bow and command fire?

The image that had blazed in the mind resurfaces once more.

**Narcissus:** Aha! I see her infamy has reached beyond our island. I'm hardly surprised.

**Narcissus:** But she is as powerful as she is cruel. I cannot stand against her alone. That's why I'm here to ask you to join me in my fight for liberation.

**Regulus:** That sounds like ...

**Regulus:** Exactly the kind of rockadelic quest I've been waiting for my whole life! Mr. APPLe, we've got a revolution to start, one that puts old Odysseus's yarn to shame.

Lost in the epic rock opera already playing out in her head, Regulus shakes her chief mate up and down without mercy.

**APPLe:** Captain, this APPLe fails to see any resemblance between this and the Odyssey.

**APPLe:** Besides, we should verify this "oracle" she is talking about before we consider starting any "revolutions."

**Regulus:** Pft. What harm ever came from toppling a tyrant? Go on, Narcissus, let's hear her crimes.

Hearing that, Narcissus's voice jumps up a notch, trembling with excitement.

**Narcissus:** She-She stole what I cherished above all else.

**Narcissus:** Th-That's why the oracle spoke of my great return with allies from afar to reclaim what is rightfully mine.

**Vertin:** Pardon me, Ms. Narcissus, but are you saying this oracle spoke of us directly?

**Narcissus:** S-Something like that. You arrived at the right hour, and you proved yourselves by driving off those Scyllas.

**Narcissus:** Though I may have lent a hand drawing a few of them away.

**Narcissus:** And just as I was brimming with joy for seeing you, you started speaking ill of Nymphs.

A wave of sorrow wells up in her.

**Narcissus:** And, as it happens, I am a Nymph myself.

**Regulus:** Oh! Well, that was just a theory. You never know what's going to happen till you get yourself stuck in it, right?

**Narcissus:** Right. But I know that I won't be defeated. The oracle has foreseen my triumph.

**APPLe:** There's no shortage of self-assurance in this young lady. It seems her title is quite apt.

**Narcissus:** The tyrant has sealed off all paths to the outside world. You need a brave insider to sneak you in.

The light figure leaps up with an enthusiastic bounce—

Only for a sudden weight to pull the one in front down toward the sea.

**Narcissus:** Come along, heroes, follow me!

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper!

Sonetto flings herself forward and reaches out a helping hand.

**Vertin:** Ms. Narcissus?

**Narcissus:** No need to worry. Just hold your breath.

A wave surges over them, rocking the ship with sudden violence.

Sonetto stumbles forward and slips from the rail.

Another hand catches her just in time.

**Regulus:** Hang on a second. This doesn't feel right. She's trying to ...

Regulus's voice comes out taut.

**Regulus:** ... drag us into the sea!

**APPLe:** Careful, Captain!

***

COMBAT:

***

**APPLe:** Captain?

**APPLe:** Ms. Sonetto? Ms. Vertin?

Left alone without his captain and crew, the chief mate APPLe spins in full circles, attempting to get his bearings.

**APPLe:** No. This isn't good. This APPLe needs to get help.

Nymphs are found mainly in the seas, lakes, forests, and valleys across Europe, with branches including Sea Nymphs and Spring Nymphs. Sea Nymphs live in coastal waters and often travel alongside dolphins and seahorses, though humans have found them increasingly difficult to observe in modern times. Spring Nymphs, meanwhile, prefer freshwater. Records indicate that they entered into an alliance with a powerful arcanist family and withdrew to an island, where they have continued to thrive.
