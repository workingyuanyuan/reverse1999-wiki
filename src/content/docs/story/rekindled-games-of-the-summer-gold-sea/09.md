## Story

**ONiON:** Hello out there to new friends and long-time viewers. You're back with Headonion.

**ONiON:** The response to our special coverage of the Aquatic Games has been nothing short of incredible. Everyone's excited for this incredible showcase of arcane talent.

**ONiON:** The question on everyone's mind is, what will happen in today's event? What jaw-dropping arcane skills await us? And what sort of amazing arcane creatures will we see?

**ONiON:** I'm sure everyone watching is just as eager as I am to dive in.

Caught up in her own anticipation, ONiON reins herself in and finds her way back to a professional tone.

**ONiON:** While we wait for things to kick off, we've arranged for some pre-game interviews.

**ONiON:** In yesterday's coverage, we put out a plea to support Team Timekeeper. I'm glad to report that two of our wonderful friends from the Suitcase have answered the call, and they're here with me right now.

**ONiON:** Let's welcome Ms. Voyager and Ms. Barcarola!

The inventive musician and the interstellar voyager share a smile, then bow with unhurried grace.

**Barcarola:** Thank you, thank you! I'm so excited to be here for the Aquatic Games. It's just like my days sailing from island to island.

**Barcarola:** There are so many wonderful one-of-a-kind sights to see here. If they ever open up for tourism, I'm sure it would become a top destination.

**Barcarola:** In fact, being here has me missing my days aboard the "Free Breeze."

**Barcarola:** It's such a marvelously intriguing and beautiful place.

**Voyager:** Heh heh.

**Voyager:** \*approving violin playing\*

Voyager plays a cheerful melody, her curiosity and warmth coming through in every note.

**ONiON:** Our viewers are itching to know just what's in store for them in the second round. Would you mind sharing a little sneak peek of your performance?

**Barcarola:** Of course! We'd be happy to!

Voyager rests her hand on the violin's neck. Barcarola closes her eyes and leans into the strange amalgamated instrument at her side.

A melodious, smooth duet soon rises to fill the air.

**ONiON:** Wasn't that something?! Hard to imagine a performance that could beat a duet from Ms. Voyager and Ms. Barcarola.

**ONiON:** If that's just a taste of what's to come, we're in for a real treat!

**Narcissus:** Huh, is this live?

A head pokes into frame, sniffing at the lens like it's inspecting it.

She steps back until her entire face fits into the frame. Then she puts her hands on her hips and puffs herself up.

**Narcissus:** Hedone! You listen here!

**Narcissus:** Victory is ours! You've got no chance!

**Voyager:** \*impassioned violin playing\*

**ONiON:** Wow! You heard it here first, sports fans. This competition is really heating up. In fact, I think she's singed my peel.

The journalist smoothly squeezes back into frame and wraps up the interview.

Right on cue, Themis takes her place at the judge's stand amid the swelling cheers.

**Themis:** Welcome one and all to the second round of the Aquatic Games.

**Themis:** In today's competition, our contestants must rely on their bond with a wild sea beast to carry them across the finish line.

**Themis:** Your skills in building connections with and controlling these beasts will be put to the test. Final scores will be marked based on speed, synergy, and technical artistry.

**Themis:** These beasts can be dangerous and temperamental. I would ask all spectators to stand well back during the exhibition. With that said, let the game begin!

**Contestant:** Aha! A Capricornus! They're not dangerous at all. Here, sweetie, take whatever you like. I've got mead, pickled olives, maybe some lotus fruit?

The beast surfaces just enough to show its eyes. It studies each offering as they appear one by one before dismissing them with a swish of its tail.

**Contestant:** Not interested? How about some barley cakes or pomegranate? No? Hmm. I have a sprig of moly.

The first contestant has clearly come prepared.

**Narcissus:** Oh, the Capricornus is moving. Look, it's heading straight for a bag of potato chips?

**Regulus:** Are those salt and pepper flavor? Unusual taste.

**Contestant:** That's it, eat up. Now come. There'll be more at the finish line. Hey, why are you lying down?

**Contestant:** What? You're full already?

**Regulus:** Hang on, a six? She didn't even get the thing off the bloody beach.

**Narcissus:** Uh, apparently, one of the judges approves of the contestant's choice of chips.

**Regulus:** That matters?

But Regulus's complaint soon melts away, because the next few contestants go far beyond anything she could have imagined.

**Regulus:** What in the name of the Holy Diver are they doing to that free-spirited Ichthyocentaur? Is that a leash?

**Regulus:** And what about this bloke? He's just sunbathing on that poor giant turtle's back. Oh, for beat's sake, he's even got a ruddy parasol.

**Narcissus:** Themis just made a ruling. She-She said time's up for them.

Turns out the visitors from afar aren't the only ones whose patience has worn thin.

**Narcissus:** Well deserved, I say. He wasn't even trying. Gods, I hope we get something good next.

The rising wave of cheers drowns out the ousted contestant's protests. Who's up next needs no explaining.

Hedone wades into the water alone, the sea rising over her ankles. Wherever she passes, beasts scatter out of her way.

**Regulus:** Huh? Is she just going out for a swim?

She slips off her arm ring and, with little effort, lobs it far out to sea.

**Hedone:** Ceto!

**Hedone:** It is I, Hedone! Beckon to my call!

**???:** ...

At the edge of the horizon, a sea creature of enormous size emerges from the water, a whale with strange squid-like tentacles.

It cuts through the sea at an unbelievable speed, silent as a loosed arrow, and closes the distance to its master and old friend in the blink of an eye.

**Ceto:** \*roar\*

One tentacle offers up the arm ring she tossed away only moments ago. Hedone reaches out and takes it.

**Hedone:** I am most heartened that you remember my voice.

**Hedone:** Now, make quick and take us to our destination, just as you did when first we met.

**Ceto:** \*roar\*

She doesn't look back, just raises the hand wearing the ring high and snaps her fingers at the crowd behind her.

Hedone unsurprisingly earns a stellar score with her display.

**Regulus:** Uh. I'm not saying this to bring down the mood—

**Regulus:** But what are the odds that our team's going to bag something better than a great honking squid-whale?

**Narcissus:** You-You never know if you don't try. I'm certain we can summon something. Umm, I mean, we've got to. We're fated to win.

**Barcarola:** One, two, three, four. All set. Ready to go?

The conductor and trained violinist clears her throat.

**Barcarola:** Hello, one and all. I am Barcarola, honored former musical director of the "Free Breeze."

**Barcarola:** And my friend and collaborator here is Ms. Voyager, our chief violinist.

**Voyager:** \*lighthearted violin playing\*

**Barcarola:** And, of course, we cannot forget our colleagues, Ms. Vertin and Ms. Sonetto.

**Vertin:** It's a pleasure.

**Sonetto:** Thank you for letting us come along.

**Barcarola:** Before we begin, I must express our regret that we did not prepare a dance floor or hot apple pie as we did aboard the "Free Breeze." Nevertheless, we hope you enjoy.

She gives the sea a light, graceful bow, then turns to signal the start of the show.

Harmonious music rises from her strange instrument, drifting out over the sea.

As the baton sweeps through the air, the violin joins in, providing a higher, subtler melody to their tune.

While the duet's tender echo still lingers in the air, two strings of bubbles suddenly rise through the calm water.

**Narcissus:** Ah!

Cries of delight and surprise rise up from the stands.

**Regulus:** Narcissus, mate, tell me straight: have I got seawater in my eyes, or are those "sea beasts" no bigger than a bass guitar?

**???:** \*puzzled squeak\*

**???:** \*cheerful squeak\*

**Barcarola:** Thank you for coming, little music lovers.

Sonetto crouches down at the water's edge, and the two young beasts nuzzle affectionately against her hand.

She's a little surprised by their sudden closeness, and her expression softens.

**Sonetto:** They seem very affectionate. We appreciate your timeliness, little friends.

**Barcarola:** Ah, wonderful! I think they've taken a liking to us.

**Vertin:** Are you two a team as well, then?

As if to answer the question, the small sea beasts playfully spray two thin jets of water into the air.

From somewhere high in the stands a familiar voice booms down.

**Narcissus:** Vertin! Those are Delphynes! They're docile and very social!

**Narcissus:** Those two likely have a whole pod nearby! I bet they'd all be willing to lend a helping hand, or fin!

Two smooth arcs slice across the shimmering water.

**Sonetto:** Ah!

It's all so new, and for a second, Sonetto just doesn't know what to do.

But then she chooses to let go of her anxiety and enjoy the moment.

**Vertin:** Sonetto, well—whoa!

The lively Delphyne gives its new friend a little bounce.

**Vertin:** Done.

**Sonetto:** It feels like they're talking to me.

Sonetto notices the small dorsal fin in front of her trembling. She looks up, fixing her eyes on the far horizon.

**Delphyne II:** \*warning squeak\*

**Sonetto:** Be careful, Timekeeper. Something's coming.

Now safe from danger, the Delphynes dive back into the sea.

**Vertin:** Hold on tight, Sonetto. We're heading straight for those reefs.

**Delphyne I & Delphyne II:** \*squeak\*

There's nothing like breaking free of the water in one leap—a feeling you'd never find on land.

When a Delphyne breaks the surface, it's like a moment of flying. Its fins become wings, and the spray it throws catches the light like visible air, sparkling and clear.

One after another, every Delphyne leaps out and dives back in, circling the two of them at the center.

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper, put your hand on their dorsal fin like this.

Sonetto gently strokes the Delphyne's back, as if she's learned a way to speak without words.

**Sonetto:** I think they're able to sense our thoughts and emotions. It's as if we were old friends.

**Sonetto:** I can feel theirs too.

She lowers her head, and the Delphyne responds with a jet of water. The cool spray hits Sonetto's face, and a smile breaks out before she even notices.

**Sonetto:** They're so full of joy.

**Sonetto:** I ... I am too!

**Sonetto:** I know we haven't reached the finish line yet, and I have to keep my guard up, but ...

**Vertin:** Don't fret over it.

**Vertin:** Some moments in life are more important than victory.

**Vertin:** Besides, I think we've already seen what's coming. It's just like in the fresco.

**Vertin:** Narcissus told us as much.

After their spectacular circuit across the sea, the finish line is finally within reach.

**Vertin:** These Games are a ritual, a reimagining of Hedone's old triumphs.

**Vertin:** And that means only one thing: we need to follow them, step by step.

**Regulus:** Are you joking?! That was bloody breathtaking, and they won't even give us a draw?! Those judges are just taking the mick now!

**Narcissus:** No ...

**Narcissus:** Wait, wait. The judge was holding up the wrong board. It wasn't a six. It was a ...

**Regulus:** We won! Haha. We won!

**Narcissus:** We defeated Hedone!

It's a hard-won victory. In the crowd, Regulus and Narcissus crash into each other's arms in excitement.

**Hedone:** Magnificent, truly.

**Hedone:** I must admit, I didn't expect to lose. But fair is fair.

Her smile is broad and genuine, yet something in it sends a shiver down their spines.

**Regulus:** Nuh-uh. Not buying it. She's going to send those golden rams out to flatten us again, isn't she?

**Hedone:** Why would I ever do such a thing? I celebrated your victory over my rams just as I celebrate now.

**Hedone:** I have no special desire for victory. It is the pleasure of competition itself that I prize.

Hedone's cape rises with the wind around her to spectacular effect.

**Hedone:** To prove my words, allow me to entertain you.
