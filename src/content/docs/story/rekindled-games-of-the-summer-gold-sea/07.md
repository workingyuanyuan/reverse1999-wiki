## Story

**???:** Testing, testing. Can you all hear and see me back there in the Suitcase?

**ONiON:** Thanks to Ephaistonos's leader, Lady Hedone, we're coming to you live with exclusive sports coverage of the Aquatic Games.

**ONiON:** Behind me is the stage for the first event: the Sea Gliding Competition. And as you can tell—

**ONiON:** The spectators are already going wild with anticipation.

The high-spirited journalist swings her mic toward the crowd, her bright yellow headgear gleaming under the sun.

**ONiON:** The competition will begin shortly. I hope you're all as excited as I am. Who am I kidding? Of course you are.

**ONiON:** So stay tuned and get ready to be glued to your seats.

Distance and some kind of protective arcane skill throw off the signal as it goes out. The picture on the display screen glitches every few seconds.

Beach, Ephaistonos

**Regulus:** Gods of rock be good. Can't believe we actually worked through the night. Coffee run, anyone? I need a quadruple espresso.

**Regulus:** Honestly, I half-expected there wouldn't be a single java bean within ten nautical miles of this place. But I spied myself a lovely little café nearby.

**Regulus:** The barista even told me they sell java by day and vino by night.

**Regulus:** How cool is that! You think they've got some kind of arcane skill that turns the café into a bar in an instant?

**X:** The simpler hypothesis would be that they just switch out the menus.

**X:** Although, imagine if they've devised a machine that can brew alcohol and coffee at the same time? I'd put that at the top of my inventions list for the year.

**X:** Right underneath this fine specimen of a fixed-wing unpowered sailplane we've built.

**Sonetto:** It's magnificent, even just for its size alone.

Mounted on wheels, the massive ship is pushed onto the beach. Its hull casts a wide shadow under the blazing sun.

Standing in the shadow, Sonetto tilts her head back to study it more carefully.

**Vertin:** It's lighter than it looks.

A finger gives the ship a light poke, and it wobbles up and down in response.

**Regulus:** Ladies and gentlemen, may I present to you: The Automated Inflatable Rock 'n' Roller Pirate Ship!

**Regulus:** See those gorgeous, grooving inflatable arm-flailing tube men? I based them off the greatest rock stars of the 20th century.

**Regulus:** And I even squeezed in a little space for a few amps, so we can blast our tracks across the skies for all to hear.

**Regulus:** And the captain gets dibs on the opening song. No objections, I trust?

**X:** Works for me. And as per the captain's design, each song sets the corresponding rock star dancing.

X jumps in, beaming with pride over the fruits of his labors.

**X:** Most of the structure is inflatable, which should significantly extend the glide. And the wheels underneath ought to give you a smooth launch.

**X:** There are also ultra-light rigid wings tucked in along the sides. That'll ensure your stability in the air, plus add a little theatrical flair.

**X:** And there you have it. I trust this design will serve you well in the competition, Timekeeper.

**X:** "Gods-speed" and have a good flight.

**Regulus:** Hold it, mate. Where do you think you're going?

**Regulus:** You're a part of this crew now. Aren't you going to join us?

X replies with an unconscious flinch, waving his hands as if to deflect the invitation.

**X:** Oh. Well, you see, I would constitute an unexpected factor in the load equations.

**X:** And it doesn't take an engineer of any real caliber to see that the ultimate terminus of this vessel is the open sea.

**X:** It's probably best I stay grounded in the auditorium, where I can appreciate my work and avoid going back to the office soaking wet.

His explanation comes across as equally rooted in both genuine scientific analysis and fear of a waterlogged lab coat.

**Sonetto:** "Soaking wet"?

**Sonetto:** Uh, I suppose we will have to swim back after it lands.

A self-evident, yet overlooked, conclusion.

As she turns, she finds that X is already halfway to the auditorium.

**???:** My, my. I take it this is your tribute to the legendary Argo of the Argonauts?

A confident voice cuts in, her tone landing ambiguously between intrigue and mockery.

**Regulus:** Oi! What's this looky-loo doing here? Quick, cover up the ship.

Regulus grabs her two crewmates and swings them out into a protective wall that does absolutely nothing to shield the ship's enormous frame.

**Hedone:** Looky-loo? Please, I have no intention of copying your design, nor am I here to sabotage it.

**Hedone:** In fact, you needn't concern yourself at all. All that we do here is beneath Themis's ever-watchful eye.

**Hedone:** I have come on a separate matter.

**Hedone:** Tell me, is this really how you intend to dress for our upcoming dip?

A teasing smile tugs at Hedone's lips as she looks the neatly dressed contestants up and down.

**Vertin:** What do you mean?

**Hedone:** Like Icarus of yore, our flight will inevitably end beneath the wine-dark waves. Your current garments look rather too puffed for swimming.

**Hedone:** With all that fabric dragging you down, we might well lose you to the watery depths.

**Hedone:** In short, if you are to compete in our Games, I must have you attired appropriately.

She lifts a hand and snaps her fingers.

**Sonetto:** Ah!

**Sonetto:** This ...

**Regulus:** What just happened? Oh, this is just not on. Not on at all. Judge, judge!

**Vertin:** ...

**Hedone:** I assure you, if I intended anything untoward, Themis would already have shown her face.

**Hedone:** I should think you owe me some gratitude for my generosity.

**Vertin:** It is very ... thoughtful of you, Lady Hedone.

**Hedone:** Think nothing of it.

Hedone gives a little wave and turns to go, leaving the team in a transmogrified stupor.

**Regulus:** Hate to say it, but she's got a point.

**Regulus:** We'd be sunk for sure if we got stuck out there in our regular togs.

***

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper, I think we're needed at the platform.

Flight Event Plaza

Themis takes the stage. As the island's most trusted judge, she's met with a wave of cheers.

**Themis:** Now, let the Games begin.

Though steady and calm, her voice still booms out to every corner of the plaza, cutting clean through the roar of celebration around her.

**Narcissus:** Is that meant to be the Trojan Horse? But how are they going to make it fly? Or float, even?

The first contestants are set on the platform, tucked inside a wooden horse, ready to leap out over the water together.

Down in the crowd, Narcissus manages to find a spot next to X as they watch the event.

**X:** Fascinating. The wingspan is optimal for a sustained flight, but that top-heavy wooden structure is going to throw off the center of balance.

**X:** My prediction: this horse is bound for a very short-haul flight.

The contestants are ready, and the wooden horse takes off across the platform.

**Narcissus:** It's ... It's actually flying.

The horse leaps, and the three arcanists kick their legs wildly in midair.

But their momentum slows, and the would-be pegasus begins a steep seaward drop, ending with a tremendous splash.

**Narcissus:** Ah! Shame.

**Narcissus:** Not that I expected much, but still ...

Cheer after cheer rolls through the crowd, and the competition speeds up.

**Narcissus:** And that one must be based on the Ship of Theseus.

**X:** A notably ramshackle construction. I wonder if that's intentional.

**Narcissus:** Ah, I suppose not. It didn't even make it off the platform.

After a few groups, the eager crowd sees what they've been waiting for: the outsiders take to the platform.

**Narcissus:** That's Vertin's team! You can do it, Vertin!

Narcissus cups her small, webbed hands around her mouth and shouts as loud as her lungs can manage.

**Regulus:** Pick it up. Let's give this beauty a proper launch.

**Regulus:** On my mark!

Regulus commands her crew with unwavering focus, a regular Horatio Nelson with rockstar flair. She's in her element—star of the show and captain of the ship.

**Regulus:** Three!

**Regulus:** Two!

**Regulus:** One!

The platform is slick beneath them. Gripping the handles, they strain as they push the oversized pirate ship forward.

**Sonetto:** Ah?

Wind rushes past their faces.

**Vertin:** ...

**Vertin:** We're airborne.

Their ship cuts an elegant arc through the air, giving those without wings a taste of what it means to soar.

**Regulus:** Haha, too right we are. Carried on the wings of rock 'n' roll, blasting tunes and blowing minds.

**Regulus:** There isn't a pirate or rock star in this world that can rival us now.

**Sonetto:** It's remarkable how long we've kept in flight. I think we have a shot.

**Regulus:** Oh! No, no, no! We're losing altitude fast. Can't we go any further?

**Regulus:** Alright. We're going down, into the sea and into legend. I wouldn't have it any other way, shipmates.

**Sonetto:** Brace for impact, Timekeeper.

**Vertin:** Hmm. We've gone much farther than I ever expected.

The gliding ship finally begins to lose altitude.

Their toes skim the surface, sending up small, cold splashes of water around them.

Then they let go, falling into the warm embrace of the sea.

**Narcissus:** I don't think I've ever seen a ship glide that far out.

**Narcissus:** I don't know what Hedone has up her sleeve, but my friends ...

**Narcissus:** You truly are all I thought and more.

She's all but spinning circles in her seat.

**Narcissus:** That's the highest score yet.

Their proud ship is hauled ashore, its mission complete.

The last contestant and her creation take their place on the launch platform.

**Narcissus:** Ah, Hedone's up. And she's got ... Wow!

**X:** That's the most extraordinary design of the day.

X's eyes widen at the sight.

**Hedone:** Let this vessel fly as far and true as my arrow!

Hedone draws her bow skyward and looses the arrow. The bowstring snaps taut, cutting the air with one sharp note.

**Hedone:** Hah. Watch me.

The world itself seems to obey her. The arrow vanishes into the blue, already forgotten, as every eye turns back to her.

The island erupts into cheers, cheers for her and her alone.

One hand is all it takes to drive her colossal ship forward. She breaks into a run, and its side wings begin churning the air.

**Hedone:** Hargh!

**Hedone:** I need not pray for the wind.

**Hedone:** For it will always harken to me.

The flight becomes less of a competition and more of a spectacle of distance and time.

At long last, the water swells as it welcomes her ship in.

**Sonetto:** It's just like the fresco.

The arrow she loosed at the very beginning falls from the sky and lands perfectly in her palm.

**Vertin:** A perfect score!

**Hedone:** Hah! I do hope you'll cheer for my victory. Better that than to weep over your defeat.
