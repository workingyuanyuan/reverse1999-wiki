## Story

**ONiON:** What a spectacle, ladies and gentlemen! The leader of Ephaistonos, Lady Hedone, has claimed victory in the first round!

**ONiON:** But win or lose, our very own Team Timekeeper completed their debut in truly epic style!

**ONiON:** It's been a real treat for everyone watching here and at home. And since this competition's recording rights haven't been issued yet, it's now or never.

She takes a note passed to her off-camera, and her excitement spills out and over her practiced newscaster voice.

**ONiON:** This just in, a bulletin for all Suitcase residents: Team Timekeeper is searching for teammates as they prepare to enter the second round of the Games!

**ONiON:** If you possess any unique aquatic skill, or have a deep passion for the ocean, please contact us through the number on the screen.

**ONiON:** Is there going to be a number?

**ONiON:** ONiON will keep you updated on all the latest from the Aquatic Games. See you at the next event.

Beach, Ephaistonos

**Narcissus:** That leaves us with no choice.

Narcissus paces the beach, flustered by their rocky start, her footprints piling over one another in the sand.

**Narcissus:** We absolutely must win the second and third round!

She throws up a fist, attempting to shake off her gloom with sheer confidence.

**Regulus:** You and Hedone don't seem all that different sometimes, like two covers of the same album.

**Regulus:** Look, I want to win as much as anyone. But this tyrant of yours is no pushover. I mean, did you see that ship?

**Regulus:** And she did that all by herself. I feel like we've got all the chance of a snowball at a kiss concert.

**Vertin:** "All by herself"?

**Vertin:** She stood there before Typhon with no one by her side, all those weapons scattering about.

**Vertin:** Did they once belong to her friends?

**Narcissus:** Yes. When her ship set sail, she had many comrades. But only Hedone returned.

**Vertin:** Does Hedone always compete solo?

**Narcissus:** Hmmm. I think so.

Her eyes drift as she casts her mind back, counting the dips along her webbed fingers.

**Narcissus:** Let's see. She was alone last time, and the time before that, and the time before that ... Yep, she's always been a lone wolf.

**Narcissus:** I doubt she could even tell you the definition of "teamwork." She's more of an "obedience" and "authority" type.

**Sonetto:** That could work to our advantage. The next competition is all about cooperation, isn't it?

**Narcissus:** You mean the Sea Beast Taming Exhibition?

**Narcissus:** Not likely. Her skill in taming beasts is legendary. She was already leading her family's trade ships through monster-infested waters at 17!

**Narcissus:** Back then, her name alone could bend even the most terrifying monsters of the Aegean to her will.

Narcissus loses herself in the telling, counting off Hedone's triumphs one by one with what might be mistaken for awe.

**Sonetto:** Just like the legends from her past life—she tamed a beast and used it to guide her to Typhon.

**Sonetto:** Then it's as we thought. The Aquatic Games intentionally mirror the events depicted in the fresco.

**Narcissus:** Yes, the Games were created to celebrate Hedone's victory over Typhon.

**Narcissus:** Each event matches one of her "triumphs," step by step.

She trails into a murmur.

The fog clouding the outsiders begins to clear.

**Narcissus:** Ugh. Now that I say it out loud, how are we expected to win anything?

Narcissus sighs, forced to face the plain truth.

**Barcarola:** Is that you, Vertin, Sonetto?

**Barcarola:** And Regulus! Oh, it's so wonderful to see you!

Their new guests arrive via teleport disk and immediately launch toward them to celebrate their long-overdue reunion.

**Voyager:** Hmm. Heh heh.

Old friends stand upon the sand, their eyes drifting over the picturesque island surroundings.

**Narcissus:** So, these are your new teammates?

Bar Helicon

**Narcissus:** Hah, Apollo. You were right. Whether you like it or not, our victory is guaranteed.

**Narcissus:** Vertin brought in some real ringers. A team of the greatest beast tamers ever!

**Narcissus:** They're so very elegant! So poised!

**Narcissus:** It's only been half a day, and they've already accomplished so much!

**Narcissus:** We've got Hedone beat this time for sure!

She drains most of her ginger ale in one go. The ice cubes tumble back into her glass with a clink.

**Sonetto:** The exhibition is tomorrow. I hope Ms. Voyager and Ms. Barcarola will be rested and ready.

Beside her, Regulus holds up a finger to the bartender and slides an empty cup her way.

**Regulus:** Oi, Barkeep! Bring us another one, eh?

**Apollo:** Of course, Captain. One moment, please.

**Apollo:** I must express my regret that my old friends cannot attend, too. Quick-footed Hermes has letters to deliver, and the ever-vigilant Themis is busy with her preparations.

She waits for her drink, taking in the strangely modern decor of the bar.

Cups and keepsakes cover the walls, glinting under the light.

**Regulus:** Drink's no Dr. Papper, but about as near as; decor's stylish, and I can't complain about the music. Hah. Got to say, you've got some solid digs here.

**Regulus:** In fact, I'm feeling inspired. I just hope Mr. APPLe won't mind helping me give the Captain's Quarters a bit of a makeover.

**Regulus:** Oh! That golden cup in your cabinet there is really speaking to me. Can I take a gander at it, Apollo?

**Apollo:** If that's what you wish, I'm more than happy for you to take a look.

Apollo reaches up and takes down the bejeweled cup for Regulus.

**Regulus:** That is cracking! Just look at these gems. Feels almost wrong to drink out of something so flash.

**Narcissus:** Feel free. We've got plenty. When you hold as many Games as we do, winning cups like those wind up in every cabinet.

Her voice is filled with pride.

**Apollo:** That may be true, but this particular specimen is a rarer treasure. According to legend, it was made by Hedone herself.

**Apollo:** Though I'm not certain if one should take stock in such legends.

Apollo smiles and turns the goblet in his hand, gold and gems flaring to life under the light.

**Apollo:** See the twelve gems around the rim? They form a laurel wreath.

**Apollo:** Each stone represents a challenge accomplished by Hedone and her companions.

**Vertin:** What were the challenges?

Catching their questioning looks, Narcissus flinches as if burned and pushes the cup away.

**Narcissus:** Don't ask me. Hedone's so-called "great challenges" are famous around here. I'm sure you could learn all about them from someone else.

She's already let too much admiration slip past her guard, and she forces down the urge to say more.

**Apollo:** Each one is well-remembered on the island. There's the challenge of the Perilous Straits, where one must sail through a narrow strait during the height of a storm in a single day.

**Apollo:** And the Drunken Shot, where one drinks 30 cups of wine, then shoots an arrow through a golden apple set upon a far-off hill.

**Apollo:** And of course, there was the challenge of taming a wild stallion and riding him over ten hurdles.

Apollo's more leisurely account only raises more questions.

**Vertin:** You mentioned Hedone completed these challenges with her companions. So, she didn't do them alone?

**Apollo:** Indeed, she didn't. She first came to the island with a band of compatriots, but none survived her battle with Typhon.

**Sonetto:** She won the island but lost her friends.

**Apollo:** That is what the legends tell.

**Apollo:** Though they have long been taken from us, their legacy remains. They shine as they ever did.

Noticing the room has gone quiet, Apollo steers the conversation elsewhere.

**Apollo:** There is another tale told about this goblet.

**Apollo:** That the great bond of the heroes who once held it granted it a unique power—

**Apollo:** Such that any of pure spirit who holds the cup would find it ever filled with their heart's desire.

**Regulus:** You mean it can make any kind of drink?

**Apollo:** You may think of it that way, yes. An unlimited tap of whatever refreshment you desire.

**Regulus:** Ahahahah! So, you're saying that if I hold this thing, it'll start filling up with Dr. Papper?

**Apollo:** So long as it is your heart's desire and your intentions are to share it among friends.

**Sonetto:** But that would mean it operates outside the traditional bounds of physics and arcanum. How could something like that function?

**Apollo:** It may sound fantastical, but I don't doubt this is far from the first wonder that you've come across in your travels.

**Apollo:** Admittedly, Lady Hedone's powers have enabled a great many things to work beyond the traditional limits of both the physical and the arcane.

**Apollo:** But then, as far as I'm aware, her powers are limited to the confines of the island. I know there are some that chafe under her rule, but I have come to see the advantages.

**Apollo:** Do you still feel she is a tyrant, Narcissus?

Apollo turns to his old friend with a pointed smile.

**Narcissus:** I ...

She looks down into the glittering cup. Its glow is impossible for anyone to ignore.

Hedone's legend shines in every corner of Ephaistonos.

**Narcissus:** I don't know, Apollo. What else can I think?

She bites her lip, visibly shrinking in her seat.

**Narcissus:** Maybe it's easier for you and the others. Your roles are so clear—prophet, messenger, judge.

**Narcissus:** And Hedone gets to call herself our leader, our sovereign, a queen in all but name.

**Narcissus:** What's left for me? All I can do is ...

Narcissus steels herself and leaps to her feet, pointing toward the wall crowded with trophies won by Hedone.

**Narcissus:** All we can do is win these Games and earn ourselves a place among Hedone's achievements!

**Narcissus:** And when I return with my trophy, I'll put our photo here. No! Right at the center!

**Narcissus:** That way, anyone who comes in will see it before anything else, and they'll be begging to know all about me.

Her words tumble out faster, and she becomes too worked up to notice the room falling silent around her.

**Narcissus:** I ... I want to ...

**Narcissus:** No, I will end the tyranny of Hedone!

Her voice fades into the silence, only for a slow clap to break it.

**???:** Yet another inspiring message.

The so-called tyrant steps up behind Narcissus until she towers over the little Nymph.

**Hedone:** "End my tyranny." The little Nymph's most frequent statement. What a fabulous claim!

**Hedone:** Perhaps my next ritual speech ought to borrow an ounce of Narcissus's electrifying fervor. What say you, Apollo?

She takes the empty seat beside Narcissus and raps her knuckles on the bar, beckoning the bartender.

**Apollo:** I believe your presence alone already carries enough weight, Lady Hedone.

Apollo smiles as he prepares her drink without asking.

**Narcissus:** Now I remember why I stopped coming here.

**Narcissus:** No matter where I go, she's always just around the corner.

Narcissus has practically glued herself to Regulus, putting as much distance as she can between herself and Hedone as possible.

**Regulus:** Can't even go to a pub without seeing her face. Is there nowhere Hedone-free on this island?

She says the words in a sharp whisper.

**Hedone:** Ginger ale. How youthful.

Hedone eyes the three empty cups on the bar, flicks a glance at Narcissus, and leaves it at that.

**Hedone:** What a shame you haven't the palate for a real drink. Many of the spirits on these shelves I acquired on my voyages, so rich that a single sip can transport you a thousand miles.

**Hedone:** Hmm? Why has the goblet been taken out?

Her gaze lands on the glittering golden cup. Each one of its gems dazzles as if it were lit from the inside.

**Apollo:** Pay it no heed, Lady Hedone. Our guests merely expressed some interest in its story.

**Hedone:** This old thing? It's little more than an ancient relic.

Her voice is flat and unemotional.

**Hedone:** Let us not waste breath on old cups, for we have today's game to glory in. Wouldn't you agree, Vertin?

**Hedone:** Allow me to express my sincerest appreciation for your team's efforts.

She raises her cup to them in salute.

**Hedone:** It was a truly thrilling display.

The crowd has gone, but Bar Helicon remains in the dim, heady twilight of a bar just past closing.

Themis, late as always, sits down at the bar with an unreadable expression. Apollo dries the glass in his hand and sets it on the rack.

**Themis:** I must ask, Apollo, what do you make of Narcissus's claims about her oracle? Is there any truth to it?

**Apollo:** Aha.

**Apollo:** Our dear friend has changed little over the years. She's as stubborn as ever in her beliefs, particularly when she thinks the gods are in her favor.

**Themis:** That almost sounds like an oracle in itself.

He offers an unconcerned shrug.

**Apollo:** Oracles are a tricky thing. The words of the gods are ever layered in meaning. All I can do is deliver them as faithfully as I can—an admittedly difficult task at times.

**Apollo:** And Narcissus has gained much from her interpretation of it: a newfound verve to rise from her failures and try again. I suppose that itself is enough.

**Apollo:** Besides, I doubt I could persuade her to understand it in any other way.

**Apollo:** She refuses to be persuaded.

**Themis:** ...

**Themis:** Were there rules for the delivery of oracles, you would stand trial for misleading your seekers.

The prophet lets out a small sigh.

**Apollo:** Then I am grateful there are no such rules.
