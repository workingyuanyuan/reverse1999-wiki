## Story

8:00Clear

Intern Dormitory, Laplace

***

***

Intern Dormitory, Laplace

A flurry of message alerts drags Eagle out of sleep on her day off.

**I'm in a bit of trouble. Could you come lend a hand? Coordinates below. —X**

**Note: If possible, bring something useful. Something that can handle a big guy.**

**Eagle:** "What happened, Mr. X? I'm on my way."

Deep in the Forest

***

***

Eagle finds X easily enough in the thick of the forest. But that turns out to be far from the biggest of today's obstacles.

**Eagle:** "Based on your message, I thought you'd stepped on someone's toes." Or that he was dealing with something that walks on two legs at least.

**X:** "Oh, I wouldn't have needed your help for that. So, did you bring anything useful?"

**Eagle:** "I guess a baseball bat won't be enough."

**X:** "Agreed."

**X:** "Just look at the thing. What is it, two meters tall? Maybe more? What even is it? Some kind of enormous Critter?"

Eagle sighs.

***

***

**Eagle:** "It's a moose."

A massive moose stands before them, head low, watching them both. Something unreadable flickers in its eyes.

They already tried to go around it, but the moose seemed to have no intention of letting them pass. In fact, it seems quite taken with the two little humans before it.

**X:** "Moose don't eat people, do they?"

**Eagle:** "No. So, how did you end up like this?"

**X:** "No idea. I was running an experiment in the forest this morning. By the time I realized it, this great beast was already standing right behind me."

**Eagle:** "What kind of experiment?"

**X:** "A mechanical one."

**Eagle:** "So you're making a machine?"

**X:** "That's right. I've been working on one that mass-produces fizzy sea-salted lemon-pepper explosive gummies. It's too large for even Laplace's biggest lab, so I had to move the whole thing out here."

**X:** "Plus, my colleagues have always had differing opinions on my candy machines."

Eagle glances at the rattling machine nearby. A thin curl of smoke leaks from its vent.

**X:** "So. Any ideas?"

**Eagle:** "Why did you call me, Mr. X? Why not someone else?"

**X:** "Being trapped by an animal isn't exactly the kind of thing you want to broadcast to all your colleagues."

**X:** "And I had a feeling you'd know what to do. You're a Scout, or a Scout enthusiast, at least. You ought to know a thing or two about the wilderness. I thought you could tie some kind of arcane knot and frighten the thing off or something."

**Eagle:** "There's no such thing ..."

**Eagle:** "Actually, I think the reason's simple. It's probably the salt."

**X:** "But this is a gummy-making machine." —A very large one.

**Eagle:** "Yes, but it makes gummies with a salty flavor."

**X:** "Perhaps it came for the lemon."

The gummy machine, with a body about as long as its name, lets out an even stranger noise than before, as if offended at being ignored for so long.

**Eagle:** "Mr. X, your machine is just a normal machine, right? No unusual functions, or an Awakened or anything? And that noise just now wasn't its way of joining our conversation, was it?"

**X:** "Not to my knowledge. Most likely the feed port is just jammed."

**Eagle:** "And what happens if it is?"

**X:** "Uh, well, it's in the name. Just before 'gummy.'"

At that very moment, a tremendous explosion tears through the forest, sending the machine to a final, shuddering stop.

**X:** \*cough-cough\*

**Eagle:** "Mr. X, watch out!"

Before the smoke can clear, Eagle seizes X by the arm, pulling him clear of the panicked moose charging straight toward them.

**Eagle:** "That was close!"

**X:** "No, wait! My fizzy sea-salted lemon-pepper explosive gummy machine—"

The moose crashes blindly through what remains of the already battered machine, then vanishes into the trees.

**Eagle:** "Phew. At least that problem's solved."

X walks over to what is left of his beloved machine. He crouches down beside it, looking rather pained, and picks through the fragments on the ground.

**X:** "Tragic. I never drew up any plans when I built it. And all that smoke ... it's covered us in salt."

**Eagle:** "Mr. X, don't let your guard down yet."

> **Choice 1：** Listen carefully to the surroundings

Eagle listens carefully to the forest around her, the way her father once taught her to.

The moose's heavy hoofbeats are already fading. It sounds thoroughly spooked. But something smaller is still lingering nearby.

No, not lingering. Changing direction—

> **Choice 2：** Carefully observe the surroundings

Eagle scans the area carefully. She knows how to read a forest.

The moose has left a trail of destruction. It's remarkable what a creature that size can do. Not far off, the shrubs sway gently in the wind.

No, not the wind. Something small is pushing through from the other side.

**Eagle:** "Wait! Something's coming." She stops.

**Eagle:** "Actually, never mind, Mr. X. It's nothing." A smile sneaks into her voice as she speaks.

Catching the shift in tone, X feels a sudden unease.

**X:** "Eagle, are you keeping something from me?"

**Eagle:** "It's fine—"

Before she can finish, X feels something wet and rough drag slowly across the top of his head.

He wheels around to find three small moose staring at him with enormous round eyes, happily licking away.

**X:** "Hey!"

**Eagle:** "It's in their nature. They want the salt."

A little further back, the larger moose has returned as well.

**Eagle:** "That one must be their mother. She won't hurt you, as long as you don't threaten her babies."

**X:** "What counts as a threat exactly? Stopping them from licking me? Wait."

The mother moose steps closer, and X finally understands what that look in her eyes meant all along.

She wants the salt too. She extends her tongue—

One hour later.

**Eagle:** "Mr. X, please don't be cross with me."

**X:** "You're going to swear to forget everything that happened today."

X attempts to smooth down his thoroughly licked hair. It does not go well.

**Eagle:** "Alright." Eagle doesn't sound entirely convinced.

**X:** "Still, you came to help. I honestly wasn't expecting you to."

**Eagle:** "Then you really don't trust me."

**X:** "Ha. Whatever you say."

**Eagle:** "Speaking of which, what would've happened if I hadn't shown up?"

**X:** "Neither of us would have liked that outcome. So, thank you."

**X:** "Here. Something for you."

***

***

Eagle takes the small object X has been clutching and examines it curiously.

**Eagle:** "This looks like a piece of copper that fell out of the machine when it exploded."

**X:** "It's not just copper. That is a one-of-a-kind Institutum Lorentz rescue medal."

**Eagle:** "But it really does just look like a copper plate, and it's sticky!"

**X:** "Treasure it, Eagle."

**Eagle:** "W-Wait, Mr. X!"
