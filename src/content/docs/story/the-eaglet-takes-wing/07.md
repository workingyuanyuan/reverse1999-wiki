## Story

Following the last note on the evidence board, Eagle arrives at an abandoned factory, figuring she might be useful for Ms. Marguerite's side job.

Besides, being a detective's assistant sounds really exciting.

Traces Track

When she activates her tracking incantation, she finds something.

**Eagle:** "Ms. Marguerite's trace? It's very fresh, which means she was just here."

**Eagle:** "This place gives me the creeps. I don't know ..."

**Eagle:** "There are lots of hiding spots here. The high points can cover each other while also having a clear view over the warehouse."

Looking around, Eagle feels a knot of worry form in her stomach.

**Eagle:** "There are other footprints." She bends down to check their shape and direction.

**Eagle:** "All-terrain boots. Judging by the depth, they're carrying about 30 kilos. Standard heavy gear weight."

**Eagle:** "Count ... 10, 11, 12."

**Eagle:** "These people aren't with Ms. Marguerite. More likely, the one she's investigating is being hunted by this armed group."

Her arcane senses catch more clues, revealing traces of radio signals heading toward those dangerous high points.

She tucks herself into a blind spot and peers up at the water tower. A faint glint catches her eye.

**Eagle:** "Sniper!"

**Eagle:** "I have to find her and get out of here!"

***

***

As she makes her way further in, Eagle detects more arcane traces.

Traps, arrays, tripwires. People who set these mean business. She navigates them carefully using her arcane skill.

> **Choice 1：** Investigate the path to the basement

Eagle edges her way along the path to the basement.

**Eagle:** "This way is totally collapsed. Based on the dust, it happened within the hour."

**Eagle:** "Arcane residue proves the collapse was intentional."

> **Choice 2：** Investigate the path to the warehouse

Inside the warehouse sit huge rusted containers. The thick layer of dust on them suggests nobody has been here for years.

As she carefully turns a corner, Eagle's eyes fall on smeared blood and frantic footprints.

She sniffs the air. Beneath the smell of gunpowder is a trace of sweetness.

**Eagle:** "Almost smells like candy."

**Eagle:** "Hmm, I can follow that scent. It leads to the control room!"

**Eagle:** "Let me see. Is it from those armed people? At least five pulled back from here."

**Eagle:** "But who attacked them?"

**Eagle:** "They ran this way, so the attack started at the control room?"

**Eagle:** "There must be more clues there."

***

***

Eagle moves quietly toward the control room, alert and keeping to the shadows.

Soon, she spots a team of heavily armed mercenaries tucked into a corner.

**Armed Mercenary I:** "I thought our bait would draw in that psycho chasing us. But so far, nothing's happened." So the chaos outside was just bait for their trap.

**Armed Mercenary II:** "Hm. She's more careful than we thought."

Peering from the shadows, Eagle watches the dark muzzles of their guns. A wave of fear and unease washes over her. Why have these mercenaries set so many traps? Who are they after?

The evidence board, the events at Laplace, the dark red stain ... All the clues connect, piecing together something huge.

**Armed Mercenary I:** "Hold on, she's here!" Through their night-vision goggles, they see a figure in the distance. She seems unaware, simply scouting the building.

**Eagle:** "That's Ms. Marguerite!!"

**Armed Mercenary I:** "Orders are in. We're putting her down. Let's roll."

**Eagle:** "She hasn't noticed the traps. I have to act." She steels herself.

But she can't face armed soldiers with her arcane power alone. What can she do to buy time and warn Ms. Marguerite?

Then Eagle recalls that unique smell from earlier.

Past the sweetness, there was a hint of sea salt and pepper beneath the gunpowder.

She knows what to do.

Traces Track

Eagle rummages in her pocket, tosses a handful of candies into the air, and triggers a precise arcane blast.

With a blinding flash, a deafening blast, and a cloud of salt and sugar powder billowing through the air, the building shudders. As the smoke spreads, it exposes where the mercenaries lay hidden.

**Eagle:** "Never thought Mr. X's crazy invention would come in handy at a time like this."

**Armed Mercenary I:** "\*cough\* What the ...?! Who's there?!"

**Armed Mercenary II:** "Dammit, we've lost eyes on the target!"

A familiar scope glint flashes.

**???:** "Thank you all for your cooperation."

Hearing that voice, Eagle lets out a sigh of relief.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "This tedious chase ends now."

Before the mercenary can say another word, a flash pierces his forehead, and he collapses instantly.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Just something to scramble their senses. They'll wake up soon."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Excellent work, my dear Eagle. Time to leave. When they wake, they'll trigger their own traps. Call it poetic justice."

**Eagle:** "B-But, ma'am. I have so many questions right now."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "And I'll listen carefully. But later."

And with that, they vanish into the shadows.

***

***

Having finally ditched their pursuers, Lorentz Butterfly turns and smiles at her assistant.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Cool-headed and quick-thinking. You never fail to impress, Eagle."

**Eagle:** "Will you answer my questions now?"

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "I'll tell you everything."

**Eagle:** "If ... If I hadn't found your evidence board and come here, how would you have handled those guys back there?"

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "But you did find it."

**Eagle:** "I guess so. But next time, please at least tell me about it!"

**Eagle:** "I don't want you getting in danger all the time."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "That's why I rely on you, isn't it?"

**Eagle:** "Yes, ma'am." Eagle takes a deep breath.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Good. Now, no need to worry about the Foundation monopolizing the sampling sites for the time being. After all, if Laplace can't access the data, how can we 'borrow' it?"

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "But before that, we need to escape. Ready to run?"

**Eagle:** "W-Wait!"
