Institutum Lorentz

The lab stays lit around the clock. Here, researchers pursuing their vision of arcanum tirelessly seek the seed of chaos, day in and day out.

**Eagle:** "Mr. 'La Biblioteca de Babel' thinks our tracking direction is right. He recommends setting the chaos particle monitoring precision to ..."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "..." There's no answer.

**Eagle:** "Ma'am?"

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "I'm just thinking."

She rests her cheek on her hand, knuckles tapping lightly against her face as she ponders.

Eagle senses something unusual about her today. She can't explain why, since as both Ms. Marguerite and Lorentz Butterfly, she looks perfectly normal.

A strange idea suddenly crosses her mind.

**What if there's more than just Marguerite and Lorentz Butterfly?**

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Well, Eagle—"

**Eagle:** "Y-Yes, ma'am?"

Eagle's sudden nervousness piques Lorentz Butterfly's interest.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "What's on your mind?" That familiar mysterious smile of Lorentz Butterfly's spreads across her face.

**Eagle:** "Nothing ..."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Tell me the truth."

**Eagle:** "I was just being silly, thinking about how many sides you might actually have."

Lorentz Butterfly stares for a moment, then laughs out loud.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "You are clever. I'm not sure even I know the answer to that."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "But no matter how many sides I have, you're still my trusted assistant. That won't change."

**Eagle:** "S-Sure!"

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Since we're both distracted today, let's call it a day."

**Eagle:** "Okay. I'll go back to the 'Storm' Observation Center to sort through the data."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Take it easy there, won't you? The gentle meteorological observer Marguerite won't mind." Her voice drifts away as she leaves.

Lounge, Laplace

***

***

A heavy pile of documents in hand, Eagle walks past the Laplace break room where small groups of researchers are chatting casually over coffee.

She glances at the data in her hands and slows her pace. Maybe she should grab a cup alongside the adults and join them in working late. At least then she could finish sorting the data before leaving.

**Lab Assistant I:** "Hey, heard the news?"

**Lab Assistant II:** "About the plant-based milk they're serving in the break room?"

**Lab Assistant I:** "Not that! Haven't you heard about that Foundation bureaucrat who disappeared recently?"

**Lab Assistant II:** "Oh. Well, I did hear Penny's project finally got approved because Benjamin got replaced. He'd been blocking it for a while."

**Lab Assistant I:** "He didn't just get replaced. He up and vanished! The Foundation found him in a hospital, demanding to quit."

**Lab Assistant II:** "Any word from the Foundation? Think Manus Vindictae was involved?"

**Lab Assistant I:** "Nobody knows yet. But, honestly, he deserved it. A top-tier political rat, that guy. Stuck his nose in all kinds of places it didn't belong."

**Eagle:** "I hope this doesn't affect our work."

Eagle jumps into the conversation.

**Lab Assistant I:** "Hey, you're the new intern, right? Don't worry. The less the Foundation meddles in us, the better, I say."

**Lab Assistant I:** "Those bureaucrats are always trying to come up with a new way to stick their fingers in Laplace's business."

**Lab Assistant II:** "Right, so no need to get hooked on coffee like the rest of us. Who knows? We might be writing fewer reports in the future."

**Eagle:** "Alright. I'll be going then."

"Storm" Observation Center, Laplace

***

***

The center feels empty after work hours. Eagle grabs her bag, and a few orange-flavored chocolate balls fall out—her current favorite treat.

**Eagle:** "I should leave some for Ms. Marguerite."

Eagle adds some chocolates to the pile of materials and instruments on Marguerite's desk, disrupting the balance and sending them tumbling to the ground.

**Eagle:** "Oh no!"

While frantically gathering the fallen pages, something catches her attention.

**Eagle:** "What's this?"

***

***

**Eagle:** "An evidence board?"

She's only seen these in whodunits:

A small board with pinned photos, receipts, and careful timelines.

Notes are jotted down next to each lead. Undoubtedly Ms. Marguerite's handwriting.

**Eagle:** "Benjamin? The missing Foundation official they were talking about in the break room?"

Seeing that familiar name makes her swallow hard.

**Eagle:** "Is she investigating him?"

She suddenly remembers the dark red stain on Ms. Marguerite's cuff.

**Eagle:** "Oh, I see now!"

**Eagle:** "Ms. Marguerite has a side job as a detective! She must have uncovered some nefarious secrets about the Foundation!"
