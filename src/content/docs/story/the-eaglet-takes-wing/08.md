"Storm" Observation Center, Laplace

"Storm" Observation Center, Laplace

Eagle steps into the "Storm" Observation Center, carrying a pile of documents. A group of researchers are clustered around a monitor, too absorbed in their debate to notice her.

**Eagle:** "What happened?"

**Researcher I:** "Have you seen Lorentz Butterfly's latest article? Its correlation with the 'Storm' critical point prediction is over 30% more accurate than ours!"

**Researcher II:** "We might as well adopt her results."

**Researcher I:** "Come on! A huge new budget is coming our way. Enough to build 20 new sampling stations."

**Researcher I:** "Once we have global data coming in, our 'Storm' forecasts will be much more accurate than hers!"

**Researcher II:** "But that Lorentz Butterfly somehow always accesses our data. Eagle, how do you think she manages it?"

**Eagle:** "Well, maybe she's a ghost living inside our transistors."

**Researcher II:** "Not impossible. Maybe we should call a ghostbuster."

**Eagle:** "Also, I have your data files."

Eagle passes the documents to the researchers and quickly makes her exit. Ms. Marguerite, of course, is nowhere to be seen. She is perpetually occupied, after all.

Test Site, Laplace

***

***

Laplace's test site is always a mess, likely the result of its geniuses bringing all of their latest wacky brainchildren here for testing. Of course, the state of this place proves that just because an idea is new doesn't mean it's good.

Eagle adjusts her detector. Lately, under Ms. Marguerite's and Mr. X's tutelage, she's been adding all sorts of new features to her weapon. Though she did veto certain proposals, like making the detector double as a bubble machine.

Another intern watches Eagle finish her calibration with a look of surprise.

**Intern I:** "Eagle? You are with the 'Storm' Observation Center, aren't you? Is that a rapid document generator you're testing or something?" Her expression turns pitying. "Oh, I get it. This must be for handling those pesky Foundation folks."

**Eagle:** "That definitely would be a useful feature."

**Intern I:** "Then, is that really a weapon? Geez, times really are tough, huh?"

**Eagle:** "Haha. Like they say in the Scouts, 'Be prepared.'"

**Eagle:** "Think of it as my little hobby. Alright, lunch break's over. Time to get back to sorting the data."

Institutum Lorentz

***

***

On Eagle's new desk at Institutum Lorentz sits a shiny instrument adorned with a bright ribbon.

It isn't a cutting-edge arcane device or an auxiliary computing unit for analysis.

Eagle recognizes it as a chaos pendulum.

Stuck to the pendulum is a small Card.

She gives the pendulum a flick, setting the simple mechanism into erratic motion. Random, chaotic, impossible to predict.

But Eagle knows that every movement of this disorder stems entirely from her initial act.

#### Notes · Card

The world is a chaotic system, yet chaos always stems from an initial condition. For instance, that butterfly in Brazil that will forever beat its wings in every version of the theory. Or the little eagle who will soon learn to flap its wings and, in doing so, is bound to stir a storm of its own. —M
