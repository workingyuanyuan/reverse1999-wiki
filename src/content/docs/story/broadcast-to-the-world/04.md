Foundation New York Branch

**Foundation Staff Member I:** "Miss, please wait here. Your interviewer will be with you shortly."

**ONiON:** "No problemo! Just happy to be here!"

The staff member in the gray-and-white checkered uniform clicks away on her heels.

Once the footsteps fade at the end of the hallway, ONiON hunches low and begins scouring over the room.

**ONiON:** "Top priority: find proof of St. Pavlov Foundation's misconduct. They're hiding some crucial truth from the public. We still don't know what they're really after, but there has to be a conspiracy behind it."

**ONiON:** "Our first step should be ..."

> **Choice 1：** To the archive room

**ONiON:** "Right, we should head to the archive room. That's where their most vital information will be kept."

**ONiON:** "If we can just get past that room, everything else falls into place."

> **Choice 2：** Develop an informant

**ONiON:** "Best not to arouse suspicions by poking around."

**ONiON:** "We should work on finding someone on the inside to give the scoop on what's happening here. I just know they're up to something nefarious. We just have to find a voice waiting to be heard."

Faint footsteps echo from the end of the hallway. Someone's coming.

> **Choice 1：** Leave this place

ONiON ties a gray-and-white checkered scarf around her neck, doing her best to blend in among the uniforms.

She slips into the inner office area just as another staff member badges the door open.

No one gives her a second look. Strange new faces are part and parcel with working in a busy branch.

***

**ONiON:** "Where to now?"

> **Choice 1：** An argument spills out from a nearby office.

ONiON heads toward the sound.

Arguments in the Office

Clue 5 - The Crack in the Foundation

**Fact**St. Pavlov Foundation hires with precision and rigor, making sure every staff member can put their expertise to full use and maximize productivity.

**Hypothesis**Internally, the Foundation runs on high-pressure standards. Blurred lines of authority, brutal overtime, and constant conflict between departments are all common. Some staff members are planning a strike.

There's no more sound. ONiON leaves.

> **Choice 2：** Beside a distant pillar, someone is on the phone.

ONiON creeps closer to the sound.

**Madam:** "They still haven't found the missing SPDM student?"

***

**Madam:** "After the last incident, I thought if we revised the regulations a little, started treating the students with a bit more humanity ..."

**Madam:** "Of course, sir. I'll take responsibility for any negative consequences that may arise. Finding that student is all that matters right now."

Missing Student

Clue 6 - Missing Student

**Fact**A student has gone missing from the SPDM, possibly linked to the school's high-pressure policies. The Foundation is apparently taking action.

**Hypothesis**The Foundation has gotten involved in the SPDM's missing student case, but things appear to have spun out of control.

The woman seems to sense something suddenly. She glances toward where ONiON is hiding and leaves.

> **Choice 3：** A stray notebook lies abandoned by the roadside.

It's a notebook with a sheepskin cover, the words Student Handbook written across it.

***

**ONiON:** "The SPDM, working under the St. Pavlov Foundation, provides education to young arcanists, using its unique teaching methods to deepen students' understanding of arcanum ..."

**ONiON:** "... cultivating a sound, controllable value system."

**ONiON:** "More than half of its graduates go on to work within the Foundation, mostly as field investigators."

Student Handbook

Clue 7 - Student Handbook

**Fact**The SPDM is a school built to educate arcanists and meant to serve human society.

**Hypothesis**The SPDM isn't a school. It's a prison. It reshapes its students through particular methods until they willingly accept a fate that ends in inevitable sacrifice for their cause.

Footsteps echo faintly in the distance. It's not safe to linger here any longer.

ONiON slips sideways into an employee lounge.
