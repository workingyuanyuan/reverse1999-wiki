## Story

0:00

Pending Release

***

***

13:16

ONiON's Home

**ONiON:** "\*panting\*"

Even back home, ONiON's heart is still racing.

**ONiON:** "Not a pleasant memory, but at least I'm in the clear for now. Time to get writing."

**ONiON:** "The truth about crop circles, UFOs, and what's really going on inside the Foundation ..."

**ONiON:** "Except ..."

Except that the crop circle was erased, the UFO vanished without a trace, and the witness seems to have been brainwashed right out of speaking. And frankly, she has no idea what's really going on in the Foundation, just a hunch.

**ONiON:** "So, what have I got? 'Belching Cows Defending the World from Alien Invaders.' Who knows? The director might go for it if it's a slow day in Hollywood."

**ONiON:** "There's got to be something more."

**Telephone:** \*ring\*

The sound cuts off her train of thought.

**ONiON:** "Hello, this is ONiON speaking."

**Director:** "Bring your project proposal for Headonion and get to my office on the double."

**Director:** "We need to talk."

14:23

Office, TV Station

***

***

The director sits in his soft leather chair, flipping through the Headonion proposal.

**Director:** "You've got the green light."

The news doesn't register at first. Her ears must be playing tricks on her or else she must be dreaming.

**ONiON:** "Could you say that again, sir?"

He clears his throat.

**Director:** "A charitable benefactor has made a sizeable donation to the studio, with one condition: they wanted Headonion on the air."

**ONiON:** "This benefactor—is it the St. Pavlov Foundation?!"

**Director:** "How do you know? Maybe you do have a knack for journalism."

**Director:** "With the money they were offering, we had no choice but to accept. So, you've got your show."

**Director:** "But we have a condition of our own. We can't call this thing news for liability's sake. So, for all intents and purposes, you're the host of an entertainment documentary show."

**Director:** "That's our offer. Take it or leave it."

Before he can finish, ONiON cuts him off.

**ONiON:** "I accept! Sir, I'll accept anything if it means getting Headonion on air!"

The director looks at her with a shocked expression.

**Director:** "Really? Gotta say that surprises me. Y'know that means people won't take what you have to say seriously, right?"

**ONiON:** "You know I'm no fan of entertainment shows, sir. But if that's the label I need to take on to be able to tell the truth, I'll do it."

**ONiON:** "With this show, I'll have the reach to help people confront what's actually happening in the world! Unknown creatures, vanishing cows, the truth about the greenhouse effect ..."

**ONiON:** "And the children inside the Foundation! They need someone to speak up for them!"

**ONiON:** "But why would they sponsor this show if I wanted to expose them?"

**Director:** "Honestly, I've been asking myself the same thing."

In an instant, the dotted lines she's been drawing in her head connect.

**ONiON:** "Hush money!"

**ONiON:** "By funding me, they get to control what I can say."

**Director:** "They didn't make any special requests about your content, aside from saying they were 'impressed with your work on UFOs.'"

**Director:** "Beats me what they're really after."

**ONiON:** "..."

Her expression turns grave.

**ONiON:** "They're far more insidious than I ever imagined."

**ONiON:** "But it won't change anything. My mission is to bring the truth to the public, and I won't compromise it, not even if it costs me everything."

**ONiON:** "I have to trust the audience's judgment. I have to believe they can tell what's real from what isn't."

**Director:** "..."

**Director:** "Sounds like you're still in. Congratulations."

The director shrugs and extends a hand.

**Director:** "I look forward to working with you."

ONiON grips it and gives him a firm and confident shake.

**ONiON:** "The pleasure's all mine, sir. I promise you, we'll have Headonion at the top of the ratings in no time!"
