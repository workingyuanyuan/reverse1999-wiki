10:48

Foundation New York Branch

She ducks into a hidden corner. No one is here. Finally, her racing heart can slow down.

**ONiON:** \*exhale\*

**ONiON:** "It seems there are quite a few cracks in the facade here. I should be more careful."

Her newspaper-covered face twists with concern.

**ONiON:** "But there still isn't enough here to write home about. I need something more."

\*tap-tap-tap\*

Too focused on her thoughts, she misses the faint tapping sound as it approaches.

**???:** "Who's there?"

**ONiON:** —!

Startled, ONiON tries to make herself as small as possible, hoping to stay hidden in the shrubs.

A soft footfall sounds, and a white-haired child appears before her, standing beside the little pond in the lounge.

**ONiON:** "You're ..."

The white-haired child watches her without a word. The mechanical device embedded in his eye twitches in time with the other pale gray one.

The Foundation, the child, the school, the news story—all the threads connect in her mind, forming the outline of the whole picture.

**ONiON:** "You ... You're that student from the SPDM?"

***

***

ONiON bolts toward him. His clothes are gray-and-white too. She becomes even more certain.

**ONiON:** "What have they done to you, kid?"

**???:** "...?"

**???:** "You think I'm a student?"

**ONiON:** "I think that the Foundation is involved in some sort of unprecedented conspiracy, not only to mislead the public, but also to brainwash and control students like you."

**ONiON:** "Just one of those things would be evil by itself, but both? Despicable."

**ONiON:** "Don't worry, kid. I'll find a way to help you. I'm an investigative journalist. I'm going to blow the lid off this whole thing!"

**"Student":** "..."

A faint smile crawls across the "student's" face.

**"Student":** "Miss, if everyone were as brave as you, this world would be a much better place."

**"Student":** "But I was actually just about to do some fishing. Care to join me?"

**ONiON:** "Fishing?"

**"Student":** "My work here keeps me so busy that I barely have a moment to myself. But whenever I can, I like to slip away and find a little joy for myself."

He speaks as he baits the hook and casts the line into the water. The bait sends out a few ripples and sinks.

**"Student":** "The fish here never bite. They'll nibble sometimes, but they never take the hook. Still, I like to think I'll catch one some day."

**ONiON:** "I don't really care for fishing, kid."

**"Student":** "What do you care about then?"

For a moment, images flash through ONiON's mind.

A UFO vanishing without a trace, five mysterious figures slipping into a building late at night, a patient whose veins were transformed into electric wires ...

**ONiON:** "The truth."

**"Student":** "Is that so?"

**"Student":** "You won't find what you're looking for here, miss."

He raises his hand and points toward the pond in front of them.

**"Student":** "From our perspective, you can see the whole pond. It's not all that large. But from their perspective, it's the whole world."

**"Student":** "Herring, striped bass ... all local sea life, all carefully chosen by the administration here for maximum compatibility. It has the latest in climate control systems, and they're fed on a routine schedule. Every effort is taken to make sure they are safe and comfortable."

**"Student":** "Unlike the wild, where danger and hunger are a constant threat."

**"Student":** "I think that's why they never bite, because they don't need to rush to snatch up the bait. There's no fear here—no survival of the fittest. That's the truth of it: happy, well-fed fish in a climate-controlled pond."

White-Haired Student's Testimony

Clue 8 - Student's Testimony

**Fact**SPDM students stay busy, but they carve out a little time for hobbies. To them, life feels orderly and safe.

**Hypothesis**SPDM students are still young and defenseless, yet the Foundation forces them to do so much every day.

ONiON searches for a response to this absurdly well-spoken child.

**ONiON:** "You're wrong."

**ONiON:** "They don't get to control other people's worlds. They don't get to keep people from the truth."

**ONiON:** "Journalist, scientist, student, president, king ... it doesn't matter. We have to present the facts as they are."

**ONiON:** "And whether you're fish in a pond or eyeballs glued to a screen, it's your job to decide what those facts mean—to judge what is and isn't true for yourself."

**ONiON:** "It isn't an easy thing to do. It's hard, but it only gets harder when people in power put their interests ahead of the facts."

**ONiON:** "Maybe you'd still prefer the world they made for you. But if you don't know the truth, how can you really decide?"

**ONiON:** "That's what I do. I show people the hidden hooks and the world beyond those constructed walls. That's what I'm going to do with my show."

**"Student":** "And how has that worked out for you so far?"

She flusters again before responding, grim but resolute.

**ONiON:** "They don't think people want to hear about these things."

**"Student":** "Why not?"

**ONiON:** "Because celebrity gossip is safe and comfortable, but watching someone tear down those walls and expose the hidden hooks means knowing you'll have to decide what to do next. Who would watch something like that?"

**"Student":** "..."

**"Student":** "I would."

His smile broadens on his face, not quite revealing any teeth. It's a smile wise beyond his apparent years.

**"Student":** "I look forward to seeing Headonion on TV."

**ONiON:** "If I could just get that funding and someone who would give me the air time, I could expose the truth behind this whole operation. I could help you and all the other students here."

**ONiON:** "But I don't have enough evidence yet to do anything. I'll have to keep digging, and I promise you this: One day, the truth will be set free."

**"Student":** "Is that right? I hope that day comes soon."

Footsteps sound nearby. ONiON turns her head, alert.

**ONiON:** "I have to go now. I can't let them catch me here. Don't tell anyone you saw me, alright?"

**ONiON:** "But I'll keep my promise."

**"Student":** "Of course."

ONiON bends low and slips off along the wall.

A moment later, someone else reaches the pond's edge.

**President:** "Constantine, any news?"

**Constantine:** "An intruder slipped past our security. We haven't located them yet. I need to get you to safety."

**President:** "I'll be just fine. Thank you."

The bobber trembles slightly. The president pulls the hook up to find the bait half eaten.

**President:** "Clever fish ... So, any updates on the investigation?"

**Constantine:** "Everything checked out on paper. And yet the money vanished somewhere in the transfers. We believe it's an organized operation."

**Constantine:** "The head of the New York branch is a strong suspect. Even if he wasn't behind it, he'd have known."

**Constantine:** "We've secured a few cooperating witnesses so far, but finding the breakthrough evidence we need will take time."

**President:** "Well, then I guess we should wrap things up here. Oh, and about that intruder—"

The president casts the hook out again. Fish gather around it.

**Constantine:** "We have some idea of who she is. A young investigative journalist who was here for an interview, though that seems to have just been an excuse. We don't know what she was looking for yet."

**Constantine:** "But she's an unacceptable risk to our operations here. We'll make every effort to track her down and find out what she's learned. If necessary, we'll do what we have to keep her silent."

**Constantine:** "We need to prevent any negative press concerning the Foundation."

A moment passes as the hook bobs up and down in the pond.

**President:** "All that's accomplished by silencing someone is proving to the world that they've found something worth being silenced over."

**President:** "Let her go."

**Constantine:** "Understood, sir."

The bobber twitches, and the president lifts the rod again. He sighs at the empty hook.

**President:** "I'm beginning to miss the south bank of the Thames."
