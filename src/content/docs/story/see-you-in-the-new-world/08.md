## Story

**Bkornblume:** I think this must have been a terrible blow to her.

Bkornblume shakes her head, holding the drawings in her hands with deep sorrow.

**Bkornblume:** I still remember the houses she drew for the locals. I don't understand much of architecture, but I thought those homes must be very comfortable to live in.

**Bkornblume:** But these ...

**Honza:** Honza told you! This lady is no architect. Someone else drewy-drew all her designs!

**Everecho:** Oh, that sounds very bad.

**Everecho:** What if Cornerstone "designs" all those famous architects' masterpieces before they're even born?

**Everecho:** That would bode poorly for humanity.

**Cornerstone:** Hey, did I just overhear a little group session of rumors about me?

**Cornerstone:** Jasio, long time no see! Apparently these "Y2K Futurism" concept designs managed to greet you before me.

**Jasio:** I don't understand them at all. I'm worried something may have happened to you.

**Cornerstone:** Huh?

**Jasio:** For example, I don't know why you designed the lettering with this huge, three-dimensional, rainbow gradient.

**Jasio:** My eyes are not enjoying it.

**Cornerstone:** Historical materials from 1999 are precious, you know. You should learn to appreciate this lost future art.

**Cornerstone:** I suggest you get used to them early. Otherwise, once we return to 1999, our tastes will be terribly outdated.

**Jasio:** Uhh ...

**Bkornblume:** What does Ms. Grundriss think?

**Cornerstone:** She refused to comment.

**Bkornblume:** That sounds about right.

**Cornerstone:** How has Olga been? I haven't heard from you all in ages.

**Jasio:** She's doing well.

**Jasio:** After that "Storm," the Foundation brought us into various branches as people with knowledge of the "Storm."

**Jasio:** Olga helps with tasks that require coordination and communication with local people, while the rest of us can only do some physical work here and there.

**Jasio:** The children are all healthy, and the food here is much better than it was in Nowa Miedź.

**Cornerstone:** I'm glad to hear that.

**Bkornblume:** I heard you had a very curious encounter in Barcelona. Laplace seems highly interested in this experience you had with an Awakened building.

**Cornerstone:** We can talk about it over dinner tonight.

**Cornerstone:** But I can't stay long. I'm flying out tomorrow.

**Bkornblume:** It sounds like you're keeping busy, even though most of the Foundation's architectural commissions should be on hold now.

**Cornerstone:** You're as well informed as ever.

**Cornerstone:** I plan to travel around the world and archive buildings worth recording, particularly the ones that only exist in this era.

**Cornerstone:** It's an excellent learning opportunity for me. Perhaps I'll even stumble across another Point Irreversible along the way.

**Grundriss:** We call that the "blind cat stumbling over a dead mouse" approach to research.

The late-arriving supervisor bluntly hands everyone a thin sheet of paper as if dealing cards.

**Grundriss:** Fill out these visitor registration forms, all of you.

**Cornerstone:** We finally manage to get together, and your first instinct is to make us fill out these long, awful forms?

**Grundriss:** So?

**Cornerstone:** I see.

**Grundriss:** Still, your idea is actually rather good.

Grundriss nods, then takes Everecho's and Honza's forms and begins filling in the basic information for the two of them, who clearly have no idea what a visitor registration form is.

**Grundriss:** The era outside is indeed "moving backward." At least, that is how it appears based on the current pattern of the "Storm."

**Grundriss:** Perhaps one day, those eyesore Brutalism buildings you love will become the height of fashion.

**Grundriss:** Until they disappear completely.

**Everecho:** Hmm, I don't quite understand.

**Everecho:** Aren't things supposed to grow older and older until they disappear?

**Grundriss:** Not in the "Storm."

**Jasio:** Well, it seems the world has finally grown tired of only killing the old.

**Jasio:** The too-old die of age, and the too-new get washed away.

**Bkornblume:** Sounds challenging. Perhaps this is a world better suited to middle-aged people?

**Cornerstone:** Acting middle-aged doesn't count, does it?

**Grundriss:** I don't know what you're implying, but according to Foundation regulations, anyone who enters areas beyond the lobby without completing visitor registration review is an illegal trespasser.

**Grundriss:** That includes the dining hall.

Everyone chatters as they hunch over a round table, doing battle with the visitor registration forms. Passing staff hurry by and stir up a gust of air.

A poster that had been lying on the table is lifted by the wind and drifts softly to the floor nearby.

The gaudy lettering is lit by sunlight spreading across the floor, like a true rainbow glow.

**Cornerstone:** Truth be told, I'm terrified of airplanes.

**Cornerstone:** Whenever the plane circles or turns and tilts slightly away from the horizon, I feel as if we'll overturn and fall to the ground in the next second.

**Cornerstone:** The angle changes until the sky outside the window disappears, and I'm forced to look down over the whole city.

**Cornerstone:** But in that moment, I forget my fears.

**Cornerstone:** I look out over all those countless buildings stacked brick by brick, slab by slab, with no shortage of planning and meticulous design poured into them by architects like me.

**Cornerstone:** In the end, they are gathered into a net woven from light, just like the one I saw in Nowa Miedź.

**Cornerstone:** Modern urban planning must always face one fact: nature retreats from the city.

**Cornerstone:** We plant streets full of lamps and neon that spread light pollution until you look up at the sky and you can't even see the stars.

**Cornerstone:** But that flight taught me one thing:

**Cornerstone:** The stars never truly left us.

**Cornerstone:** All we did was pluck them from the sky and sow them into the ground.

**Cornerstone:** When passengers on an airplane look down over our city at night, they see a glittering galaxy below.

**Cornerstone:** I hope that you'll watch over us like them from above.

**Cornerstone:** All my friends and family.
