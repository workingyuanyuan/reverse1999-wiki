## Story

Bar Helicon

**Hermes:** Oh my, you're just letting her drink like this? And here I thought you were the most reliable prophet-bartender in all Ephaistonos!

**Narcissus:** zzzZZZZ ...

At the bar, Narcissus lies face-down on the counter, unconscious.

**Apollo:** I haven't given her a drop of alcohol.

The bartender pours a small cup of warm water and slides it within her reach.

**Themis:** She just needed to cry herself out. Let her rest a while.

**Themis:** The funeral for Coryphaeus Doris was held today.

**Apollo:** We'd met once before. She was a good Nymph.

The prophet lets out a soft sigh.

**Hermes:** That she was. The Nymphs rarely show themselves on the island. Ms. Doris and Narcissus are the only two I've ever spoken with.

The judge drapes a blanket over Narcissus. A faint tremor stirs beneath it.

Her grief has followed her even into sleep.

**Themis:** Narcissus came to me once and asked if she could enter the island's competitions. There are no rules against it, of course, so I told her yes.

**Themis:** Even though no Nymph had ever competed in the Aquatic Games before.

**Themis:** Doris truly valued Narcissus. But I don't believe that's why Narcissus felt she needed to prove herself in the arena.

Her gaze settles on Narcissus's drooping ear fins as she talks about how they first met.

**Hermes:** Haha, the first time I met her, I saw her running laps around the coastal road.

**Hermes:** So I slowed down and offered her some advice: for a Nymph, wouldn't it be faster to swim to wherever she's headed?

**Hermes:** But she told me she was building up her strength on land.

**Hermes:** Then ...

**Hermes:** "A Nymph, competing in the Aquatic Games?" I thought, "What could be more interesting than that?" Of course I was going to support her!

With their stories told, both sets of eyes land on the bartender, who only shakes his head helplessly.

**Apollo:** Friends, I never meddle in another's fate.

**Apollo:** So when Narcissus asked me to discern the outcome of her feat, I gave her no answer.

**Apollo:** And for anyone who comes to a prophet seeking answers, silence is a response in itself.

**Apollo:** Even so, Narcissus still ended up here.

**Hermes:** Well, we gained a good friend because of it, didn't we? I'd say that matters more than anything else.

**Themis:** Hermes, you can't simply ignore what's right in front of you. Narcissus lost the one who mattered most to her, and now she's dealing with her failure, too.

**Themis:** She has been through many trials, yet she's never once stopped, even after losing more than once.

**Themis:** If I'm honest, I can't help but wonder if I made the wrong call.

**Apollo:** Themis, most fates are not decided by any single choice.

**Apollo:** Whether you approved it or not, Narcissus was always going to compete. And as for Doris, her fate was more inevitable still.

**Narcissus:** Apollo's right.

The three of them pause.

Narcissus lifts her face. Her eyes are swollen, and tear tracks stain her face.

**Narcissus:** Doris meant so much to me.

**Narcissus:** She was the only one willing to tell me those things, the history every Spring Nymph refuses to speak of.

**Narcissus:** At first, we were nothing but outsiders in Ephaistonos.

**Narcissus:** After our habitat was destroyed, our people endured tremendous upheaval before we finally found a home here.

**Narcissus:** Our bodies are too fragile to withstand any waves or storms.

**Narcissus:** Along the way, we lost more of our kin than could ever be counted.

**Narcissus:** Until the surviving Nymphs reached Ephaistonos, and Hedone took them in.

**Narcissus:** That was a long, long time ago.

**Narcissus:** But the Nymphs who survived the migration didn't survive what came after. One by one, they were lost.

**Narcissus:** Doris was the only one left. To the Nymphs born on Ephaistonos, it's a history soaked in horror.

**Hermes:** Nymphs can hardly bear that kind of chaos. I'm not surprised they're so scared.

Narcissus looks at the trophies gleaming in the display case. Something different flickers in her eyes.

**Narcissus:** But not me. I never saw it that way.

**Narcissus:** Our history fascinates me.

**Narcissus:** How did my ancestors cross those raging waves, hurricanes, and storms? I've never seen a Nymph capable of that.

**Narcissus:** Doris also told me they once ran into pirates on a massive ship, pirates who had kidnapped a prophet.

**Narcissus:** It was Doris who untied the prophet, with her own two hands! A Nymph, saving her from pirates!

**Narcissus:** So the prophet pointed the way to Ephaistonos.

**Narcissus:** "A sovereign rules that island, temperamental but generous."

**Narcissus:** "The land she rules will become your second home."

**Narcissus:** Her adventure stories about Spring Nymphs are my favorite bedtime stories.

She lowers her eyes, as if sinking into memory.

**Apollo:** Sometimes, a disaster and an epic are two sides of the same coin.

**Narcissus:** I was born in Ephaistonos. I've never lived through those "epics" myself, and the other Nymphs never talk to me about them.

**Narcissus:** But I ... I just long for the sea so much.

Her webbed hands clench.

**Narcissus:** I think maybe I don't really belong among the Nymphs. Except for Doris, no one has ever understood my longing.

**Narcissus:** That's why I left the lake deep in the forest and explored every corner of Ephaistonos to satisfy my own curiosity.

**Narcissus:** But by the time I had seen it all, my curiosity had only grown.

**Narcissus:** It simply could not be tamed!

**Narcissus:** It makes me feel like an outsider among my own kind. I can't go home. I don't even feel like myself.

**Narcissus:** Or maybe I'm becoming more like myself.

**Narcissus:** I look out at the sea from a place where the Nymphs never go, and it seems like it has no end.

**Narcissus:** I imagine the lands I can't see from here, however far away they are, still sharing the same sky.

**Narcissus:** Everything in me yearns for wider waters and a broader world.

**Narcissus:** I can't ignore the call of that shimmering ocean.

**Narcissus:** That day, I slipped and fell into the sea.

**Themis:** ...

**Narcissus:** Ah ... ugh ... ah!

**Narcissus:** \*cough\*

The sea has never been home to the Nymphs. Caught in its grip, she is nearly lost beneath the waves.

Sharp pain tears through her as the salt begins to burn away at her fins and skin.

**Narcissus:** So salty! Bah! It hurts!

**Narcissus:** I'm ... \*pant\* ... I'm going to die here.

**???:** You won't, Narcissus.

The unexpected voice cuts through the pain, if only for a moment.

**Narcissus:** Doris?! What are you doing? \*cough-cough\*

The bitter seawater floods her mouth before she can finish.

Then someone takes her hands and lifts her body.

**Doris:** Narcissus, you must learn how to read the waves. Listen to your heart. Find their rhythm.

**Narcissus:** Rhythm?

Her chest rises and falls as she slowly finds her breath again, and with it, the clues hidden within the waves.

Incredibly, she finds the courage to let go.

**Narcissus:** The waves ... they're holding me up! Doris, I did it!

**Narcissus:** Ah! \*splutter\*

The waves surge again, a sharp reminder that she's nowhere near mastering this place.

**Doris:** Narcissus, don't panic. Let your breathing follow the rhythm.

**Narcissus:** I ...

Floating in these boundless waters for the first time, she truly realizes just how vast the world is.

And all her doubts, questions, and curiosity fade to nothing beside it.

**Narcissus:** Doris, how do you even ...

That day, the Coryphaeus she loved so deeply gave her no answer.

She just smiled at her, there in a sea that was never meant for them.
