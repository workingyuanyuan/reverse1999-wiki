## Story

Theater

**Hermes:** I don't know, Narcissus. I'm still a little worried.

**Hermes:** Are you sure you actually met Thalassa herself?

He's clutching the ticket he received from Narcissus but clearly doesn't buy her story.

**Hermes:** Couldn't she be a fake? Not that I don't trust you. Merfolk are just sketchy.

**Hermes:** Apollo, have you ever met one before?

He elbows the prophet standing beside him.

**Apollo:** I've heard legends. They have many branches, just as Nymphs do. There were wars between them, deep in the ancient sea.

**Apollo:** Some of them remained trapped in the deeps forever. Others split off, scattering to the shallows, even onto land.

**Apollo:** But that is only a rumor.

Beside them, Narcissus pays no mind to the conversation. She's just staring at the stage, waiting for the curtains to rise.

**Narcissus:** Shh, quiet! The show's about to start!

**Audience Member I:** Amazing! Incredible!

**Audience Member II:** The friendship between a Spring Nymph and a Merfolk ... Oh! How touching!

**Audience Member III:** Praise Thalassa! Praise all the incredible stories that you've given the world!

**Narcissus:** Well? Wasn't Thalassa's story amazing?

**Themis:** Indeed. It lives up to its reputation.

The judge offers a measured assessment.

**Apollo:** Thrilling, one might say.

**Hermes:** I'm officially a Thalassa fan now!

**Narcissus:** Ooh! Ooh! It's not over yet!

Like some hawker peddling a once-in-a-lifetime tale, Narcissus waves her friends back to the stage.

**Audience Member I:** Huh? Isn't the show over?

**Audience Member II:** Maybe it's a special performance for the festival! Haha!

**Audience Member II:** Good thing I managed to get a ticket.

**Audience Member II:** Ah!

The woman's happy sigh breaks off, and her eyes grow wide.

**Audience Member III:** What's that? Good heavens!

**Thalassa:** Welcome, everyone.

Maybe the stage lights are too bright. Maybe the crowd's silence has hit too hard. Either way, she looks lost, adrift in a sea of eyes.

But she steps forward all the same, letting her face meet every one of those stares.

**Thalassa:** Sometimes, at a show's curtain call, the playwright steps out to take a bow alongside the cast.

**Thalassa:** This is something I've always wanted to do.

**Thalassa:** After all this waiting, I am finally standing here today.

She blinks her round eyes and draws one deep breath, there on a human stage.

**Thalassa:** Please, allow me to introduce myself. I am—

**Audience Member I:** NO—!!!

**Audience Member I:** Monster!!!

**Audience Member II:** Please, I'm begging you. Don't say you're Thalassa!

**Audience Member II:** This is absurd!

Some are stunned. Some are furious. Some are in tears.

They can't accept it. Thalassa, a real, living Merfolk!

**Thalassa:** ...

**Narcissus:** Thalassa!

A split second later, a small figure comes flying onto the stage, knocking away a hurled glove on her way.

She throws her arms wide, placing herself between Thalassa and the crowd.

**Narcissus:** You heard her! She said her name is Thalassa!

**Narcissus:** She is Thalassa!

**Narcissus:** Stop covering your eyes and ears! Face the truth!

Furious, Narcissus's ear fins bristle.

**Narcissus:** How could you all love her stories and art so much and still not accept her for who she truly is?

**Narcissus:** You say you love Thalassa's soul, but now that she's actually standing here in front of you, you won't even allow her to keep her own name!

**Narcissus:** The world is so, so big! There's so much we've never seen.

**Narcissus:** Sometimes you'll find you don't even know the people closest to you!

She's out of breath, her face burning redder by the second.

**Narcissus:** And then what?

**Narcissus:** You stay in the shallows forever, watching the same world, making the same choices? How could you ever understand the sea's vastness, its endless changes?

**Narcissus:** You all owe Thalassa an apology!

Dead silence.

At least no one is denying Thalassa her name anymore.

**Narcissus:** A-Alright, just promise you'll never hurt her again.

As the adrenaline wears off, she starts stammering.

**Hermes:** Narcissus! Get down! The police are coming!

The silence breaks and voices surge.

**Audience Member III:** Get off!!

**Narcissus:** Huh?!

She ducks, dodging the bottles and paper balls flying at her. She turns and grabs Thalassa's hand.

**Narcissus:** Thalassa! Run!

The two of them bolt backstage.

But the crowd's fury doesn't let up.

**Audience Member I:** They're in on it together!

**Audience Member I:** Frauds! Pretending to be Thalassa for the money! They've disgraced her name!

But among the anger, there are murmurs of doubt.

**Audience Member II:** Thalassa is a Merfolk.

**Audience Member II:** Could that be true?

Backstage

**Narcissus:** \*panting\*

**Narcissus:** Thalassa, are you alright?

She turns to check on her, heart pounding like a drum. This has gotten way out of hand.

**Thalassa:** I'm fine. Thank you, Narcissus.

**Thalassa:** I have to say their reaction is what I expected.

**Narcissus:** Then why did you ...

Her eyes go wide.

**Policeman I:** They're in here!

Before she can get an answer or even catch her breath, Narcissus starts running again.

**Narcissus:** Come on, Thalassa! Let's go!

**Policeman II:** Don't let them get away!

Feet never made for land pound against the ground.

Town

But she keeps moving, one clumsy step at a time, stumbling through these streets far from home.

**Thalassa:** \*pant\* ... Same ...

**Thalassa:** Same goes for Merfolk, haha.

**Narcissus:** Hahahaha ...

The two "fish" catch each other's eyes and laugh as they race on.

**Narcissus:** Ah! Hermes!

Her friends from Ephaistonos arrive like a break in the clouds.

**Hermes:** Narcissus, Thalassa, get in!
