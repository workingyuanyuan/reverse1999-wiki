## Story

**Narcissus:** Ahhhhh!!!

Narcissus screams at the top of her lungs.

Then, a light comes on.

A Secluded Residence

She hasn't fallen into some terrifying underground dungeon.

She's fallen into a bedroom. Canvases of every size line the walls, neat and orderly, and the air carries a faint smell of paint.

**Narcissus:** Thalassa?

Her voice trembles as she whispers the name. It's almost a plea for help, as if "yes" is the only answer she can bear to hear.

The light in the room reveals both the furnishings and the room's owner.

**Thalassa:** Hello, Narcissus.

The voice is strange, as if it carries the echo of the deep sea.

Standing before her, dressed simply and neatly, is a fish-headed lady.

**Narcissus:** Ah! How do you know my name?

**Thalassa:** I've known you for a long time, Ms. Galene.

Hearing that, the Nymph recoils another two or three steps.

**Narcissus:** Y-You even know my real name?

**Narcissus:** Could it be your arcane skill? Seeing a person's every secret?

Narcissus is so frightened she nearly shrinks to half her size.

**Thalassa:** When you were born, Doris wrote to me with the news and the name she'd chosen for you.

**Thalassa:** Every Spring Nymph receives the same kind of blessing at birth. Your name, Galene, speaks into being a calm and peaceful life.

**Thalassa:** But Doris had a feeling you'd take a name completely different from your given one.

**Thalassa:** You'd only just been born. Doris was ahead of her time in many ways, like a prophet.

The fish-headed lady gives Narcissus a soft smile as she recounts the past.

Yet it takes the Nymph a full five minutes to take in her words.

**Narcissus:** You and Doris knew each other long ago before I was born?

**Narcissus:** Then you must know Doris's story from before she came to Ephaistonos, right?

**Narcissus:** Thalassa, please! I need to know!

Narcissus almost scrambles to Thalassa's knees, her hands clasped together in a desperate plea.

**Thalassa:** ...

**Thalassa:** Doris was right. You truly are a special child.

**Thalassa:** Take a seat, Narcissus. I'll tell you everything.

She pats the chair beside her lightly, gesturing for Narcissus to sit.

**Thalassa:** I've waited a long time for this day.

**Thalassa:** I come from a deep-sea fish clan. Territorial wars in ancient times cost us our chance to reach the shallows.

**Thalassa:** In the world above, the only stories people tell of us are terrifying tales of man-eating creatures. It's only natural that they'd distrust creatures that live far below in the darkness.

**Thalassa:** In fact, for the first half of my life, I never once reached the shallows or saw the light.

**Thalassa:** Not that I had any need of it. Our kind's nature and history determined we'd live in the dark.

**Thalassa:** But there are always exceptions.

**Thalassa:** There was a deep-sea fish named Thalassa. She didn't need light to survive, yet she longed for the world above the sea.

Thalassa's gaze drops. Whatever longing and conflict she once carried, she feels nothing but relief now.

**Thalassa:** Why would a deep-sea fish yearn for the world above? There's nothing up there she needs to live.

**Thalassa:** Besides, a longing like that could cost her life. A deep-sea fish can't even withstand the pressure of the shallows, let alone survive in the world above the water.

**Thalassa:** But every clan has an adventurer. A boundary-pusher. Someone determined to leave and chase after something that makes no sense at all.

**Narcissus:** ...

Narcissus is spellbound, a strange light caught in her unblinking eyes.

**Thalassa:** Doris was also one of them.

Thalassa tilts her head slightly, meeting Narcissus's eyes.

**Doris:** Thalassa, the incarnation of the goddess of the sea. A truly lovely name.

**Thalassa:** Those fins ... Are you a Spring Nymph?

That day, the deep-sea Merfolk and the Spring Nymph met in the sea and exchanged names.

Surprise flickers across her new friend's eyes. Few can recognize a Spring Nymph at a glance.

**Thalassa:** Plenty of sunken human ships have parchment books in them. I'm an avid reader.

**Thalassa:** I once read one about the different types of Nymphs. Aren't Spring Nymphs supposed to live in fresh water?

**Doris:** Yes, that's right.

She nods, her body swaying in the sea that was never meant for her kind.

**Doris:** Thalassa, isn't it uncomfortable for you to be so close to the surface?

**Doris:** You belong to the deep sea.

**Thalassa:** Hmm, but I'm curious.

**Doris:** Curious?

**Thalassa:** All I ever see down here is blurry light. What's it like, those "waves that flow like gold beneath the sun"?

**Thalassa:** I've always wanted to know.

**Thalassa:** If I could see the sights those words describe someday, I think I could live with this discomfort.

**Thalassa:** But, truth is, I'm rather timid. Even now, it's still just a thought in my head.

Her voice grows hesitant again.

**Doris:** Hahahaha!

**Doris:** It's a beautiful sight! Just like the words describe, no, more beautiful than that.

She turns amid the tide, swimming with ease.

**Doris:** Thalassa, you really must see it with your own eyes to know what it's truly like.

A Secluded Residence

**Narcissus:** And then?

The Nymph is so excited she nearly climbs onto the chair.

**Thalassa:** Doris gave me a scale that day, when we first met.

**Thalassa:** "The path ahead belongs to you alone."

**Thalassa:** "Stretching wherever you dare to go."

**Thalassa:** "Move forward, and you shall arrive."

**Thalassa:** That's what Doris told me.

**Narcissus:** Wow!

**Thalassa:** It can only be used three times, and the user must know their own heart and exactly what it is they long for in order for it to work.

**Narcissus:** So, so! Doris used it to reach the sea, and you used it to reach the surface!

**Narcissus:** Oh my! What a gloriously magnificent story! A story that belongs to heroes and heroes alone!

Narcissus nearly bursts into dance. She's so giddy.

**Thalassa:** Yes, child, you're right.

**Thalassa:** The Doris I knew gave her body and soul to the sea. There are countless stories of her embracing the waves.

**Thalassa:** She was most radiant when in its waters. And once I finally came to live on the surface myself, I spent my long life traveling the world.

**Thalassa:** Because Doris once told me, "You must see it with your own eyes."

**Thalassa:** In the end, I chose to settle in this city, to keep our story alive.

**Narcissus:** But why did Doris never tell us any of these stories?

**Narcissus:** They're too wonderful to be kept a secret!

**Narcissus:** Maybe it's because everyone who hears it would want to be like Doris, or worse, they'd want to become Doris!

**Thalassa:** Haha, Narcissus.

She offers a warm smile, and something familiar stirs in Narcissus.

**Thalassa:** I can't give you the answer directly. That's something only you can find.

**Thalassa:** But I have faith in you.

**Thalassa:** I only came to know you through my correspondence with Doris. I've waited a long time for this day, for us to finally meet.

**Thalassa:** I've always believed that a longing this strong is fate's own guidance, that the answer is already out there, just waiting to be found.

**Thalassa:** And the day you truly find your answer will come too, just like today did.

**Narcissus:** Alright! When that day comes, I'll tell you and Doris right away.

She's too happy to notice that she's been quietly avoiding a fact.

A truth that could break her in an instant.

**Narcissus:** Oh, uh, Doris is gone.

**Thalassa:** I know.

She reaches out a hand and rests it gently on Narcissus's shoulder.

**Thalassa:** She had a long, wonderful life, didn't she?

**Narcissus:** ...!

Her head jerks up.

Tears rise in her eyes like tiny oceans of her own.

**Narcissus:** Y-Yes.

Thalassa smooths Narcissus's hair.

**Thalassa:** Narcissus, let me tell you another secret.

**Thalassa:** Tomorrow, at the theater downtown, I'll walk out on that stage.

**Thalassa:** And I'll tell the world that I am the artist behind those works.
