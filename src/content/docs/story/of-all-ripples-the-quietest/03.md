## Story

Bar Helicon

**Hermes:** So, you two went swimming in the sea together?

But aren't Nymphs born knowing how to swim? They're born in water, right?

**Themis:** They can only survive in fresh water. The waters suited to them are limited. The sea has never been their territory.

**Hermes:** Hmm.

The messenger tugs at the brim of his hat, his eyes gleaming with inspiration at his friend's adventurousness.

**Apollo:** Still, there's no clear record of whether Nymphs who live in fresh water are able to enter the sea at all.

**Apollo:** Ms. Doris and Narcissus might be the two exceptions.

**Narcissus:** If you'd seen Doris in the sea, you wouldn't have been able to tell what kind of Nymph she was.

**Narcissus:** The way she looked ...

**Narcissus:** It was like she had belonged there since birth.

The memory pulls her back from her grief, however briefly.

Nymph Habitat, Ephaistonos

**Spring Nymph I:** Narcissus, you stink!

**Narcissus:** What? Me?

She sniffs herself, but all she smells is salty seawater.

**Narcissus:** I-I fell into the sea, and ...

Her companion shrieks so loudly that she never gets a chance to finish the story.

**Spring Nymph I:** What?! You fell into the sea?!

**Spring Nymph I:** You ... you ...! Are you okay?

**Spring Nymph I:** Spring Nymphs can't touch the sea! If we do, we'll ... we'll ...

**Spring Nymph I:** Burn to ash! Shatter into a million pieces!

**Narcissus:** ...

**Spring Nymph I:** Narcissus! Are you still alive? Or, are you a ghost coming to haunt us?

The Nymph stumbles back and bolts in panic.

**Narcissus:** I ... I'm not dead.

The "ghost" prods at her own face. The whole thing is so strange even she can hardly believe it.

She stares down blankly at her webbed hands and pinches one without thinking.

**Narcissus:** Ouch!

**Narcissus:** It wasn't a dream.

**Narcissus:** It wasn't a dream! I can ... I can swim in the sea!

A rush of joy sweeps over her. She hops up and down, again and again, then staggers forward.

Lakeside, Ephaistonos

**Narcissus:** Doris!

She leaps into the Coryphaeus's arms like a flying fish.

**Narcissus:** We were swimming in the sea together! It was all real! It's real, it's real, right?

The youngest in the clan, eyes bright with excitement, is desperate for something to prove this dream true.

**Doris:** Shh.

The elder rests her webbed hand gently over Narcissus's mouth with the softness of a falling leaf.

**Doris:** Narcissus, let's keep this our little secret for now, alright?

**Narcissus:** But why?

**Narcissus:** We should tell everyone! Maybe we could all go to the sea together!

**Narcissus:** Then they'll stop-stop calling me the most reckless Nymph in the clan!

Her brow furrows, caught somewhere between anger and hurt.

**Doris:** Narcissus, not everyone longs for the life you do.

**Doris:** They have the right to remain in their peaceful world to enjoy the calm before the storm.

**Doris:** And right now, I simply hope that you become a seed.

The elder runs her fingers through Narcissus's hair as she speaks.

**Narcissus:** A seed? Sure! But what do I have to do?

**Narcissus:** Just tell me, Doris! I feel like I can do anything right now!

Doris smiles.

**Doris:** More often than not, a bird that carries a seed has no clue what kind of plant it will grow into.

**Doris:** So, Narcissus, all you need to do is be yourself.

**Doris:** The real you.

**Narcissus:** Myself ...

Beach, Ephaistonos

**Games Host:** Our next competitor is Narcissus!

**Games Host:** This one's special. The first Nymph ever to stand on this stage!

The host sweeps an arm toward the crowd in an attempt to hype them up.

**Games Host:** What a historic achievement! Narcissus's supporters, let me hear you!

**Hermes:** Narcissus, my friend! You can do this! We're all rooting for you!

Only one voice rings out from the crowd, so loud you'd think this tiny Nymph had already claimed the podium.

**Themis:** ...

**Apollo:** Enjoy the match, dear Narcissus!

Her two more reserved friends wave to her from a distance.

Not a single Nymph sits in the crowd, but she has three brand-new friends instead.

**Narcissus:** I shall!

She pumps her fist, proclaiming a victory she hasn't yet won.

**Narcissus:** I'll beat Hedo—

**Narcissus:** Aaahhh!!

**Hermes:** Dear Narcissus, what keeps you chasing after the "tyrant" Hedone, even after losing to her again and again?

He mimes holding a microphone, as if interviewing a defeated hero.

**Narcissus:** I ... I ...

She bows her head, hands curled tight.

**Narcissus:** Because ... because I admire her!

As always, she fails to hide her true feelings.

**Narcissus:** When I was little, I used to sneak away from the lake to look at the fresco on the shore ... the stories about Hedone.

**Narcissus:** I envy her wealth, her adventures. She's the only one on the entire island with such a history—a true one of a kind.

**Hermes:** Whoa.

**Hermes:** Looks like you've already won first place in Ephaistonos's first "Bare Your Heart" competition.

**Hermes:** And for that, she deserves a prize, dear Themis.

He snaps his fingers.

**Themis:** Of course. As long as you're willing to pay to have a solid-gold laurel made for her.

**Hermes:** Well, uh, I just bought a new motorcycle—wiped my savings clear out. So ...

**Narcissus:** Then start saving again! I'll come claim my prize upon my return!

Narcissus never turns down a reward, even one offered as a joke.

**Hermes:** I'd better start upping my letter deliveries, then.

**Hermes:** Wait, where are you going?

**Apollo:** ...

Apollo lets out a long sigh.

**Narcissus:** I'm leaving the island. I'm going to find a treasure that'll ensure I win!

She hops down from the barstool and claps both hands against her cheeks.

**Narcissus:** That's what I need to do now. That's what I promised Doris!

**Apollo:** Narcissus, I'm fairly certain those weren't Ms. Doris's exact words.

**Narcissus:** It's my decision and the oracle's guidance, isn't it?

**Apollo:** ...

For once, the prophet seems at a loss. He merely rubs his forehead.

**Themis:** Apollo, what did you tell her?

She shoots him a sharp look.

**Hermes:** Narcissus, do you even know how to buy a ticket out of here? The outside world's far too complicated! Why don't you slow down and make a plan first?

**Hermes:** You should at least learn to read a map!

**Narcissus:** I'll learn as I go, alright? Now, you three just stay on the island and await my good news!

**Hermes:** That's a long trip to make alone! Shouldn't you bring a friend or two?

He glances at the other two, signaling them to come up with something.

**Narcissus:** No!

**Narcissus:** Hermes! I have a feeling this trip will be unlike any other before it. I must do it alone!

**Hermes:** What are you talking about?! You've never even traveled off the island!

**Narcissus:** No time to lose! I've got to get ready!

And with that, she bounds off out of the bar.

**Hermes:** So you divined an oracle for Narcissus saying that she's guaranteed to defeat Hedone in the next competition?

Hermes grabs the brim of his cap, the wings on it drooping.

**Apollo:** No, an oracle cannot promise anyone absolute victory. I would never divine such a thing.

**Hermes:** But still, it's sent a Nymph—who can't even leave fresh water, by the way—traveling the world, Apollo!

**Themis:** There's no need to get so worked up about it. Narcissus is older than the three of us combined.

**Themis:** How do you know her courage and experience won't be enough to get her through it alone?

**Hermes:** Themis, you know Nymphs don't grow at the same pace we do. And Narcissus is growing the slowest of them all!

Silence follows.

Hermes's words are true.

**Apollo:** Grief and despair are enough to cause a Nymph to waste away.

**Apollo:** I had to give Narcissus some hope.

**Apollo:** But believe me, my friends, I would never offer false hope.

**Apollo:** Narcissus will have a happy ending. And not only because the oracle suggested it.

**Apollo:** Doris must have told her something that enlightened her. We just don't know what.

**Apollo:** It was she who brought about this change in Narcissus.
