## Story

Cropfield

**Narcissus:** How can I find it?

**Narcissus:** The oracle never pointed me in any specific direction.

For the first time ever, she's standing on land beyond the island.

But any sense of her destined fate has failed to show itself. Nothing is going as smoothly as she thought.

**Narcissus:** Doris always taught me that you must observe and experience before you can reap rewards!

**Narcissus:** Yet she never told me where my journey leads.

**Narcissus:** The world beyond the island is just too vast.

**Narcissus:** Right, why don't I start by marking down all the places I've been so I can rule them out?

**Narcissus:** Hmm, that's a lot of places left. This is going to take forever.

Her spirits sink a little.

A wind blows, tearing the self-made map straight from her hands.

**Narcissus:** Huh?!

**Narcissus:** My map!

The wind curls, sending her map fluttering like a flag.

Then another hand catches it.

**???:** Hey there, lady.

**???:** We're lost. Could we borrow your map?

**???:** We're total novices when it comes to foreign travel! How I wish there were someone kind and experienced to show us around.

The messenger leans against his motorbike. In the convertible beside him sit the island's judge and prophet.

**Narcissus:** Hermes, what are you doing here?

**Narcissus:** And Themis and Apollo, too. But surely you can't leave Ephaistonos. What about the rituals and competitions?

Narcissus looks totally shocked, but Themis just rests against the window frame, calm as ever.

**Themis:** Ephaistonos doesn't restrict its residents from coming and going. And, given the schedule lately ...

**Themis:** It is time I had a little vacation.

She speaks with the same gravity as if she were still standing in the arena, calmly announcing a fair result.

**Apollo:** I couldn't agree more. This is a journey ordained by the fates.

Through the window, Apollo offers a genuine smile.

**Narcissus:** No, no, no, no! This isn't what I had in mind!

**Narcissus:** I need to finish my journey alone. If I'm with all of you, what's the difference between this and my life on Ephaistonos?!

She snatches her map back from Hermes and takes several quick steps back, kicking up sand as she goes.

**Hermes:** But, Narcissus, this isn't Ephaistonos.

**Hermes:** Even with us here, this journey is sure to be a success!

**Hermes:** In all these years, you're the first Nymph born on Ephaistonos to leave the island, aren't you?

**Narcissus:** But ...

**Themis:** Narcissus.

The car door opens, and Themis steps out.

**Themis:** Your goal is to find that special item as quickly as possible. The thing that matters to you most.

**Themis:** But you won't get very far if you do it alone.

Her steady gaze falls on the unmarked map in the Nymph's hands.

The world is still waiting to be explored.

**Themis:** Since Apollo has already given you an oracle, he should take responsibility and offer you more guidance.

**Apollo:** I can't give anything precise ...

**Hermes:** Right! Absolutely right! Come on, Narcissus, you know we can't ignore the judge's ruling!

He throws up his hands, cutting off the prophet before he can continue.

**Narcissus:** Well, fine, then.

She lowers her head, still thrown by the sudden change to her plans.

She keeps the map clutched tight in her webbed hands, the wind still tugging at its corner.

**Narcissus:** From that day on, I started marking my map little by little.

**Narcissus:** A lot happened along the way, but ...

**Narcissus:** I still hadn't found the treasure or anything close to a clue.

Town

**Narcissus:** We're moving much too slowly!

**Narcissus:** We've only been to a few places. Look how much more there is left on the map!

**Narcissus:** At this rate, we'll never get back to Ephaistonos!

**Narcissus:** And this whole time, Hedone's been winning one competition after the other!

She pauses and frowns. There's a doodle on the corner of her map.

**Narcissus:** Hey, Hermes! You can't just draw on my map!

Hermes is browsing souvenirs at a stall nearby, turning a little statue in his hand.

**Hermes:** Narcissus, doesn't this look like you?

**Apollo:** Hmm? This is ...

**Apollo:** This is the first thing we've seen outside Ephaistonos that has anything to do with Nymphs.

**Apollo:** I'd say this is a work of art in its own right.

**Narcissus:** Oh?

The hawker notices their interest and jumps right into the conversation.

**Hawker:** These are Thalassa's works! The greatest artist in the city!

**Hawker:** Remarkable detail, wouldn't you say? You can see every fin on the Nymphs and every scale on the Merfolk.

**Hawker:** That's the main focus of Thalassa's work—Nymphs and Merfolk. Together, they've been through all sorts of great adventures that transcend species, time, and space.

**Hawker:** But judging by the dates on these pieces, Thalassa must be more than one person. A lot of people think they're a family or some kind of generational artist collective.

He is clearly more interested in sharing his awe of Thalassa than in selling his product.

**Narcissus:** These Nymphs ...

**Hawker:** You must be one of Thalassa's followers too, am I right?

**Hawker:** Many wear decorative fins like yours to show their support for her.

Narcissus, "decorative fins" and all, holds the statue the messenger hands her and takes a deep breath. Her face is pale.

**Narcissus:** They ... they're all ...

**Narcissus:** They're all Doris!

**Narcissus:** I'm not wrong! That has to be Doris.

**Narcissus:** That's exactly how she looked! Her exact expression!

**Themis:** If that's Doris, then what of the Merfolk?

**Themis:** Narcissus, did Doris ever tell you anything about her life before Ephaistonos?

Themis's balanced nature prompts her to work through the facts.

**Narcissus:** No, she never told me anything.

**Narcissus:** I begged her, over and over, but she'd only ever tell me about the journey to Ephaistonos.

**Narcissus:** When and where Doris was born, what kind of life she lived, what adventures she'd been through ...

**Narcissus:** I thought I'd never get the chance to know any of it!

Getting this close has sent Narcissus's heart racing and her ear fins standing straight.

**Narcissus:** But none of this makes sense! If Spring Nymphs can only live in fresh water—

**Narcissus:** Then how did my ancestors and Doris cross the sea to reach Ephaistonos?

**Narcissus:** If things really are as we see and understand them, then how, in this place so far from Ephaistonos ...

**Narcissus:** ... could a figure of Doris be here at all? How could she be part of the stories here?

Her voice is shaking.

**Themis:** Narcissus.

Themis presses her cool palm to Narcissus's forehead. She's gotten so worked up she's practically steaming from the ears.

**Themis:** The answer may be right before you.

**Themis:** Right now, you need to calm your mind if you hope to find it faster.

**Narcissus:** You're right ... \*gasp\* ... Themis.

**Narcissus:** Now—

She looks up at the crowded street. Posters for art exhibitions are plastered all over the city.

**Narcissus:** I need to find the one who created these stories. I need to find Thalassa.

**Citizen I:** Thalassa? They're a favorite of the family!

**Citizen I:** You're in luck, actually. Tomorrow is the Thalassa Art Festival—only happens once every five years. There'll be performances at the theater downtown.

**Citizen I:** As you can see, the whole city has become Thalassa's stage.

**Narcissus:** Sir, do you know how I could meet them in person?

**Citizen I:** Of course. All you have to do is take a seat in the audience and you'll get a heart-to-heart of your own.

**Narcissus:** No! That's not what I meant.

**Citizen II:** You want to meet Thalassa?

**Citizen II:** Haha, good luck with that! They haven't shown their face in centuries.

**Citizen II:** But they must be as devout and beautiful as their work—they have to be.

**Citizen III:** Oh, maybe you should ask the lady who lives at the Horton Apartments.

**Citizen III:** Thalassa's new work is always released through her. She rarely leaves her home and is every bit as private as Thalassa.

**Citizen III:** A lot of people guess Thalassa isn't even in this city, maybe not even in the country.

**Citizen III:** Makes sense to me. If they were here, why wouldn't they join the festival with the rest of us?

**Narcissus:** This apartment ... this is where Thalassa's spokesperson lives.

After all the twists and turns, every clue has led here. She stops at the door.

No one answers. No one greets her. She steps into the dead quiet.

**Hermes:** Narcissus, are you sure this is the place?

**Hermes:** There could be traps or something in here.

**Narcissus:** I know this is it. I asked all around, and they all said—

Her voice halts.

Finding nothing but air beneath her foot, she drops like a stone.

**Narcissus:** ARRGGHHH!!!
