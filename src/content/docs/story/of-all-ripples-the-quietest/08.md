## Story

Lakeside, Ephaistonos

**Narcissus:** Doris, I've always felt like my life wasn't supposed to be like this.

The youngest Nymph in the clan has lost her first competition. Her head hangs low, her ear fins drooping like wilted petals.

**Doris:** And what kind of life is it that you want?

**Narcissus:** I think ... I think ...

She frowns, struggling to find something solid to hold onto.

**Narcissus:** I want to live like Hedone!

**Narcissus:** There's a fresco on the island showing her war against Typhon. People still sing about it.

**Narcissus:** She's amazing! Surrounded by treasure, but still shining brighter than any of it!

**Narcissus:** If only I could be like her! I just don't know how.

**Narcissus:** Doris, do you know how? Could you tell me?

She looks up, hoping the wisest elder in the clan can give her an answer.

**Doris:** Hedone truly is someone worth learning from.

The elder strokes Narcissus's hair softly.

**Doris:** But Narcissus—or should I say, our little Galene—is no less impressive.

Hearing her real name, the little Nymph beams with pride. But the confusion doesn't fully fade.

She still wants a real answer.

**Narcissus:** Doris, do you mean—

**Narcissus:** If I never give up on besting Hedone, someday I'll win?

**Narcissus:** I'll defeat her, right? Right?

She tugs at the elder's arm.

**Doris:** Is that what you want to do, Galene?

**Narcissus:** I-I want to!

**Narcissus:** I've always wanted to! Doris, you have to come watch when it happens!

**Narcissus:** Watch as I ascend to the top step of the podium and hold the champion's reward high above my head!

**Narcissus:** My life will be completely different by then!

**Doris:** Narcissus.

Narcissus falls quiet. It is only a soft, gentle call, but something about it leaves her with a strange feeling.

**Doris:** I can feel my time drawing near.

**Narcissus:** W-What do you mean?

Her face goes pale in an instant.

**Doris:** The end of my life.

**Narcissus:** Doris, are you saying ... are you saying you're going to ...

She can't bring herself to say the word.

**Doris:** Die.

Her beloved Coryphaeus says that word as if it were any other.

**Narcissus:** No! No, no, no, no!

She claps her hands over her ears in fright, crushing her fins against her palms.

**Narcissus:** No ...

But Doris never lies.

And Narcissus's tears spill forth.

**Narcissus:** You can't. Y-You can't let that happen, Doris.

**Doris:** You needn't be frightened, dear.

**Doris:** And no matter how long a Nymph's life may be, death comes for us all eventually.

**Narcissus:** B-But I don't want you to die.

She can only cry, helpless and heartbroken, until Doris takes her hand.

**Doris:** It's alright.

**Doris:** Death is but the final ripple in life's waters, perhaps the calmest of all.

**Doris:** And while those waters yet stir, Narcissus, you should ...

Cropfield

**Narcissus:** With this necklace, I'll be utterly unstoppable!

**Hermes:** So you've decided how you'll use it, then?

**Narcissus:** Hmm, no, not yet!

**Narcissus:** But with the necklace, the oracle, and the last thing Doris told me—

She tips her head back and looks up at the sky.

**Narcissus:** All of it has made me realize something.

**Hermes:** Oh, really? A revelation on the meaning of life? I'd be delighted to hear it!

**Narcissus:** Remember the story Doris told me? Long ago, on the way to Ephaistonos, the Spring Nymphs saved a prophet from pirates.

**Narcissus:** I've been thinking: what if that prophet was Doris?

**Hermes:** You mean she was kidnapped by pirates?

**Narcissus:** No, no, I mean there was never any prophet at all!

**Narcissus:** The whole time, there was only one Spring Nymph searching for a way through the waves. No stranger ever came to guide her.

**Narcissus:** She was the first of us to leap into the sea, and in doing so, she became her own prophet.

**Narcissus:** But that's just my guess! Doris always said they were only stories. It's just, I've always been convinced they actually happened.

**Narcissus:** And there's no way to prove it now.

She unfolds the map in her hands and notices some changes on it.

All the places they've traveled to together are there, on the page before her.

**Narcissus:** Still, my journey's only just begun.

She runs her fingers over the paper.

**Narcissus:** I still have plenty of time to think about what the rest of Doris's journey was like.

**Narcissus:** Of course, priority number one is to get back to Ephaistonos and take down Hedone!

**Hermes:** Hahahaha!

**Hermes:** I'm sure you will! What about once you beat her? What's next?

**Hermes:** Take on some responsibilities around the island? Share in Lady Hedone's riches?

**Hermes:** I'm sure she won't be stingy with the reward.

The messenger adds another tempting stroke to the Nymph's picture of the future.

**Narcissus:** Hmm, I don't know.

**Narcissus:** I haven't thought that far ahead!

**Narcissus:** Right now, all I need is this win. But believe you me, this is only the beginning!

The Nymph tilts her head back and answers into the wind.

**Apollo:** Narcissus.

The car keeps pace with the motorbike.

**Apollo:** You should know that necklace you found is incredibly valuable. It is a true one of a kind.

**Narcissus:** You betcha!

**Apollo:** I only wish to remind you that our esteemed island ruler happens to be a collector of excellent taste.

**Themis:** ...

Themis throws Apollo a meaningful glance from behind the wheel.

**Hermes:** True, true! You'd better keep it well hidden!

**Hermes:** Hedone has a real nose for treasure.

**Narcissus:** Don't worry! I'll keep it around my neck! It's not going anywhere!

The passing wind carries her words off toward the horizon.

In that moment, her heart brims with anticipation, a lightness unlike anything she's ever known.

**Narcissus:** Before the final ripple comes ...

**Narcissus:** I'll stir up as many great waves as I can!
