## Story

A Secluded Residence

The secluded underground residence has never hosted so many guests before.

**Thalassa:** I'm sorry for dragging you all into this.

Even after just running for her life, the Merfolk remains gentle and composed. She rubs her fish head, a little embarrassed.

**Narcissus:** Don't be sorry, Thalassa! We stole the show today!

The Nymph still hasn't caught her breath, but whatever panic she felt while running is completely gone.

**Narcissus:** That was my first time speaking on a stage that big!

**Narcissus:** It felt amazing!!!

Her eyes are shining, as if the chaos and the chase were nothing but harmless play.

**Hermes:** Haha! And here I thought the show was already incredible on its own! You two really surprised me.

**Apollo:** I suppose fate does tend to enjoy a little drama.

He gives a resigned smile.

**Narcissus:** But I still don't understand! Why was everyone so awful to Thalassa?! They didn't seem like bad people.

**Narcissus:** I only found her in the first place because everyone pointed the way. Every clue and message of theirs showed how much they admired Thalassa.

Unable to make sense of it, she cups her face, looking troubled.

**Narcissus:** Did something I said confuse them? Did I make them misunderstand her?

**Narcissus:** I can't accept that. I have to go explain this to them properly!

She springs to her feet and makes for the door.

**Themis:** Don't, Narcissus.

Themis catches her by the arm, stopping her just in time.

**Narcissus:** I understand, but ...

**Thalassa:** Thank you, Narcissus.

**Thalassa:** You didn't cause any misunderstanding. And they aren't bad people.

The Merfolk's round eyes catch the light. Her voice is calm, steady enough to settle the nerves in the room.

**Thalassa:** It's just for a lot of people; when they run into something they've never seen before, their first instinct is to push it away.

**Thalassa:** Because they can't tell whether the unknown is safe or dangerous.

**Narcissus:** Hmm? Is that so?

Her brow furrows slightly as she works through Thalassa's words.

**Narcissus:** There are plenty of things that scare me, too. But if it's something new, I'm always more curious about it.

Thalassa smiles.

**Thalassa:** Narcissus, I know you've never once feared the unknown. That's what makes you who you are.

**Thalassa:** The one and only.

**Narcissus:** Naturally!

She accepts the precious compliment with delight.

**Thalassa:** But I don't think that comes out of nowhere, Narcissus.

**Thalassa:** There's still something about Spring Nymphs I haven't told you yet.

On the Sea

**Doris:** Thalassa, there's something I want to tell you.

**Thalassa:** Oh?

It's the Merfolk's first time in the world above the sea. Still overwhelmed by curiosity, she answers in a daze.

In the light and wind of this new world, everything is too bright, almost blinding. It isn't exactly comfortable.

But she also feels incredibly free.

**Doris:** Spring Nymphs are born fragile. Our fins can't take too much force, especially against waves.

**Doris:** That's why everyone assumes we are only able to live in calm water.

**Doris:** I used to think so too, until I read about an ancient migration on a stone tablet back home.

**Doris:** When disaster struck the quiet forests and lakes, the Spring Nymphs had no choice but to flee.

**Doris:** Someone had to be the first to leap into the sea and lead the others out.

**Doris:** Did she conquer her fear, or did some part of her already want to go? The tablet doesn't say.

**Doris:** But it gave me an idea. Maybe Spring Nymphs aren't as fragile as everyone thinks. Maybe we just need a seed.

**Thalassa:** A seed ...

**Thalassa:** Your people have never lacked the courage to leap into the sea.

**Narcissus:** ...

**Thalassa:** Narcissus.

**Thalassa:** This necklace should be yours.

**Narcissus:** M-Mine?!

Her webbed hand trembles as she touches it. It's glowing with a strange light.

**Thalassa:** I believe Doris told you something, something that brought you here to meet me.

**Narcissus:** Yes, she did.

**Thalassa:** I also believe Doris didn't give you much to go on. So you must have struggled a great deal to get here.

She gently raises a hand and fastens the necklace around the dazed Nymph's neck.

**Thalassa:** Please forgive her. Doris always had her way of doing things.

**Narcissus:** No, I've never blamed Doris.

Her webbed hand closes around the necklace on her chest, cold and hard against her palm.

**Narcissus:** I just ... I just miss her. But I can never talk to her again.

**Narcissus:** I wanted Doris to see how much I'd changed, to see me win.

**Narcissus:** But none of that will ever happen now.

This time, she forces herself to hold back her tears.

**Narcissus:** Thalassa, you and Doris both told me that I just have to be myself.

**Narcissus:** Even though I still don't know exactly how to do that, or what it even means to be myself!

**Narcissus:** But I'll find the answer. Doris wouldn't want me to give up!

**Hermes:** And you've still got us, remember!

Her friends from Ephaistonos immediately voice their support.

**Narcissus:** That's right. Us, and Thalassa! Right?

**Thalassa:** Of course, Narcissus.

**Thalassa:** I know you'll make this last use of the necklace count and make your dream come true.

**Narcissus:** My dream ...

She lifts the scale in her hands, her eyes shining bright.

**Narcissus:** The treasure that will aid in my victory!

Cropfield

**Narcissus:** Apollo! Haha!!

**Narcissus:** Your oracle was right after all!

**Narcissus:** I barely had any clues to start with, but I still found the treasure in the end!

**Narcissus:** Let's head back to Ephaistonos! I just can't wait to defeat Hedone!

The Nymph, with her shining necklace and newfound confidence, grabs her helmet.

**Hermes:** I thought ...

**Apollo:** What?

**Hermes:** I guess I'd hoped that, after all that, Narcissus would've walked away with some kind of deep lesson about herself.

**Hermes:** Or at the very least, she'd stop being so obsessed with winning.

He pulls his hat lower and quietly watches Narcissus as she bounces with excitement nearby.

**Apollo:** Well, isn't that a revelation? Even our ever-practical Hermes has come to care about "deep lessons."

He pushes up his glasses as he delivers the joke completely straight-faced.

**Hermes:** Defeating Hedone is incredibly difficult. And now, she believes in that oracle more than ever.

**Themis:** I don't think Narcissus will ever stop in the face of an insurmountable challenge.

**Themis:** That's just who she is.

**Themis:** But we are all responsible for this, especially you, Apollo.

**Themis:** I must say, I'm looking forward to seeing how you deal with it.

Under the judge's icy stare, Apollo has no choice but to look away.

**Narcissus:** Aren't you ready to leave yet? Time is of the essence, people!

Narcissus gives them an impatient wave.

Apollo is happy to be given a way out of Themis's probing.

**Apollo:** All I can say is ...

**Apollo:** Narcissus's story is far from over.
