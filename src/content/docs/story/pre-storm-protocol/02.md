## Story

**Enigma:** So the whole of Laplace can't figure out how to fix this ridiculous box problem once and for all?

**Medicine Pocket:** Quit your whining, Adler. Got a letter for you.

**Enigma:** For me? This just looks like a piece of regular Laplace calculation paper. What makes you think it's for me?

**Medicine Pocket:** One, it was shoved into YOUR door. Two, there's a big "READ IT" written on it in black ink.

Medicine Pocket rolls their eyes at having to yet again spell things out.

**Medicine Pocket:** Honestly, who does something like that unless it's some kind of complaint?

Enigma unfolds what looks like a piece of regular Laplace calculation paper, but what's on it is clearly far from ordinary.

**Enigma:** No name on it. They're proposing construction of some ... tower. A tower to standardize all linguistic systems inside the toybox.

**Hissabeth:** Sounds like something from a detective novel. Hey, Adler—sorry for eavesdropping. But besides us, who else knows about the missing core?

**Enigma:** Perfect. Our security protocols might as well be town crier announcements.

**Medicine Pocket:** It's gotta be Institutum Lorentz. They must've sniffed out info on our little "arcane accident" somehow.

**Enigma:** As suspicious an institution as they are, their idea is theoretically feasible. Creating a unified language system, no matter what it is, would solve all our problems.

**Ulrich:** But what would Lorentz gain by helping us? Ms. Marcus, are you able to glean any information from this note?

Enigma passes it to her.

Marcus looks down at the small piece of paper. Normally, words are easy for her to gather information from, but now ...

**Marcus:** Hmm ...

Her brow furrows.

**Marcus:** The handwriting is very precise, almost like a machine—typical of a perfectionist, or someone who likes order ...

**Marcus:** Yet the uneven pressure almost suggests some carelessness, which, well, contradicts my initial assessment ...

**Marcus:** The writing's purpose seems to be ... purposeless? Like they're just random ink marks ... That can't be correct. I ... I can't make sense of this ...

**Enigma:** Clearly, they're suffering from the same language disorder. Either they're rushing to fix it, or—

**Medicine Pocket:** Whenever something even slightly connects to the "Storm," they show up in swarms like Critters. I guess even a simulated version counts to them, especially now that it's obviously showing anomalies.

**Enigma:** Regardless, our task now is to build a massive tower inside that box to somehow get everyone to speak the same language. Amazing.

It seems another trip into the toybox will be necessary. At least, this time, they know exactly what needs to be done.​

**Ulrich:** I'll be sure to offer support from the outside, Researcher Adler.

**Enigma:** The best "support" you can offer us now is to get us all hard hats, since we'll all be in a construction zone soon.

**Enigma:** Oh, and make sure to get an extra-extra-large one for yourself to fit over that fish tank of yours.
