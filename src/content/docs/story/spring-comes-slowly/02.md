## Story

White Horse School, ???

**???:** Heron?

**???:** Heron!

**Paper Heron:** Finch? I thought you were due to be back tomorrow?

**Pearl Finch:** So, what did you do with yourself while I was away?

**Paper Heron:** The only thing there is to do: recite texts, morning and evening, over and over.

**Pearl Finch:** And Master sighing in the courtyard has nothing to do with you?

**Paper Heron:** Well, he asked me a question, and I answered honestly.

Pearl Finch lets out a resigned, knowing sigh.

**Pearl Finch:** "Answered honestly." And what might that answer have been, exactly?

**Paper Heron:** Yesterday, before the morning class, Master went into his long lecture as always, and then asked each of us about our plans for the future.

**Paper Heron:** Brother Liǎng's aspirations are the same as always. He dreams of mastering the art of the blade, wants to carry the White Horse School's name far and wide.

**Paper Heron:** You can guess what Master said to that, "Our school's sword is not a tool for violence."

**Paper Heron:** Not a bad impression, eh?

**Pearl Finch:** Well, you would know how to do it. You are the one who puts that look on his face most often.

**Paper Heron:** Then Brother Sān stepped forward and said all who practice Daoism ought not to disrupt the natural flow of things but rather tend to their own minds—the very idea passed down from Ancestor Lǚ. You know the words: "When one's mind is tranquil and free of distracting thoughts, all things between heaven and earth will become one with it."

**Paper Heron:** Master got so angry he did that thing where he paced back and forth with his hands clasped behind his back.

**Pearl Finch:** Oh, what am I to do with you all?

**Paper Heron:** That's exactly what Master said!

Paper Heron clears her throat and puts her hands behind her back.

**Paper Heron:** "Ahem. Your honesty is commendable. However, you have chosen narrow paths, ones lacking true vision or growth. Pearl Finch, your senior sister, however, possesses a more expansive vision. You would do well to look to her as your role model."

**Paper Heron:** I was so happy for you, hearing such praise, that he caught me off guard when he suddenly asked me to share my own desires!

**Pearl Finch:** You told him you want to go down the mountain and sell mung bean cakes, didn't you?

**Paper Heron:** Yes, I ... Wait, how did you know that?

**Pearl Finch:** Brother Liǎng greeted me with the news before I even stepped through the gate.

Paper Heron's stern senior finally smiles.

**Pearl Finch:** If I recall, it was Taosu last time. Why the turn to mung bean cakes?

**Paper Heron:** Well, Taosu are much too tricky to make. I'd never get the crust crisp enough. Mung bean cakes are far easier.

**Pearl Finch:** Clearly you've thought this through.

**Paper Heron:** Of course I have. If spiritual development demands our full focus, then I say daily life does too.

**Paper Heron:** Master teaches that the Way is in everything. Brother Liǎng seeks it in the sword, Brother Sān in the mind. As for me, I'll look for it in my cakes. Perhaps we'll each find our way.

**Pearl Finch:** No wonder Master is upset. One fights, one studies, and one dreams of running a cake shop.

**Pearl Finch:** The White Horse School has soldiers, scholars, and even wanderers. Yet none will answer its true calling. None will take up the duty of subduing Lǎnzhū.

**Paper Heron:** That's not true. We still have you.

**Pearl Finch:** Me?

**Paper Heron:** We all know Master favors you. And you're the one who's been seeing to the school's affairs. It's only right that you inherit its legacy.

**Pearl Finch:** Me? I ... I don't think so.

**Pearl Finch:** Keeping Lǎnzhū subdued is our shared duty. A duty we all accept without hesitation. But, when the day is done, we each have different gifts.

She shakes her head and smiles, her shoulders dropping as she decides not to finish her thought.

**Pearl Finch:** Never mind.

**Pearl Finch:** So, I brought some fresh Taosu from town. I'm not certain they'll be to your liking, but ...

Paper Heron's hand darts out, retrieving a Taosu with masterful precision. She takes a bite, and her expression melts with satisfaction.

**Paper Heron:** Heavens above! This is the taste! Hm, why do mine never taste like this?

**Paper Heron:** Well, this confirms things. For me, the Way does not lie in Taosu. It is only in mung bean cakes that I shall discover my path.

**Pearl Finch:** Perhaps wait until you try some tomorrow before declaring your life's new mission.

**Paper Heron:** Tomorrow? What's so important about ... Wait! Do you plan to take me down the mountain?!

**Pearl Finch:** Not for fun. We've got some business to attend to down there.

**Pearl Finch:** And only if you promise me one thing. If Master asks you what you desire, please stop telling him you want to sell paper cuttings or snacks.

**Paper Heron:** Alright, fine. I shall tell only you.

**Pearl Finch:** He has high hopes for you.

**Pearl Finch:** It hurts him to hear you say it.

A ladle of hot oil splatters over the tofu, releasing the sharp aroma of spring onions and chili that hits the customers immediately.

**Pip:** Ah-Ah-choo! This tofu ... Is it supposed to have this white fuzz?

**Waiter:** Don't worry, miss. This is a Huanzhou specialty, hairy tofu. You won't find it anywhere else!

**Pip:** Oh, wow! It tastes incredible. Heron, you must try ... try some ... Ah-choo!

**Paper Heron:** Hmm, it's alright. Not too different from the tofu I've had before.

**Pip:** You don't like it? Let's get you something else.

**Paper Heron:** Ah, don't you fret. I'm just being picky.

**Pip:** No, no. I promised you a good meal, so a good meal you'll get. Excuse me, may I have—

**Innkeeper:** You want more? But you've already ordered eight dishes.

**Pip:** Oh, heh, would you look at that!

Pip glances at the full table and hesitates.

**Pip:** It seems none of these dishes were to your liking, Heron.

**Pip:** Will you still help me?

Paper Heron can't help but laugh.

**Paper Heron:** You've been generous to me, despite my picky habits. It would be wrong of me not to.

**Paper Heron:** You mentioned something about Lǎnzhū, correct?

**Pip:** We're both Daoists, so you must know the story, the one about the Huanzhou immortal who sealed the beast away.

**Paper Heron:** Yes, I know it. And you want my help dealing with this beast, eh? Hmm, that won't be an easy task.

Pip is startled by her directness.

**Pip:** Of course not! We don't have that kind of strength!

**Pip:** What I wanted to ask was, are you familiar with Qi stagnation?

**Paper Heron:** In a manner of speaking, yes. Though I'm not certain we're thinking of the same thing.

**Pip:** I've only heard my elders mention it. So, please correct me if I'm wrong.

**Paper Heron:** Go ahead. Tell me what they said.

**Pip:** The beast was subdued, but its influence on Huanzhou remains, causing some places to suffer from a stagnation in the flow of Qi.

**Pip:** I was told the danger varies. Sometimes it's little more than a tremor; sometimes it causes mountains to crumble or rivers to change their course.

**Pip:** But no matter the scale, it cannot be ignored. Left to fester, even minor stagnation can spiral into a catastrophe.

**Paper Heron:** Sounds about right to me. Though few possess this knowledge.

**Pip:** Mhmm. Those touched by the immortal's power are rare.

**Paper Heron:** The immortal? Oh, you mean the Daoist who once subdued Lǎnzhū.

**Pip:** Yes. About two hundred years ago, one of my ancestors passed near Zhu County and was fortunate enough to receive guidance from that very same immortal.

**Pip:** Of course, we don't come close to their level of skill. But we can still discern a thing or two through the flow of Qi.

**Paper Heron:** Two hundred years. Has it really been that long?

**Pip:** They say, for an immortal, a century is but a moment.

**Pip:** Anyway, while I was passing through, I noticed signs of stagnant Qi flow nearby, enough to tell me it could be serious.

**Pip:** Would you help me investigate them?

**Pip:** I swear, if you help me, I will come to your aid whenever you need it.

**Pip:** I don't know if I'll be of much use to someone like you, but I'll do whatever I can!

**Paper Heron:** Mmm. Alright.

**Pip:** What? You've agreed? Just like that?

Paper Heron drums her fingers on the table.

**Paper Heron:** Well, you know what they say, "Destiny brings people together."

**Paper Heron:** And it seems, in our own ways, we're both bound to that immortal.

**Paper Heron:** A rarer touch of destiny than most encounter in their lives, wouldn't you say?
