## Story

**Enigma:** ...

**Enigma:** What an absurd experience.

**Enigma:** Using my eyes to gauge the distance, what I saw was a far-off tower and the many difficulties to reach it.

**Enigma:** That, I realize now, was another result of my "observation."

**Enigma:** Yet the moment I stop trying to logically deduce its location, lo and behold—here it is.

He touches the white wall before him, as if to confirm it's really there.

**Enigma:** If arcanists truly live in a world of such unpredictability as this, it's little wonder many think they have mental problems.

**Enigma:** Alright. Since everything's been reset, that thieving raven will likely reappear at the tower with his shiny contraband in tow.

**Enigma:** The only problem is getting inside.

Where a door should be, there is only a wall.

Enigma tilts his head back and estimates the tower's height, weighing whether it's worth the climb.

**Enigma:** There aren't enough footholds. And I'm not certain whether that structure up there is in fact a window.

He pauses, then lowers his voice.

**Enigma:** Open sesame.

The wall doesn't answer, nor does it mock him for trying.

**Enigma:** Arcanists can't just make up incantations, it seems. I thought as much.

**Enigma:** Surely I don't have to delve into my imagination.

He closes his eyes, still facing the stubborn wall.

**Enigma:** Arcane skills. Arcane skills! How on earth do they figure out these things?!

**Enigma:** I should've asked for a copy of A Basic Guide to Using Arcanum before cutting off communications!

**Enigma:** Never mind. The principles ought to be about the same. Focus your mind and let the intensity of your thoughts bore a hole in the wall.

**???:** Not hoping to turn your thoughts into a sledgehammer, are you?

Enigma's eyes snap open. The unchanging darkness is gone.

He follows the sound upward. The raven glides down for a smooth landing.

**Ludwig:** I never expected the ever-logical Adler would have such a wild imagination.

**Enigma:** Enough wasting time. Hand it over.

**Ludwig:** You didn't make all that effort making your way here simply to rob me, did you? Hm, seems Laplace hasn't been so busy these days.

**Enigma:** As you can see, your instincts have interfered with our work.

**Enigma:** If you enjoy collecting shiny things so much, I'll give you this communicator. Take it, or any other thing you want.

**Ludwig:** Empirical judgments, overly confident speculations. Yes, that's the way you think.

**Ludwig:** And why you'll never comprehend the use of arcane skills.

**Enigma:** That's irrelevant.

**Enigma:** Has the simulation been upgraded, or are you being affected by corrupted data?

**Enigma:** The others haven't shared much personal information on "Ludwig." Are you German?

Ludwig just mechanically turns his head and fixes his gaze on him.

**Ludwig:** I understand what you want exactly.

**Ludwig:** You've come to this tower many times, seeking to destroy it and this farce of an experiment.

**Ludwig:** Then, something changed. You became curious—wanted to uncover its secrets. The natural instinct of a researcher.

**Ludwig:** But you turned far too hasty in your pursuit, even starting to explore matters you could never hope to understand.

A constant stream of words flows from Ludwig's beak.

**Enigma:** Enough chit-chat. You ...

**Ludwig:** DukannstdasArkanumnichtbegreifen.

**Enigma:** Stop!

He finally understands the source of his discomfort.

He has never sensed arcane fluctuations before. Not until now.

Of course, he can't immediately detect the abnormalities quietly building around him.

**Ludwig:** DarumkannstdudieseTürnichtöffnen.

Enigma grabs the communicator and throws it at the raven, who effortlessly dodges with a flap of his wings.

**Ludwig:** You're the one interfering with my experiment, Researcher Adler. I need the core to help me unveil the mysteries of language.

**Ludwig:** In fact, not long ago, I made a rather decent discovery.

Another flap of black wings. Enigma notices data flowing across them.

Their silhouette begins to melt away, giving way to the cold gleam of metal beneath.

**Enigma:** Was that hypnosis? A curse? Or a prophecy?

**Enigma:** All that nonsense, and yet nothing has come of it. Most likely needs time to activate.

Enigma checks his timer.

**Enigma:** There's still half an hour left. Enough time to get you out of my way.

**Ludwig:** I need the core. It stays here.

***

**BOSS: The Ultimate Solution**

***

The fortress crumbles. The metal dissolves like foam. Ludwig twitches on the ground.

He spreads his wings and struggles into an upright position. Data flashes in his black eyes.

**Ludwig:** My data. You've utterly destroyed it.

**Enigma:** We both know the box will reset. And you'll go back to your chattering, Raven.

**Ludwig:** Ah, yes. The pieces exist in a world that may be reset. One might think their lives are full of opportunity and that everything is recoverable.

**Ludwig:** But under special conditions, these pieces can also "die."

**Enigma:** What do you mean?

**Ludwig:** The core's contents have the ability to interfere with everything around it. It can tear your mind apart, even scramble your data into a jumbled mess.

**Ludwig:** Opening its protective shell won't bring you the results you're looking for.

**Ludwig:** Ludwig already paid the price for his curiosity. His experiment failed, and his mind was imprisoned here. It wasn't until a short while ago that he finally departed.

**Ludwig:** I am merely a segment of leftover data. An attempt to warn other curious minds to stay well away from danger.

**Enigma:** You mean to say your actions came from good intentions?

**Enigma:** Care to explain that incantation of yours? It still hasn't taken effect.

**Ludwig:** It was meant to change your mind—to prevent you from comprehending the arcane presence here. In stopping you from entering the tower, you would be protected from the madness and ruin that awaits.

**Ludwig:** There's no need for you to go the way of the other pieces who came here, breaking down piece by piece until you eventually vanish into nothing.

His feathers begin to fall. More symbols flicker across his body.

**Ludwig:** But you needn't worry. It won't be long until my arcane skill ceases to function.

**Enigma:** I've never had the opportunity to work with Ludwig. But based on your simulation of him, I can guess he's a stubborn, conceited, curious, egotistical fellow.

**Ludwig:** Oh, not really. Just a raven cursed with knowledge trying his best to protect those who come after.

**Enigma:** \*sigh\* A typical Laplace employee.

**Ludwig:** You're unhappy.

He studies Enigma with eyes that no longer shine.

**Enigma:** No. I was only thinking that perhaps there should be someone who helps bear the risks and make decisions on behalf of these geniuses.

**Enigma:** Working alongside colleagues with no understanding of lab safety protocols comes at too great a price. Having a branch director go missing for so long must have certainly affected productivity.

Ludwig catches the anger in his words and the trace of grief beneath it. He tries to move past both.

**Ludwig:** But you and I have never worked together.

Enigma checks his timer again.

**Enigma:** We've been colleagues for the last ten minutes.

**Ludwig:** You're not going to heed my warning, are you? You're still going to remove the core—to satisfy your curiosity.

**Enigma:** I AM going to take it. Because I want Laplace to remain a place for people like you.

**Enigma:** Someone has to do something.

Ludwig nods.

**Ludwig:** What a coincidence. Some of the previous pieces who came here said a similar thing.

**Ludwig:** Their data was also destroyed. Unless new data is uploaded, they'll never appear in the box again.

**Ludwig:** I'm not entirely sure what to call it. I hope you'll forgive me for presuming it as "death."

**Ludwig:** Which, I believe, is precisely what I'm experiencing now.

**Ludwig:** Farewell, my colleague. I wish you all the best.

Ludwig vanishes into the air, and on the wall behind where he had stood, a door appears.

**Enigma:** ...

**Enigma:** Farewell.

Enigma approaches the door.
