## Story

***

**Look Around I**

***

**Jessica:** This place is nice, but it doesn't look like a place for dining.

In a bright, open hall, she sees tables and chairs lining up in perfect order, and in those silver platters on the tables, exquisite food keeps giving off great smell into the air.

**Jessica:** Anyway, I dine wherever there's food.

**Jessica:** What's this dark water with bubbles? Let me see ... \*coughing\* Whoa!

**Jessica:** I hate this. \*spits\*

Nevertheless, she manages to fill her tummy with what she can find. She won't have to wake up in the middle of the night out of hunger anymore.

**Beep:** CO-092146, Jessica. Break time will be over in 5 minutes and 30 seconds. Please return to your dormitory immediately.

**Jessica:** Oh, time to go back to the dorm. Let me see which way it is ...

***

**Look Around II**

***

A shabby canteen, filled with the smell of food.

**Jessica:** Hmm? There's new food today?

**Jessica:** Purple soup ... Hmm!

**Jessica:** It's surprisingly good! It tastes sour, sweet, bitter and hot at the same time! I've never had anything this good!

**Jessica:** Hmm ... Who made it?

***

**Look Around III**

***

**Jessica:** The smell ... It's the delicious soup again!

**Jessica:** More, more ... Hmm?

> **Choice 1：** Observe the pot

She bows and takes a closer look at the pot. There are strands of dark brown hair and a couple of footprints left next to the soup pot.

**Jessica:** Is this ... hair?

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

**Jessica:** What's going on? My stomach ... hurts a bit ...

**Jessica:** Feels like I just had expired poisonous mushrooms ...

**Jessica:** I think I need some white mycelium. I always feel better after eating that ...

***

**Look Around IV**

***

**Jessica:** Hmm ... What would leave hair by the pot?

**Jessica:** Hmm?

**Jessica:** Oh, that's ...

**Chef's Hat Critter:** Chirr purr.

The tiny critter, waving a spoon too huge for its size, is stirring the soup.

**Jessica:** So it was a Carbuncle!

***

**Look Around V**

***

**Chef's Hat Critter:** Chirr, chirr!!

The critter makes squeaky noises, as if it is complaining about something. Perhaps... it needs a reward.

**Jessica:** You are mad. So ... you believe you are the best cook here, huh? And you want a prize ...

**Jessica:** Hmm ... What kind of prize should I give you?

***

**Look Around VI**

***

**Chef's Hat Critter:** Chirr purr!

The critter gives a spin, as if expecting something.

> **Choice 1：** Search for a gift

**Chef's Hat Critter:** Chirr purr, chirr purr!! Purr!

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

**Jessica:** Hmm? I don't have anything for you ...

**Chef's Hat Critter:** Chirr purr!

**Jessica:** Ouch! That hurts!

**Jessica:** You've got a bad temper, little brat!

***

**Look Around I \[Permanent\]**

***

**Jessica:** Grrrrr …

**Jessica:** \*sniffs\* It smells good as usual today!

**Jessica:** Let's see what food there is today ...
