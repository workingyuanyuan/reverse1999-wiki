## Story

> **Choice 1：** Graduation

**Instructor:** Congratulations, Ms. Jessica, your final exam marks have met the graduation criteria.

**Instructor:** I am glad to see that you've gotten rid of your past bad habits and begun to walk the path of reason under the tutelage of our school.

**Instructor:** Here is your Certificate of Social Adaptation.

**Jessica:** It's gorgeous. Look at the shimmering gold color. I could make up a great story about it!

**Instructor:** ...

**Blonney:** Good! Jessica! You're a graduate now!

**Jessica:** Jennifer, you're here!

**Blonney:** Of course. It's your final exam day, so I gave the crew a vacation and came here early in the morning. Many others are also waiting for you.

**Sonetto:** That's awesome. Congratulations on passing the test, Ms. Jessica.

**Jessica:** Whoa! Everyone's come to see me!

**Jessica:** I have no idea what "graduation" is, but I like it. Can I graduate again?

**Vertin:** You'll have plenty more time to spend with people after this.

**Vertin:** I look forward to it. Outside the correctional school and beyond Green Lake, there are many games, many fresh ideas, and many stories to uncover. I'm looking forward to exploring the outside world with you.

**Jessica:** I sure would love to, Vertin! A world full of stories and unexpected surprises ... Let's go now!

**Blonney:** Hey, Jessica, wait! We forgot something important!

**Blonney:** It's your graduation. Graduation, of course, comes with uniforms, flowers, and, most importantly, a party! We've got to party and dance all night!

**Blonney:** I brought all the stuff. Here, put this on.

**Jessica:** Is this a costume? Like a white veil and a mask?

**Blonney:** No, this is different! It's a graduation uniform, symbolizing a new stage in your life. It has a lot of special meanings.

**Blonney:** But right now, it's you and only you that matter.

**Blonney:** Look at that camera over there. They've come to take your graduation pictures!

**Everyone:** Happy graduation!

A fun-filled adventure comes to a close.

The child born in the laboratory has gained a lot from it.

But there is still fun to be explored in this little corner of the world.

Don't worry, we will come back.

> **Choice 2：** Failed

**Instructor:** I am very sorry, Ms. Jessica. The test results indicate that you have not met the socialization standards recognized by the Foundation.

**Instructor:** In other words, you failed the test.

**Jessica:** Oh, what does that mean? Did I lose this game?

**Jessica:** Like being caught in hide-and-seek, or like the awakened witch being stopped by the clever girl?

**Instructor:** It is clear that your knowledge and behavior are not up to par. We cannot let you graduate.

**Jessica:** Do I have to continue with this odd game, Ms. Instructor?

**Jessica:** Well, it might be monotonous and boring, and the stories you tell are always the same, but I'll keep playing.

**Instructor:** ... Looks like we still have a long way to go.
