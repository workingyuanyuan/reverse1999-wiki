## Story

***

**Look Around I**

***

**Librarian:** Hello, what books are you looking for today?

**Jessica:** Book? What is a book? Is it one of those things with the colorful squares in them?

**Librarian:** I'm not quite sure what you're talking about ... Maybe you could take a look at these?

**Librarian:** We have Ms. Misery (The Illustrated Edition), and The Frog Prince. Which one do you prefer?

**Jessica:** Wow! Please let me see them both.

***

**Look Around II**

***

**Librarian:** Hello, is there anything particular you are looking for?

The deer girl walks among the shelves.

**Jessica:** ...

**Librarian:** Principle of Arcane Skills? Or Boyo Crittish?

**Librarian:** Or... Are you here for that "Man in Books"?

> **Choice 1：** Answer the administrator's questions

**Jessica:** What is "Man in Books"?

**Librarian:** Oh, em, some random rumors among the students...

**Librarian:** You know, "Pray to the man in Books then you will pass the exam with full marks"... That kind of childish wish.

**Librarian:** But since you have no idea what I am talking about, then please stay quiet! And help yourself.

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

**Jessica:** I'm here for Coo Coo Ga Ga. Please help me find it.

**Librarian:** I don't think we have that book here, Ms. ...

**Jessica:** \*sobs\*

**Librarian:** O-Oh! I mean ... We have an extensive collection, so I'm sure I can find you the book you're looking for. Please wait a moment!

**Jessica:** Heh heh.

Principle of Arcane Skills, Boyo Crittish, The Witch's Crucible: An Introduction to Potions, Making an Americano with Bananas, The Book of Political Jokes ...

The deer girl has found a better way to get books than borrowing them.

***

**Look Around III**

***

**Jessica:**Least of ... Arrrr ... Cream … (List of Global Arcanum Creatures) ... Hmm ... Can't find it ...

**Jessica:** Nor here ... Hmm?!

**A bunch of Critters:** Chirr!!

**Jessica:** It's the furry friends ... 1, 2, 3 ... That's a lot of them!

***

**Look Around IV**

***

**Jessica:** ... Last one.

**Jessica:** This is weird ... Didn't find anyone in the books ...

**Giant Critter:** Chirr!!

**Jessica:** You are huge, furry friend ... Are you the one "who lives in the book"?

**Jessica:** But you don't look like a "man" at all. Can you change your appearance too?

**Jessica:** Hey, what are you ... No! The mirror's going to fall over!

That massive standing mirror is leaning towards her.

**Jessica:** The book says, when something is falling down, I should ...

**Jessica:** Right! Hold it up!

> **Choice 1：** Prevent the mirror from falling

**Jessica:** So many shiny pieces, but they sting a little ...

Jessica rubs her back and then her legs.

**Jessica:** Hm? There's something stuck under the pieces ...

**Jessica:** This is ...

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

**Jessica:** \*coughs\*

**Jessica:** So many shiny pieces, but they sting a little ...

The shattered mirror fragments form a tiny, unshakable hill.

**Jessica:** Humph ... You're not a furry friend, you're just a weirdo!

**Jessica:** I know what to do with someone who hurts me ...

**Giant Critter:** Chirr!

**Jessica:** Don't run!

***

**Look Around V**

***

Stand in front of that giant mirror in the library, gently lift up its thin handle with craved patterns and reveal that thin gap... This is where the "Man in Books" is.

**Jessica:** Fabulous! Matilda's exam paper is still here!

**Jessica:** Her handwriting is beautiful, and it smells good, like flowers ...

**Jessica:** A-Achoo!

***

**Look Around \[Permanent\]**

***

**Librarian:** ... Have you finished the book already?

**Jessica:** Yeah. There are so few pictures in it, so it didn't take long!

**Librarian:** ... I had no idea this was an ... illustrated book.
