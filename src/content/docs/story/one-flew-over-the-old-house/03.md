## Story

***

**Take Classes I \[Permanent\]**

***

**Beep:** CO-092146, Jessica.

**Beep:** Please recover your consciousness and be prepared to take up today's study.

**Jessica:** \*Yawns\* ... Hmm?

**Instructor:** Ms Jessica, today's first lesson will start shortly.

After a while in the classroom.

***

**Take Classes II \[Permanent\]**

***

**Beep:** CO-092146, Jessica.

**Beep:** You have been distracted for over 30 seconds. Attention control failure detected. Please refocus on your studies.

**Jessica:** You annoying thing, still noisy as ever.

**Jessica:** Why can't you be like those little conchs with all kinds of patterns? They always speak softly, and have the most gentle and beautiful voices!

***

**Take Classes III \[Permanent\]**

***

**Instructor:** Ms. Jessica, please sit properly.

**Instructor:** The class will begin shortly.

**Jessica:** Oh ... Another boring game ...

**Jessica:** I must do something to make it fun ...

Spent some time in the classroom.

***

**Take Classes IV \[Permanent\]**

***

The sound of hoof steps goes into the classroom.

**Jessica:** I'm ready!

**Jessica:** Hmm?

**Jessica:** ... Is the smileless friend late today?

**Instructor:** Ahem ... Get ready for class.

**Jessica:** Hello, smileless friend.

After a while in the classroom.

***

**Take Classes V \[Permanent\]**

***

**Beep:** CO-092146, Student Jessica.

**Beep:** Please make sure you are sitting properly and prepare yourself for class.

**Jessica:** Hm? But ... they all sit on their chairs like this in the book.

**Instructor:** Ms. Jessica! Please return to your seat!

**Instructor:** This chair is too small for you!

As she stands up, the narrow chair remains stuck to the deer's body.

**Jessica:** Oh, looks like the chair wants to be my friend, too!

Sitting or standing, she has spent some time in the classroom.
