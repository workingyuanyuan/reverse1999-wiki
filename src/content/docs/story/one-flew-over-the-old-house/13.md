## Story

***

**Invite I**

***

**Tooth Fairy:** Hello, Ms. Jessica. Allow me to properly introduce myself. I'm Tooth Fairy, one of the medical members of SPDM.

**Jessica:** Are you here to play with me?

**Tooth Fairy:** I will try to make the process less tedious, though my task is not to play with you.

**Tooth Fairy:** The Foundation has sent me to run a physical examination for you. This shouldn't take long.

**Jessica:** What is a physical examination?

**Tooth Fairy:** Considering your current stage of development, it would take us some time to adequately explain the term to you. You may take it as a less interesting game... Like "Do As I Say" .

**Jessica:** Oh ... Is it like what those people in white did in Zeno?

**Tooth Fairy:** No ... I think our game will be much gentler.

**Tooth Fairy:** If you are willing to cooperate ... maybe I can prepare something more interesting for our next appointment.

**Jessica:** Really?

**Tooth Fairy:** I will try my best.

**Jessica:** So what should I do now? Are you giving me pills as well?

**Tooth Fairy:** Before we get to that, please lower your body and squat for me, so that I can easily lay my hands on you... That's right, thank you, Ms. Jessica.

**Tooth Fairy:** Now, follow me. Say "ah"...

**Jessica:** Ahhh.

**Tooth Fairy:** Good... Very good... You have very beautiful teeth.

**Jessica:** Eau ... iul ...? (Beau ... tiful ...?)

**Tooth Fairy:** Exactly. This means you are in good health.

**Jessica:** Is the "physical examination" over?

**Tooth Fairy:** If you are asking about this particular component of the test, yes. But there will be more health assessments in the near future, which means we will see each other again.

**Jessica:** Then I hope the next one is more fun, like catching a giant, furry friend!

**Tooth Fairy:** I'm afraid that may be slightly challenging. Please don't get your hope up because I would hate to disappoint you. Till then.

***

**Invite II**

***

**Tooth Fairy:** Hello, Ms. Jessica.

**Jessica:** You really ARE here again.

**Jessica:** I love it! You keep your promise.

**Tooth Fairy:** I am so glad that I didn't let you down. You were very cooperative in our last meeting, and the result was also pleasing.

**Tooth Fairy:** As I promised to you earlier, I have prepared some gifts for you, so that we can try out a new game during the coming test.

**Tooth Fairy:** I should clarify beforehand that this is still a test, not a game. We need to give you an evaluation on your mental stability.

**Tooth Fairy:** Please relax, and don't stress yourself. Take this as a special story time—I remember you are very fond of stories.

**Jessica:** What can I offer you? How about a story? You know, like the exciting ones Jennifer told!

**Tooth Fairy:** Tell me about your dreams. The ones you have repeatedly described to us.

**Jessica:** Yes, and I always dream the same dream these days.

**Tooth Fairy:** Always?

**Jessica:** I dreamed of myself wandering in the woods ...

**Tooth Fairy:** Is it the same woods by Green Lake?

**Jessica:** No, no. It was brighter, with a gentler breeze, and the air smelled like acorns. The sound of a babbling brook came from afar, along with birds' chirping. It was like a song.

**Tooth Fairy:** It sounds like a beautiful place... Have you ever been there?

**Jessica:** I don't know.

**Jessica:** But it felt familiar in the dream, as if I was meant to be there.

**Tooth Fairy:** ...I see. Then perhaps I have brought the right gift with me.

**Jessica:** This is …?

**Tooth Fairy:** An instrument, people call it "The Tin Whistle".

**Tooth Fairy:** You will love its sound.

***

**Invite III**

***

**Tooth Fairy:** Watch out!

**Tooth Fairy:** Are you hurt?

**Jessica:** No, I'm fine. It's much bigger here! It feels so nice to run and jump without constraint.

**Tooth Fairy:** Looks like indoor trainings are far from enough for you... I should have applied for an outing.

**Tooth Fairy:** You have very good stamina.

**Jessica:** What is stamina?

**Tooth Fairy:** It means... you can jump high, run for long distance, and almost never feel tired after playing for such a long time.

**Jessica:** Why? I don't get it. Why would anyone get tired from playing? I had so much fun in every meeting with you.

**Jessica:** I like you, Tooth Fairy!

**Tooth Fairy:** I'm glad to see you enjoying this.

**Tooth Fairy:** These closed-off trainings must be burdensome to you, but as long as you keep working on them... Vertin will sort out the rest for you.

**Jessica:** I know! She seems trustworthy.

**Tooth Fairy:** She's very reliable... Are you feeling a bit tired?

> **Choice 1：** Confirm constitution

**Jessica:** Not at all.

**Jessica:** I can jump over a few more stumps like this!

**Tooth Fairy:** Your physical strength is way beyond my expectation. In a good way, of course. This is a satisfying result. Now, we only have one more test to do.

**Jessica:** Will that be our last meeting?

**Tooth Fairy:** Temporarily, yes. Once you have completed the curriculum, we should be able to see each other more often.

**Jessica:** Wow! GREAT!

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

**Jessica:** Yeah, a little!

**Jessica:** Maybe we should take a break and play a different game!

**Tooth Fairy:** Perhaps next time.

**Tooth Fairy:** Your heart rate is higher than usual. We should rest.

***

**Invite IV**

***

**Tooth Fairy:** Stunning ... This is a ... Maltese tiger.

**Jessica:** So, do I look real?

**Tooth Fairy:** I have never seen one of those blue tigers with my own eyes... Your arcane skill is fascinating. May I have a look at the teeth in your current form?

**Jessica:** Ahhh.

**Tooth Fairy:** So that's what they look like... it is worthwhile to get you out of the school.

**Jessica:** Check this out!

**Tooth Fairy:** Interesting, very interesting. A Globster …

**Jessica:** Ahhh... o u ae a een inere in ee? (Are you interested in teeth?)

**Tooth Fairy:** I'm afraid that is my only hobby. The more bizarre and strange the teeth look, the more I feel fond of them.

**Tooth Fairy:** One of the reasons I traveled around the world was to collect different types of teeth.

**Jessica:** I see. Thank you for taking me to the forest! I thought I had to wait a much longer time to get some fresh air!

**Tooth Fairy:** This is, in fact, another test of your arcanum stability. So it was beneficial to both of us.

**Tooth Fairy:** Outside of Green Lake, your abilities have declined, making it difficult to maintain the transformation stably ... I have documented it all.

**Tooth Fairy:** The school will make adjustments to your Arcane Skill training regimen to reduce the intensity of your training.

**Tooth Fairy:** And I ... got to see a variety of different teeth, so it was beneficial to both of us.

**Tooth Fairy:** Thank you, Jessica.

**Tooth Fairy:** The test is over, we still have some time before the end of today's outing. You may enjoy your free time now. Just remember, do not walk out of my sight.

**Jessica:** Wow! Tooth Fairy, you're the best!

**Jessica:** I will make you happy as well! I know the word for this behavior, it's called a tooth ... for a tooth!

**Tooth Fairy:** ...Hmm?
