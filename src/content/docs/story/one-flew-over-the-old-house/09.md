## Story

***

**Look Around I**

***

An empty classroom, clean, as if it has never been put to use.

**Jessica:** Oh, a classroom ... It's just like mine.

**Jessica:** Ugh, classrooms ... always make my stomach sick. Let's call it a day.

***

**Look Around II**

***

An empty, dusty classroom. It hasn't been used for a while.

**Jessica:** I like the sunshine here, but it's too quiet. There are no friends around.

> **Choice 1：** Hang about in the classroom

The fawn spins around, the tip of her tail brushing against a dusty chair friend with an odd bulge.

**Chair:** ...!

**Jessica:** Ouch!

**Chair:** ...

**Jessica:** This chair ... bites?

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

**Jessica:** Well ... I don't like such a quiet place.

**Jessica:** Even though my friends aren't here, I can still make a lot of noise on my own!

**Jessica:** Come on! Let's roar! Rooooar!

***

**Look Around III**

***

**Jessica:** My tail ... Boo-hoo, it hurts whenever I come back here ....

The strange hump on the chair seems to have moved a bit.

**Jessica:** What's that on the chair ... Are they hyphae?

**Jessica:** Whoa! It's on my tail!

**Jessica:** My tail ... It hurts! I've got to get out of here!

***

**Look Around IV**

***

**Jessica:** I'm just taking one more look, secretly ...

**Chair:** ...

**Chair:** \*Sobs\*

The strange hump makes a rustling sound.

**Chair:** Boo... Aww...

**Jessica:** Is it a ... flat furry friend? Oh, it's a ... critter friend!

***

**Look Around V**

***

**Squashed Critter:** Boo-hoo ... Aww ...

**Jessica:** You don't seem well. Shall I help you out?

**Squashed Critter:** Boo-hoo ... Aww!

**Jessica:** You're so stuck ... If only I could get something to chew the mucus.

***

**Look Around VI**

***

**Squashed Critter:** Oww ... Boo-hoo ...

> **Choice 1：** Help the critter out of the chair

**Jessica:** Oh, I get it!

**Jessica:** Andy! Andy will be able to bite through the mucus and get you down!

Jessica rummages through her pockets and places a small bug on the chair.

**Squashed Critter:** Chirr, chirr!!

**Jessica:** Almost there, Andy!

The mucus snaps, and the squishy critter jumps off, whimpering with delight.

**Squashed Critter:** Chirr!! Chirr boo-hoo!

**Jessica:** Great! You're free!

**Squashed Critter:** Chirr, chirr!!

They cheer and applaud for the long-lost freedom.

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

**Squashed Critter:** \*sobs\* Roar!

**Jessica:** Oomph ... Just hold on! I'll try harder ...

**Jessica:** \*pants\*

**Squashed Critter:** Ugh ...

**Jessica:** I'm so sorry ... I thought I had grown strong enough to pull you off.

**Jessica:** It seems I need some Mr. Bites, like those biting bugs in the forest! Hmm, but how am I going to find them here?

***

**Look Around \[Permanent\]**

***

The sound of footsteps echoes clearly in the empty classroom.

**Jessica:** Hmm ... As I remember, this is a table ... and this is a chair!

**Jessica:** But why are they laid out like this?

**Jessica:** They don't look good at all this way ... Alright! Let me stack them on top of each other ...
