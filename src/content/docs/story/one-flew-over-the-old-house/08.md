## Story

***

**Look Around I**

***

**Jessica:** Wow! There really are houses here.

**Jessica:** Was Vertin in here before? Is it one of the interesting places she talked about?

**Jessica:** Hmm? Is someone in here?

**Boy's Voice:** Hey! Anyone there? Anyone hear me? I heard your footsteps... Two people, maybe?

**Boy's Voice:** Oh, please... I'm so bored in the guardhouse...

**Boy's Voice:** Please stay and talk to me, pleeeease!

> **Choice 1：** Talk to the stranger

**Boy's Voice:** \*sobs\* Is anyone else here? I don't want to stay alone anymore!

**Jessica:** ...

**Jessica:** I can hear you.

**Boy's Voice:** Oh! Awesome! Hmm? Just you alone? Ah, whatever...

**Boy's Voice:** Talk to me. Tell me anything!

**Jessica:** Huh? Can't you come out of it?

**Boy's Voice:** Are you new here? Of course not.

**Jessica:** Alright. What would you like to know?

**Boy's Voice:** Oh yes! There are sooo many things I want to talk about ...!

**Boy's Voice:** Let me see where to start ...

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

**Jessica:** Huh ... The black door is talking.

**Jessica:** Like my friends in the cave.

**Jessica:** They always made noise at first. Maybe this one is the same. I should leave him here to shout until he loses his voice.

Her footsteps sound crisp and clear as she trots away.

***

**Look Around II**

***

**Jessica:** That sounds fun. So why are you locked here?

**Boy's Voice:** Oh, I...I cheated in the exam...

**Boy's Voice:** You know, just write the answer on your palms with the Seedling Grass juice, blow on it and the writings will appear again!

**Jessica:** That's clever of you.

**Boy's Voice:** But this was the wrong thing to do. I could have gotten away from cheating, but ... someone sold me out! \*sighs\*

**Boy's Voice:** I will never let any other living thing know about my plan again!

**Jessica:** ...?

***

**Look Around III**

***

**Another Boy:** I'm following your instructions, Instructor.

**Jessica:** Hmm? You are not the boy I know.

**Another Boy:** You aren't the instructor? Sorry to disappoint you, people come and go frequently in this room.

**Jessica:** Do you want me to chat with you, too?

**Another Boy:** No, I don't like talking to people. I prefer to stay with my friend Andy.

**Jessica:** Andy?

**Another Boy:** It can crush anything into pieces with its strong jaw. It is the strongest worm in the world.

**Jessica:** ...?

The deer tilts her head and spins in a circle.

**Jessica:** So what is it?

The boy no longer says anything. It seems he really doesn't like talking to people.

***

**Look Around IV**

***

**Jessica:** ... There's nobody here.

**Jessica:** Where is the little boy? And Andy? Where is it?

Jessica shouts out to look for Andy.

**Jessica:** Andy! Andy!

A rustling sound comes from the corner.

**Jessica:** Hmm? Andy, is it you?

> **Choice 1：** Search the guardhouse

**Jessica:** Oh. It's ... an Andy!

**Jessica:** I already have an Andy. Even though it likes to bite ... I'm still its friend!

**Jessica:** I know how to make friends, but ... Andy is too small to be kept in a cave ...

**Jessica:** I need to find a very, very small cave!

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

Soft and slimy.

**Jessica:** Ah! I think I stepped on something ...

**Jessica:** Mmm ... Something is stuck to my hoof. Eww ...

Shaking her hooves, the deer lowers her head.

**Jessica:** Oops ... I'm sorry. Are you the boy's friend, Andy?

The tiny Shamir worm called "Andy" is squashed flat and no longer moves.

... Then, more "Andys" gather.

**Jessica:** Ouch ... That hurts!

**Jessica:** The "Andys" are biting me. Ouch, another bite!

**Jessica:** That hurts! Stop biting me! Ouch!
