## Story

***

**Invite I**

***

**Vertin:** Okay. I see.

On the other side of the door, the girl sitting in the room is fiddling with a tree branch in her hands -- most likely it was a trophy taken from the only hazel tree in the Behavior Modification School.

**Jessica:** ♪～♪～

**Vertin:** ...

**Vertin:** ......

**Vertin:** Jessica, I was told that you have been running around in the school behind the instructors and breaking quite a lot of stuff.

**Jessica:** I don't get it. I was just trying to do some exercise.

**Vertin:** Maybe we can find you another way to exercise. Something gentler.

**Vertin:** Acting without thinking may get you in trouble. However, if we adopt the right approach, we may smooth the problems away.

**Jessica:** Um ... The right approach? Sounds hard to understand.

**Jessica:** Anyway, look, I got this branch! That was one big hazel tree!

**Vertin:** Yes. I heard this school was once deserted, and that's why these trees happily showed up in here in the first place.

**Vertin:** Making me feel nostalgic for the good old days.

**Jessica:** ...?

**Vertin:** You can walk down this hallway and turn left at the end, then turn right... All the Foundation's schools share an astonishingly similar design. I'm almost certain that I can walk from here to the office with my eyes closed.

**Jessica:** The office?

**Vertin:** A room for the instructors to prepare for classes and rest.

**Vertin:** That is a lovely tiara you made from the hazel tree branches.

**Jessica:** You can take it! Oh, but you're already wearing a hat ... There's probably no room for it ...

**Vertin:** Thank you. I will be happy to wear it. But please remember what I just told you -- don't run around in the school by yourself again.

***

**Invite II**

***

**Vertin:** Here.

**Jessica:** What ...?

**Vertin:** This was Sonetto's notebook when she was in school.

**Vertin:** I hope you find it helpful.

**Jessica:** How did you get her notes?

**Vertin:** It's a long story.

**Vertin:** Do you remember what I told you earlier, "the right approach helps you to smooth things out"?

**Jessica:** Yes.

**Jessica:** And I remember the "office" you talked about.

**Vertin:** Good.

**Vertin:** Sonetto always takes detailed notes. In this notebook, you will find all the key points to memorize and the "approaches" to understanding the knowledge.

**Vertin:** These are very good methods. With this notebook, I was able to stay out of that guardhouse for a long time because of doing poorly in homeworks or exams.

**Jessica:** Guardhouse?

**Vertin:** A kind of punishment. If they have anything similar here ... it should be in that darkest corner behind the teaching building.

**Jessica:** So, you are trying to help me?

**Vertin:** Yes.

**Vertin:** It'd be great for us if you could successfully pass the exam.

**Jessica:** Okay.

**Jessica:** I see!

**Vertin:** ...Good.

***

**Invite III**

***

**Jessica:** Is this the key?

**Vertin:** Yes.

**Vertin:** I have settled a room for you in my suitcase, right next to your best friend Jennifer's room. This is the key to it.

**Vertin:** Of course, the decoration isn't solely made by myself. Blonney, Ms. Tooth Fairy, and Sonetto ... everyone took part in preparing the room.

**Jessica:** What about that guy with glasses? Um, Horro ... Horropedia! He's not here?

**Vertin:** No. Just like you, he is not yet allowed to leave.

**Jessica:** Like me?

**Vertin:** He has been grounded for violating the regulations and being absent from duty without permission.

**Vertin:** He is going to stay in the School of Discipline and Behavior Modification for a while before he's permitted to return to duty again.

**Jessica:** School of Discipline and Behavior Modification ... which is, here?

**Vertin:** That's right. Maybe you should go find him.

**Vertin:** He is warm-hearted and will be glad to help.

**Jessica:** Help ... me?

**Jessica:** Thank you. I don't know what that means, but chatting is great. I like to chat!
