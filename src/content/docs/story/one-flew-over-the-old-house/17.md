## Story

***

**Mid-term Exam**

***

**Jessica:** Things seem different today. No more books?

**Instructor:** This is the midterm exam, a test of what you have learned in the past few days.

**Instructor:** We will be using the same set of questions you did before the enrollment: Societal Level Preliminary Test A-001 (revised ed.).

**Jessica:** Okay, I see, another quiz game. Actually it's not that fun ...

**Instructor:** Please take a serious attitude to the test, and provide answers to the questions I ask.

**Instructor:** I hope you can do it better this time, to prove that we have delivered you from barbarism onto the path of reason.

**Instructor:** Now, close your eyes. You will see a sheet of white light. Place your finger on the designated spot.

***

**Midterm Results**

***

**Instructor:** The situation is not good. We can hardly feel optimistic about this.

**Instructor:** The result shows that you haven't been strict with yourself.

**Instructor:** We are going to make some adjustments to the curriculum to achieve a better result for you.

**Instructor:** But before that happens...

**Instructor:** Please return to your dorm in 15 minutes and freshen up.

**Instructor:** An urgent call will be put through to your dorm shortly. Please do not leave the room before answering it.

**Instructor:** Remember what I have taught you -- always express your gratitude to the people who are willing to lend a helping hand. If it weren't for that lady, you wouldn't have these special treatments.

**Jessica:** Hmm?

**Jessica:** Who do you mean by "that lady"?

**Jessica:** Whoa!

**Jessica:** Vertin!

**Vertin:** Hello, Jessica.

**Jessica:** I knew it was you!

**Jessica:** Many friends have come and played with me these days! But still this place is too small. I wanna go somewhere bigger!

**Vertin:** I will ... look into that as soon as possible.

**Vertin:** If I remember it correctly, you have an exam today, right?

**Jessica:** You mean that special game I played earlier?

**Jessica:** Yeah, it was the exact same game, quite a boring one.

**Jessica:** But it's still more fun than listening to the instructor's story!

**Vertin:** I'm glad ... to find that you have kept your energy up.

**Vertin:** Whether the exam result turns out to be good or not, we have plenty of opportunities to turn things around.

**Vertin:** You will have the rest of the day all to yourself. Make good use of it and have fun.

**Vertin:** You can also get in touch with other friends whom you want to meet. Perhaps they can offer some help.

**Jessica:** I will!

**Vertin:** That's good. I sincerely hope that you will find joy in the upcoming sessions.

**Vertin:** I look forward to seeing your final performance.

***

**Before Finals**

***

**Vertin:** Jessica.

**Jessica:** Vertin!

**Vertin:** Tomorrow is your final assessment. How are you feeling? I hope you aren't too anxious about it.

**Jessica:** What is "anxious"?

**Vertin:** ...Very well.

**Vertin:** I wouldn't hope for any answers other than this.

**Vertin:** Trust me. I will help you. Just go in there and do your best. I will sort out the rest for you.

**Jessica:** Is anything bothering you? Are you in trouble?

**Vertin:** ...It's nothing, don't worry.

**Vertin:** I'm on my way to the school. I will see you very soon.

**Jessica:** Fabulous! Finally, Vertin! I miss you so, so much!

**Jessica:** I've run out of the good berries, but I guess I can prepare a song for you!

**Vertin:** Thank you. See you soon.

***
