## Story

***

**Look Around I**

***

**Jessica:** The sign says "toilet" ... So this is what a toilet is like? Doesn't seem anything like those in stories ...

> **Choice 1：** Loiter in the toilet

**Jessica:** Hmm ... Let me take another tour ...

She steps across the puddles, wandering around in this empty space beneath the ground. She has found nothing.

**Jessica:** Thank goodness I don't have to use it. It's even dirtier than Green Lake.

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

**Jessica:** Hmm ... Maybe this is what a "washroom" looks like in this school ...

**Jessica:** It's not much different from my room, just darker.

**Jessica:** Does that mean I can use my room as a "washroom" at night?

***

**Look Around II**

***

**Jessica:** Hmm ... "200-xxx-xxxx ..."?

On the wall of the "toilet," there is a series of numbers written in crooked handwriting.

**Jessica:** They even do math games in the toilet? The people here do like studying.

**Jessica:** What if I tell this to the smileless friend? Will she smile?

***

**Look Around III**

***

**Jessica:** Hmm? The number here ... 211-xxx-xxxx ...

**Jessica:** The game has changed! It looks like the numbers on that instrument ...

Jessica pulls out a battered piece of paper from her pocket. Paper always remembers what the brain cannot.

**Jessica:** 2, 1, 1, and ... and what? They're gone again?

***

**Look Around IV**

***

**Jessica:** The tune sounds weird no matter how I play it ... and the number has changed again.

The numbers emerge again, more clearly, and in bigger fonts.

**Jessica:** Maybe I need to play it here. Great, I happen to have carried a flute!

> **Choice 1：** Play according to tune

A beautiful melody comes out from the end of her flute, sending ripples across the puddles. A white shadow emerges from the reflection.

**Poltergeist:** Gre...great, someone finally reaches me... Can ... can you see me?

**Jessica:** Yes, and you are ...

**Poltergeist:** Aaaahh! Please stay away from me!!!

**Jessica:** Whoa! Where did that bucket come from? I'm all wet!

**Jessica:** Hmm? Where's that new friend?

> **Choice 2：** Pause / Failed Check

The notes coming from the flute are rough and harsh, and it is difficult to make a tune out of them. Then the pool of water on the ground churns, and a ghostly white figure leaps up through its surface.

**Poltergeist:** Gah! That sound is killing me ... Stop it!

**Jessica:** Wah! I'm all wet! You ...

**Poltergeist:** Ahhh! Stay away! Don't come near me!

**Poltergeist:** I've got to, I've got to protect myself! Yeah, protect myself!
