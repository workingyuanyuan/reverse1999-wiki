## Story

***

**1st Playthrough - Intro I**

***

**Instructor:** Good morning, Ms. Jessica.

**Jessica:** Hello, lady I don't know.

**Jessica:** Oh ... you look very serious. Perhaps I should call you the "smileless friend"?

**Instructor:** ...

**Jessica:** !!!

**Instructor:** Ms. Jessica! Please be quiet.

**Instructor:** We have learned all about your misbehavior in Green Lake Campsite.

**Instructor:** Since Ms. Vertin has vouched for you and the Foundation has decided to be lenient with you, you will be exempt from grounding and fasting. However, it doesn't imply that you will be free from other means of discipline in the future.

**Instructor:** Whatever the Foundation has in store for you, you will be staying here to complete a 12-day behavior modification course.

**Instructor:** You will only be allowed to graduate if you pass the final assessment and receive a certificate of successful social adaptation.

**Jessica:** A sesame? What's that?

**Jessica:** Do I still have to take those pills? Wow, look at these white walls. It's just like the room I used to live in ...

**Instructor:** Ms. Jessica.

**Instructor:** I will have to remind you that according to Article 13 of the Personnel Management Regulations Manual, you have constituted a breach of discipline for interrupting an instructor multiple times.

**Instructor:** Come see me at lunch today. We are going to take an extra session on conversation etiquette.

**Instructor:** Now, please pay attention to the following rules and keep them in mind all the time.

***

**Intro II**

***

**Jessica:** Very funny. Is this a game you play here? I've never tried anything like this. It sounds great fun!

**Instructor:** Apparently your understanding of the world is more limited than we expected.

**Instructor:** Don't worry too much about it, Ms. Jessica. We will make you an excellent student.

**Jessica:** But I don't wanna...

**Beep:** New visit request. Visitor: Sonetto, Employee ID: SF38000000801102Y.

**Instructor:** Time's up. It's time. You have a visitor today, please follow me.

***

**Intro II**

***

**Campus Security:** Ms. Sonetto ... That's right. You are on this list. This way.

**Sonetto:** Thank you.

**Jessica:** I remember you! You were with Jennifer ... Um, Sone- ...

**Instructor:** Good morning, Ms. Sonetto.

**Instructor:** Visit to students in probationary period shall not exceed 15 minutes. Please make sure you have left the room before the countdown is finished.

**Instructor:** By the way, please send our regards to the Timekeeper.

**Sonetto:** Thank you for the kind reminder, I will...

**Jessica:** \*Sniffs\* Hmm, this is the puppy smell I know.

**Sonetto:** Little ... puppy?

**Jessica:** I miss you guys so much!

**Jessica:** Are you here to play with me? There's no one else here, not to mention friends. Even Vertin is gone.

**Sonetto:** Timekeeper is negotiating with the Foundation for your case. She asked me to come here to ...

**Jessica:** Hmm? What's this? A gift for me?

**Sonetto:** Here is a phone for you to ask for meet-ups with us. Timekeeper has applied to the Foundation for a permission that allows you to see other people on a more frequent basis.

**Sonetto:** I have put my contact details in this booklet. You can also add in others' details, so that all of us can stay in touch.

**Sonetto:** But please note that only one meet-up is allowed each day, so please think it through carefully...

**Jessica:** A phone? Is it like an instrument?

**Sonetto:** No, please don't flip it up so hard. You might break the...

**Jessica:** Wow! It lights up!

***

**Intro III**

***

**Sonetto:** Alright. Very well, you have figured out how to use it ...

**Beep:** CO-092146, Jessica. Your visit will end in 20 seconds. Please leave the room at your earliest convenience.

**Sonetto:** Our time is up, I should go now. I will see you soon.

**Jessica:** Oh ... fine.

***

**Intro IV**

***

**Jessica:** I'm starving ...

**Instructor:** I'm afraid you are not allowed to dine, Ms. Jessica.

**Instructor:** Because of your problematic manner earlier, we are going to cancel your lunch break and review the course of conversation etiquette instead.

**Instructor:** Please remember how you feel right now, so that you will not make the same mistake again.

***

**Intro V**

***

**Jessica:** Hungry, stomach hurts. Gotta find something to eat right away ... Hmm?

**Jessica:** Something smells ... good ...

**Jessica:** Probably coming from that way ...

***

**Intro VI**

***

**Jessica:** I hate this. The bed here is stone-hard compared to fur and moss, and it smells moldy ... Yuck.

**Jessica:** Whoa!

**Jessica:** The instrument is singing!

**Jessica:** Uh, it's getting annoying!

**Jessica:** Let me see what I can do to stop it ...

After a violent attack, the tiny, strangely-shaped machine opens up and makes a rattling sound.

Then a familiar voice comes from inside.

**Vertin:** Hello, Jessica. This is Vertin.

**Jessica:** Vertin! How did you get into this instrument?

**Jessica:** Wait ... Are you in trouble? Is this the trouble you usually have in the "outside world"?

**Jessica:** Don't worry, I'll smash this odd instrument open and free you right away!

**Vertin:** No, please don't do that.

**Vertin:** I'm sorry, Jessica. I'm not ... in this instrument.

**Vertin:** You may take this as a kind of arcanum. As we speak, I'm actually in a different place, but I promise I will come and see you soon.

**Jessica:** Alright, you'll never lie to me ...

**Jessica:** Right?

**Vertin:** Yes. You are right.

**Vertin:** I have received your assessment result... Although it is not the best result we hoped for, it is within our estimation.

**Vertin:** I want you to know that everything is still under good control.

**Jessica:** You mean those odd games? They made me hungry, but I was fine. I found something to eat soon enough.

**Jessica:** Anyway, this place is fun! I met strangers and did things I've never done before!

**Vertin:** Well, this is good news.

**Vertin:** Trust me, these are all temporary.

**Vertin:** You will go through some modified curriculum in the next few days. I believe Sonetto has given you the contact booklet.

**Jessica:** Yes, she said I could use this instrument to get you guys here!

**Vertin:** Exactly. Put down the names and contact details of your visitors. Whenever you feel like some help, call us ... or them.

**Vertin:** We will all be glad to hear from you, even if you just call for a brief meet-up.

**Vertin:** I hope you will become more comfortable with these trainings soon.

**Vertin:** I'm looking forward to hearing more good news from you.

***

**2nd Playthrough**

***

**Jessica:** \*Stretching\* Mmm! I'm up, cool!

**Vertin:** Hello, Jessica.

**Jessica:** Wow, Vertin! Hi! But ... am I dreaming? What are you doing here?

**Vertin:** I'm here, as real as it can be. This is not a dream.

**Jessica:** But ... the little speaker didn't mention your name. How come...?

**Vertin:** Because I'm not here to visit.

**Vertin:** I'm here to go through some procedures for Horropedia, and to bring you some good news on the way.

**Jessica:** What is it?

**Vertin:** Starting from today, you won't have to take classes by their schedule. You can only go to the classes you like.

**Vertin:** This should provide you with more flexibility throughout the program and allow you to enjoy it more.

**Vertin:** Now, I have other things to attend to, I'm afraid I can't stay for any longer today.

**Vertin:** This is my contact detail. You can call me anytime.

***
