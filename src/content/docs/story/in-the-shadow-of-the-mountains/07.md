## Story

8:00Clear

Military Camp Office

Military Camp Office, Outside the Ancient City of Ai-Khanoum

***

***

A brief message is placed on the lieutenant's desk.

November 11, 1920. Ceasefire on the Western Front.

Your forces remain stationed in Ai-Khanoum. Recruit, prepare, and await further orders.

**Moldir:** "..."

**Moldir:** "Their war is over."

**Moldir:** "But mine isn't."

She opens the window, facing out toward the snow-covered mountains as they glimmer in the morning light.

From a distance, she can see her new recruits—young people from the village—leading their horses to the river after their morning patrol.

She idly twists a medal in her hand. Not a mark of battle or a reward for service.

It was one of a kind, a gift for the "World's #1 Sister."

> **Choice 1：** I hope I'll see her somewhere on the Eastern Front.

**Moldir:** "Maybe she'll end up deployed to the Eastern Front."

**Moldir:** "I don't know. Maybe they won't send her to the front lines again."

**Moldir:** "But then again, who knows? Father is there, after all."

Her voice falls into a low rumble.

She stares down at the rusted medal, damaged and tarnished. She doubts it would survive any attempt to mend it.

> **Choice 2：** I hope she isn't anywhere near the Eastern Front.

**Your decision changes what is yet to come.**

**Moldir:** "She'd be better off staying away from this war. In fact, I pray we never see her at all."

**Moldir:** "Father won't show her any mercy."

She clenches the medal, the cold metal carrying a warmth that's hers and hers alone.

**Moldir:** "She wouldn't either."

She picks up her rifle and turns to head outside. Her troops are waiting for her.

The crisp fall air fills the sky outside, clear and boundless, illuminated with the rising sun.

Meanwhile, on the other side ...

She's nearing the end of a long interrogation—the final round.

**Lopera:** "I've got nothing more to say."

**Lopera:** "They're traitors, and he's gone. I don't know who's running their little rebellion now."

**Interrogator:** "It's Igor; he's alive. Our intelligence is certain of it."

**Lopera:** "What about Moldir?"

**Interrogator:** "We haven't heard anything about her."

**Interrogator:** "Ever thought about going to the Eastern Front, soldier?"

She holds her silence for a long time.

**Lopera:** "I'll go wherever I'm ordered to go."

Office, St. Pavlov Foundation

On the other side, a long interrogation is finally coming to a close.

An unexpected visitor arrives as a letter is slipped into the room.

**Foundation Staff Member:** "Sorry for the interruption, but I have news for Ms. Lopera."

A Foundation staff member steps into the room and hands an envelope to Lopera.

**Lopera:** "..."

She opens it and pulls out the contents in full view of everyone. No one stops her.

**Lopera:** "Eastern Front. Igor."

She reads in silence; her ever-easy smile falls away into frustration.

She scans the page again and again, as if searching for another name.

**Foundation Staff Member:** "We thought it best to share this with you."

**Lopera:** "Thank you."

**Foundation Staff Member:** "We haven't received any intelligence on former Lieutenant Moldir."

**Lopera:** "That doesn't surprise me."

**Lopera:** "If she's still alive, if she's somewhere out there, I just hope she doesn't come to regret her choices."

**Foundation Staff Member:** "Sounds like she did some work for us in Antarctica."

**Lopera:** "Is that so?"

**Lopera:** "If there's any news of her, I want to know first."

**Foundation Staff Member:** "We'll do what we can."

The blinds of the interrogation room are pulled open, and the dazzling light of day floods in.

On this late-fall afternoon, the sunlight slants into the cold, bare room.

Lopera stands by the window, lifting a hand to shield her eyes as she squints toward the world outside.

**Lopera:** "You said earlier that the 'Storm' has changed the era again."

**Interrogator:** "Yes. We're back in 1920, 71 years before the last reversion."

**Interrogator:** "Only for some reason the Great War isn't over; the timeline as we once knew it has changed."

War goes on.

Lopera doesn't say anything further, her face tangled with emotion as she looks out toward an unreachable horizon.

In the daylight, somewhere far across the world, the woman she worries for rides beneath the mountains, holding her reins in a firm grip.

Valley, Outside the Ancient City of Ai-Khanoum

**Moldir:** "Fall in!"

The lieutenant's command cuts through the air. At the valley's edge, her soldiers wait, fully armed.

The troop falls into formation, silently waiting for whatever order comes next.

**Moldir:** "Forward!"
