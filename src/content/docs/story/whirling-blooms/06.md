## Story

Living Room

The purification proceeds smoothly. The lost spirits depart once more, the corners clear, and the smoke trails settle.

She shakes the incense stick. The ember dims, leaving only fragrance.

**Ramona:** It's done.

**Journalist:** It took you longer than last time.

**Ramona:** Yeah. Was trying to understand why they keep coming back, but I came up with nothing.

**Journalist:** Señorita Ramona, I'm confused.

**Ramona:** What?

**Journalist:** The papers say you can intimidate and control spirits through arcane skills. Some even claim you can train them if you so desire.

**Journalist:** But watching you, I see that you approach things with caution, not with the kind of power they describe.

**Journalist:** Are the papers exaggerating, or are you hiding your strength?

Ramona wants to avoid the question until she catches Renée's searching gaze.

She has never told anyone this secret.

Because of it, she has endured troubles both internal and external.

But she can't keep running forever. Perhaps this is the moment to begin.

**Ramona:** Actually ...

**Ramona:** Those are lies told by my parents.

**Ramona:** I can't control spirits. All I've got is a little talent for sensing and purifying them.

**Renée:** What?

She feels the mother's and daughter's shock, and the twinkle of excitement in the journalist's eyes. An excitement that burns.

**Ramona:** That's the truth of it. My parents are con artists. The "exorcist family," the "special bloodline," my fame—it's all marketing.

**Ramona:** They played you all for fools. Some people even defend them.

**Journalist:** Yet you refused to cooperate with their lie. After that exorcism, many called you a fraud.

**Journalist:** There was no trace of purification in the building. It just looked like any other abandoned place. The crowd quickly lost interest.

**Journalist:** If there's no flash, no pizzazz, then things disappear from the public eye in an instant.

**Journalist:** I heard one investor even withdrew funding.

**Ramona:** I know. But, truth is, there were no spirits in that building. And I'm not a liar.

**Renée:** You mean you intentionally left no evidence so that people would expose you?

**Ramona:** ...

As usual, she stays silent. She never meant it as an act of noble resistance. She just followed her conscience.

**Journalist:** I always thought there was something profound behind your silence. I suppose it turns out the reason was simple.

**Ramona:** Like I said, things really aren't that complicated in this city.

**Ramona:** And the truth can be found in things other than words.

**Journalist:** Interesting perspective. I should write that down.

**Renée:** No! Mamá, she never lied to us. And this is a private conversation.

**Journalist:** Mm, alright.

**Journalist:** Forgive my eagerness. Let's go back to talking about the spirits.

**Ramona:** That's what I was thinking too.

**Ramona:** Hey, at least you're more open-minded than I thought.

**Ramona:** Just, please, don't go publishing our chat in the paper.

**Journalist:** So in the eyes of a teenager, I'm not completely beyond hope after all?

Ramona relaxes.

**Ramona:** Like I said before, spirits aren't inherently good or evil. They're drawn back to the living world by certain things, for example—

**Ramona:** Family.

**Renée:** What do you mean? Are you saying those spirits are our ancestors?

**Journalist:** But, they're not buried anywhere near here.

**Renée:** Perhaps ghosts don't follow the rules of the physical world?

**Ramona:** It's not about the ghosts. It's about you, Renée.

Two pairs of eyes turn to Renée, and she suddenly feels an inexplicable fear.

The fear of being seized, of being erased.

A fear that is not her own, and she has to hide it.

**Ramona:** Renée, if I've got it right, some kind of arcane power has awakened in you.

**Renée:** What?

**Ramona:** Your arcane energy draws spirits to you. Far as I can tell, one of your ancestors belonged to an ancient "House of Song" or was an Aztec priest, maybe.

**Ramona:** That's why you love to dance the Jarabe Tapatío. In older times, dance was a way to communicate with spirits and gods.

**Renée:** Ramona, you're not pulling my leg, are you?

**Ramona:** If I'm right, there's a spirit residing within you at this very moment.

**Ramona:** Not a common thing. Ghost stories call it possession. That's why your personality's changed.

**Renée:** Stop! I'm getting goosebumps.

**Ramona:** I'm not trying to scare you. This is the first time I've seen it too.

**Ramona:** It was drawn to you and merged with your body. Revitalized, in a way, letting it completely mask its presence.

**Ramona:** That's what's been calling the other spirits here again and again.

**Ramona:** Renée, those repeated destructive acts, they remind you of anything?

**Renée:** No ...

**Ramona:** Or maybe I should call you "ghost," yeah?

She raises the incense stick and, for the first time, chants toward a living person.

**Ramona:** Ilusiones,apartaos.Laverdadserevela.

**Renée:** No!

**Journalist:** Renée!

"She" awakens.

The arcanum in the host's bloodline is too weak for "her" to even see clearly.

"She" makes out only scattered lights and two shadows, instinctively knowing they're two living people.

Someone speaks near "her," but "she" cannot hear the words.

**Journalist:** Renée, Renée!

**Journalist:** Thank goodness, you're awake!

**Journalist:** Señorita Ramona, wh-why isn't she saying anything?

**Ramona:** It's confused. It needs guidance before it can leave.

A calming fragrance reaches "her," soothing the stabbing pain of her memories.

A hand rests on "her" forehead—dry, warm—and the burning sensation fades.

**Ramona:** Estelugar,esteinstante.Reveladoantemí.Mostradoparamí.

**Ramona:** No, the spirit's lingering consciousness is affecting her body.

**Ramona:** Renée's reaction is so strong because ...

**Ramona:** ... that fragment of awareness carries powerful emotion.

That long-suppressed longing tries to burst into words, but the sounds jam in her throat, and her body trembles.

**Journalist:** What's happening?! Renée! Renée!

**Ramona:** I ... I don't know what it wants to say.

She cannot see or hear it clearly. She has no way to answer the feeling.

And "she," trapped in silence, cannot speak.
