## Story

They circle behind the stage and come to the other end of the building—silent, save for the echoes of their footsteps.

**Grundriss:** Hm? The signal's been restored.

**Bkornblume:** Because I drove that girl away. Wherever she goes, our signals get jammed.

**Cornerstone:** Huh. She's not some kind of radio tower, is she? I didn't notice any antenna popping out of her head.

**Bkornblume:** My working theory is that she has some sort of attraction to high-energy zones, and her presence interferes with electromagnetic fields.

**Bkornblume:** This building has its quirks. It may take you some time to learn your way around it.

**Bkornblume:** It consists of two main areas. We were just in the west wing, which is where those creatures often appear.

**Grundriss:** "Creatures"?

**Bkornblume:** The locals call them "Shades." But the girl you just saw is a little different than the rest.

**Bkornblume:** She seems to have a physical body. But she can use an arcane skill to become intangible like the others.

**Bkornblume:** Creatures like them are rare. Even arcanists seldom encounter them. Seeing so many in one place is even rarer. This building must have something to do with it.

**Bkornblume:** You'll likely hear noises. Some come from the Shades, yes, but others seem to come from the building itself.

**Bkornblume:** Or to be more precise, some of its bricks. Locals call them "Echo Stones."

**Cornerstone:** That's a fitting name.

**Bkornblume:** As for how these Shades come to be and why they are gathering here, I haven't found a clear answer.

**Bkornblume:** These creatures are such vivid images of who they once were that, at times, it feels like they're still alive. In fact, many have made that very mistake.

**Bkornblume:** But there's no need for concern. They won't interfere with our work so long as we leave them undisturbed.

**Grundriss:** Do you figure these Shades could have something to do with the building's immunity?

**Bkornblume:** I was just coming to that.

She stops and tilts her head.

**Bkornblume:** This way, please.

**Cornerstone:** I recognize this corridor.

Familiar light. Familiar shadows. A familiar hallway.

**Emil:** Stasia! Hurry up!

**Young Cornerstone:** Wait, Emil, Róża!

**Young Cornerstone:** That light ... It's almost like the building is breathing.

**Róża:** Like magic, isn't it?

**Róża:** But you can only see it when things are totally silent.

**Cornerstone:** ...

The exit is just up ahead.

The exit is just up ahead.

Cornerstone takes a deep breath and steps forward into that backdrop of so many of her dreams.

Atrium, The Building

**Cornerstone:** ...

Loss.

The light from her memories has faded. Everything is hazy. Even the sun above seems weak and dim.

**Bkornblume:** We have some of the survivors of the Temporal Anomaly that took refuge here.

**Bkornblume:** They might have some idea what this place is and why it was immune.

**Grundriss:** —!

**Grundriss:** I figured there would be people living here, but I didn't expect so many.

**Bkornblume:** It's alright, everyone. Please, step into the light.

She smiles and waves at the shadows.

**Bkornblume:** These two ladies are the friends I mentioned earlier. They also survived that strange rain.

**Bkornblume:** They mean you no harm. You have my word. They're only here for a field survey.

**Cornerstone:** Yes, once we fill out our forms, we'll be out of here in a flash.

Before long, they are joined by more people.

And then even more.

Like ghosts haunting a ruin that time abandoned, people in old, tattered clothes stand still and silent in the shadows.

Until eventually, one speaks.

**Drunkard?:** We meet again, miss.

**Grundriss:** You're the one from the station, "nine o'clock."

**Jasio:** Or you can call me Jasio. Welcome. This is our home.

He thinks for a moment, then elaborates.

**Jasio:** Hopefully not for long.

**Bkornblume:** They'd been taking shelter here when the Temporal Anomaly occurred. As you can imagine, it has only worsened their situation. Most of them are hoping for a chance to start again, with a job, for instance.

**Bkornblume:** I already explained to them who we are and what we'll be doing.

**Bkornblume:** As long as we don't add to their troubles, they won't pay any mind to our investigation.

**Cornerstone:** Very thoughtful of you, Ms. Bkornblume.

**Bkornblume:** Happy to help. This should be all the setup you need. I'll let you get on with it.

**Bkornblume:** I'll be in my monitoring station. There have been a few scavenger "visits" lately, so it's best I keep an eye on the equipment.

She nods and retreats into the dark hallway.

**Cornerstone:** She seems a little cold. Did I say something wrong?

**Grundriss:** I've found she needs time to warm up to new people. You'll know she feels comfortable around you when she starts giving you flowers. I suppose she just prefers actions over words.

The residents leave as they speak. Soon, only the man named Jasio remains in the corner.

**Cornerstone:** They're gone. Didn't seem all that interested in our work here.

**Jasio:** Don't stick your nose into other people's business. That's the number-one rule around here.

**Cornerstone:** But this isn't just "our business." It involves all of you. If this place really is a safe zone against that strange rain ...

**Cornerstone:** It could save many more people if it happens again.

**Jasio:** Lady, take a good look around you. We all survived that rain. Do you think any of us feel lucky?

He leans against the wall and lights a cigarette. Despite the somewhat confrontational question, he seems calm.

**Jasio:** Bkornblume said you went through the rain, too. So tell me honestly: do you feel grateful to have survived?

**Grundriss:** ...

**Grundriss:** Then why are you still here? Why not walk away like the others and live your own life?

**Jasio:** Heh, yeah, been wondering the same thing myself.

He takes a drag and shrugs.

**Jasio:** Got nowhere to go and nothing better to do. Maybe it's as simple as that.

**Cornerstone:** ...

**Cornerstone:** This place is completely different from what I remember.

With a smile, Cornerstone whips out her sketchbook.

**Cornerstone:** All the same. Let's get started, shall we?

**Cornerstone:** First question: Where were you on the 31st of December, 1999?

**Pale Boy:** Right there, where you're standing now.

The shadows in the corner stir, and from them, a boy's ghostly white face appears.

**Pale Boy:** Look up.

Cornerstone follows his gaze upward.

**Pale Boy:** There used to be a dome up there. We were all under it when the rain fell.

**Pale Boy:** When it stopped, the dome came crashing down.

**Cornerstone:** Hmm.

She squints to examine what little remains of the dome.

**Cornerstone:** Grundriss, can you make out those concrete remnants around the drum wall? The arrangement is very particular.

**Grundriss:** Given their size, it has to have been supporting a larger structure. One, two ...

**Grundriss:** Three sets. Each pair opening up at an angle of ...

**Grundriss:** 60 degrees.

**Cornerstone:** An equilateral triangle—the most stable structure. Once it broke, collapse was inevitable.

Cornerstone jots her findings down in the sketchbook, but her analysis doesn't stop there.

**Cornerstone:** Ah, speaking of which ...

**Cornerstone:** What happened after it fell? Are the pieces still around?

**Pale Boy:** They're over here.

He pats the gray rubble piled up beside him.

**Cornerstone:** Heh. That's a relief! For a moment, I was worried you'd sold them or something.

**Pale Boy:** Not all of them. These are just what was left.

**Pale Boy:** Some strange arcanists came and bought the others. They told us the stones were the work of devils.

**Cornerstone:** Ah, well, on the bright side, at least you've all been trying to improve your lives instead of just sitting around and moping.

**Pale Boy:** ...

He lets out a huff through his nose and falls silent, but he doesn't leave.

Meanwhile, Cornerstone crouches down to bring her ear to the stones.

It's been so long.

We've been doing well in Gdańsk. The sky is no longer dark.

■■■ family has grown, a beautiful new daughter.

I'm sure Lu■■■■ will be happy for us ...

It's been so long.

We've been doing well in Gdańsk. The sky is no longer dark.

■■■ family has grown, a beautiful new daughter.

I'm sure Lu■■■■ will be happy for us ...

**Cornerstone:** ...

Not the same voice as before.

She sighs, though not even she can say whether it's in disappointment or relief.

**Cornerstone:** Similar shade of gray and these whispers ... They must be the Echo Stones Ms. Bkornblume mentioned.

**Cornerstone:** Do you know anything about the whispers coming from these stones?

**Pale Boy:** Whispers? It's just random noise.

Cornerstone presses her ear even closer.

The whispers are no more.

After a silent moment, she hears another discordant sound.

**Pale Boy:** Wait. That sound ... Stay away from that stone!

The warning comes too late.

Cornerstone has moved it away to reveal a nest of Critters, who are now glaring up at their intruders.

**Cornerstone:** ...?

**Critters:** ...?
