**Cornerstone:** But there's only so much disappearing stationery I can take before resorting to desperate measures, so I lured you out.

Though Cornerstone can't see her, she knows she's listening.

**Cornerstone:** Of course, if you're not willing, I'll be happy to hand this little puppet over to our site supervisor.

**Cornerstone:** Ms. Everecho, is there a reason why you're obstructing our work?

**Everecho:** ...

**Everecho:** I-I do not like what you're doing.

She has no intention of backing down, even with all these outsiders and the "devil four-eyes" questioning her.

**Everecho:** I have seen your drawings. You don't understand this place ... Mistakes, all over.

**Everecho:** And the people ... they are uneasy. I feel it.

**Cornerstone:** Uneasy? Why do you think that?

Everecho's gaze moves from one face to the next until, ultimately, it meets Cornerstone's.

Colorful glints shimmer in her left eye, like a rainbow refracted in a kaleidoscope.

**Everecho:** What should not be forgotten—the past, not the present ... voices ... writings ...

The voices fade. Silence falls.

**Everecho:** You should not have come here.

A wandering soul, forgotten by time, left to guard a grave.

**Everecho:** ...

The statement throws her for a loop.

**Everecho:** Can you do that?

**Cornerstone:** If you help me find the right design, then of course I can fix it.

**Cornerstone:** What do you say?

**Everecho:** ...

**Everecho:** Then, will you free Honza?

**Cornerstone:** ...
