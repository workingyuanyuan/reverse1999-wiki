## Story

**???:** How are things going in Nowa Miedź?

**Bkornblume:** Less than ideal. They're really feeling the lack of experienced workers on site.

**???:** Nothing surprising there.

**???:** We're under no obligation to help. But still, if they're struggling, a little nudge wouldn't hurt.

**Bkornblume:** Understood.

**Bkornblume:** Maybe just a few hints? I know we shouldn't meddle too much.

\*rumble\*

Whoa!

Distant applause accompanies the sound of a wall crumbling to the ground.

**Bkornblume:** Mm?

**Bkornblume:** Sorry, I'd better take a look.

Bkornblume approaches the window and peers outside.

**Grundriss:** Well done. Now, remember which group you're in?

**Grundriss:** Szymon, Marcin—Group A. Move the bricks and clear a path. Watch out for the cables on the ground.

**Grundriss:** Group B—yes, Jasio. Come with me.

She flits through the construction site, deftly naming the workers despite having only recently made their acquaintance.

**Marcin:** Got it, boss. And after we're done with the bricks?

**Grundriss:** Pick out the Echo Stones we marked and bring them to the atrium.

She spots an exposed head and points a finger.

**Grundriss:** Marcin! Where's your helmet?

**Marcin:** My head was feeling stuffy, so I took it off and left it, ehh, somewhere.

**Grundriss:** Go find your helmet and put it on. Now.

She takes a deep breath before assigning the rest of the work.

**Grundriss:** Group C, keep working on dismantling those walls. There's still one more to go before your break. You were all late today, so I don't want to hear any complaints.

**Bkornblume:** ...

**Bkornblume:** If I may, I'd like to adjust my report.

**Bkornblume:** Despite a little early friction, things seem to be on the right track now.

**Bkornblume:** I don't think they need any further help from me.

One, two, three!

\*rumble\*

Another wall comes down.

**Construction Worker I:** Alright, climb over here. Let's find those marked stones.

**Construction Worker II:** Give me a hand, huh?

The workers help pull each other over the mountain of collapsed stones.

A testament to their efforts, a hard-won symbol of victory.

**Jasio:** Not bad, everyone.

**Olga:** Ah, I'm just in time. You all must be worn out. I've brought pierogi.

**Marcin:** Pierogi for dinner? Hah. I must be dreaming! I'll have ten!

Long-absent smiles grace the workers' grubby faces. Jasio, on the other hand, takes a seat on a concrete slab and lights a cigarette.

**Bkornblume:** ...

Piles of rubble, relieved smiles ...

Just like that momentous autumn she remembers so fondly.

The clamor doesn't make it to the other side of the building.

Here, only a hazy light floats to and fro, as if it's checking and double-checking something.

**Everecho:** You're back! They didn't see you, did they?

**Honza:** Not me, no. Of course not. This is for you.

**Honza:** The architect's wacky-bob, her secretly-secret weapon! She was playing pick-up-put-down with it when she did-do her work.

Honza drops a used eraser into Everecho's palm.

**Everecho:** Secret weapon? If we could make it disappear ...

Everecho lifts the eraser, steels herself for a moment, and swallows it whole.

**Everecho:** Urh. It tastes awful.

**Honza:** No! Don't eat it, no! It'll only go splodey-crack or turn you green and blue! Out with it! Spit it out!

**Everecho:** \*cough\*

Time and use have left the eraser covered in germs and who knows what else.

It's bound to conflict with her energy if it comes into direct contact with her.

**Honza:** Poison true, greeny-green and bluey-blue ...

Honza falls silent, his eyes turning into those of an ordinary, inanimate puppet.

**Everecho:** No, no ...

An obstructing force, viscous and ominous, spreads through her body.

The flow of air around her becomes heavy. Each breath is a struggle.

In the end, she falls unconscious.

**Everecho:** ...

\*thwack\*

\*thwack\*

**Everecho:** \*cough\*

A palm comes down on her back, dislodging the eraser from her throat.

Fresh air flows into her body once more. She can breathe freely again.

**Cornerstone:** Feeling better now?

**Everecho:** \*pant\* Yes ... yes ... Thank ... Ah! You! When did you—

Cornerstone's hand stays resting on her back.

Her fingers apply the tiniest bit of pressure, just enough to feel coercive.

**Cornerstone:** When you tried to destroy my secret weapon, of course.
