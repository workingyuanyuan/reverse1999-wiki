25. June 1996.

Partly Cloudy.

**Grundriss:** Today marks the 23rd day since the project was officially halted.

**Grundriss:** We lost Lucjan, the tender young man that loved watching television.

**Grundriss:** At the same time, the model and some other Echo Stones have reawakened. How exactly, we don't fully understand. There is still a lot we have to learn.

**Grundriss:** But irrespective of that, we're going to keep moving forward.

**Bkornblume:** One horizontal, the other vertical, intersect at the center, and ...

Bkornblume tapes the box to form a neat criss-cross.

**Bkornblume:** Done! Perfectly packaged!

**Jasio:** Impressive. You've done any factory work before?

**Bkornblume:** Just a trick I picked up from my last job. It'll keep the model's shape stable during transport.

Not far off stand Cornerstone and Grundriss.

They watch the box with the intensity of parents sending their child off for their first day of kindergarten.

**Grundriss:** You sure about this?

**Cornerstone:** From what Everecho said, we can infer that she shares the same origin as the Echo Stones and Shades in this place.

**Cornerstone:** Which means the energy the stones need to reawaken comes from the Shades themselves. I should've realized it sooner.

**Grundriss:** So that's why Lucjan—

The air becomes heavier.

The wound is still too fresh to say his name freely.

**Cornerstone:** Maybe it sounds like a fairy tale, but it's the best explanation we have.

**Cornerstone:** Fortunately, most of the Echo Stones have already reawakened. We shouldn't need any more Shades to ... do what he did.

**Cornerstone:** Though I fear the repaired dome may not be able to shelter a large number of people, it's still a start.

**Cornerstone:** From there, we can look for other ways to expand and improve it—widen its protection to even more people.

**Cornerstone:** First things first, someone needs to deliver our findings to the Foundation. Fingers crossed the higher-ups will agree to restart our project.

She raises her eyes to look ahead.

Bkornblume, seemingly sensing Cornerstone's stare, walks over.

**Bkornblume:** Is that everything? If so, I'll take the model and head straight to the airport.

Cornerstone raises her cup of coffee in a toast.

**Cornerstone:** Godspeed, Ms. Bkornblume!

**Bkornblume:** I'll do my best to persuade Madam Constantine. Between our completed model and the video we prepared, I'm confident in our prospects.

Once she's placed the box in the trunk, she smiles and waves to the team.

**Bkornblume:** Don't worry, ladies. I should be back in contact within the week!

**Honza:** ...

**Honza:** This is goodbye, Everecho.

**Honza:** Honza is not needed anymore. Now his heart is broken, just like his face.

**Everecho:** Honza? No, I-I didn't mean to.

**Lucjan:** Everecho, do you want to watch TV together?

**Lucjan:** I feel so cold, trapped in the stone.

**Everecho:** Lucjan!

**Lucjan:** There's no TV here. No Old Oak. Only cold.

**Lucjan:** Help me, Everecho.

**Honza:** La la la-la-la, la-la-la la la. Stone is tomorrow; tomorrow is stone.

**Honza:** I kicked tomorrow yesterday, upsy-down and oopsy-ways. And when I came upon it today, dead-■■ing on the ground it stayed!

**Honza:** Dead still it stays! Still stays it dead!

**Honza:** Ha■■hahaha!

**Everecho:** —!

She wakes from the nightmare with a jolt.

The room is empty. The only noise is the news playing on the TV.

**TV Host:** A new round of economic policy adjustments has just been announced,

**TV Host:** proving that marketization has become an unstoppable force. And while concerns over unemployment and social welfare continue to rise,

**TV Host:** we won't hesitate to keep forging ahead on the right path.

**Everecho:** The "right path" ...

She looks at her trembling hand.

It's pale and transparent. It's as though she left a part of her body in the sea of light.

Honza is still on her hand.

**Honza:** ...

He snuggles up against her, but he doesn't say a word.

He hasn't been able to speak since the injury.

**Everecho:** Is this truly what you want, Honza?

The door is pulled open.

**Cornerstone:** There you are.

**Cornerstone:** Olga's made her signature bigos again. I intend to enjoy it with a nice, crisp beer. Care to join us?

**Everecho:** ...

She stares unflinching at the TV, eyes practically piercing through the screen into the empty space beyond.

**TV Host:** The local food and hospitality sectors are facing both unprecedented opportunities and challenges.

**TV Host:** As new international fast food chains continue to open their doors in the city, they are quickly becoming a hit with young consumers.

**Cornerstone:** Everecho?

Cornerstone has no choice but to hazard a guess at the thoughts behind that vacant stare.

**Cornerstone:** What's up, not a fan of hamburgers?

**Everecho:** Huh?

Everecho's gaze finally flickers away. But just as soon as it does, it returns to the screen.

On it, a clown wearing red and yellow is performing for a cheering crowd.

**Everecho:** No, there is something else. I feel so worn down lately. My memories seem to be slipping away from me.

**Everecho:** 1999—the world before the rain—do you remember what it was like?

**Cornerstone:** ...

It's a number she hasn't heard brought up in some time.

It was once a symbol of hope. Now, it's a wound that has yet to close.

**Cornerstone:** 1999 ... Let me think.

**Cornerstone:** Modernism was still the dominant force in architecture. More and more of those great big boxes of glass and aluminum alloys rose up in every city.

**Cornerstone:** As for everything else, there was the internet, 3D video games, MP3 players ...

**Cornerstone:** Oh, and even more fast food chains, just like in the news.

**Everecho:** But it's scary, don't you think?

She fiddles with the hem of Honza's smock, and her voice falters.

**Everecho:** I feel as though we've started to forget the future.

**Everecho:** We all just go on as if nothing is wrong, even though we're now living in a world where time flows backward.

**Everecho:** We lived through that time, but it doesn't feel real anymore. It has been left to gather dust in a corner, like old hamburgers.

**Everecho:** One day we might even forget Lucjan.

Cornerstone sits beside her and aids in the tidying of Honza.

**Cornerstone:** We won't, Everecho. We can't forget the past, nor the future.

**Cornerstone:** Because they still exist, even if just in their ideal form. Like this building, even if we use new materials and new techniques to rebuild, its heart—the Echo Stones—remains.

**Cornerstone:** No matter what changes come, big or small, the past will continue to have been and will always be with us. And that means Lucjan will always be a part of this place.

**Cornerstone:** Now, lift that chin up. I know he wouldn't want to see you looking so sad.

**Everecho:** ...

Everecho doesn't say anything further.

She reaches out an arm and holds it under the sun pouring through the skylight.

Gold specks of dust—messengers of days gone, each carrying untold secrets and unspoken words—float through the glow.

Let me be an echo in the stones. That way ...

Even if I disappear, I can still ...

I can still be with you every day and every night.

With the words spoken, the weight in her chest disappears.

**Everecho:** Lucjan ... He is in the light.

**Everecho:** In the leaves of the trees, in every warm cup of coffee.

**Everecho:** And right here, too.

She points first to her own heart, then to Cornerstone's.

**Cornerstone:** Yes, forever.

Everecho's lips curve upward at the confirmation.

**Everecho:** I see it now, Stasia.

**Everecho:** Thank you.
