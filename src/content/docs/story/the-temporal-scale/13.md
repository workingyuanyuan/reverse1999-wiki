**Simone:** Indeed. The letter also noted the Echo Stones and the building they belong to are highly and likely inherently interrelated.

**Simone:** If this interrelationship is severed, the building's resistant properties will be lost forever, even if you create an exact replica.

**Cornerstone:** ...

**Simone:** Very well.

**Cornerstone:** Allow me to finish, Ms. Simone. I'll be brief!

**Cornerstone:** I would suggest that these attacks from the Echo Stones may well be a sign that we're on the right path.
