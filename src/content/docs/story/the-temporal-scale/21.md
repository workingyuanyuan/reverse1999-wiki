## Story

**TV Presenter:** Breaking News: As of 4 AM this morning, Karolinska University Hospital has admitted a total of 37 patients showing symptoms of this inexplicable "wired vein" disease.

**TV Presenter:** Medical facilities across the municipality are reporting overwhelmed emergency departments and surgical units.

**Reporter:** As you can see, the line outside the hospital now stretches across three city blocks.

**Reporter:** We advise the public against gathering at hospitals unless absolutely necessary!

**Grundriss:** Time is running out.

Grundriss hangs up and turns to her companion. All color has drained from her face.

**Grundriss:** We have only two hours to get to the Foundation's last evacuation flight, and we're over a hundred miles from where it's flying out.

**Bkornblume:** We have a chance if we leave now and speed the whole way there, and hope the traffic police don't stop us. How are your driving skills?

Lightning tears through the sky.

**Grundriss:** This is just what it looked like then.

**Grundriss:** I'm worried about you.

Grundriss glances at the residents.

They're scattered beneath the dome, either standing alone and silent or whispering to one another, insignificant beneath the coming rain.

**Olga:** Oh, hush now, dear. You've already done more than enough for us.

**Olga:** It's about time we took the reins of our own fate.

**Everecho:** Then, this is goodbye. I'll miss you.

She looks to the ground, and the flickering colors in her eyes fade, leaving only pale, desolate white.

**Everecho:** Actually, I already miss you now.

**Grundriss:** ...

**Cornerstone:** You two go ahead. I'm staying here.

Cornerstone leans on a pillar in the corner, sipping a hot cup of coffee.

Dozens of eyes turn to stare in disbelief.

**Everecho:** What?!

**Jasio:** Hmph, I knew you'd do something crazy like this.

Cornerstone puts her cup down and turns to face the group she's come to see as her rag-tag family.

**Cornerstone:** I made up my mind the moment this project was proposed.

**Cornerstone:** I'll see this through to the very end, not as an observer but as part of the experiment.

**Cornerstone:** When the Temporal Anomaly comes, I'll stand under the dome with everyone here and face whatever comes together.

**Cornerstone:** It's the only way I could consider this project finished.

**Grundriss:** ...

**Grundriss:** Then we're on the same page. Saves me the trouble of convincing you.

This time, they aren't using models, probabilities, or "theoretical possibilities" to prove anything.

Now, they're using their own time and flesh to put their judgments to the test.

**Grundriss:** What about you, Ms. Bkornblume?

**Bkornblume:** I ...

Bkornblume closes her eyes and listens for the pitter-patter of raindrops. Not yet, but soon.

**Bkornblume:** The sight of this building wrapped in a curtain of rain must be breathtaking.

**Bkornblume:** I'd love to see it for myself, even if only for a second.

**Cornerstone:** It's set, then. I hereby declare—

With a grin on her face and a cup of coffee in her hand, she offers a toast.

**Cornerstone:** That you are now co-conspirators in this mad experiment!

Clouds gather in the sky. The people gather below.

And the scents of drenched coats and concrete mix beneath the dome.

**Grundriss:** Wait, why are so many people coming in from outside? We've already exceeded the projected limit.

**Resident IV:** Th-That's my sister. We won't take up any more space, I promise.

Another unfamiliar face squeezes in through the entrance. She's holding a baby swaddled in an old blanket.

**Grundriss:** This isn't something we can bend. There's no guarantee the dome can shelter this many people.

**Resident III:** We're sorry, miss, but we couldn't just leave them out there.

Many pairs of guilty eyes avoid hers.

**Bkornblume:** This isn't good. The awakened Echo Stones barely cover half the dome's surface.

**Bkornblume:** We don't have any more room, but what can we do? Drive them off? Even if they don't know what's happening, it's still cruel.

**Cornerstone:** ...

**Resident I:** Oh no, a storm's coming!

**Resident III:** It's just like last time. That means the rain is coming again.

The people are completely overcome with panic.

Scuffled footsteps and curses fill the atrium as people from outside are pushed into the already crowded outer perimeter.

They squeeze their limbs tight to their bodies, trying to take up as little space as possible.

**Resident II:** Ah, my foot! Who did that?!

**Female Stranger:** S-Sorry, we shouldn't have come in. We'll leave as soon as we can.

**Everecho:** ...

Memories resurface as Everecho watches the chaos from a corner.

She's seen scenes like these play out time and time again, in different cities, towns, and villages.

Fragile people, real people, doing all they can to survive.

**Everecho:** Stay. Please, survive.

**Cornerstone:** Wait.

**Cornerstone:** I could use my arcane skill to build a temporary mezzanine—shelter more people under the dome.

**Grundriss:** Know your limits, Cornerstone. Your hand is still injured.

**Grundriss:** Besides, there's no way we can anchor it properly in such a short time.

**Bkornblume:** Sorry to interrupt, but has anyone seen Everecho? I can't find her anywhere.

**Grundriss:** What?

The crowd quiets as eyes scour the atrium, but no one spots Everecho.

It dawns on them that they haven't seen her in some time.

**Resident II:** She's up there! In the sky!

**Grundriss:** Up where?

A resident points to the dome.

There, on the other side of the glass panes, flutters the hem of a familiar dress.

**Resident II:** Look, right above the dome!

**Cornerstone:** ...

**Cornerstone:** Everecho?

**Cornerstone:** Come back, Everecho! You'll vanish!

**Everecho:** I have lived a long, fulfilling life on this land.

**Everecho:** As for the future, I have friends to walk toward it in my place.

**Everecho:** Remember one another. Remember this land.

**Everecho:** And I, among the fading whispers, will always watch over you.

**Everecho:** I was never meant to be in the future, yet.

It's an era between ending and beginning.

An era yet to be defined.

An era of nothingness.

**Everecho:** I ask that you have faith in tomorrow.

**Everecho:** It all begins in the past, begins with the hopes you carry.

♫ The floating song murmurs sure ♫

♫ The riverbank whispers low to me ♫

**Everecho:** Goodbye, my dear friends.

Her song ends, yet the stones of the dome carry it on. And in their hum come beams of gold, warm and radiant.

Then, the golden light fades. And the rain falls.

The humming fades into the raindrops, and the world plunges into nothingness.

A long darkness.

...

A longer silence.

No one dares even breathe.

...

Then, they see it, a faint beam of light.

It glows brighter and brighter until it's utterly blinding.

Until it fills every space and crevice.

**Residents:** Whoa, look at that!

An intact dome.

A simple gray triangle, silently watching over them all.

And through it, a golden strand of light flows down to the earth and its people.

It embraces the dust floating in the air, filling the entire space and sealing it off from the downpour.

**Grundriss:** So beautiful. It's a miracle.

One by one, it caresses and kisses the residents' faces.

Until at last it gently flows to Cornerstone's feet.

**Cornerstone:** ...

Nothing remains but silence.

Silence.

Long-buried memories unearth themselves.

April daisies, swings, sketches of the Mediterranean, the wrinkled faces of her elders ...

It's as if it all existed solely for this moment, solely to point her to this final, singular, and everlasting light.

**Cornerstone:** ...

She lets her eyes fall shut and lets the quiet submerge her, just as she had as a child.

**Cornerstone:** I understand.

**Cornerstone:** You were always there, just waiting to be found.

She opens her eyes and smiles.

**Cornerstone:** No wonder you always felt like an old friend.

**Cornerstone:** It's as if we've known each other for a long, long time.
