## Story

A genius has decided to leave behind her orderly and familiar realm to dive into the chaotic currents of a new world.

She stands on the beach, lost in her thoughts, the waves gently lapping at her feet.

> **Choice 1：** Tap on her shoulder

**37:** Ah!

The sudden touch surprises her; she quickly turns around, her expression like a startled dolphin's.

**37:** What-what's wrong? I'm analyzing the ocean currents of the phenomenal world!

> **Choice 2：** Stand beside her

**37:** Vertin?

She notices your arrival, but her eyes are still fixed on the sea.

**37:** What's wrong? I'm calculating the perfect time to set sail.

> **Choice 1：** I know a person who only speaks in numbers

**37:** Really? I've had that idea before and brought it up with the Orator Congregation,

**37:** because the language of the phenomenal world was not precise enough, it often led to misunderstandings.

**37:** I'm surprised there's another great mind like mine in the phenomenal world. They must have a special number!

**37:** This question is unresolved.

**37:** The outside world is too murky, too distant from the essence.

**37:** My mind becomes clouded and dull, limiting the power of my arcane skills.
