6:00Clear

A Narrow Mountain Path

A Narrow Mountain Path

...

A cool drop of dew falls onto the musician's forehead, jolting him awake.

***

***

He returns to his senses and finds that he is already standing on the stone steps leading down the mountain.

Behind him, the path leading up is choked with weeds, unrecognizable. The Miemeng he spoke with is nowhere to be seen.

**Musician:** "Could it be that it was all just a dream?"

He feels the weight of his broken guqin still resting on his back.

**Musician:** "..."

He stands there quietly for a while, then turns to face the lush foliage of the mountain path and begins speaking to no one in particular—

**Musician:** "I ... I will go down and find somewhere peaceful to stay a while."

**Musician:** "I've heard of a place nearby called Pei City, beyond royal authority, where both arcanists and humans live."

The musician pauses.

**Musician:** "From now on, I'll continue to study this instrument."

**Musician:** "Before that, though, if I may have such fortune, I'd like to hear the piece my ancestor once played."

He hesitates, holding his breath while he waits.

...

...

There is only the sound of wind rustling the trees and grass.

**Musician:** "I understand."

The musician murmurs, as if having reached a decision. He takes the broken guqin from his back and carefully sets it on the stone steps.

**Musician:** "This instrument doesn't belong to me. It should be returned to its owner. If you are willing ..."

Unable to finish the sentence, he shakes his head and bows deeply toward the empty air.

Then, he straightens up and makes his descent down the mountain without looking back.

A Shabby Mountain Hut

***

***

The Miemeng's wings give a slight tremble as the newly repaired guqin settles onto the table. The fracture has been properly mended with an arcane skill, leaving behind only a faint seam.

**Getian:** "It truly is a fine instrument. The maker must have been meticulous, polishing it again and again to achieve such perfection."

**Getian:** "Only ..."

He lifts his wings once more. The guqin floats into the air, revealing its underside. He narrows his eyes, noticing that the pair of goose feet is not perfectly aligned.

**Getian:** "Using bone for the instrument's goose feet is uncommon. Perhaps this is the reason for the maker's slight flaw."

**Getian:** "Or perhaps his heart was heavy simply because these were his friend's remains?"

He shakes his head.

**Getian:** "Regardless, I do not see the bones of the maker."

**Getian:** "And you ... What did you think?"

Getian asks the bones. They offer no reply.

He lets out an almost imperceptible sigh. Left with no answer, he uses his imagination instead:

Many, many years ago, on a spring night, a Miemeng quietly descended the mountain, with no word to his kin.

He traversed mountains that seemed endless.

Crossed rivers that zigzagged and intertwined.

Passed human villages, from which came the barks of dogs and the crows of roosters.

Like the divine birds of legend, he flew ninety-nine thousand miles.

Yet he flew higher than the mountains where immortals dwell and farther than the crimson clouds that topped the peaks. Even the heaviest dew could not dampen his wings.

Only when the sky darkened did he stop to rest on an ordinary parasol tree.

The moon sank westward.

Humans and arcanists alike slept.

The forests, the fields, the mountains and rivers—the entire world was fast asleep.

...

Getian ponders: What, exactly, tempted him to stay?

**Getian:** "Perhaps ..."

**Getian:** "No. Surely not for some mortal joy."

**Getian:** "Or perhaps ..."

As if answering himself, he immediately denies it again.

**Getian:** "No. Not even for that piece, or the one who played it."

He looks down to the world below the mountain. The morning mist has not yet fully dispersed, but the silken trail of the Peilin River can already be seen glinting in the sunlight as it flows into the distance, toward unfamiliar towns, villages, lakes, and plains.

Separated by a thin veil of mountain mist, life unfolds: some are born, some die; some laugh, some weep.

**Getian:** "So fleeting, so hurried."

Like morning dew, with more days gone than to come.

**Getian:** "..."

**Getian:** "Perhaps that is precisely why it is worth cherishing."

The thought stirs his heart, beautiful and unforgettable.

**Getian:** "..."

After a long while, the Miemeng places the long-silent guqin before him once more.

Beyond the Mountains, Peilin River

***

***

A boat rocks upon the water.

The musician suddenly springs to his feet and looks toward the mountains.

**Musician:** "Sir, did you hear that? Someone's playing a guqin!"

With his sudden movement, the small boat lurches violently, and the boatman stumbles.

**Boatman:** "Hey, sit still! Don't go startlin' an old man like me!"

He clicks his tongue in annoyance and rows hard, steadying the boat.

**Boatman:** "You must be hearin' things. The Peilin River's runnin' high today. Can't hear nothin' but the water itself."

The musician does not reply, his gaze fixed on the mountains. A moment later, he suddenly points ahead and shouts excitedly—

**Musician:** "No, sir, someone really is playing a guqin! Look to the bank over there. Even the birds have taken flight!"

The boatman cranes his neck to peer into the distance, then sighs.

**Boatman:** "The sun's out, 'course the birds are lookin' for food. What's so strange about that?"

**Musician:** "But ..."

**Boatman:** "How loud could one person's playin' be, anyhow? You think it'd reach this far? And only you'd hear it?"

**Musician:** "Maybe it's an immortal who's playing?"

The words slip out of him.

**Boatman:** "..."

The boatman cannot fathom what strange spell has come over this young fellow he picked up on a whim.

**Boatman:** "Ain't no immortals in this world. 'Less you've seen one with yer own eyes?"

**Musician:** "I ..."

He opens his mouth, then sinks back down, defeated. He lowers his gaze to the shimmering surface of the Peilin River.

**Musician:** "It's nothing. I must have imagined it."

The boatman clicks his tongue again.

**Boatman:** "All this silly chatter. Just tell me where yer headed."

**Musician:** "I want to go to Pei City."

**Boatman:** "Pei City? An' where's that? I've been rowin' for more than thirty years and never heard of the place."
