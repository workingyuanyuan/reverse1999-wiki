The rain does not stop until nightfall. The dark clouds disperse, and the moon makes its quiet ascent.

In its light, a single drop of dew slides down a slender leaf, inching its way toward the tip.

**Getian:** "This is the place."

The Miemeng draws in his wings and lands in a clearing halfway up the mountain.

Last year's fallen leaves are soaked with dew, the new grass beneath yet to grow in. It is as soft as a blanket.

Cross the path, and the Shabby Hut is right ahead.

With a lift of his wings, his bone staff floats lightly into the air and taps at the door.

A Shabby Mountain Hut

Above the last pitiful dregs of firewood, the musician sprinkles the crushed tea leaves into water drawn from a mountain stream.

**Musician:** "Even though the water only boiled once ..."

The flame in the small clay stove slowly dims. He sighs and places down a pair of ceramic cups he fished out from some corner.

They're charmingly antique. Not of recent make. He fills them with the tea.

A cup is pushed toward the visitor.

**Getian:** "Thank you."

The Miemeng does not drink tea, yet still extends a wing to pull the cup closer. On a chilly spring night, the touch of warm water has a way of settling the heart.

**Getian:** "The aroma of this tea seems different from before."

The musician moves the tea set aside and sits down across from Getian.

**Musician:** "Though you live in the mountains, you seem far more familiar with the lands below than I'd expected."

Getian takes a cautious sniff of the tea before nudging it away.

**Getian:** "I take little interest in what goes on down below."

**Getian:** "But my kin left behind many records. Thus, I have some small knowledge."

**Due to your decision, you've grown more confused.**

> **Choice 2：** I have indeed heard a thing or two.

Getian cautiously inhales. Fresh, but with a hint of bitterness. Curious.

Perhaps because everyone brews it to their tastes, he thinks.

**Getian:** "The Miemengs ferry news from below, along with the latest tools and techniques."

**Getian:** "Thus, I have some knowledge."

**Getian:** "I have never ventured there myself, though. I can hardly claim familiarity."

**Getian:** "..."

The Miemeng shakes his head and changes the subject.

**Getian:** "Has your instrument been repaired?"

Following his gaze, the musician looks to the plain guqin at his side and gently presses a hand against the strings.

**Musician:** "There are no materials in the mountains. It will have to wait."

**Musician:** "A lamentable shame. I had hoped to play something for you."

**Getian:** "Pay it no mind. Your bones have healed well. I dare say your departure from this mountain is approaching."

**Musician:** "Yes ..."

He stares absently at the wavering candlelight.

\*crack\*

In the silence, the lonely wick flickers and crackles.

The faint sound startles the musician, returning him from his momentary stupor. He immediately smiles and turns toward Getian.

**Musician:** "I've imposed for too long. Should I stay any longer, I fear I'd be overstepping."

**Musician:** "The mountains are pleasant, but they are not where I belong. When I arrived, the river was still frozen. But spring has likely arrived in the lands below by now, and the flowers along the Peilin River should be in bloom."

**Musician:** "If I leave now and catch the east wind along the river, I might still make it back before summer. Perhaps even in time to ask a woodcutter for a fine piece of parasol wood."

**Getian:** "And what of your search for that legendary score? Will it continue?"

The musician shakes his head.

**Musician:** "This candlelight is far too dim. Would you mind brightening it a little?"

**Musician:** "The score, ah, yes, that was why I came into the mountains."

**Musician:** "The day you saved me, I watched the sky grow darker and darker, heard the howling of wolves surround me. With a broken leg and my instrument clutched in my hand, all I could do was sit beneath a tree."

**Musician:** "Even then, my thoughts were fixed on that legendary score."

**Musician:** "Oh, how I wished for an Immortal to descend from the heavens at that time, like your kin who once gifted my ancestor that score."

**Getian:** "There is no need to name us as such. Miemengs are no different from humans. We, too, will one day fade away."

**Musician:** "But to me, this truly is the blessing of an immortal!"

In the candlelight, the musician spreads his hands. His fingertips and palms are thick with calluses.

**Musician:** "Though I never found the score, these hands were spared. That alone is thanks to you."

**Getian:** "..."

The Miemeng gives an uneasy shake of his feathers.

**Getian:** "Fine. Call it what you will."

The musician smiles at Getian.

**Musician:** "But now that I think of it, after searching for so long, the score is probably not in the mountains, or perhaps I am simply not destined to find it. Such things can't be forced."

**Musician:** "Besides, I have already met an immortal on this journey, just as my ancestor once did. When I return and compose a new piece, who knows? It might be even greater than that one!"

**Getian:** "..."

The Miemeng thinks in silence, his talons lightly tapping the ground.

**Getian:** "You have made up your mind, so be it, but the path is dangerous at night. Come dawn, I will see you down the mountain."

**Getian:** "However, there is still one place amid these peaks that may hold clues about the score. Since you will leave tomorrow, perhaps you might come tonight and see for yourself."

#### Notes · Shabby Hut

A plain, low thatched hut, with a few slabs of bluestone weighing down the ridges of its roof. It belongs to one of Getian's kin, who descended the mountain before the two ever met, never to return. Now it has been made the home of a human musician who wandered into the mountain by chance.

#### Notes · Immortal

To humans, the Miemeng might as well be immortals. Arcanists with human faces and bird bodies, they dwell deep in mountains no ordinary mortal can reach, blessed with long lives. Yet what truly awes and tempts is their ability to alter one's bones, and with it, one's fate.
