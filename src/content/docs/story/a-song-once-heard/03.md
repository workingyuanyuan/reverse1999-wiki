## Story

22:20Cloudy

A Narrow Mountain Path

A Narrow Mountain Path

***

***

On this early spring night, in which the ice and snow have already melted but new greenery has yet to grow, it is quiet and lonely.

The musician carries his guqin on his back, following Getian along the mountain path toward the Temple. From afar, they look like two sprigs of duckweed, drifting in a boundless sea.

The candlelight is barely bright enough to illuminate the ground beneath their feet. Even with the Miemeng leading the way, the musician looks somewhat ragged, the night dew soaking his sleeves as he pushes through the waist-high grass.

When Getian stops once again to wait, the musician speaks up, apologizing.

**Musician:** "You saved my life, for which I'm already deeply grateful, and now I'm causing you even more trouble. It isn't right of me to put you through so much."

**Getian:** "Saving others is but a simple gesture; I never thought to ask anything of you in return. But you shared something with me I never expected. By that logic, it is I who owe you."

**Musician:** "Something you never expected?"

The musician ponders for a moment, then taps his head in realization.

**Musician:** "Oh, your kin who once dealt with my ancestor. After all these years, you still remember him. I suppose you were close?"

**Getian:** "Not really."

**Musician:** "Then, at least old acquaintances?"

**Getian:** "No."

**Musician:** "You can't mean you were complete strangers?"

The Miemeng gives a slight nod.

**Getian:** "We never met."

**Musician:** "Well ..."

Getian extends a wing, brushing aside a clump of white cogon grass blocking the musician's path.

**Getian:** "I am simply curious why he—or rather, they—descended the mountain."

**Getian:** "I have read every word of every record in the temple, yet found nothing of the answer I seek."

The Miemeng tilts his head toward him.

**Getian:** "You come from the lands below. I'd like to hear your answer."

**Musician:** "I see."

The musician suddenly understands.

**Musician:** "After you mentioned it last time, I gave it some more thought. To a Miemeng, centuries may pass in the blink of an eye, while the world down below has changed beyond recognition."

**Musician:** "After so many years of retelling, the details of the story are bound to become unclear. What I've shared is all I've heard. As for any further details, I truly don't know."

**Musician:** "Still, from the perspective of those below, what the story describes does make sense."

He pants as he climbs another stretch of steps.

**Musician:** "Humans may differ in temperament and appearance, but in the end, everyone living in this world wants for something. That never changes."

**Musician:** "Some seek power. Some seek wealth. Some seek generational fame. Others seek everlasting glory."

**Musician:** "And, of course, some seek a peerless score."

He can't help but smile, pointing at himself.

**Musician:** "I risked my life climbing the mountain for it. If your kin truly lingered in the human world for the same reason, that would be understandable."

**Musician:** "Of course, this is just the superficial view of a mere mortal such as myself."

**Musician:** "And you, I mean, immortals—do they also have things they seek?"

**Getian:** "..."

This time, the Miemeng does not correct his choice of words.

**Getian:** "Very well. Since you've spoken frankly of your own matters, I shall also be truthful with you about my concerns."

The musician lifts his head to look at him. The moonlight casts its silver glow on the Miemeng's face, lost in thought.

***

Like humans, Miemengs developed different dispositions over the years ...

> **Choice 1：** The last Miemeng to descend the mountain in memory.

**Getian:** "Some were quiet and reserved."

One had a favorite camphor tree. On moonlit nights, Getian would see him standing silently on the highest branch, wings outstretched.

Getian assumed he likely wouldn't leave.

**Getian:** "And others were excessively curious. I knew one who would bring back all manner of human oddities from the lands below—oddities that would enrapture him day and night."

Passing by his dwelling, Getian had thought to himself: sooner or later.

**Due to your decision, you've grown more confused.**

> **Choice 2：** The first Miemeng to descend the mountain in memory.

**Getian:** "Some were excessively curious."

One would bring back all manner of human oddities from the lands below—oddities that would enrapture him day and night.

Passing by his dwelling, Getian had thought to himself: sooner or later.

**Getian:** "And some were quiet and reserved."

One had a favorite camphor tree. On moonlit nights, Getian would see him standing silently on the highest branch, wings outstretched.

Getian assumed he likely wouldn't leave.

**Getian:** "And some Miemengs were mischievous and fond of pranks."

One such bird loved to laugh. Getian can't recall his face, only his cackling when he startled his kin by suddenly flying up from below a cliff.

**Getian:** "Later, they all descended the mountain, never to return."

**Getian:** "All I recovered were their bones. Bones that gave me no answers."

**Getian:** "I read of where they went after leaving the mountain, whom they met, and the places they passed. But ..."

The Miemeng hesitates, his feathers trembling slightly.

**Getian:** "I still do not know why they left."

**Musician:** "It doesn't sound completely unsolvable, though."

The musician pushes aside a clump of mugwort and takes a deep breath. He smiles at Getian.

**Musician:** "Life in the mountains may be peaceful, but your kin must have longed for something important deep in their hearts. Important enough to give up their longevity."

**Musician:** "Longevity ... For those below the mountain, it is often a pursuit. But to your kind, it's merely a bargaining chip to be exchanged."

**Musician:** "..."

**Musician:** "In the lands below, some work to preserve life through any means, no matter how shameful. Yet there are also those willing to sacrifice themselves for something greater."

**Musician:** "As for what that something is ..."

The musician thinks it over again and again, then shakes his head.

**Musician:** "Well, I am but a human from below. I can't possibly grasp how the Miemengs in the mountains think."

He adjusts the guqin on his back. The instrument is nearly too heavy for him to carry.

Fortunately, the temple is just up ahead.

#### Notes · Temple

Records of the Miemeng clan's history since their arrival in the central plains are kept in the Temple of Getian. If an archived copy of the lost score still exists somewhere on the mountain, it may well be there.
