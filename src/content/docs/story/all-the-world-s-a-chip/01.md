Indoors

11:20

Inside a dim room, in front of a TV.

**???:** "... % ... Welcome ... a ... &# ..."

The signal is always poor on the Westside.

The rusted, twisting copper wires add a layer of buzzing static over the flickering, unclear face of a man.

No one can enjoy TV like this.

Straighten the antenna

***

***

A few rough twists straighten the antenna. The man on the screen begins to emerge from the fog of static.

**???:** "Today is August 19th, your ... Jax Bailey ... is back with another ... of Witch for a Day ..."

**Jax Bailey:** "To celebrate our anniversary special, we're paying a visit to our most beloved 'witch' of all."

**Jax Bailey:** "The one who kisses her chains, the undefeated escape artist, forever blessed by Lady Luck and her golden coin—"

Every eye is now glued to the flickering screen. At the host's words, their throats tighten. The name escapes their lips in unison.

**Jax Bailey:** "That's right. It's none other than Ms. Centurion!"

**Jax Bailey:** "Her luck, her confidence, and her courage have won over the hearts of all our viewers."

**Jax Bailey:** "Even now, when times are tough, her spirit remains unbroken. That's why her fans, from every corner of this great country, place their hopes in her."

**Jax Bailey:** "Before we put our 'witch' back in her cage, we've arranged a little meet-and-greet."

The camera pushes through the clutter, its movement producing a soft rustle, like an enormous snake weaving through a crowd.

It pushes on until it finds its destination and stumbles into a sudden patch of light.

Backstage

***

11:25

A room coated in dark red velvet, stacked full of props and accessories. And there, in the midst of it all, are three people: two adults and a child.

Centurion, with two of her fans.

She flashes a showman's smile as she spots the camera, then gestures toward a shy, brown-skinned girl, signaling the host to talk to the girl first.

**Jax Bailey:** "Oh, looks like one eager young fan has already found our star. She's a bit shaky on her feet but very sweet."

The girl flinches at the camera. Her small hands twist the fabric of her beige dress.

**Jax Bailey:** "Don't worry, kiddo. No need to be so nervous. Would you tell us your name? I love your dress. It's darling, isn't it? Is it new?"

**???:** "I-I'm Milagro. They call me M-Mila."

**Mila:** "The dress? Oh, it is new! But the fabric is old. Uncle Joaquin made it into a dress for me."

**Jax Bailey:** "Looks brand-new to me, and it suits you so well, Mila. Now, let's say hello to your hometown! Which little slice of America will be cheering for you tonight? And is that your father with you?"

Mila glances at the man beside her, dressed all in black with a matching hat pulled low.

Centurion doesn't need to wait for an answer. He doesn't look a thing like her.

As the camera approaches, he tightens his grip on Mila's hand.

**Mila:** "Oh, ah, yes, that's my papá. We're from around here, Westside actually, and we both love watching Centurion."

**Mila:** "When Witch for a Day airs, José, Jasmine, and I always watch it in the thrift shop. At first, it was just us, and then, some grown-ups started watching with us, because they've got lots more free time during the day."

**Mila:** "Everyone says that la Señorita Centurion is a superstar. Nothing can keep her down."

**Mila:** "They also say she represents hope for people like us. Someone to be proud of. One day, I want to be brave and lucky like her."

She stumbles over every word, almost too shy to continue, and looks up at Centurion with admiration in her eyes.

Centurion crouches down and wraps Mila in a hug.

**Centurion:** "You already are."

**Centurion:** "I was just telling Mila that coming all this way to see me makes her the sweetest, the bravest ..."

**Centurion:** "What else did I say? Remind me, Mila."

Starstruck, Mila's cheeks flush as she tries to stammer out words.

**Mila:** "The luckiest and the most blessed girl in the whole neighborhood, just like Centurion."

**Centurion:** "Exactly, just like me. So, remember, you can go anywhere and hit the jackpot!"

Centurion tilts her head to flash a winning smile, her open palm extended in invitation.

The surprise washes slowly over the girl's face. Her hand begins to rise, then hesitates as she looks back toward the silent man.

A tension hangs in the air.

**Jax Bailey:** "What are you waiting for, little girl? The show's about to start. One lucky high-five from her may just double your fortunes."

**Mila:** "Actually, I was wondering ..."

She looks down; her eyes lock on the shining coin dangling over Centurion's chest.

**Mila:** "Could I touch it, please?"

**Mila:** "Everyone says your luck comes from that coin."

**Mila:** "We don't have much money. I thought if I touched it, maybe some luck would rub off on us."

Sonoran Desert

***

12:00

A sheer cliff of red sandstone erupts from the harsh desert. Wind and sand have sculpted it into this massive, scarred form.

The sun's intense rays beat down on the ground and the artificial lake far below, but they cannot beat down the spirit of the crowd.

They are waiting.

Waiting for the "witch" to take her death-defying leap.

**Jax Bailey:** "Our one and only queen, are you ready to reclaim your throne?"

**Centurion:** "You know I'd never let you down, my dears! The ratings are about to skyrocket once again!"

Centurion gives a playful wink to the camera. She raises her hands, displaying the binding chains to the crowd below. They reply with a thunder of cheers.

She then turns and steps into a tall, narrow iron box.

**Jax Bailey:** "Just look at her. Not a trace of fear."

Two strong men heave the iron door shut.

Just as the last light fades, Centurion blows a kiss toward the camera.

Inside the Box

***

***

A loud, grinding noise fills the air as the box is inched toward the cliff edge. Then, it stops.

In that suspended silence, Centurion knows: this is it.

She allows herself to fall backward.

**Audience:** "Woo!"

Weightlessness takes her. The crowd's screams blend fear with excitement. And then, that familiar rush.

Danger is a wild beast she has tamed.

Then, as she always does, she reaches for the coin at her chest, to the familiar roughness of the centurion's face.

But something's wrong.

**Centurion:** "Huh?"

The texture is off. She glances down, angling her chest into a narrow band of light in falling dark.

The shape and weight are correct, but her Roman officer now bears a jester's hat.

And he wears a new face. No longer bearing sharp Romanesque features, he looks a little like Mila's father.

\*bang\*

The realization hits her at the same time as she hits the water. Accompanied by screams and the crashing of iron on water, the "witch" in the box plunges into the lake.
