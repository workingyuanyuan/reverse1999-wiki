Westside Outskirts

17:00

She takes off after him, running through the dusty outskirts of town.

Familiar sights flash past: the narrow, weeping river, the rundown pale cabin, and the distant blur of the city further on.

They reach the Westside.

**Passerby I:** "Huh? What's going on?"

The man pushes through crowds and slips into an alleyway. Centurion follows, breathing hard. People stop working to watch.

**???:** "Out of the way!"

**Fruit Vendor:** "¡Idiota! You just knocked over my stand!"

The breaking of wood and the noise of movement draw attention from all around. More residents lean out of windows to see. Someone in the crowd recognizes Centurion.

**Passerby II:** "Wait a second. Is that Centurion? Is she filming something for her show?"

**Centurion:** "\*pant\* Stop, you coin thief!"

**Passerby III:** "Hold on. Look at her."

Some onlookers seem to catch an idea of what's happening. Others just enjoy the spectacle. But the tension spreads, pulling everyone in like they are part of it.

**Passerby IV:** "Let's go, Centurion! You've got this!"

**Passerby V:** "Run! Don't let him escape!"

**Fruit Vendor:** "Serves you right for messing with my stuff. Take that!"

A rotten tomato falls near the man's foot, almost hitting him.

After the first tomato, other things fly from the crowd: dried corn, cans, a broken bulb ... More trash follows.

**???:** "What the ... S\*\*t!"

The man ducks and dodges, messy but quick. He avoids most of it, only stumbling once on a banana peel.

Their efforts are not entirely helpful. A chair with a missing leg flies past Centurion's head as she just barely manages to slide beneath it.

**Passerby II:** "Hey! Ever heard of aiming?"

**Passerby III:** "Not my fault! I'm no Pancho Villa!"

The noise grows louder. People start running behind Centurion. Others shout her name. She keeps running, waving at her fans watching from above.

**Centurion:** "Thanks for the help, my dears!"

Westside Alley

17:15

The first evening light has brushed over the somber face of the "Mother" just as Mila reaches the alley's entrance.

***

***

She walks to the mural, kneels, and lets the sunset fall over her. She closes her eyes and prays softly.

**Mila:** "Dear 'Mother,' please listen. I hope Señorita Centurion catches that man soon. I want to make up for what I did."

**Mila:** "Please be kind. If you help me with this, I'll do anything."

Before she finishes, noises rise up behind her.

Confused, Mila stands and brushes off her skirt. She turns just as a man runs straight at her.

**Mila:** "Wh—"

A hard force hits her neck, pushing her toward the wall. They nearly crash into the mural.

The world spins. Pressure tightens on her neck. Something cold and hard presses against her forehead, and through the ringing in her ears, a voice she knows cuts the air.

**Centurion:** "Mila, no!"

Mila looks up and sees Centurion, out of breath, surrounded by a gawking crowd.

Centurion and the townspeople have chased the man into this alley. No one knew that she was here.

The miracle she asked for came all too soon.

In a panic, the man locks his arm around Mila, pressing a gun to her head, as he gasps for breath.

**Mila:** "Huh? What's happening? Ah!"

Mila tries to fight back, baring her teeth as she tries to reach his arm. In response, he hits her in the head with his gun.

**Centurion:** "¡Oye! What kind of coward hits a child?"

Hearing this, the man lifts his head. His face is tired, pale, and half-wild.

The face is familiar. She locks eyes with the same strange fake guardian who had accompanied Mila before.

**Fake Guardian:** "What's cowardly is you people coming here and taking what's ours. This is your fault."

The crowd erupts. Someone even makes a move to rush in and attack him, but Centurion raises a hand to stop them.

The fake guardian does not thank her. He pulls Mila back, step by step.

His whole body shakes. His eyes shine with a frantic, unnatural light.

**Fake Guardian:** "Ha, haha, hahahahaha! Stop pretending, Centurion!"

**Fake Guardian:** "If you vermin hadn't come here and Taken Our Jobs, we wouldn't be stuck in this dump."

**Fake Guardian:** "But that stupid show still puts people like you on a pedestal. Ha, everyone acts like you're some kind of lucky charm, a light in their sad, pathetic lives."

**Fake Guardian:** "But it was your fault we were miserable in the first place. You took everything from us, and yet you get treated like a hero."

**Fake Guardian:** "No one talks about how many of us lost our livelihoods. No one sees our kids going hungry. Nobody cares. This world is rotten."

The man shoves the gun into his pocket. He holds out a hand and counts on trembling fingers.

**Fake Guardian:** "Roberts, Marshall, Miller, Harrison, and so many others. I'm doing this for all of them."

**Fake Guardian:** "I'm fixing things, bringing back what's fair. I'm doing what's right."

His conviction is obvious, even though he's barely making sense to anyone but himself.

Centurion can almost feel the gaze of the "Mother" looking down on him with pity, though her eyes are still a blank space.

The reality of the moment and the crowd's stunned silence take the fire out of his eyes, but the madness remains.

**Fake Guardian:** He pulls a thin cord from around his neck. A gold coin dangles from it.

She sees the glinting Roman officer's profile. It's the genuine coin.

**Fake Guardian:** "I know your secret, you wicked witch!"

**Fake Guardian:** "All your fame and fortune, your good luck, comes from this pretty coin, doesn't it?"

**Fake Guardian:** "People like you don't deserve that kind of luck. The more praise you get, the more this country rots at the root."

**Fake Guardian:** "I'm just cleaning up the mess you made, putting fortune back where it belongs."

The madman breathes out slowly. A shaky pride lights his eyes.

**Fake Guardian:** "Thought you'd be finished without this coin. Figured I could sell it. Shame!"

**Fake Guardian:** "But it came to me. Luck's still on my side. Even this weird portrait of yours agrees."

**Centurion:** "You're wrong."

Centurion stops. Her throat feels tight.

**Centurion:** "The luck was never in the coin."

**Mila:** "That's right! It's like you never even watched her show!"

Mila struggles to answer. The man sneers and smacks her over the head.

This time Mila only whimpers, then goes quiet.

Centurion's hands clench into fists.

**Fake Guardian:** "Oh, really? Heh."

He tightens his grip on Mila, puts the coin away, and digs into his pocket with his free hand.

He pulls out his revolver and points it squarely at Centurion.
