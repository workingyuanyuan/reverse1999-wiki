A lonely cabin stands beside a narrow, dirty river, near a mint that releases small clouds of white steam.

Her trail leads her to this cabin. Her fingers trace the door's worn white paint.

> **Choice 1：** Knock

**Centurion:** "Hello? Anybody home?"

The pale door doesn't move. She looks around, finding nothing but wild grass that gently sways with the breeze.

Until, after a wait that almost feels too long, the door creaks open by a sliver, revealing a messy-haired boy.

**Boy:** "Can I help you? Wait, you're Centurion from the TV!"

**Centurion:** "You got me, kid. Who else could find this place so quickly?"

**Centurion:** "I'm looking for Mila. She home?"

The boy says nothing, but his eyes seem to light up, becoming suddenly alert and cautious.

**???:** "Amador, is someone there?" A soft voice asks from inside.

She recognizes the voice immediately and calls in past the boy.

**Centurion:** "It's me! I've come to see you, Mila!"

Hurried footsteps approach, and a figure dressed in beige pushes past the boy to the door.

The girl stares up at her for a moment, then she rubs her eyes, as if still doubting whether she's awake or dreaming.

**Mila:** "¡Ay, madre mía! Did my wish really come true twice?"

Mila's Home

***

***

The soft smell of mint tea wafts up from an iron pot, its white steam curling in the warm light of the room.

**Mila:** "Please come in. We, um, still have some soup and bread, if you want."

Centurion politely declines the offer. She has more pressing matters.

Though Mila has been over the moon with excitement since she let Centurion in, as they sit together in the small living room, she becomes quiet and tense. Her brother, Amador, stands nearby, his eyes fixed on them.

Centurion is certain these two are not the thieves. Whatever else, she trusts that Mila would not have taken the lucky coin from her willingly.

**Centurion:** "Mila, the reason I'm here is—"

She calmly explains everything that happened after Mila left.

The girl's expression changes from shock to shame. She lowers her head and covers her face with her hands.

**Mila:** "I'm sorry. Amador told me not to go, and, and I went anyway. \*sob\*"

**Mila:** "I wanted to see your show because my brother and I wanted to leave here. You see how things are."

When she looks up, her eyes are wet. She glances around the room and bows her head again.

**Mila:** "We just didn't know what to do. So, I thought if I met you, you'd give me courage, like you do on TV."

**Mila:** "But my wish coming true, it only brought you trouble."

Tears start to fall. Amador shifts beside her. His hand reaches out but falls just short of her shoulder.

> **Choice 1：** Changing your luck isn't a bad thing.

**Centurion:** "So, I made your dream come true. What's wrong with that? Since when is a bit of luck a bad thing?"

Centurion leans in closer and wipes the tears from Mila's cheeks with a heartfelt smile.

**Centurion:** "But I worry that sometimes, when people see a lucky coin or a lucky girl, their first instinct is to use them for something bad."

**What you choose will echo in what follows.**

> **Choice 2：** Good luck and bad luck are just sides of a coin.

**Centurion:** "Please, don't cry, Mila. Luck is just like a coin. It can change with a flip."

**Centurion:** "Sometimes it lands one way, then the other. Trust me, I know."

**Centurion:** "Besides, without both sides, life would be pretty boring, don't you think?"

**What you choose will echo in what follows.**

Mila looks up at Centurion through her tears and sniffs. The messy streams of tears begin to dry.

Amador has kept silent the whole time, but as he refills Centurion's cup, his expression softens.

**Amador:** "There was a guy. We don't know him, but he showed up a while ago."

The words creak out from his lips.

**Centurion:** "Oh? What sort of guy?"

**Amador:** "It was after Mila told 'Mother' that she wanted to see your show. He showed up and said he could be her Guardian to get her in."

**Amador:** "Our parents went to California for work years ago. They left us behind to fend for ourselves. So ..."

**Mila:** "The man said he was a fan of yours too. He said we'd pretend to be a family for the day, go together, and come back."

**Centurion:** "Do you know where he came from? Or where he went after the show?"

Both siblings shake their heads.

Centurion leans over to rest her hand on her chin, thinking things through.

She takes out her false coin from her pocket.

**Centurion:** "How about this? Have you seen it before?"

Amador takes it. The golden foil on the coin is cracked and peeling. He studies it, then bites it lightly.

He recoils, face twisting as he spits out the taste.

**Amador:** "Lead and zinc!"

**Centurion:** "Really?"

**Amador:** "Yeah. It's a lead core mixed with zinc. They use them for templates sometimes, but this one has a cheap golden foil coating."

**Amador:** "I've done a few shifts at the mint across the river. Lots of guys there run side jobs, making counterfeits."

Mila looks at him, surprised, and Centurion turns to him with a sly grin.

**Amador:** "Don't look at me like that! I never did anything, but ..."

**Amador:** "Wait, you don't think—"

Centurion realizes the real gold coin might be closer than she thought.

**Centurion:** "Looks like I'll be paying the mint a visit. Hopefully, it'll be my last stop."

She stands up, practically leaping like a cat to her feet, when a hand tugs at her pant leg.

**Mila:** "Maybe I can help, Señorita Centurion."

**Mila:** "I could try to spot him for you."

**Amador:** "Absolutely not."

Her older brother says the words with a final protective authority.

**Amador:** "He could come after you, after us. I've seen what those counterfeiters can do."

**Amador:** "Last time was bad enough. I'm not letting you take that risk again."

**Mila:** "I ..."

Silence follows.

Mila looks both shy and hopeful. Centurion offers her an appreciative pat on the shoulder.

But her brother doesn't budge. He has every right to his concerns.

Centurion bends down and ruffles Mila's hair.

**Centurion:** "No worries, my little princess. Maybe there's another way."

**Centurion:** "Since the 'Mother' listened when you prayed before, why not pray again? This time for me?"

**Centurion:** "Ask her to help me catch that man quickly. I'm sure she won't refuse you."

Hearing this, Mila looks up, and the tight worry on her face softens a little.

**Mila:** "That's all I have to do?"

Centurion smiles.

**Centurion:** "That's it. It would mean a lot to me."
