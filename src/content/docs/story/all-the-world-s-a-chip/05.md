## Story

Mint Entrance

Mint Entrance

***

16:30

The mint is a squat, pale factory standing alone by the river.

She pauses at the entrance. A dark doorway opens before her, filled with mechanical sounds and hot, drifting steam.

Then, casting aside all concern, she steps inside.

Fog

Inside the Mint

***

***

Inside, rough, black machines spread out like entrails. Conveyor belts move forward with a constant clank.

Workers hammer, cast, and cool metal. One man lifts a glowing mold with tongs and plunges it into a water tank. It hisses as it sends up a cloud of vapor.

Thick white steam fills the air, making faces hard to see. Only hands move, hammering the coins as they flow down the line.

**Centurion:** "..."

No one speaks. The only sounds are metal striking metal and her footsteps as she walks past the rows.

**Centurion:** "How can I find him in all this steam?"

***

***

Centurion stops and clears her throat, then continues through the hot narrow space.

**Centurion:** "This is a real mint as far as I can tell."

**Centurion:** "But someone here has been making a special kind of counterfeit."

Her voice carries over the whirring and clanking of the conveyor belts. With every step, she speaks another sentence.

And as she does, an intangible force, like flowing water, wells up and spills out from within her.

**Centurion:** "It took the place of a lucky coin that belonged to the unluckiest person."

**Centurion:** "So unlucky that the charm turned into a curse."

The factory's steam still hangs in the air. The catlike woman goes still, her gaze sweeping the area like a leopard in search of her prey.

That force moves through the tight air, blending with the steam, searching.

**Centurion:** "Now, whoever holds it carries that curse. For instance—"

**Centurion:** "Right now, the unlucky person who still managed to survive has come back to haunt him."

She finds something, or rather, she senses it.

Suddenly, shouts and curses come from one corner of the mint.

Centurion looks over. A man's strap is caught on an automatic hook, pulling him into the air.

**Worker I:** "¡Dios mío! Someone's caught on the f\*\*king hook!"

**Worker II:** "Someone turn that thing off!"

**???:** "Damn it! Damn it!"

Chaos breaks out in the mint. Centurion leaps forward, running toward the commotion.

Tap, tap, tap. Her heels click as she weaves around startled workers.

The man sees her coming and struggles wildly to break free. There is a snap as he frees himself and falls to the floor.

**Worker I:** "Are you crazy? Do you want to get yourself killed?!"

Ignoring the shouts around him, he gets up and runs for the door.

**Centurion:** "Whoa!"

Centurion is on the chase immediately.

She squeezes between tight conveyor belts and vaults over the cluttered floor. After a few running steps, she is almost close enough to touch his back.

Only for him to spin around as if he expected her.

A loud bang. A bullet hits a metal barrel beside her. Silver blanks spill out like a waterfall, clattering over the ground in a cascade of noise.

Centurion steps on one of the blanks in the noisy metal shower and nearly loses her balance.

**Centurion:** "That was too close. But there's no cheating in this game."

When she looks up, the man is already out the door, sprinting toward the city.
