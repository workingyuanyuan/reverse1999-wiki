## Story

15:00Clear

Westside Alley

Two Days Later

Westside Alley

***

***

15:00

**Drunk Man:** "\*burp\* Hey, Señorita, could you spare a nickel? I need one more sip of mezcal. Just one."

At the alley's end, a drunk man wearing a felt hat lies on the ground. His right hand stretches out, a five-cent coin loosely held between his fingers.

He mumbles something, and the coin slips from his hand, rolling down the alley until it stops at a pair of black boots standing at the entrance.

> **Choice 1：** Pick up the coin

The owner of the boots picks up the coin and follows its path back toward the drunk man.

**Centurion:** "Ha! And that makes ten cents. Now I can call Jax. Bet they're all crying over me."

**Centurion:** "Sorry, buddy. Next time I win big, I'll buy you a hundred bottles."

Centurion looks up. There is an unfinished mural above the drunk man. It shows a middle-aged woman dressed in blue jeans and a blue sweater.

***

***

Her face is incomplete. She wears A Crown of Roses sprinkled with gold dust. Her arms are raised, leading a crowd forward.

Centurion follows the direction the painted woman points to. The afternoon sun throws the shadows of trees across yellow walls, where dusty cars are parked at the corner. Nearby, three children are kicking around a flat tire.

More people arrive at the alley's end. Some glance toward Centurion curiously. Others ignore her and quietly pray before the mural.

Their prayers are soft and hard to catch. Centurion moves closer, trying to catch the words.

**Lanky Woman:** "May our 'Mother' bless us. Help my Alicia to do well in school."

**Bulky Woman:** "You might want to pray that man of yours makes it home by midnight."

**Sad Young Woman:** "No, Dagoberto. Our 'Mother' will definitely help you find a new job here, then we can stay together."

**Sad Young Man:** "I want to believe, Blanca. I really do, but ..."

People bow their heads, whispering toward the portrait of the woman above them, then leave.

Only the "Mother" remains, fixed in her grace, a refuge for any who look up.

**Centurion:** "Hmm, 'Mother' ..."

Something brushes against Centurion's leg.

**Small Boy:** "Why are you just standing there? Aren't you going to pray?"

**Small Boy:** "Grandma says you have to ask her, ask 'Mother' for luck, for miracles."

**Small Boy:** "She really listens. Without her, life is hard. That's what my grandma says anyway."

Centurion crouches down and looks at the little boy, then at the mural. She can feel the eyes of the other petitioners on her now.

> **Choice 1：** It's not enough.

**Centurion:** "Sure, but it's not enough."

**Centurion:** "When life gets tough, that's when people need miracles most."

**Centurion:** "But, you can't just wait around for miracles. You have to be diligent, take action yourself to make her happy."

She winks at the boy.

**What you choose will echo in what follows.**

> **Choice 2：** Your grandma's right.

**Centurion:** "She's smart. I mean, who wouldn't want a miracle to land in their lap?"

**Centurion:** "Life is hard enough as it is. Sometimes all people need is something to hope for, to believe in."

She studies the woman in the mural. There's a strength and sturdiness to her, yet kindness too. If anyone could intercede on their behalf, it might just be her.

**What you choose will echo in what follows.**

**Small Boy:** The boy nods slightly.

**Drunk Man:** "So, you're a believer then. How about showing a little charity? Just enough for one more sip."

**Drunk Man:** "I painted her, hehe, you know."

**Drunk Man:** "It's not finished yet, but with another drink, I can sober up."

It's obvious to everyone around that he's spouting off nonsense.

But that just makes the stakes higher.

> **Choice 1：** Return the coin to the drunk man

Centurion flips the coin, then places it, along with one of her own five cents, into the man's open hand.

**Centurion:** "To your health then. I'll look forward to seeing it finished."

His eyes focus on the coins. He pinches them tight, staggers to his feet, and wobbles out of the alley.

Westside

***

***

15:15

Clouds gather. The pale sun hits a wooden church steeple, casting a sharp shadow on the gray road.

Few cars pass by. Women lean out of windows, beating dusty quilts and shouting at the kids playing by the road.

Centurion walks along the sidewalk, admiring the graffiti and murals alike.

They depict entwined eagles and snakes, the resolute, sorrowful face of Zapata, and inspiring historical scenes.

Some are finished. Others are not. The young painters pause when they see her and call out greetings.

**Painters:** They call out to her in Caló.

**Centurion:** She responds in kind.

She attempts to mimic their accent but doesn't quite pass for local, at least judging by their laughter in response.

She shrugs and walks over.

**Centurion:** "Hey, since we're all buddies now, y'know of a girl called Mila? She'd be about seven or eight. She was wearing a beige dress last I saw her."

The painters, with their colorful palettes in hand, exchange glances.

**Painter I:** "Plenty of Milas around, and beige dresses too. We've got a couple right over there."

Two girls step forward eagerly, introducing themselves.

**Girls:** "I'm Mila, and she's Mila too. Which one of us are you looking for?"

Centurion smiles and shakes her head.

**Centurion:** "No, and ah, no. But thanks anyways, my dears."

**Girls:** "You're welcome. But you look familiar ..."

Knowing she won't find her Mila here, Centurion moves on, heading deeper into the neighborhood.

She walks past further walls of vivid murals, across faded asphalt, and through the metal forest of yellow and gray. Past the pharmacy, the butcher, the fruit stand, and the laundromat.

Here, people labor day in and day out, shop owners hawk their wares, and people maintain their fragile lives together, while occasionally casting curious glances outward.

Centurion notices a curious little boy starting to follow her.

**Centurion:** "Hmm? You a fan of mine?"

**Curious Boy:** "N-No. I just ... You're ... You look different."

She pats his head and continues through the neighborhood, but it doesn't take long before more and more people notice her.

**Passerby I:** "Hey, she looks familiar."

**Passerby II:** "Wait, let me put on my glasses. Hmm, isn't she from Witch for a Day?"

**Passerby III:** "¡Ay! Could it be? That's the Queen of Escapes, Centurion!"

First one, then two, then a dozen, until recognition fills the eyes of every person present. And without thought, each one begins to fall into step behind her like a line of ducks.

**Passerby I:** "Señorita! Can I get your autograph?"

**Passerby II:** "Oh, let me see. Where are my glasses? Hey, it really is her!"

**Fruit Vendor:** "¡Oye! Mind the fruit! Watch it!"

Centurion turns and waves with her trademark ease. Her attention sends a wave of excitement rippling through the crowd.

**Centurion:** "Thank you, thank you, everyone! I just love the Westside! Everything here is so alive! What's not to love?"

The crowd gets even more worked up. Cheers bubble up like shaken cans of cola.

**Passerby III:** "Centurion! We love you!"

**Centurion:** "Oh, I know you do, my dears! But can you answer me one thing?"

**Passersby:** "Yeah!"

**Centurion:** "Alright then. You all know Witch for a Day, right? Well, I'm looking for a little fan of mine who loves it too. A girl named Mila, in a beige dress. Anyone know her?"

Hands shoot up from the crowd.

**Passerby I:** "She must be my daughter! She watches Witch for a Day all the time while she's taking care of my granddaughter and doing the chores!"

**Passerby II:** "She said she's looking for a little girl, not a grown woman!"

A few other girls push forward, jumping to be seen.

**Girl I:** "My name is Mila, and I love watching Witch for a Day! Are you looking for me?"

**Girl II:** "Stop lying. You don't even know when Witch for a Day airs! I must be the one she's looking for!"

The jostling fans go from excited to heated. Girls pile up at the front on the verge of a scuffle. Seeing this, Centurion tries to take control of the situation.

**Centurion:** "So happy you're all my fans! There might be lots of Milas here, but I'm trying to find the little girl who came to see us film the show earlier today."

The crowd falls silent.

After a long pause, a drunken voice slurs out words from within the crowd. All eyes turn, and the mass of people parts, creating a narrow aisle that leads straight to the source.

**Drunk Man:** "\*burp\* Told you my head clears up when I drink."

**Drunk Man:** "I know who you're looking for. She's my niece, Mila Félix. She came by earlier to ask 'Mother' for a wish. She wanted to see Witch for a Day live. \*burp\* Even said she wanted to touch your lucky coin."

**Drunk Man:** "\*burp\* I even made her that beige dress you're talking about."
