## Story

17:20

Westside Alley

Westside Alley

***

***

17:20

The evening light continues to wash over the Westside, warming every corner it touches—

Even the cold barrel of the gun pointed at Centurion's head.

***

***

**Fake Guardian:** "If Lady Luck really is on your side ..."

The madman grins. With one hand, he flicks open the cylinder and turns it toward her.

**Fake Guardian:** "Let's see how she holds up her end of the bargain."

He licks his lips, lifts the gun, and lets the bullets fall free from the cylinder.

Brass rounds tumble over the asphalt, all except for one. Using his thumb, he holds it in and pushes it back into place.

**Fake Guardian:** "I've got your lucky coin, and what have you got? A cheap replacement. Fitting, isn't it?"

**Fake Guardian:** "Just like your whole ridiculous life, every bit of luck that you stole from us."

He spins the cylinder again, then snaps it shut with a sharp flick of his wrist.

**Fake Guardian:** "So, what do you say? Do you have the guts?"

The dry desert air is still. Centurion looks into his eyes. She senses the gaze of every person in the street is on them now.

No one shouts. No one dares move. The entire town waits silently. For a moment of triumph or shame, a battle fought with a single bullet.

> **Choice 1：** Trust in fate

A chill runs through her. A feeling rises in her throat: to trust in herself, to trust in fate.

**Centurion:** "Coin or no coin, I'll come out clean. So go ahead. Make my day."

**What you choose will echo in what follows.**

> **Choice 2：** Look at the gold coin

Her eyes linger on the coin hanging from his neck. It shines, bright and glaring—a tangible symbol of all the luck she's ever had.

But she knows that even without that coin, a "moment" is coming. Everything comes down to this, a gamble she can't resist.

**Centurion:** "Let's play your game then. I'm feeling lucky."

**What you choose will echo in what follows.**

She spares a reassuring glance toward Mila. The girl's eyes are alight with fear and wonder.

She is hoping Centurion will win, hoping her faith and her prayers will be enough.

**Fake Guardian:** "Then, let's do this."

He hesitates only a second before he places the revolver against his own head. Then, without a blink, he pulls the trigger.

\*click\* The man laughs. The sound falls somewhere between relief and mania.

He throws the gun to Centurion. It spins through the air toward her. She catches it in a single smooth motion.

**Fake Guardian:** "Your turn, witch."

She lets the revolver rest in her hand, weighing what might be her final thoughts, then presses it to her own temple without hesitation.

\*click\* A sound as dry as a desert wind.

**Centurion:** "Hah."

The man's lips curl into a sneer.

**Fake Guardian:** "Not so fast. This isn't over yet."

The sun sinks lower, its last golden rays cutting across the alley, splitting light from shadow.

All the people in the street hold their breath, eyes fixed on the two as they pass the eight-shot revolver from hand to hand. Even the shadows themselves seem to be playing a silent witness to their game of life and death.

Only one sound fills the alley on repeat, clean, mechanical, pure.

\*click\*

\*click\*

\*click\*

A soft wind stirs the awnings and window shutters. Cold sweat beads down the man's unblemished forehead.

Two chambers left.

**Fake Guardian:** "Ha, haha, hahahahahaha! The Queen of Escapes? What a laugh!"

**Fake Guardian:** "Just as I suspected, with this coin, luck is on my side now!"

The man laughs wildly as he tosses the gun to Centurion.

**Centurion:** "Well, now we're down to the long odds. You or me, that's how it's got to end."

It's her turn.

Centurion lifts the revolver to her head. She looks at the coin around his neck. Then she looks at Mila, and a thought occurs.

**Centurion:** "He'll go back on his word. Even if my brains are still in my head in a second, he'll just shoot Mila or someone else."

She needs a different ending to this story. A strange feeling washes over her.

That coin had been her lucky charm. But did that luck come from it or somewhere else?

Thoughts rush in, dizzying. For a moment, she remembers winning before.

Thousands of slot machines spitting out gold tokens, the glare of casino lights, the desert sun, and the old man's face shattering in sunlight. These images blend with the coin, with the halo over the "Mother's" head.

***

***

**Centurion:** "I hope this is the last round."

\*bang\* The firing pin strikes. A bullet flies.

Primal instinct kicks in. Without even a fraction of a second to spare, Centurion tilts her head, just enough for the red-hot bullet to graze her scalp and slice through a few hairs.

The lead shot continues onward, striking obliquely against the wall of the alley before it ricochets beneath the hands of the saint herself.

The first gasps of surprise have barely had time to register what happened before the bullet rips through the man's body.
