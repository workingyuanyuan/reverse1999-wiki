## Story

12:00

Underwater

Underwater

***

***

Sight and sound blur into a thick, dark chaos. A few stray beams of light manage to penetrate, only to be twisted and scattered by the turbulence of the water.

Pressure pushes into her from all sides.

**Centurion:** "Argh ..."

The shock of the impact has forced the air from her lungs.

Now an immense rush of water invades her mouth and nose in its place.

**Centurion:** "\*cough\* Ugh!"

A sharp, icy pain pierces her chest as the water rushes down her throat and into her lungs.

But the brisk rush of adrenaline focuses her racing mind.

She needs the pain.

**Centurion:** "Ahh!"

Or rather, it's a pain she has long expected.

She stops resisting. She allows herself to cough, then forces her racing heart to calm, pulling herself back from the edge of a blackout.

She twists her wrists, using the water's weight to work the chains loose.

**Centurion:** \*exhale\*

The more deadly the situation, the calmer she becomes.

After all, this is a rare, truly genuine "performance."

Centurion flexes her wrists, resetting bones that have dislocated. Her fingers explore the iron walls, searching for a weak spot.

**Centurion:** "Not here. No gap wide enough around."

**Centurion:** "Unless I've landed right on the lakebed. Then there'd be a sharp rock nearby, probably at two o'clock from here."

**Centurion:** "Come on, you mangy tin can. These corners are way too thick."

She searches every angle of the box but finds no clear way out.

Her lungs feel like they're on fire, burning up the last vestiges of oxygen in her system.

But it comes with a new exhilaration.

It rushes from her spine to her limbs, forcing her to pause and fully savor the feeling.

In the darkness, she relaxes her body, floating within the water-filled iron box.

***

***

Sound inside the iron box comes through heavy and muffled. A faint light seeps through the seams around the door, refracting and projecting fractured patterns onto the walls.

Her eyes track the light, up, to the side, and down toward the floor.

Wait.

Near the bottom, a sliver of light flickers. It bends where it meets a specific metal plate.

A hidden door! They managed to slip a new trick into her act!

**Centurion:** "Ha! Perfect."

She pushes off the wall, pressing upward against the ceiling.

She twists around in the water and kicks down hard at the metal plate.

With a dull thud, blinding light and water rush toward her.

Clear

12:09

**Centurion:** "\*pant\* Heh. Haha, hahahahahaha!"

The sun lavishes its light and warmth upon the blue water as she rises from the lake.

Centurion brushes wet hair from her face as she looks out over an empty expanse of the vast Sonoran Desert.

She does not end up surfacing where she had originally planned. Instead, she finds herself emerging next to a patch of saguaro cacti on the far shore.

**Centurion:** "What a scene! Amazing, or a complete mess."

She winces, rubbing her ankles.

**Centurion:** "Good thing it's the 19th, or I'd have been done for."

**Centurion:** "Must be why I've always liked it, 19, my Lucky Number. Seems like it likes me too."

The memory resurfaces now, dark and vivid.

She shakes the water from her hands, pulls the chain from her neck, and holds the false coin up to the light, squinting at it for a closer look.

The sun reveals the whole truth.

The jester's face is covered in a thin golden foil. It looks stiff and wrinkled, resembling the old man who traded her the potion for her coin.

She rubs her thumb over the tiny face. The foil cracks and flakes away. Underneath, it is a dull gray metal.

Like the old man's shocked face when it shattered in the end.

**Centurion:** "Huh. Not good."

Misfortune, large or small, found everyone who took the coin from her. And the old man, it seemed, had borne the worst of it.

Life is a gamble. To win a miracle, you must pay out something equal to the prize.

And as she knows all too well, some people can't afford that price.

The old man could not. Mila cannot.

She does not want to see the girl's face shatter like his did.

**Centurion:** "Now, let's see."

Centurion examines the coin, then she looks out across the land. It is barren and hot. Scattered cacti dot the cracked earth. Beyond them, the air shimmers. Dark, blocky shapes seem to waver in the heat, like reflections in disturbed water.

**Centurion:** "Ah, she said she lived in Westside, didn't she?"

#### Notes · Lucky Number

Centurion has always believed that 19 is her lucky number. "The best way to get through life is living on the 19th floor, putting 19 layers of hot sauce on everything, and winning 19 jackpots in a row!" On her 19th birthday, an old man with milky eyes found her. In his hand was a green potion.
