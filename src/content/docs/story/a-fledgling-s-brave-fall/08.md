## Story

Street, Nowa Miedź

**Cornerstone:** Whew, I think that's everything moved.

Cornerstone shuts the trunk of the car, meeting the eyes of Grundriss as she walks up beside her.

**Grundriss:** I'm not sure it makes much sense to me. She helped the old man break out of his obsession by reenacting a scene from some puppet show?

**Cornerstone:** I have a theory about it, not that I'm any kind of expert in human psychology.

**Grundriss:** Let's hope it's not as far-fetched as some of your other theories.

**Cornerstone:** The old man, who so closely resembled Lucjan's father, happened to also be a father who'd lost a child.

**Cornerstone:** That child happened to closely resemble the puppet version of Everecho, and the cause of death was also similar to the puppet show's ending.

**Cornerstone:** In the process of recovering her own memories, Everecho also managed to untangle the old man's inner troubles.

**Cornerstone:** Was this the guidance of the "Sanctuary of Memories" at work, or just an unintentional coincidence brought about by the "Storm"?

**Grundriss:** The "Storm" doesn't work like that.

**Cornerstone:** Ha, that's exactly why I said that it's only a theory.

**Cornerstone:** Has our friend finished saying her goodbyes yet?

**Honza:** Honza does not want to! But if Everecho wants to go out and take a look around, I'll go with her!

**Everecho:** I ... I'm still not sure if I want to leave.

Olga pats her on the shoulder.

**Olga:** Child, follow your heart.

**Olga:** Nowa Miedź has gotten better, but there are plenty of wonderful things out there in the wider world waiting for you.

**Olga:** You've already done so much for us. We want you to go out and meet others too.

**Everecho:** But there's still so much I don't remember: the dome collapsing, the sacrifice you all talked about ...

**Olga:** It will come back to you in time.

**Olga:** And you are always welcome to come back. We'll be waiting here for you.

**Olga:** Your room will still be here, the Shades will still be here, and we can sit and talk about the old days together.

**Jasio:** It may take you some time, but you've always had plenty of that. The world out there has many surprises waiting for you.

He hands her a stack of loose papers. As she takes them, the unmistakable scent of cigarettes washes over her. He never could kick that habit.

**Jasio:** Spread the word about my poems out there, and come back and tell us what's new.

**Everecho:** Why wouldn't you go out and see it yourself?

**Jasio:** Oh, I think I'll stay put. I've got myself a stable job here.

**Jasio:** And besides, this place is my home now—an eternal wellspring of inspiration for a poet.

Everecho turns around and sees Zuzanna walking toward her from a distance.

Zuzanna had taken a leave of absence after the events with her uncle. The two haven't seen each other before now.

**Zuzanna:** Hey, Everecho.

**Everecho:** Hey, Zuzanna ...

**Honza:** Waity-wait-wait! Did you put the stone back?

**Zuzanna:** Yes, as promised, Honza. Besides, the Foundation has taken over the area to protect the stones and the Shades going forward.

**Zuzanna:** My uncle passed away last night.

**Everecho:** Oh ...

**Zuzanna:** It's all right. I actually came to thank you.

**Zuzanna:** We still don't know what really happened to my cousin back then. But what you did has helped him. I think he died at peace with himself.

**Everecho:** Was he happy?

Zuzanna smiles bitterly.

**Zuzanna:** I want to believe he was. He was smiling right up to the end.

**Zuzanna:** And this is strange to say, but seeing that stunt you pulled gave me a kind of comfort too.

She turns around to look out over the gray city behind them.

**Zuzanna:** It feels like a weight that I've been carrying for so long has finally been taken off of me, but there's still something that draws me here.

**Zuzanna:** Why does Nowa Miedź keep calling out to me in my dreams?

**Everecho:** What will you do now?

**Zuzanna:** Oh, I think I'll stick around for a few more months and take a proper look at Nowa Miedź in spring.

**Zuzanna:** It's changed so much.

**Zuzanna:** But I don't think I'll stay, not forever.

**Everecho:** I'll remember you.

Zuzanna smiles and waves her hand.

**Zuzanna:** You're talking like I'm about to drop dead! We'll have plenty of time together.

**Everecho:** I'm leaving too.

**Zuzanna:** Is that so? Well, until we meet again.

**Cornerstone:** Let's go!

The architect calls out to the girl, holding the car door open for her.

**Everecho:** Goodbye.

She waves as she climbs into the car alongside Grundriss.

The car turns the corner, and the building begins to shrink behind them.

**Until we meet again.**

**Until we meet again.**

An old memory surfaces from the sea of light.

**Everecho:** You ...

Everecho sticks her head out the car window, looking back at the people waving goodbye.

**Everecho:** You're—!

Who are you? She can't find a name. She never once knew the puppeteer's name.

A name, a name connected to you, a name you once told me, the hand puppet's name, the name of the puppet show's lead role ...

**Everecho:** You're Honza!

**Honza:** Whatity-what?! Everecho, I'm right here!

The figures of the people begin to disappear in the haze.

**Everecho:** I understand now why you're all here. Maybe all of you carry some regret in your hearts.

**Grundriss:** Get back in here! That's dangerous!

Grundriss pulls the girl back into her seat.

**Grundriss:** Whew, I can never figure out how to handle you.

**Grundriss:** You'll end up losing your head that way! Don't you ever do that again!

**Cornerstone:** Hah, our little friend still has a lot left to learn.

The easygoing driver reaches over and flips on the car radio without a second thought.

**Radio:** City hall has just passed its latest ordinance, which stipulates that ...

Everecho remembers a certain radio station.

**Everecho:** FM 92.4 ...

**Cornerstone:** What did you say?

**Everecho:** Could we switch the station to FM 92.4?

**Cornerstone:** Um, sure, why not?

After a burst of crackling static, she hears what's playing on the radio.

**Radio:** Good afternoon, everyone. Coming up next, please tune in for the grand finale of The Cosmonaut.

**Everecho:** ...!

**Cornerstone:** Oh, seems like it's some kind of audio drama.

**Radio:** ♫ Here and now, everything is quiet. My hometown is empty. ♫

**Radio:** ♫ Where can I go from here? ♫

**Radio:** ♫ Should I stay here forever, in this silent spring, or set off for the unknown reaches of space? ♫

**Everecho:** ...

A fine thread of memory is tugged.

**Everecho:** ♫ Yes, I am the cosmonaut. ♫

**Radio:** ♫ I'll carry all your memories with me when I go. ♫

**Everecho:** ♫ I'll remember every bit of your laughter and all of our memories. ♫

**Everecho:** ♫ Dear friends, we will meet again one day. ♫

The music swells then fades away, and the radio bids its audience farewell with its closing theme.

**Radio & Everecho:** ♫ I'm singing. I'm singing. ♫

**Radio & Everecho:** ♫ Until, from far off in the light, an echo comes drifting back. ♫

**Everecho:** ♫ You knew it true, it's me, my dear. ♫
