## Story

Noon sunlight spills evenly across the room, the freshly painted gray-and-white checkerboard pattern reflecting its soft glow.

The clerk behind the counter pushes yet another document in front of the architect.

**Cornerstone:** Haha, I hope this is the last thing I need to sign.

**Cornerstone:** I'd bet that if we gathered up every document ever signed in this lobby, we could be able to build an entire new Foundation building out of just paper.

She scans the instructions quickly, then signs her name in the bottom right corner.

The clerk gives the application one last careful check.

**Clerk:** Mm, so you'll be returning to headquarters, where you'll submit an enrollment application for a new investigator.

**Clerk:** And during that time, the new arrival will stay temporarily in a guest room at the Nowa Miedź office.

**Clerk:** Since she has no fixed residence, and she needs to ... "adjust to being around people"?

The clerk's suspicious gaze slides past Cornerstone.

It lands on a blonde girl swaying gently in place, oblivious to their conversation.

**Everecho:** Hmm?

But when she notices the unfamiliar gaze on her, her eyes widen slightly.

Cornerstone clears her throat.

**Cornerstone:** I don't think this application will take very long. I've already reported on this newcomer's situation to the higher-ups.

**Cornerstone:** Half a month at most. Once everything's settled, I'll come straight back here and bring her to headquarters.

The girl suddenly pipes up, as if a signal had finally come through.

**Everecho:** Are we leaving?

**Cornerstone:** Yes, just like we talked about before, if you're willing.

**Cornerstone:** We lost you once. I'm just glad you came back.

A faint, gentle smile crosses her face.

**Cornerstone:** You're the same as before and yet different. Perhaps that's a trait of a certain kind of arcanist.

She stops herself, thinking for a moment.

**Cornerstone:** We'll have to look into this properly at headquarters.

**Everecho:** Headquarters ... You said there are so many voices there and many new friends too.

**Cornerstone:** Yes. Over the past two years, the Foundation has helped relocate a few Shades willing to leave this place. Their impressions of their new homes have generally been good.

**Cornerstone:** Now that it's become an office, this building serves a different purpose. It's not so ideal for wandering anymore.

The clerk puts the papers into a folder and ties it with string.

**Clerk:** There we go. Lucky for us, the office just opened. So, they've still got plenty of empty rooms.

Everecho watches the clerk take a large ring of keys off the wall and walk toward her.

**Clerk:** Come along. This way.

Everecho nods to her friend as they part ways.

**Everecho:** Goodbye, Cornerstone.

**Cornerstone:** Wait!

**Cornerstone:** Everecho, do you remember what happened before?

**Cornerstone:** The "Storm," and Olga, and Jasio, who came to visit you.

**Cornerstone:** And Lucjan, who disappeared.

**Everecho:** I only became like this a month ago.

**Everecho:** Before that, when I was what I was before ... Everything I remember of that time is hazy.

**Everecho:** I don't remember them. I want to, but I don't.

**Everecho:** I've asked the Shades here, and none of them know Lucjan.

Guest Room, New Office

**Clerk:** We've only just finished with the renovations, so there's still a bit of a smell.

**Everecho:** Oh, I hope it won't upset my daisies.

The door closes, the clerk's footsteps fade into the distance, and Everecho lies down on her soft new bed.

The hand puppet, having held his patience long enough, leaps off her hand and chimes up.

**Honza:** Everecho, we can't leave!

**Everecho:** Mm ...

She sinks into the cloud-soft mattress, her thoughts drifting away. But this does nothing to dampen her friend's impassioned protest.

**Honza:** This is our home! Uh, sure, we don't know why we ended up looking like this, and we don't remember what happened before. But this, without a doubt, is our home!

**Honza:** Then out of nowhere, these peepy-people barged in, ham-hammering here, bang-bangering there, and now the whole place is covered in checkers!

**Everecho:** But the building didn't refuse this renovation. The stones and the Shades allowed it.

**Everecho:** And once the renovation was finished, people seemed happy too.

**Honza:** Even so, they still can't just drive us out.

**Everecho:** It's not driving out anyone. This is an invitation to a new place.

Her noisy little friend falls quiet for a moment.

**Honza:** Everecho, do you want to leave?

**Everecho:** I ...

A rush of whispers floods into Everecho's mind, drowning out her train of thought.

Leave this place, leave this place ...

**Shade:** Leave this place, leave this place ...

Don't disturb us.

**Shade:** Don't disturb us.

She straightens up and shakes her head.

**Honza:** Everecho, what's wrong?

**Everecho:** The Shades ... They're uneasy, like there's an intruder.

**Everecho:** It's on the east side. A few of the Shades like to stay there.

Old Restaurant, New Office

It's a corner not yet invaded by scaffolding and paint cans, a place that was once a shelter for drifters.

Now it is silent, with only Shades to wander beneath its skylight.

Now, the Shades have gathered together, surrounding a human in gray-and-white checkered clothing standing in the middle.

**Everecho:** Her clothes look a lot like that woman's—the one with the big ring of keys.

**???:** Oh, come on, you should all calm down. I'm just here looking for someone!

A tiny Shade is gnawing at the trespasser's pant leg.

**???:** Hey!

Leave ... Leave ...

**Shade:** Leave ... Leave ...

**???:** Isn't there a single Shade here who can understand human speech?

**???:** They told me the Shades here could talk and sing.

Everecho drifts closer and hums an unfamiliar little tune, soothing the restless Shades.

**Everecho:** Don't be afraid. She won't hurt us.

**Everecho:** Let me talk to her.

The low, threatening murmurs gradually fade, and the Shades clear a path for her.

The unfamiliar woman in the gray-checkered uniform breathes a sigh of relief and looks her up and down.

**???:** Finally, a ghost I can actually converse with!

**Everecho:** Uhm, I'm not exactly ...

**Honza:** Rude! Everecho is no mere ghostie-spirit!

**???:** Hey, I didn't mean anything by it, kid.

**???:** Everyone around here knows this place is haunted. I think most people could go their whole life without ever seeing a Shade. But in this building you practically trip over them walking out the door!

**???:** Though someone said these Shades aren't really Shades at all, but some kind of echo.

**???:** Anyway, since the Foundation established their office here, it seems like they prefer to spend time in these quiet places.

**Everecho:** That's true. We don't often go into busy places, and most of them can't go outside. You should not come here, either.

**Everecho:** We should all stay on our own "island."

The hand puppet shakes his head back and forth, as if vigorously agreeing with his friend.

**Honza:** Island! Yep-yep, now when strangers come to our island, they should introduce themselves! Get with the naming!

**Honza:** Just who are you, exactly? And what are you snakey-sneaking around here for?!

The woman sighs and sits down on a peculiarly lustrous stone.

**???:** Calm down. I'm not an intruder, and I don't mean you any harm, but I suppose I owe you an explanation.

**Zuzanna:** I'm Zuzanna. I arrived at the branch here just last week.

**Zuzanna:** And I actually came looking for someone like you.

**Zuzanna:** The going theory about these Shades is that they're people who develop an obsession with this building in some way.

**Zuzanna:** When they die here, their spirits are bound to it in some way. Is that true? Does it sound like anyone here?
