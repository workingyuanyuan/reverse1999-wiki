## Story

Everecho rises up toward the top of the dome, approaching the gray triangular panels.

**Everecho:** Doesn't exist at all? If that's true, then why did Zuzanna and that old man even decide to come to this building?

**Everecho:** Why is it that everything I find is from my own memories? That puppet ... why would it so closely resemble the cousin Zuzanna described?

**Everecho:** That puppeteer ... What happened to her?

One mystery leads into the next.

**Everecho:** Lucjan, if you really did become an echo, why won't you speak to me?

**Everecho:** What am I supposed to do?

She reaches out and touches the Echo Stone once more.

**Puppeteer:** I'm sorry I broke you. It was all my fault.

**Puppeteer:** I spent so much time fixing up the other puppets that somehow, I forgot to patch you up too. I suppose I assumed a Shade wouldn't have that kind of problem.

Everecho reverts back to her original form, free from the puppet. She lingers over what's left of it, letting her hand rest on her braid.

**Everecho:** I'm fine.

But her friend won't accept the words. She rubs her knuckles and pours herself into mending the little broken puppet.

**Puppeteer:** We were so close, but a blunder like that—Havel would never have done something so bone-headed.

**Everecho:** It was a wonderful show. Everyone applauded. The Shades all loved it.

**Puppeteer:** They're just being kind.

**Puppeteer:** I wanted my puppet show to bring the audience joy, to comfort people. Instead, it was the Shades who ended up comforting me.

The puppeteer sighs, and Everecho moves to rest her hand on her shoulder.

**Everecho:** Then let's put on another show next week. I'll gather everyone again!

The puppeteer falls silent for a moment.

**Puppeteer:** That's ... I wish I could. But I have to go. This show was going to be my way of saying goodbye.

The light cascading around them seems to still.

**Everecho:** Why?

**Puppeteer:** I have to accept that the theater is never going to open again.

**Puppeteer:** Master Havel has already left this place, gone off to another country with his writer friend.

Her voice cracks with pain.

**Puppeteer:** He left me behind. I guess I have to accept that I didn't have what it takes.

**Everecho:** You're the best puppeteer I've ever known!

**Puppeteer:** Only because I'm the only puppeteer you've ever known!

**Puppeteer:** I've been out of work for months. I can't survive off of scraps. I need a real job.

**Puppeteer:** My parents can't stand me lazing around either, but I'm not going to the coal plant! I'll go find work somewhere else and take on some other kind of apprenticeship.

Everecho says nothing more.

**Puppeteer:** But I don't think you're able to leave this place.

I don't know what to say.

**Everecho:**I don't know what to say.

Please don't leave.

**Everecho:**Please don't leave.

**Everecho:** I can't go with you. Someone has to stay behind to speak for the others.

People always left, like that gray river at the outskirts of town, always flowing, always leaving. She thought she had learned how to say goodbye.

**Puppeteer:** ...

**Puppeteer:** I made you something.

She takes out a small, funny-looking brown hand puppet.

**Puppeteer:** I call him "Honza."

**Puppeteer:** Back in my grandmother's hometown, the lead role in a puppet show was always named "Honza."

**Everecho:** Honza.

Everecho slips it onto her hand.

**Everecho:** It looks a lot like you.

**Puppeteer:** Hah! I guess so. We're friends, aren't we?

Everecho nods without a word, choking back her own emotions.

**Puppeteer:** Well, until we meet again.

**Everecho:** Until we meet again.

**Puppeteer:** ...

**Puppeteer:** Hey, don't look so sad! Who knows, maybe I'll end up in my grandmother's hometown and become a real puppeteer there!

**Puppeteer:** And when that day comes, I'll come back for my best puppet performer, Everecho! You'll see posters for The Cosmonaut plastered all over Nowa Miedź!

**Puppeteer:** I'll come back some day.

**Puppeteer:** And when I do, I'll tell you my biggest secret!

She packs up the rest of her things and leaves.

Through the long years, clouds turned to falling rain, and rain rose back up into the sky as vapor.

Maybe more people came, maybe more people left, but no one ever noticed that beam of light again.

After all, it was just a beam of light.

Just a bit of light you could find anywhere in the middle of the day.

I never knew ...

**Everecho:**I never knew ...

**Everecho:**It could be so lonely.

**???:** This won't do for doing, Everecho!

**Everecho:** I'm waiting for someone, waiting for a friend.

**???:** Silly-billy! What do you wanna do? Wait-waiting forever?

The light raises its own left hand. A small hand puppet waves his arms toward her.

**Everecho:** Honza. I didn't know you could talk. Will you be my friend?

**Honza:** Of course, I can! Everecho! Hurry up and remember yourself!

**Everecho:** Myself?

**Everecho:** What do I even look like?

A faded poster drifts into view before her eyes.

**Everecho:**The Cosmonaut ...

**Everecho:** Yellow braids ... rainbow-colored light ... a dress ...

The light weaves itself together in midair, following her thoughts.

She sees her own arm, glowing with light, then a long golden braid, a white headscarf ...

She takes a step forward, both feet touching the ground.

**Everecho:** So, this is where our names came from, Everecho and Honza.

Sound remembers. That's why you never change shape.

**Everecho:**Sound remembers. That's why you never change shape.

Having listened to countless echoes of memory, she now knows where she came from.

She becomes, once more, everything within the sea of light.

She sees Everecho and Stasia's first meeting and remembers the first handshake. She sees the soldiers with their rifles and remembers the workers laboring on the scaffolding.

**Lucjan:** Everecho, you've come back.

She sees a family out on a picnic, the wind lifting the corner of their picnic blanket.

A thin, frail boy takes a bite of bread and breaks into a smile.

**Everecho:** Lucjan.

**Lucjan:** Go.

**"Everecho":** You have to help them. You have to guard their memories.

**Everecho:** Me?

**"Everecho":** You are yesterday's blessing. You are tomorrow's wish. You have already walked through your own memories.

**You are Everecho, and you are everything.**

**Everecho:****You are Everecho, and you are everything.**

The wind sweeps across the glass of the dome, scraping out a harsh, grating sound.

Everecho steps toward the edge of the triangular panel, and as she does, she spots Zuzanna and her uncle below.

**Zuzanna:** Come on, Uncle, it's time to go.

Spots of light rise and fall in the air, and Everecho opens her mouth.

**Everecho:** ♫ Yes, I am the cosmonaut. I'll carry all your memories with me when I go. ♫

The sound resonates through the entire hall, carried by the Echo Stone, and the other clerks lift their heads.

Everecho draws a deep breath and leaps off the edge.

She falls, like a fledgling bird spreading its wings for the very first time.

**Old Man:** ...!

**Zuzanna:** Everecho!!

Zuzanna sprints in a mad panic toward the base of the dome, but she has no chance of reaching her.

Yet, despite her worries, Everecho drifts down as gently as a feather.

**Zuzanna:** Everecho?

But the girl does not answer the question. She walks past Zuzanna toward her uncle.

She recognizes this face, a face resembling the Lucjan of her memory, a face far more aged than the man beside the picnic blanket.

**Everecho:** Maybe I really did know you.

**Those memories, glowing like light—I miss them dearly,

but they are gone now and will never come again.**

**Everecho:****Those memories, glowing like light—I miss them dearly,

but they are gone now and will never come again.**

**Will you forget those voices too?

Will you find your happiness once more?**

**Everecho:****Will you forget those voices too?

Will you find your happiness once more?**

She stands in front of the old man.

**Everecho:** Would you like one more memory?

**Old Man:** ...

**Everecho:** I'm home.

The long-ailing old man's lips tremble.

**Old Man:** Lucjan?

**Old Man:** You're here?

**Everecho:** Yes.

Her hand comes to rest over the old man's eyes.

**Everecho:** Yes, this is a sweet memory.

**Everecho:** It's a sweet dream.

**Everecho:** Sleep now. You'll live on in the past. You'll find your happiness.
