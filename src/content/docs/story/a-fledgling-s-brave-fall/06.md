## Story

Everecho pulls off her hand puppet and sets it down on the ground.

**Everecho:** Come along.

**Everecho:** I think I'm getting the hang of this.

***

**GAMEPLAY**

***

**Puppeteer:** Everecho, did you call me here for some kind of Shade party?

Dozens of translucent figures drift among the pillars and geometric windowpanes, watching them quietly.

**Everecho:** You said yesterday that The Cosmonaut was finally ready to present.

**Everecho:** So, I invited everyone here to be your audience.

**Everecho:** They're excited to see a real show. They can't leave the building, so for many of them, this will be their first show since. Well, ever, I guess.

The puppeteer turns her head to one side.

**Puppeteer:** Great! I mean, this isn't exactly how I pictured my first ever audience, but I suppose I'm happy to be of service.

She sets up her makeshift puppet theater box at the center of the courtyard and sets up everything for their premiere performance.

**Puppeteer:** Hey! Settle down, everyone, and gather round. Take your seats or whatever Shades do! The show is about to begin!

The Shades gather together in a semi-circle around the puppet box. Everecho bows and then jumps behind the curtains to join her friend in the final set-up.

The puppeteer stands with her hands at her sides, seemingly waiting for something before drawing back the curtain.

**Everecho:** Oh!

**Everecho:** Applause, let's start with a round of applause.

The signal successfully reaches its destination. Scattered applause rises up from the crowd.

The puppeteer bows, then pulls the curtains open.

**Puppeteer:** The wind has stopped, the night has gone still, and the stars glimmer across the sky.

She twists the control bar, and the first puppet dances onto the stage.

**Everecho:** ♫ The spaceship drifts further away from me, a faint echo drifting back from the void. ♫

They've rehearsed this so many times already.

Their timing is flawless.

The sun slides to the west, and in the distance, the school bell rings for the end of class.

**Everecho:** ♫ Here and now, everything is quiet. My hometown is empty. ♫

**Everecho:** ♫ Where can I go from here? ♫

There is a murmur of quiet sobbing from the Shades.

Everecho twirls as she moves into the final section of the play.

**Everecho:** ♫ Should I stay here forever, in this silent spring, or set off for the unknown reaches of space? ♫

The puppet climbs to the highest point.

**Everecho:** ♫ Yes, I am the cosmonaut. ♫

**Everecho:** ♫ I'll carry all your memories with me when I go. ♫

Everecho leaps.

The Shades hold their breath.

**Everecho:** How strange! They shouldn't be able to breathe.

Her mind wanders again. But does that really matter? All she needs to do is fall, the same way she always has.

Once again, she hears a faint crack from her left leg.

**Everecho:** ♫ I'll remember every bit of your ... ♫

**Puppeteer:** Everecho!!

A sharp, cracking snap. Her left leg goes flying off.

**Everecho:** Hmm?

To compensate for the sudden loss of balance, she throws her right leg up hard, well beyond the range any normal puppet could be made to move by its strings.

**Puppeteer:** That day, I forgot to fix you!!!

Another piece breaks off.

Everecho crashes onto the floor and watches her own right leg arc overhead, like a shooting star streaking through the sky.

**Everecho:** ...?

It hasn't quite dawned on her what's happened yet.

The dome fills the center of her vision. Its harsh light leaves her a little dizzy.

Everecho narrows her glass eyes.

The glare fades, and reality comes into focus—pale gray stonework and a friendly face.

**Zuzanna:** You fainted again.

**Zuzanna:** Forgive me for breaking my promise and coming back again. You drifted off so fast. I was a little worried.

**Everecho:** I still haven't found her. I must have already gone through the very deepest part of the memories.

She feels an overwhelming sadness and confusion.

**Everecho:** This isn't right. I'll look again. I'll look more carefully!

**Zuzanna:** That won't be necessary.

**Zuzanna:** Everecho, please, I don't need you to do this to yourself.

Zuzanna pulls Everecho up and helps her put the hand puppet back on.

**Zuzanna:** The doctors at the Foundation say my uncle doesn't have much time left, and his episodes will only get worse.

**Zuzanna:** I need to move him to a hospital outside as soon as possible.

**Everecho:** Does that mean you're giving up?

Zuzanna shakes her head.

**Zuzanna:** There's no time left, Everecho. I told Granny Olga about all this, and she urged me to let it go too.

**Zuzanna:** He's dying. Right now, I need to be with him through this final journey. That matters more.

**Everecho:** But won't he have regrets?

Zuzanna takes her hand and leads her downstairs.

**Zuzanna:** I think that's just what life is. We will always have regrets.

**Zuzanna:** Sometimes I feel like this city calls to me in my dreams.

**Zuzanna:** I wanted to come back while I still had the chance. But it's just a city.

**Everecho:** Ever since the office was built, everyone's lives have been improving.

**Zuzanna:** That's just an illusion, isn't it? The newspapers, the memories—all of them are lost.

**Zuzanna:** I think none of it here ever really mattered to begin with.

Everecho walks down the hallway back toward her room. It's been almost two days now since she slept in her own bed.

There's nothing else left for her to do.

**Honza:** No more snibbly-sniffling Everecho! All that you could you did!

**Honza:** Not every echo sticks around, you know!

**Everecho:** But why not?

**Clerk:** Hey!

The clerk at the front desk stops her and holds out a phone receiver.

**Clerk:** There's a call for you.

**Everecho:** Oh ...

Everecho copies what she's seen others do, holding the red device up to her ear.

**Everecho:** Hello?

**???:** Hi there, new colleague. Hope you haven't forgotten me already.

The outline of a thick-browed girl surfaces in her mind.

**Everecho:** You're the one who was with Ms. Cornerstone, that girl who always looks so stern.

**Grundriss:** Grundriss. I guess I'll take that as a compliment.

**Grundriss:** Olga let me know that you've been up to some detective work lately.

**Everecho:** Oh well, not anymore.

**Grundriss:** I wouldn't say I'm surprised. It didn't seem like it was going to work out from what I've heard.

**Grundriss:** But explaining that fully would mean getting into some classified information.

**Everecho:** I'll keep our secret safe.

**Grundriss:** ...

She pauses for a moment, as if searching for the right way to put it.

**Grundriss:** Do you remember the "Temporal Anomaly" we told you about?

**Everecho:** Cornerstone did mention it. She said the dome collapsed because of a "Temporal Anomaly."

**Everecho:** But I don't remember any of it, and she never explained any further.

**Grundriss:** Well, these days, we call it the "Storm." There's a "Storm" out there that washes the inside and outside of this building into two separate eras.

**Grundriss:** The world inside the building has never changed, while the world outside has already changed twice. The "Storm" carries off everything from the old era—family, memories ...

**Everecho:** Jasio said that word too.

**Grundriss:** I looked into Zuzanna's background. She's just a temporary clerk the office recruited locally, and she knows nothing at all about the "Storm" or the nineties.

**Grundriss:** She comes from the world outside, from the new era, after the "Storm." Her memories most likely don't belong to this old building at all.

**Grundriss:** Lucjan ...

She has some difficulty verbalizing the name.

**Grundriss:** Inside this building, there was only ever one other Shade named Lucjan, and he already merged into the echoes.

**Grundriss:** Zuzanna's cousin, Lucjan, never existed inside this building and never really died inside it either.

**Grundriss:** Better to stop wasting time.

**Everecho:** ...

**Grundriss:** Everecho?

**Grundriss:** You still there?

**Everecho:** ...

It's an ordinary overcast day. The soft patter of rain rustles against the window.

**Everecho:** What should I do?
