## Story

The sun sinks toward the west, flooding the audience chamber in golden light.

Hedone's retelling comes to a sudden halt.

**Hedone:** Do you know anything about Aganippe?

The scholar's pen moves faster than her mind. She adds a few more words before realizing this question isn't part of the story at all.

**Elma:** Sorry, what did you say?

**Hedone:** Do you know anything about Aganippe? The new recruit back then.

**Elma:** Oh, one of the members of that expedition, as you described.

**Elma:** All I know is that she died at the very start of the battle against Typhon, struck down by Typhon's flames.

Elma furrows her brow as she attempts to dredge up whatever related information she can.

**Elma:** Her grandmother's far more famous, though! Nyx once seized the Golden Fleece on a voyage, and she helped you slay the great bear.

**Elma:** But this Aganippe ...

**Hedone:** She trained hard, had extraordinary talent, and possessed remarkable power in taming sea beasts.

**Hedone:** She should have earned countless achievements. She should have lived out a full life, whether as an ordinary woman or as a hero.

**Hedone:** The rest of the warriors, my friends, were the same. They rushed into war, chasing glory and power.

**Hedone:** So that their names might be remembered.

**Warrior:** Arrgh!!

Another flaming chunk of rock crashes down. A warrior leaves behind a spiraling scream as he tumbles down the cliff face.

**Hedone:** Gods smite it!

**Aganippe:** I shall win, Hedone! I've done well, have I not?

**Aganippe:** I shall be the first to land an arrow on Typhon!

Aganippe scrambles upward on both hands and feet. Hedone notices her calves trembling slightly where they're braced against the rock.

**Hedone:** Do not spend all your strength on the climb! A brutal fight yet awaits us at the top!

**Aganippe:** I know!

The Volcanic Vent

Aganippe is the first to reach the crater's rim.

With a final kick, Hedone hurls herself up over the edge a moment later.

At the very center of the crater, amid a monster of flowing magma, a single enormous eye stares back at Hedone.

The many-headed beast lets out a frenzied roar—its declaration of war.

**Hedone:** Typhon ...

***

**Eurus:** Watch out!

**Eurus:** Ugh!

Eurus shoves aside a panicking warrior, only to be bitten by one of the serpent heads himself.

At the same moment, Hedone's last arrow tears straight through the monster's skull, then flies on.

**Typhon:** \*roar\*

The arrow stands buried in the great beast's eye, like a flag of victory.

Typhon writhes in pain, spitting out Eurus.

**Warrior:** Hedone, your arrow struck true!

**Eurus:** \*cough\* Did we win?

**Hedone:** Not yet—

**Aganippe:** We did it! We won!

Aganippe is closest and most eager to land the final blow.

She springs up, leaping through the air in a magnificent arc.

**Hedone:** Aganippe, fall back, now!

**Aganippe:** Finally, my moment has come. Typhon's head is mine!

**Aganippe:** This will be a great victory, and I will earn a glorious new title!

**Aganippe:** Grandmother, I shall become a hero, just as you were!

Her spear tip is a hair's breadth from piercing Typhon's heart.

**Aganippe:** ...!

Fire bursts from Typhon's mouth, engulfing the young warrior in an instant.

**Aganippe:** Ughh!

Hedone catches the stench of burning hair.

Her blackened body tumbles out of the air, colliding with a warrior and dragging him off the cliff along with her.

**Hedone:** Aganippe! Amphimachos!

Typhon lets out a piercing shriek and beats up a great cloud of dust as it tries to retreat back into the volcano.

The wind is powerful enough to block Hedone's next shot.

**Hedone:** \*cough\*

After a violent tremor, all that remains in the crater are the corpses of monsters and the warriors.

The falling fire and raging wind both halt, and the enraged volcano seems to sink into a kind of rest.

Only the dark sky and the billowing white smoke hint that the danger isn't over yet.

The warriors step over the monster remains scattered around the crater, searching for surviving companions and any weapons still worth recovering.

**Gaunt Warrior:** Ugh, any others still alive?

**Tall Warrior:** Not that I see. It seems only the six of us remain.

Their crashed warship had held a full forty men.

**Tall Warrior:** Gods smite it! Typhon was even stronger than we imagined.

**Gaunt Warrior:** Never before have we lost so many to a monster.

**Gaunt Warrior:** \*sigh\* Can we truly defeat it?

The soot-covered warriors gather back together. Their weapons have already been chipped and dulled.

Eurus, having bandaged his wounded left shoulder, is the first to speak.

**Eurus:** I say we retreat.

**Eurus:** Typhon has already fled back into the depths of the volcano. If we wish to pursue it, we should have to leap down into the volcano itself.

**Eurus:** Descending into the unknown heart of a volcano is nothing short of suicide. No warrior can face such a thing, no matter how powerful they may be.

**Old Warrior:** Do you truly mean to suggest we turn tail and run? Think of the companions we have already lost!

The gray-haired veteran furiously strikes his shield.

**Eurus:** They did not make their sacrifice so that the rest of us might cast our lives away in vain.

**Eurus:** This is a nigh-impossible battle. We have all seen what Typhon is capable of.

**Eurus:** We have already lost our finest warriors. Who will remain to protect our trade routes?

Eurus speaks calmly and at length, as though delivering a formal argument in the public square.

**Eurus:** And if we die inside that volcano, what hope do our people have then?

**Eurus:** What if we were to lose even Hedone because of this?

A heavy silence falls over everyone.

**Tall Warrior:** I agree. This is Typhon we are facing, a god-like monster!

**Tall Warrior:** No mortal can challenge a god! Not even Hedone!

**Old Warrior:** But Hedone has already struck Typhon with an arrow! We have it at its weakest. Are we truly to retreat now, of all times?

**Old Warrior:** Once it recovers its strength, we have no hope against it!

**Quiet Warrior:** Do not forget that we came here to establish a new polis.

**Quiet Warrior:** Do you truly think abandoning this island and returning to drifting the seas is our best choice?

**Eurus:** I want to build a new polis as much as anyone.

**Eurus:** But the ocean is vast. I believe the gods will guide us to a new and better home.

**Eurus:** What do you say, Hedone?

All eyes turn to their silent leader.

Hedone runs her hand over her worn golden bow and looks over her exhausted companions.

**Hedone:** You have all fought alongside me for years. I trust your strength and your conviction, the same way you have trusted me.

**Hedone:** These years at sea, we have faced more hardships than I can count.

**Hedone:** You all remember, I am sure, when we fought the beast in the labyrinth. We turned our backs to one another, trusting each other with our lives. And when I was locked in a life-or-death struggle with that great bear, it was you who raised your shields for me.

**Hedone:** And I shall admit it too: This is the most perilous battle we have ever faced. Never before have we lost so many of our companions.

Hedone lays it all out calmly, as if forging her anger into a blade and letting it cool.

**Hedone:** Which is exactly why we cannot let their deaths be in vain.

In a moment, her anger finally breaks loose.

**Hedone:** They must be revenged!

**Hedone:** Whether it be for the sake of our second homeland or for the volcano's power.

**Eurus:** Hedone!

**Hedone:** I will slay Typhon to lay their spirits to rest, even if it costs me my life!

She raises her golden bow high above her head.

**Hedone:** You all know this bow. From the day it was given me by the priest of Apollo, I was fated to become a hero!

**Hedone:** And a hero I will be! For I shall prove to you that even a mortal can defeat a god!

**Hedone:** If you believe in me, then follow by my side.

**Hedone:** If not, then stay. Whether I live or die, someone must carry the news back to our people!

**Hedone:** All mortals must eventually die. The Kymaions will not end just because I do.

**Eurus:** ...

Hedone grips her bow tight and steps to the edge of the crater.

The mouth of the volcano billows with white smoke, like the gates of the underworld thrown open for whoever dares enter.

**Hedone:** Typhon!

**Hedone:** I'm coming for you!

She leaps down, plummeting toward the heart of the earth.
