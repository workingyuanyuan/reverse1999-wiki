## Story

**Hedone:** You know well what happened after that.

**Elma:** Yes, it was a brutal battle. Your companions followed you down into the volcano.

**Elma:** If you'd rather not speak on it any further, I understand.

**Hedone:** No. I'm not afraid to talk about death. It's only that, in my reincarnation, many of the details are a haze to me.

**Hedone:** But I do recall how the battle ended.

**Hedone:** They died, and I survived. That is all there is to be remembered.

**Hedone:** I could have faced Typhon alone.

Hedone closes the box and stands up, holding it in her hands.

**Hedone:** I appreciate the gift, but I can't answer the question you came here with.

**Hedone:** You will take no money for it, but perhaps I might offer you something of interest instead—a historical curiosity.

**Hedone:** Not many still know these old tales.

**Elma:** Oh, no, I've already taken up enough of your time.

**Hedone:** Walk with me.

The scholar obediently stows her notebook and does her best to keep up with Hedone as she strides into another room.

Treasure Vault, Anaktoron

The walls are lavish, stacked with countless dazzling treasures.

The most striking among them is a row of carefully preserved weapons of every kind.

Forgetting her manners for a moment, the scholar rushes up to the glass cabinet, her eyes wide.

**Elma:** If I'm not mistaken, this is a two-thousand-year-old shield. It even has a Gorgon carved into it!

**Hedone:** You have the right of it. This is my hall of treasures. These here are the weapons left behind by my lost friends, a collection I gathered in my first life.

**Hedone:** Their faces may have dulled in my memory, but their weapons gleam with every bit of their past splendor.

She walks over to stand beside Elma.

**Hedone:** This shield belonged to Xanthos. It was his weapon of choice, though he did not carry it into the battle against Typhon.

**Hedone:** He was the oldest among us, a very beardy man. On idle days, he told epic tales with the accompaniment of his lyre.

**Old Warrior:** Die, Typhon! Arghh!!

**Elma:** And that one? Whose was that?

Behind another pane of glass hangs a brass-colored spear.

**Hedone:** That belonged to Metis. It was snapped down the middle. I had it repaired.

**Hedone:** She was reserved, quiet. I must admit I don't remember a great deal about her.

**Quiet Warrior:** Hedone, catch! Hrgh!

Hedone takes a step to the right, her eyes resting on a pair of ornate daggers.

**Hedone:** These two siblings were masters of Kottabos. One had a hearty laugh, and the other a heavy sigh.

**Gaunt Warrior:** \*cough\*

**Tall Warrior:** ...

Hedone pulls her hand back and opens the empty glass case beside them.

She takes out the bronze sword from its box and sets it carefully inside the glass case.

**Hedone:** Eurus was my childhood playmate. We'd known each other a long time.

**Hedone:** He was a master of political affairs, presenting arguments and cutting costs.

The rust-covered short sword sleeps behind the glass, long since worn past reflecting anyone's face.

**Hedone:** ...

**Hedone:** TYPHON!

She roars the great beast's name and draws her bow with all her strength.

**Hedone:** FACE MY WRATH!

Deep Within the Volcano

She tears out the massive, blood-soaked eye and casts it aside.

**Hedone:** Putrid monster! Damned beast!

**Hedone:** Get up! GET UP, I SAY!!!

Hedone stands over Typhon's body. She grips an arrow and drives it into its heart, again and again, twisting and churning it.

Thick, sticky blood splatters over her. Anyone could see that Typhon is truly and completely dead.

**Eurus:** ...

**Eurus:** Stop.

**Eurus:** It's dead.

Outside the crater, the once-thick clouds have cleared away, and the cliffs echo with nothing but the sound of waves crashing below.

**Hedone:** We will descend from the other side.

**Hedone:** They were our finest warriors. They deserve the Kymaions' highest respect.

**Hedone:** I would not have us come upon their bodies in haste. Once we return by ship, I shall host the grandest funeral pyre in their honor.

**Eurus:** They might have lived, had you not given the order.

**Hedone:** I gave no order. Do not underestimate their courage.

**Eurus:** Hah, courage? Must you always ...

The faint trace of resentment in his voice makes Hedone's brow furrow.

**Hedone:** If you have something you wish to say, just say it.

Eurus closes his eyes for a moment, but in the end only turns and starts making his way down the mountain.

**Eurus:** It's nothing. At least Typhon lies dead.

**Eurus:** Their sacrifice meant something.

**Hedone:** Sacrifice ...

Looking out from the crater's rim, the crescent of outer islands is covered in a lush green. A few seagulls fly past overhead.

**Hedone:** A second homeland ...

At the Foot of the Volcano

The contest's referee is waiting for them on the shore.

**Volcano:** I welcome your return, my victors!

**Volcano:** You have brought back Typhon's eye as I asked, Hedone. You have honored your half of our deal.

**Volcano:** And thus I shall honor mine. I will now grant the volcano's power to the victor.

**Volcano:** Hedone, you tore out Typhon's eye, and thus, you shall be the volcano's new master.

**Hedone:** But my friends are dead. We lost so many of our finest warriors.

**Hedone:** Was that part of your plan too, Volcano?

**Volcano:** Typhon is a legendary beast. I warned you that challenging it could cost you your life.

**Volcano:** But you held no fear of the danger. You fought fiercely, all in the pursuit of revelry.

**Volcano:** And for that, you have my respect.

The exhausted champion doesn't reach for the medal and glory right away.

**Hedone:** I gleaned no thrill from any of this at all.

**Hedone:** All that remains in me now is anger.

The girl tilts her head, somewhat puzzled.

**Volcano:** Perhaps it is because this contest was prepared with rather too much haste.

**Volcano:** I trust that your performance will be even more pleasing next time.

Hedone perks up at those last words.

**Hedone:** Next time?

**Volcano:** Next time you fight Typhon.

**Volcano:** You did not think this was your final battle, did you? Typhon and the island share an intrinsic bond. It will resurrect.

Hedone seizes the girl by the throat, raising her high into the air.

**Hedone:** You dare to fool me?!

**Volcano:** I worked no trickery. Whoever slays Typhon gains the volcano's power. The rules of the contest were clear and shall always remain so.

**Volcano:** But like as not, Typhon will not resurrect for another thousand years. Your fleeting lives will not see you that far.

**Volcano:** But there is a solution to that problem. The volcano's power grants the blessing of reincarnation.

**Volcano:** A thousand years hence, you will resurrect as a warrior! And you will fight for your people, and I shall revel in the thrill of battle once more!

**Hedone:** Hahaha!

Hedone breaks into laughter at the absurdity of it all.

**Hedone:** The thrill of battle? What you chase is merely the "thrill" of watching on from the sidelines!

**Hedone:** You are no different from those Roman lords. To you, we are naught but an amusement! We are just gladiators to be maimed and used for your pleasure!

**Hedone:** Then what about my dead friends? What was the meaning of their sacrifice?

With her tightening grip, the girl's lips have begun to turn blue.

**Volcano:** \*cough\* I fail to understand why this angers you so greatly.

**Volcano:** But you cannot kill an illusion.

**Hedone:** ...

She glares at the girl for a moment, then releases her grip.

**Hedone:** I will take no power from you.

**Eurus:** What?!

**Volcano:** You refuse?

Hedone throws Typhon's eye at the girl's feet and strides toward the sea.

**Eurus:** If you refuse to accept it, they died for nothing!

**Eurus:** You cannot squander what was gained at such a wretched cost! You must accept this power!

**Eurus:** Return to our people, and you will become—

**Hedone:** I will not allow any man nor god to insult my friends' sacrifice!

**Volcano:** You would forfeit a great deal of power! And was it not power that you lacked? The very thing that drove your people from their homeland?

**Volcano:** These shores can be the second homeland you wished for.

**Hedone:** We are Kymaions. We do not become playthings in others' games.

**Hedone:** And I will not allow my people to become pawns in yours!

**Hedone:** As for power, even without accepting yours, I shall remain the strongest hero in all of Greece.

She puts two fingers to her lips and lets out a sharp whistle. Ceto's back surfaces on the water.

Then, she hears the sound of something cutting through the air behind her.

**Hedone:** ...?!

Before she can turn her head, an arrow pierces her left shoulder.
