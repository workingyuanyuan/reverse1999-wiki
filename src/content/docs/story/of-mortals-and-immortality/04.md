## Story

The wind and rain stop in an instant. A hint of daylight shines through the dark clouds above.

The disheveled warriors exchange stunned glances with one another.

**Aganippe:** Has Typhon's power run dry?

**Hedone:** No.

The water beneath the ship begins to churn, quickly transforming into a rapid current.

**Aganippe:** It's a whirlpool! Never have I seen a current move so swiftly!

**Warrior:** A whirlpool of this speed could drag the entire ship to the seafloor!

Sure enough, the ship begins to slide toward the center of the whirlpool.

**Hedone:** Eurus!!

**Impatient Warrior:** Can you not control the wind? Must you fall short of Hedone even now?

**Eurus:** I'm trying.

**Restless Warrior:** Look! Behind us!

Countless sea monsters, seemingly drawn in by the whirlpool, leap out of the water, lunging at the warriors.

Hedone draws her bowstring back into a full moon.

**Hedone:** Ha!

The arrow flies from the string.

***

Shrieking and wailing, the sea monsters retreat. A gust of wind struggles its way in from the sea, billowing the ship's tattered sails.

**Eurus:** Need more wind, more.

A second gust, a third ... More and more wind gathers, slowly lifting the ship from the water.

**Eurus:** Hold fast, everyone!

**Hedone:** ...!

A tremendous gust of wind hurls the ship up into the sky. The warriors cling to the mast, their breaths held.

The whirlpool and the sea monsters have been left far behind. They are sailing through open air.

**Eurus:** I shall ensure our ship comes down slowly, toward the volcano.

**Eurus:** Typhon's power is great, but I might contend with it yet.

**Restless Warrior:** Eurus, my friend! Well done!

**Hedone:** Hah, I shall have to invite you to our next expedition.

**Tall Warrior:** Indeed you will! I had no idea you held such talents!

**Hedone:** Everyone! Sharpen your spears and tighten your bowstrings! Let us ready ourselves for the battle ahead!

The warriors murmur their agreement and return to their positions.

**Hedone:** That arcane trick of yours was truly a sight to behold, my friend!

**Eurus:** I only did my best.

**Eurus:** There is something I need to tell you. This morning, before setting out, we received a carrier pigeon from Apeiron.

**Hedone:** Those number-worshippers? Now that is a rarity!

**Hedone:** About Greece, is it?

**Eurus:** It is under the assault of the Roman Empire. The last polis has now surrendered.

Hedone slams a fist on the railing beside her.

**Hedone:** Hades with it!

**Hedone:** If we were still in Greece, we'd have ...

**Eurus:** There is nothing we could have done. We already chose to carve out a new home rather than protect our old one.

**Hedone:** I could have put an arrow straight through that legion commander's skull!

**Eurus:** Not everyone is like you, Hedone.

**Eurus:** A year past, while you were out hunting that Harpy, our trade fleet crossed paths with the Romans.

**Hedone:** Both sides took heavy losses. Aganippe ... Nyx died in that battle.

**Eurus:** Yes. Word is the Empire has been attempting to track us down. We cannot continue to drift the seas forever.

**Eurus:** That is why I pray with everything I have that this expedition succeeds, that we may build a new polis.

**Aganippe:** Hedone! Look ahead!

A ball of fire tears through the clouds and slams into the stern of the ship.

**Hedone:** ...!

**Eurus:** Typhon?! How could it have found us so quickly?

**Hedone:** It's the volcano's power. Amphimachos!

**Impatient Warrior:** We're in the air! I cannot bring the water up from such a height!

**Eurus:** Then we have no choice but to push forward! If we descend to the sea again, there will be no escaping the whirlpool!

**Eurus:** I shall do what I can to keep the ship under control. Should be possible as long as the sails don't burn through completely.

**Eurus:** We can make it to the volcano before then!

Eurus runs to the base of the mast and slowly raises both hands, his muscles strained.

**Impatient Warrior:** Just see that you do not get us all killed!

More and more fire hurtles onto the deck. Flames spread across the ship.

The warriors raise their shields to bat away the falling embers, but they're helpless against the growing blaze.

**Aganippe:** Hedone, what are we to do?

**Eurus:** Is it really hopeless?

**Hedone:** Fireballs above, whirlpools and sea monsters below.

**Hedone:** Heh, Typhon has managed to drive me into a corner.

The burning hull begins to lose its balance, tilting toward the raging sea below.

They're still within range of the great whirlpool. Countless sea monsters circle the enormous mouth at its center.

**Hedone:** Perhaps these sea beasts can be commanded, as I have done when hunting beneath the tides.

**Hedone:** But we're too high up for them to hear my call. Should I leap down?

**Volcano:** Come, try my power.

**Hedone:** —!

She remembers the golden rings around her arm.

**Volcano:** The storms on the periphery are dangerous. The sea beasts know how to navigate them.

**Eurus:** You should not use this gift lightly, Hedone.

**Panicked Warrior:** Aaaahhhh!

A warrior catches fire and, in his panic, hurtles off the side of the ship.

**Hedone:** No time left to hesitate.

She pulls the golden rings off and hurls them out over the side.

**Hedone:** Come! I need you now!

The rings flash once in the air, then vanish into the dark waves below.

**Eurus:** Urgh!

The last of the sails burns away completely, and the ship plunges toward the sea.

**Worried Warrior:** We're falling!

**Restless Warrior:** Gods above!

A massive shadow approaches beneath the water, scattering the sea monsters.

An enormous whale breaks the surface, its back gently unfurling a spread of tentacles.

**Hedone:** Ceto!

**Hedone:** Everyone, slash a sail and jump ship! Try to glide to Ceto's back!

After a few unsure glances, the warriors quickly tear at the sails and find the courage to leap.

**Restless Warrior:** Gods watch over us.

One warrior after another vaults over the railing, tattered sails billowing and smoking behind them.

Hedone scans the ship for any injured or left-behind.

Beneath the blackened mast, Eurus stands firm.

**Aganippe:** Hedone! Make haste and jump!

**Hedone:** You go ahead.

**Aganippe:** But who will speak to the sea beast?

**Aganippe:** Arrgh!!

Hedone shoves Aganippe overboard, rolling to dodge the falling fire.

She charges across the tilting planks and in a few strides reaches Eurus's side.

**Hedone:** Eurus!

She grabs him by the shoulders and pulls him around to face her.

**Hedone:** I said, jump ship.

**Eurus:** I can do this. You must trust me! We are almost at the volcano!

A faint wind still swirls between his fingers.

**Hedone:** You can't!

**Eurus:** —!

Once again, she imposes her will and drags Eurus toward the edge of the ship.

**Eurus:** Let go of me, Hedone!

**Eurus:** Not everything has to rest on your shoulders!

Hedone has already leapt into the air.

Behind them, the flames swallow the ship whole.

It plummets into the sea, flashing for a moment before vanishing into the ocean depths.

**Eurus:** That was our best ship.

**Eurus:** I bought it from a Phoenician general, through the olive trade. It was their finest warship.

**Hedone:** It is only a ship. Let it go.

Ceto's tentacles wrap around the two of them as they fall, guiding them to a gentle landing on its back.

**Eurus:** But where will we buy another? The poleis under Roman rule have all cut off trade with us.

**Hedone:** We shall build another, right here upon this volcanic island.

**Hedone:** At this moment, there is no greater goal than slaying Typhon.

Hedone claps Eurus on the shoulder and fixes him with a serious look.

**Hedone:** That was dangerous back there. On the battlefield, you follow my orders.

Eurus falls silent for a moment.

**Eurus:** Forgive me, Hedone. I was of little use.

**Eurus:** In the end, it is always you we must rely on.

**Hedone:** Typhon is stronger than we first thought.

She strides past the crew resting on the whale's back and comes to stand beside the navigator.

Aganippe presses close against Ceto's skin, murmuring a spell to tame the sea beast under her breath.

**Hedone:** Nice work, Aganippe.

**Aganippe:** Hedone! I have never controlled a sea beast of this size before!

The girl's voice carries an unusual note of excitement.

**Aganippe:** I've already bid it carry us to the foot of the volcano!

**Aganippe:** This is all far more thrilling than I imagined! Grandmother should have let me join you long ago!

Ceto swims through the sea, tentacles lifting their scattered companions out of the water.

But some are too far away. Hedone can only watch as a warrior in the distance gets pulled under.

**Hedone:** ...

Letting emotion take over in the middle of a campaign is dangerous.

**Aganippe:** Hedone! I'm not afraid. I shall grow stronger than Grandmother!

**Aganippe:** Hedone?

**Aganippe:** What troubles you? Is this battle not satiating you?

**Hedone:** For the first time since this campaign began ...

**Hedone:** I have an ill feeling in my chest.

The volcano's outline comes into clear view, its cliffs rising before them as if cut by the blades of the gods themselves.
