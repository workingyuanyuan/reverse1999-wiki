## Story

**Charlie:** ...

The cavalry left the town, dejected and broken.

As the gates closed, the bells chimed once more.

But this time, its toll was slow and drawn out.

**Charlie:** They're...

**Charlie:** They're gone...?

Charlie staggered back as if drained of all her strength.

She gasped violently, filling her lungs with air.

**Charlie:** T-they...really left?!

As she tried to compose herself, the people around her and the burning buildings lining the streets began to sway.

Finally, the whole scene shattered like a popped bubble, revealing the true appearance of the street.

The festive bonfire continued to blaze in the square, its flames turning the surroundings as bright as day.

But only a small portion of the crowd had vanished; more people remained behind her.

Yes, it wasn't an illusion, but rather they had been all, or almost all, too real.

After a few moments of silence, a cheer erupted from the crowd.

**Old Man:** We did it! I can't believe it, we actually drove them away!

**Vendor:** Hurrah!

**The Fool:** A flawless performance, Troupe Master Charlie! A flawless performance!

**The Fool:** A haunting performance of unprecedented magnitude!

**Charlie:** L—Let's not talk about it anymore!

**Charlie:** Oh... I... I was so scared. Just thinking about what happened... I can't stop shaking!

**Jeffrey:** You... You did it! You really scared off the Duke's men!

**Charlie:** No... I didn't do anything at all! I might have put everyone at risk!

**Charlie:** If it weren't for all of you ... my poor tricks would've been exposed ...

**Old Man:** There's no need to be modest. You gave us the courage to resist. We came together because of your call.

**Jeffrey:** This is an outright victory, Troupe Master Charlie.

**Jeffrey:** You gave them a taste of their own medicine and taught them a lesson for all of us.

**Charlie:** R-Really...?

**The Fool:** Mr. Jeffrey was right. These knights will never reveal what happened today.

**The Fool:** The tyrant's lackeys rely on lies and fear to maintain power, fearing people might discover how weak he truly is...

**Jeffrey:** Their actions are driven by self-interest. They won't speak of this; it would never serve their purpose.

**Charlie:** That's amazing! We really did it!

**The Fool:** But, Troupe Master Charlie, I must remind you of something.

**The Fool:** Though emotions will run high for now, please don't forget there's another act still to come.

**Charlie:** Something greater still...

**Charlie:** Oh!

**The Fool:** Our earlier performance was but a dress rehearsal. What's comes next is the real show, the most crucial of our performances.

**The Fool:** The eager crowd has already gathered; step onto the stage and answer their call.

With the bells tolling, their audience converged on the square from all directions.

**The Fool:** Their faces filled with anticipation for a performance befitting the festival.

**Charlie:** ...Yes, yes! You're right, Mr. Fool! Now begins the most important part of the show!

She took a deep breath, her expression turning serious.

It was the same look she always wore when she stepped onto the stage.

**Charlie:** Let's get started, Mr. Fool.

**Charlie:** May we echo the promise we made to our audience and let them know that the theater we cherish won't end here.

**Charlie:** Because the joy of theater can have a lasting impact.

**Charlie:** It can overcome hardship, defeat fear, and even conquer the greed of Minos.

**Charlie:** Yes, even if it's an illusion, what people experience is far from some meaningless bubble.

**The Fool:** That is where our little story ends. I wonder, Miss Vertin, was the story to your satisfaction?

**The Fool:** You see, that is the true meaning of theater.

**The Fool:** Whether real or illusory, people will find in the performance an inspiration to resonate with.

**The Fool:** Were you wondering about which play the troupe is performing?

**The Fool:** If you ask me, it's still a little too early for Christmas.

**The Fool:** But for a story like this, ending it with a little comedy seemed quite fitting.

**The Fool:** I'm curious... What do you think?

**The Fool:** Oh, there's no need to answer. I believe deep down inside, you already know.

**The Fool:** —But all this remaining the same, I hope you enjoyed the story.
