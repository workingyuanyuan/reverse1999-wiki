Outside the School, SPDM

The sense of fulfillment that had filled Matilda's chest slowly fades as she steps beyond the gate, dimming with the evening sky.

**Matilda:** "\*sigh\*"

**Beryl:** "It seems my daughter had a little difficulty today."

**Matilda:** "Maman? I told you, you didn't have to come pick me up."

Matilda's voice grows smaller under her mother's calm gaze.

**Matilda:** "Hmph. Looks like my promotion is done for."

**Matilda:** "But I have no regrets. If I had to do it again, I would still make the same choice."

**Principal:** "I don't recall putting that on the curriculum."

Behind the heavy walnut desk, the man's tone reveals nothing—no anger, no approval. Lavender, standing behind the monitor director, trembles slightly.

The director lowers her gaze, a look no student has likely ever seen before.

**Monitor Director:** "My deepest apologies, sir ..."

**Monitor Director:** "This was all ... a failure in my supervision. I'll make no excuses."

**Principal:** "You?"

The man behind the desk pauses, lost in brief thought. A heavy silence fills the room.

**Principal:** "But from what I've heard, it was that monitor assistant, Ms. Matilda Bouanich, who arranged this whole thing, wasn't it?"

**Monitor Director:** "As you said, she's only an assistant—"

Her words are gently, yet firmly, cut off.

**Principal:** "She reminds me of you when you were younger—always full of ideas."

**Principal:** "And the young lady behind you, your name's Lavender, correct? Madam Harper has spoken of you."

**Principal:** "What's your opinion of Matilda Bouanich?"

**Lavender:** "I ..."

**Principal:** "No need to hold back, Ms. Lavender. I've already heard about Ms. Bouanich's promotion to level three investigator. I'm genuinely pleased for her."

**Principal:** "Whatever answer you give today, I assure you no one will be penalized. After all, the Committee's decisions aren't ours to control."

**Principal:** "I just want your honest opinion."

**Lavender:** "Yes, Principal. Please wait a moment. I've recorded everything that happened today."

In truth, she doesn't know how to answer. She doesn't want to hide anything, but she still isn't sure how much she should say.

What's the right answer? What does "right" even mean?

Her mind goes blank as she flips open her notebook.

"The most important thing is to be yourself—to become the person you want to be."

The image of the person who said those words flickers in her mind.

What would she say? And what does she want to say?

**Lavender:** "I think ..."

She takes a deep breath.

**Lavender:** "I think Ms. Matilda is an impressive monitor assistant."

**Lavender:** "And in the distant future ... maybe, she'll become the Great Matilda Bouanich."

Outside the School, SPDM

\*beep-beep—beep-beep\*

The cheerful tone makes Matilda jolt upright. She fumbles out her portable communicator, her hands trembling.

**System Notification:** "Ms. Matilda Bouanich, your clearance level has been updated to level three investigator. The St. Pavlov Foundation looks forward to your continued contributions—"

She doesn't read the rest. Holding the device close, her emotions soar like a roller coaster shooting straight from the depths up into the clouds.

**Matilda:** "Maman, Maman! Look—"

Matilda leaps into her mother's arms, and their day ends with a hearty celebratory dinner.

The next morning, Matilda Bouanich reports to the St. Pavlov Foundation, ready to receive her first mission as a level three investigator.
