## Story

8:00Clear

Guest Room, St. Pavlov Foundation

***

***

Guest Room, St. Pavlov Foundation

**Matilda:** "Well? What do you think, Maman?"

The girl twirls across the black-and-white tiles, showing off her coat embroidered with the same pattern. It hangs a little loose on her, but the joy in her movement makes such a detail irrelevant.

**Beryl:** "You look beautiful, my dear. But ..."

**Beryl:** "There. Much better."

Beryl brushes the fringe from her daughter's forehead and nods with a gentle smile.

**Beryl:** "My, how you have changed!"

**Matilda:** "Of course, Maman! I'm all grown up now."

**Matilda:** "Look!"

Matilda hops over to her desk and grabs a letter.

Tap to open the letter

***

***

She carefully unfolds it, as if afraid it might tear.

**Matilda:** "In recognition of special investigator Matilda Bouanich's outstanding performance, the Committee has decided to promote her to official investigator level three."

**Matilda:** "This appointment is effective immediately. Identity and clearance levels will be updated shortly.

—St. Pavlov Foundation, Investigator Management Center"

***

***

**Matilda:** "Finally! I've waited so very, very long for this. Aha! Finally!"

**Matilda:** "Now you can't make any more excuses, Maman!"

She puffs her cheeks and shoots her mother a playful glare.

Some time ago, her mother had vanished without a trace. Even the Foundation couldn't find her. Matilda had spent many nights tossing and turning over it.

Then, a few days ago, Beryl reappeared, safe and calm as ever. Rumor had it she had returned to meet with Madam Z and Matilda's old classmate, the one who became the "Timekeeper."

But no matter how Matilda pressed for answers, her mother only smiled and said—

**Beryl:** "You must uncover the truth yourself. I have already taught you everything you need to know."

Just as she is now.

That's how her mother has always been. Matilda has learned to accept it.

**Matilda:** "Alright. The only thing left is to hand over my duties as monitor assistant. They said they have already found someone, and I must meet her today."

**Matilda:** "While I'm away, the newcomer will take over disciplinary duties at the school. So, today is my great foray into tutorship! Lucky for me, it's Field Trip Day, so there won't be any students around."

She confidently puffs her chest, then tilts her head, a slight frown growing on her face.

**Matilda:** "Still, ever since I woke up this morning, I've had this uneasy feeling. Like something bad is going to happen ..."

**Matilda:** "I tried divination with my orbuculum, but the reading was unclear."

Matilda glances at her mother, already knowing what kind of answer to expect.

**Beryl:** "I have taught you more than just divination."

**Matilda:** "Aha! I know what's bothering me."

**Matilda:** "It's that awful woman—the monitor director. She doesn't like me. Hmph, well, I don't like her either."

***

***

Main Hall, The Foundation

**Matilda:** "Huh? That's ..."

It was not long after Matilda's graduation, on one of her many visits to St. Pavlov Foundation, that she caught sight of a familiar figure in the hall.

**Matilda:** "Hello, Madam Z!"

**Z:** "Hello, Matilda."

There was no way she'd let this opportunity slip by.

**Matilda:** "I submitted my application to become an investigator, but I still haven't heard back."

**Matilda:** "All my classmates have been assigned except me! Do you know why? Hm? Do you?"

**Z:** "Investigator?"

She paused for a moment, then gave a faint smile.

**Z:** "I'm not sure about the details, but if I remember correctly, you're younger than most of your classmates, aren't you?"

**Matilda:** "You mean ... my age?"

This wasn't the answer she was hoping for, and not something that could be fixed through determination alone.

**Matilda:** "But ..."

**Z:** "I know you did well in school. I'm sure you'll make an excellent investigator one day."

**Z:** "But this is a dangerous line of work. Perhaps for now, you could start as a monitor assistant—build some experience. What do you say?"

**Monitor Director:** "What?"

An exclamation of disbelief from a voice Matilda had hoped she'd never hear again. The monitor director stormed over, her face dark with outrage.

**Monitor Director:** "Madam Z, I know I asked for help in finding a candidate, but ... her? Please tell me you're joking."

The director shot Matilda a glare, her expression a mix of shock and fury.

**Monitor Director:** "She's got to be one of the least disciplined students we've ever had!"

#### Notes · Field Trip Day

It's an extended learning curriculum launched by the SPDM in the past two years, sending students to explore Foundation branches, Laplace laboratories, and Zeno training grounds. While the options are limited, at least from now on, the students have one more event to look forward to besides the annual Parade Ceremony. It's said that the proposal for this extended learning curriculum went through a multi-year process before it was ultimately approved and executed.
