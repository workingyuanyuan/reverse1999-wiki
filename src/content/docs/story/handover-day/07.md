## Story

***

Guardhouse

**Matilda:** "Alright, enough stories. Now you two stay put and finish your detention properly."

**Matilda:** "If you're as curious as you say about the outside world, then don't do any more silly things!"

**Matilda:** "That way, next year, you'll get to go on the field trip with everyone else."

Matilda turns to say goodbye, but the kids' faces crumple into disappointment.

**Girl:** "Even if we behave, we won't get picked."

**Matilda:** "Picked? What do you mean?"

**Matilda:** "Field Trip Day should be for everyone."

**Boy:** "It was that way last year. But this time, they said only the ones they choose get to go."

The boy hangs his head. Something in his tone makes Matilda's eyes narrow.

**Matilda:** "You mean there are more children still left in school?"

**Matilda:** "Where are they?"

***

***

Classroom, SPDM

Matilda knocks once before pushing the door open.

The classroom is full but dead silent—so silent it's suffocating.

All the blank young eyes turn toward her, pens freezing mid-stroke. Behind the podium, the monitor director rises to her feet.

**Monitor Director:** "Lina, John?"

She stares in disbelief at the two children behind Matilda, then glances toward the back row—two desks piled high with books.

**Monitor Director:** "Hah! You two never learn your lesson, do you?"

**Monitor Director:** "Well done, Ms. Bouanich. You two! Up front, now—"

**Matilda:** "Madam Harper, I'm here for you."

Matilda cuts her off.

**Matilda:** "May I have a word outside?"

Something in Matilda's eyes makes the director hesitate. After a moment's scrutiny, she agrees.

**Monitor Director:** "Continue your self-study. I'll have no fooling around, understood?"

***

***

Hallway, SPDM

**Lavender:** "Ms. Matilda, what are you going to do?"

In the few seconds before the monitor director steps out, Lavender tugs gently on Matilda's sleeve.

**Matilda:** "You knew about this, didn't you? Why didn't you tell me?"

But when she sees Lavender's face, she swallows the rest of her words.

**Matilda:** "Don't worry. Leave it to me."

**Lavender:** "But ..."

The classroom door swings open. The director's gaze is as sharp as a blade.

**Monitor Director:** "Ms. Bouanich, make it quick. I don't have time to waste."

**Matilda:** "I'd like to know why so many students weren't allowed to join the field trip. What is this new selection system about?"

**Matilda:** "It doesn't seem to match the reason why Madam Z introduced the Field Trip Day."

**Monitor Director:** "What are you implying? It's a school decision."

Matilda takes a deep breath, her voice trembling slightly, but she's already made up her mind.

> **Choice 1：** Make a stand

**Matilda:** "Then the school's decision is wrong."

She'd promised herself she would "stay out of trouble" today, but she's changed her mind.

She was once like them—a scolded child in a classroom, looking out the window, hoping any passing grown-up would speak up in the name of fairness.

But no one ever did.

But now, she can be that grown-up she always hoped to see.

**Monitor Director:** "Wrong? Do you hear what you're saying?"

**Monitor Director:** "We announced the rules long ago. Anyone who breaks school regulations or fails classes loses the right to participate."

**Monitor Director:** "Yet these children still refused to behave and still ignored their studies. Is that fair to the good students? Those who did everything right?"

> **Choice 1：** That's not the point.

**Matilda:** "You're right, good behavior deserves reward, and mistakes should have consequences."

**Matilda:** "But this punishment isn't right!"

She looks toward the classroom—at the eyes peeking up at her, filled with quiet hope.

**Matilda:** "Even the so-called 'bad kids' have a right to be curious about the outside—a right to see the world they were born into."

**Matilda:** "You know how precious this opportunity is for them."

> **Choice 2：** But ... but ...

For a moment, Matilda can't find the right words. She tries to steady her voice, but it's too late. The director has already decided to end it.

**Monitor Director:** "Ms. Bouanich, I suggest you watch your words."

**Monitor Director:** "Now, if you're done, I have to get back to class."

**Matilda:** "Wait!"

A sudden wave of inspiration washes over her.

**Matilda:** "At least ... at least don't lock them up in the classroom!"

**Monitor Director:** "What do you mean by that?"

**Matilda:** "Even if they can't go on the field trip, there is no rule saying they have to stay in the classroom for self-study, is there?"

The director doesn't answer. It seems she guessed right.

> **Choice 1：** Every child deserves this right.

**Matilda:** "When Madam Z first proposed the Field Trip Day, the goal was to let all students safely and appropriately explore the world beyond school walls."

**Matilda:** "That is exactly what last year's notice said. It's still pinned on the main hall bulletin board."

**Matilda:** "You heard me, Madam Harper. It says 'all students.'"

**Monitor Director:** "What exactly are you trying to say, Monitor Assistant Matilda Bouanich?"

**Matilda:** "I ..."

> **Choice 2：** So ... so ...

For a moment, Matilda can't find the right words. She tries to steady her voice, but it's too late. The director has already decided to end it.

**Monitor Director:** "Ms. Bouanich, I suggest you watch your words."

**Monitor Director:** "Now, if you're done, I have to get back to class."

**Matilda:** "Wait!"

A sudden wave of inspiration washes over her.

**Matilda:** "At least ... at least don't lock them up in the classroom!"

**Monitor Director:** "What do you mean by that?"

**Matilda:** "Even if they can't go on the field trip, there is no rule saying they have to stay in the classroom for self-study, is there?"

The director doesn't answer. It seems she guessed right.

> **Choice 1：** They should be part of the field trip.

**Matilda:** "I will take them."

**Monitor Director:** "You?"

Her voice rises sharply, eyes locked on Matilda as though she's staring at some foreign creature.

**Monitor Director:** "You don't have the authority. Not even I do."

**Monitor Director:** "Ms. Bouanich, you're being reckless. You have no idea what you're doing."

**Matilda:** "I know exactly what I'm doing."

**Matilda:** "The door is right here beside me. All I have to do is open it, and they will be free."

**Matilda:** "I bumped into the 'Razor' Squad earlier. They have a car to drive us to the site. We will all be safe with them there."

**Matilda:** "The children may have missed the morning events, but if we leave now, we'll still make it in time for the picnic and afternoon activities."

**Monitor Director:** "Do you understand the consequences of this?"

**Matilda:** "I do, Madam Harper."

Matilda takes a few steps forward and grips the doorknob.

She opens the door.

> **Choice 2：** I-I think ...

For a moment, Matilda can't find the right words. She tries to steady her voice, but it's too late. The director has already decided to end it.

**Monitor Director:** "Ms. Bouanich, I suggest you watch your words."

**Monitor Director:** "Now, if you're done, I have to get back to class."

**Matilda:** "Wait!"

A sudden wave of inspiration washes over her.

**Matilda:** "At least ... at least don't lock them up in the classroom!"

**Monitor Director:** "What do you mean by that?"

**Matilda:** "Even if they can't go on the field trip, there is no rule saying they have to stay in the classroom for self-study, is there?"
