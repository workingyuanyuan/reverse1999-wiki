## Story

10:15Clear

Behind the School Building

***

***

Behind the School Building

**Girl:** "Oh no, it's the monitor assistant!"

**Boy:** "Go! Climb, climb!"

The kids can't be older than six or seven. The moment they spot Matilda and Lavender, panic flashes across their faces, and they scramble upward in a flurry.

Isn't today supposed to be Field Trip Day? What are students still doing here?

There's no time to think. The boy slips, his foot missing the ledge below.

**Lavender:** "He's going to fall!"

**The window to act is gone in an instant.** The incantation Matilda learned in class springs to her lips—

***

***

> **Choice 1：** Cast the spell.

The boy freezes midair, then slowly drifts down onto the grass. His face is as pale as chalk.

**Matilda:** "Hah! Good for you, the Great Matilda Bouanich was here! That could've ended very badly!"

> **Choice 2：** Do not cast the spell.

**Matilda:** "Ŝvebi sta—"

Bad news: she's a split second too slow.

The incantation that came effortlessly to her in class catches on her tongue. The boy is about to crash headfirst—

> **Choice 1：** No!

In a burst of adrenaline, Matilda lunges forward.

**Matilda:** "Ouch!"

**Lavender:** "Ms. Matilda! Are you ... are you alright?"

Matilda doesn't have the breath to answer. She pushes the boy off of her, groans, and rolls twice across the ground before managing to sit up.

Bad news: she's pretty sure there's a lump forming on her head.

Good news: no one cracked theirs open in front of her.

> **Choice 2：** This can't happen!

In a burst of adrenaline, Matilda lunges forward.

**Matilda:** "Ouch!"

**Lavender:** "Ms. Matilda! Are you ... are you alright?"

Matilda doesn't have the breath to answer. She pushes the boy off of her, groans, and rolls twice across the ground before managing to sit up.

Bad news: she's pretty sure there's a lump forming on her head.

Good news: no one cracked theirs open in front of her.

But Matilda has let her guard down too soon.

**Girl:** "Stoney! Are you okay—ahh!"

The girl leans forward to look and, in the panic of seeing her friend fall, loses her balance.

**Girl:** "Ahhh!"

She comes hurtling down, and this time, Matilda is too slow.

A thought flashes through her mind, heavy with dread: It's over.

But what follows isn't the dull thud she expects, nor a child's frightened wail—it's a familiar voice.

***

***

**???:** "—Hey! What's going on?"

**???:** "I've seen rain and hail fall from the sky, but never kids!"

A young man in silver-white armor stands before them. Luckily, he appeared just in time to catch the girl.

He gently sets the girl down. She is unhurt, but pale and shaken.

**Matilda:** "'Razor' Squad? Superb timing! But how did you ...?"

She freezes as she notices the face beneath the helmet.

**Matilda:** "La vache! ... J?!"

**J:** "Long time no see, French Fry! Didn't think I'd run into you today."

**Matilda:** "What are you doing here? You joined the 'Razor' Squad?"

She steps closer, her eyes lighting up as she examines his gleaming armor.

**J:** "Not exactly. I'm a ... what's the word? Right—an intern."

**J:** "The squad's doing a presentation here soon, so we came to scout the place. Gonna be a mock battle too—the captain's making me do it ..."

**J:** "Ugh, I get that it's an honor or whatever, but I ain't the kinda guy to pretend to fight—cramps my style."

**Matilda:** "What? You're a great show-off! It should be easy, non?"

**J:** "Hey, I ain't a show-off; I'm the real deal! Don't gotta put it on when cool just oozes from you."

An unexpected reunion, full of laughter and nostalgia.

**J:** "So, you're a mentor now, huh? Not bad."

He glances at Lavender standing quietly behind her, then gives Matilda a pat on the shoulder.

**J:** "Make sure you set a good example, huh?"

***

***

With a wave, the young man strides off. Time to get back to business.

**Matilda:** "Do you two have any idea how dangerous that was? Well? What do you have to say for yourselves?! Why were you sneaking into the building? Planning a prank, hm?"

The two kids exchange a nervous glance.

No matter how Matilda questions them, they just mumble.

**Matilda:** "You do realize that according to school rules, this means ... you'll be sent to ..."

Matilda can't bring herself to finish the sentence, but Lavender delivers the verdict coldly.

**Lavender:** "The guardhouse."
