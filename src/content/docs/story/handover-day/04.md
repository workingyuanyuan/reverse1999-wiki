## Story

10:45Clear

In Front of the School Building

***

***

In Front of the School Building

**Boy:** "I don't want to go to the guardhouse! It's dark and cold in there."

**Girl:** "And the rats are as big as puppies, and they bite!"

**Girl:** "Please, just this once, let us go, please?"

The two continue to plead as Matilda marches them to the guardhouse. She almost gives in. She's done the same before.

Sure, mistakes should be punished, but it should be something like a week without candy or half allowance for a month.

She knows what the guardhouse is like. The place has cruel little "lessons" meant to make naughty children "remember their mistakes."

**Matilda:** "No. Rules are rules. If you didn't want to end up there, you shouldn't have been so reckless."

Or you shouldn't have been caught. Matilda finishes the thought silently and steels herself.

***

***

As their cries continue behind her, she glances back at Lavender.

She's scribbling once again in her notebook.

**Lavender:** "..."

***

***

Guardhouse

**Matilda:** "Not this one. Much too drafty, they'll catch a cold."

**Matilda:** "And this one. Why is the floor all wet? Ugh, disgusting! That will need cleaning later."

Matilda opens door after door until only one room remains.

**Matilda:** "Hmph, what are all these Shamirs doing here?! I swear I cleared them out last time!"

**Matilda:** "Well, I suppose there's nothing for it—this is the only one left."

The moment she steps inside, the kids turn pale.

**Girl:** "But ..."

**Matilda:** "Don't worry. I will chase them out."

***

***

A burst of flame flashes across the cold tiles. Startled, the Shamirs scurry out through the crack under the door.

By the time the last one has wiggled its way out, the room already feels warmer.

**Lavender:** "The handbook doesn't mention cleaning the guardhouse room before locking students inside."

It's the first time her successor has spoken up.

**Matilda:** "Hmph. And does it say we cannot?"

**Lavender:** "... I see."

Matilda notices Lavender's eyes widen slightly before she flips her notebook open again to jot something down—who knows what.

Either way, Matilda just can't bring herself to leave a couple of kids who look like they just started Elementary Arcanum locked up in a room full of Shamirs.

**Matilda:** "Alright, get in. According to the rules, you'll have to stay in here until evening."

**Matilda:** "I'll return later to check on you. As long as you behave and think long and hard about what you've done, you will make it out in time for dinner."

Matilda turns to leave but feels someone tug at her coat.

**Boy:** "Can you stay with us?"

**Girl:** "Everyone else went out on the field trip. It's boring being stuck here alone."

A thought crosses Matilda's mind.

**Matilda:** "That's right. Today is Field Trip Day! What are you two still doing here?"

**Girl:** "We wanted to go, of course! But ..."

**Lavender:** "They found an old notebook with instructions on how to make a football in it—apparently from a former student. They smashed the window on the second floor."

**Boy:** "I'm sorry ..."

**Girl:** "We were just curious!"

The girl's cheeks flush bright red.

**Girl:** "We only wanted to know what it's like outside—what people out there do!"

**Lavender:** "That's against the rules."

**Lavender:** "The Student Handbook clearly states that students must not engage with the outside world unless given express permission."

**Girl:** "But if a teacher's watching, then it's fine, right? Otherwise, we wouldn't have field trips or Parade Ceremonies!"

The girl puffs up her cheeks and looks at Lavender, her fingers tightening around Matilda's coat.

**Girl:** "Tell us a story about being a 'special investigator'! I heard you talking with that big boy just now. You've been to lots of places, haven't you?"

**Girl:** "India, America ... I've only seen those names in books!"

**Boy:** "Please!"

All eyes fall on Matilda. She has no intention of saying no.
