## Cover

An Arcanist artwork completed on July 22nd. First put on exhibit in the 1990s and has remained on display for 20 years. It was originally displayed in the southeastern United States before being moved to the northeast.

Dimensions: 65 9/64 in ×30 13/64 in ×15 9/64 in

Medium: Design drawing

Fragrance notes: Flora, rose, pink pepper, raspberry flower, papyrus

Afflatus: Relatives from Venus (Star) - Monster Designer

## Item

### Candy-colored Accessories

Garish in color and cheap in texture, they go well with golden hues without obscuring the luster of long, meticulously cared-for tresses.

Every blonde girl ought to have some, and they're easy to come by.

### Pink Necklace

A seasonal release from a young luxury brand. The respondent couldn't care less about its disastrous price tag or its even more disastrous design—just as long as it makes her look more like a genuine "Pink Lady".

### Doll Plushie

Merch from the sensational "Li'l Scarface" series, this doll was sold for a limited time at the premiere. It was not cheap. Through playful shoving, squealing, and giggling with her female companions, she skillfully pocketed it under the pretense of congeniality.

## Story

### Blurred Over Time

Youth—the youth of an era, the youth of the entire United States. The boys danced to Jackson while the girls donned cotton headbands, clashing wristbands, and rainbow stockings. It was a world of twisting, shaking, and sweating on both sides of the television screen.

The new house her parents moved to was also on this vibrant stage—or rather, on a spinning roulette wheel. Wealth was the hand that spun the wheel, spinning it into a blur of color. As for the secret base in the hushed forest, the cool, placid lake, and the huge, ferocious monsters of her fantasies... these gloomy hues were swallowed up by more brightly-colored variants until eventually all she could see was a mottled mass.

It's okay, don't worry. They haven't disappeared.

They just became part of that mottled blur.

### Emerging Director

Pandora Wilson: What made you choose your major? I know you love cinema and I've heard stories about your stubbornness.

Pandora Wilson: But I'd like us to discuss something deeper, a reason.

Blonney: Oh... okay. It's simple, I think I have a gift.

Blonney: You can't deny that if you've seen my design drawings. I don't want to waste that gift.

Pandora Wilson: Every Arcanist knows that those "gifts" whisper in the crevices of their spine late at night so as not to be forgotten.

Blonney: Exactly.

Blonney: It tells me not to bury myself, not to let the world lose my buried monsters, or they'll regret it for the rest of their lives.

Blonney: So, when those sandwiches were put in front of me, I threw them out.

Pandora Wilson: ...

Pandora Wilson: I admire your ambition.

Blonney: You'd also admire my abilities.

### The Giallo Grind

> We have a cabin, a lake, and a cautionary tale that you'll definitely want to avoid.

"You want me to act in a local tourism promo? Sorry, my weekends are fully booked."

The girl waves politely, smiling as she turns down the eager talent scout, just like she always does. But then she calls out to the retreating figure.

"Wait ... there's a party I might be able to cancel."

She reaches into her bag and grabs her dream-filled notebook. Maybe it's time to make better use of opportunities that fall into her lap. "Fine, I'll model and act. But I'll be directing and editing it myself. Deal?"

Grindhouse, mill films, B-horror flicks ... she adores them all. But she's never been satisfied just playing the pretty, textbook girl in front of the lens. Maybe this is her shot to shatter the cliché.

#### Custom Double-trigger Revolver

A prop specially customized at the interviewee's request during an ad shoot.

Double-action, 5-round capacity, .45 caliber—this is no model gun. The prototype was based on a low-budget movie that the interviewee is particularly fond of.

#### "Good Kid" Plushie Texas Edition

A merch of the sensational "Li'l Scarface" series, only distributed in Texas cinemas during the movie's release. A priceless item among collectors. Once buried at the bottom of the film crew's prop vault, it was dug up and brought back into the public eye by the interviewee.

#### "The Heartseeker"

A necklace made by the interviewee using on-site props. Rather than wear the fake pearl necklace prepared by the costume team, she opted to express a little of her true self during the shoot.
