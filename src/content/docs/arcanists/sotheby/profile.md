## Cover

An arcanist's work. Exhibited in the early 20th century for 13 years. Completed in spring, on April 15. Exhibited in the City of Westminster, London, United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland.

Dimension: 64 9/16×28 7/10×27 9/16in.

Medium: Emerald

Fragrance note: Floral - Cananga Tree, Daisy, Vanilla

Inspiration: Concerto of the Woods \[Plant\] Potion Production

## Item

### Daedalus×CB

A pair of designer low heels of Daedalus's with decorative gems from Coro-na Borealis. The first version took eleven months, from measurement to production. Due to the long manufacturing time, the finished product did not fit Sotheby who had grown taller during this period. Daedalus, therefore, had to redesign a second version and speed up the process.

### Ruby

From the private collection of Sotheby's family. It is deduced that this set was made by an arcanist who produced jewelry for Elizabeth I, yet their name is no longer known. It was later purchased by Sotheby's family. This set is said to be a relatively affordable one in Sotheby's private jewelry collection.

### Typhon

Lady Sotheby had her maid at home use discarded cotton and fabric waste to sew this small doll, "Brave Typhon." Its design is based on Lady Sotheby's description and her maid's imagination. It is far from the Typhon in Greek mythology, and its inspiration was presumed to come from a rat.

## Story

### The Rise of Cities

The shadow of war has drifted away in the 1920s. Businesses and investments became the focuses of society once again and, with the expansion of the city, the desire for them grew swiftly.

Steel and concrete covered all available areas, creating a concrete forest inhabited only by humans and pets, taking up the space which once belonged to the trees, animals and stories of the past.

An increasing number of people left the countryside and moved into the city, including some arcanists.

As time passed, the arcanists' changed their meeting place from the wooden houses in the forest to the posh balls of high society.

The city turned out to be a great place for most arcanists. The only issue was that something seemed to be missing ...

For instance, animals.

As well as the stories.

I would like to share the latest research achievement I have made! Last month, the Nutrient Potion I made was proven to be very effective. Even the weakest maid has gained weight in a short time, but somehow the merchants don't want to promote it. I will keep trying.

Although I am not yet completely able to tell the difference between normal and arcanum animals, I have come up with a good way to identify the former quickly!

All the existing books on arcanum animals use normal animals to describe them. For example, "A Behemoth's body is like that of a hippo." So does that mean we can go the other way around? Like "A hippo's body is like that of a Behemoth"!

I believe this idea can help many friends who have the same problem as me!

...

Anyway, thank you again for reading my diary!

Feel free to ask me if you want to read it again! I will always show you ... even though I don't know who you are going to be!

### Dream of Abundance

Pandora Wilson: Hello, I am—

Sotheby: A talking mouth! This table with a mouth must be some kind of arcanum animal. Let me see, you are ...

Pandora Wilson: It's just a table. The talking mouth is my arcane skill.

Sotheby: Alright. Anyway ... amazing arcanum!

Pandora Wilson: Could you introduce "Typhon" to me?

Sotheby: Great, great! I'm glad to hear that question! What would you like to learn first? Shall we start with this pendant? "Ms. Mouth," can you see this cute "Typhon" pendant?

Pandora Wilson: I do see some pendants, but I'm not sure if it's cute or if it's the "Typhon" you talked about.

Sotheby: Yes, it is! There is no doubt that it's cute! My dear lady, don't you think it's cute? Just like your mouth conjured out of the table!

Pandora Wilson: I'm not quite sure if that's a compliment.

### The Red Thread of Fate

> With threads intertwined, in time we'll find our hearts realigned.

An elderly woman in Yongfeng Town often claimed she once encountered a spirit in her youth. She said that as a child she dug up an enormous ginseng root in the mountains, but before she could tie a red string around it, it burrowed back into the earth and vanished. The old woman believed it had transformed into an immortal spirit. Though her neighbors dismissed it as nonsense.

Her son worked the fields for a living. One day, after finishing his chores, he returned home to find a young girl playing by the doorway, twirling a red string between her fingers. A faint glow shimmered around her, though her face was oddly blurred and indistinct. Confused, he approached to ask who she was. The girl seemed to sense him and slipped away into the grass, leaving only a piece of red string behind.

He brought the string home to show his mother. The old woman clapped her hands in excitement. "It's her! She's the ginseng spirit I saw all those years ago!"

#### Bronze-Handled Silk Hehuan Fan

The fan bears embroidered intertwining branches, with tinkling tassels that shield against the sun, wind, and dust, while concealing many secrets. Frequently used as a token of love, the fan's giver and recipient remain known only to a matchmaker who ties their names to a red thread.

#### Love Lock

A gold lock adorned with pearls and red string knots, said to bind two hearts together, ensuring they never part. The interviewee accepts all requests, and a glimpse reveals countless names etched densely on the back—even the names of a pair of Critters.

#### "Meant to Be"

A thin and flexible red thread symbolizing all the connections and ties in the world. The interviewee uses it for a kind of string game, weaving intricate patterns between her fingers.

She kindly asked if we'd like to learn this ancient game, but for the sake of our readers' love lives, we politely declined.
