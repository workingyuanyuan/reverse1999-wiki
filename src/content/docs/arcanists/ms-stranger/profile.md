## Cover

An arcanist's work. Exhibited in the 20th century for 19 years. Completed in spring, on April 13. Originally displayed in Iceland, it was later secretly relocated to St. Pavlov Foundation. Once briefly exhibited in Manus Vindictae.

Dimensions: 70 5/64 x 34 1/4 x 25 19/32 in.

Medium: Phototaxis

Fragrance note: Woody - Black Pepper, Heliotrope, Honey, Sandalwood, Smoke

Afflatus: Dusted Scales \[Mineral\] Agent

## Item

### Token of Identity

While working, the interviewee always wears something moth-shaped that hints at her true identity—sometimes a pattern or print, sometimes a piece of jewelry. It's said to help her reveal who she really is when necessary. She never worries that this will draw unwanted attention. Few people can tell one insect from another.

### Graceful Disguise

The fan from the interviewee's time undercover among upper-class circles. A steady wave was enough to pull focus during a conversation, giving her an edge when reading people. Now it helps her manage her own expressions. She seems to have lost her precise control over every expression since retiring. A development that she finds delightful.

### Klein Blue Ribbon

A ribbon suited to the interviewee's former working environment. She picked it out herself at a clothing store, just an ordinary item off the rack. When asked if the look meant anything, she said it was group culture, something to build a sense of unity.

## Story

### A War over Loyalty

That Manus Vindictae possesses some means of securing its members' loyalty is a hypothesis that has long circulated.

"Otherwise, how could a bunch of slogan-shouting nobodies steal our supporters?"

And so a competition began—a test of unwavering loyalty. A considerable number of classified techniques were thrown into the fight.

"This is a safeguard for special circumstances. A backup in case the first line of defense falls."

The competition ended with loyalty brought under total control. A byproduct of the Loop Laboratory's experiments had made the Phototaxis a reality.

Yet a competition built on hypothesis could never prove its own worth. The plan was permanently erased.

Today, only a handful of traces remain, their very existence proof that this conflict in the shadows once took place.

### Her Special Way of Processing Secret Files

The finance officer stands in a public phone booth, watching his building burn. The black smoke is thickest right where his office was. Three minutes ago he had been in there, working late on some critical documents.

"Who are you?! You tricked me into coming out here, and now look what's happened!"

"It's for your own good." A young woman's voice comes through on the other end. "Forget about today. Your transfer order will arrive tomorrow. You'll have an easy, well-paid job."

The line goes dead.

...

"My orders didn't include doing that financier any favors."

"He has no memory of the misplaced files. All traces have been removed."

In the black-and-white checkered room, the girl watches the moth resting in her palm and answers her guardian's question.

### Based on a True Story

Pandora Wilson: You seem excited for this interview.

Ms. Stranger: Yes. This is the first time I've ever spoken to the press as my true self.

Pandora Wilson: Does it feel like stepping into the light?

Ms. Stranger: In a way, yes. Avoiding journalists and cameras used to be an ironclad rule for me.

Pandora Wilson: A lot of undercover agents find that years of staying hidden leave them with few outlets for self-expression.

Pandora Wilson: And that isolation makes it hard for them to express themselves, so they turn to something like novels instead.

Ms. Stranger: I don't think that's quite the case for me.

Pandora Wilson: You have your own thoughts on that.

Ms. Stranger: Take me as an example ... If I wanted to run a serial in your magazine ...

Pandora Wilson: It would sell. You could have your own column—we're open to any format.

Ms. Stranger: So, would you rather the first issue catch Manus Vindictae's attention, or expose the Foundation's secrets?

Pandora Wilson: I see. When you put it that way, it does seem a little dramatic.

Ms. Stranger: You need a little drama to say anything worth saying, ma'am.

### Waltz of the Depths

> Through her weightless dance, secrets travel on currents between meeting eyes.

The dance was meant to flow like currents.

Underwater dance demands it, and yet she falls short. Repeated training has taught her the precise execution of every movement, but it has also worn away some of her natural grace. Her exacting command of her own body makes up for part of the loss, though: as a spy, she knows how to make herself appear soft and pliable when the occasion calls for it.

Mark the position. Strike the pose. Execute every detail, then adorn it with a smile. Oh, what would a waltz be without a smile?

A dance between two people, gliding across the floor and through the crowd, draws the target closer, then seems to let them slip away, only to draw them back in. Its rhythm is the rhythm of attraction, grace, and desire.A little more lightness in the step, a little more mystery in the eye ... Water is a medium that can take a thousand forms; a dance within it can be all the more expressive.

O Performer of such rare skill, what's on your mind when you gaze at the water's reflection?

#### "Fleeting Radiance"

A curved headpiece made of pearls and blades of grass.

Its sheen is soft yet fragile, and several of its pearls have dulled after long immersion in seawater. Perhaps such blemishes matter little in a performance—not every spectator will care about a tiny crack in a magnificent show.

#### "Constancy of Self"

A handful of lush green flower wands, used primarily to enhance underwater performances by creating bubbles, scattering petals, and the like.

This bundle of flower wands also helps performers adjust their posture and maintain their balance underwater. To the interviewee, it seems to carry a special significance concerning such ideas as "self" and "unity."

#### "The Hem of the Mediterranean Sea"

A shawl adorned with mother-of-pearl and inlaid shell, woven by water spirits using a special shuttle-weaving technique.

The iridescent light shimmering across its sheer fabric is mesmerizing, as though imbued with the water spirits' arcane skill. During "that crucial performance," it briefly dazed a target seated in the audience, giving the interviewee a chance to obtain vital intelligence.
