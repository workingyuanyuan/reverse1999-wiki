## Story

### Prank War

After WWII, the eagle and the bear bared their fangs at the throne.

The Iron Curtain speech signaled the beginning of the lengthy Cold War. The government picked out the children with arcanum talent from all over the world and sent them to Zeno Armaments Engineering and Technology Academy where they would be trained to become a hidden yet powerful force.

Under the strict adminstration of Zeno, they have grown into obedient, persistent, efficient and silent soldiers.

But they were still young children after all, and children always make trouble.

They came from different countries and had different faiths, ideas and habits, which led to conflicts. The most noticeable troublemaker among them was, without a doubt, the pilot girl from Russia.

That was the 13th time she defeated her challengers. The exhaust of her broom raised a cloud of dust as her opponent cried.

"Told you ... it won't work. We shouldn't have messed with her. Why won't you listen to me?!"

"He's right. Don't overestimate yourselves." She made a face towards the child who fell to the ground, "The director wants to see me. Take your time, losers! Boo-hoo, mommy ..."

> Swordsmanship—a composite model of points, lines, and surfaces.

"Swordsmanship? You guys have a good sense of humor."

"Cut the crap. Just show me what you've got!"

Starting from the point of her sword, a line is drawn, then a plane, an angle ... All these elements combined together, they become an art, demonstrating the truth of geometry.

#### "Koch" the Sword

A sword that excels in both slashing and thrusting, existing for the most rational of swordsmanship. Combining straight lines, curves, and points, it vanquishes all irregularities. "Cutting is the basic means to solving geometric problems." So said the inventor of this sword technique.

#### Hexagram Pendent

A waist accessory with a star shape, linked with circles and triangles. It is a symbol of the graduate swordsman. The geometric lines represent endless exploration in the art of combat. It has been said that this trinket can command a small army.

#### Golden Laurel Crown

A crown made of gold and silver, each leaf intricately carved to show her victories and glory. However, the thorns and weight also constantly remind the wearer of the sacrifices made to achieve this glory.

Originally designed for long-range hearing tests, this arcanum device now serves as a starting gun for referees. The interviewee noted that its rhythmic whirring is loud enough to scare off any critters lurking in the stadium.
