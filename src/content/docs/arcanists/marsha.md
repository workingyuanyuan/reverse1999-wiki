## Incantations

### Virtue of Restraint

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 200% Reality DMG. Inflicts 3 stacks of \[Burn\] on the target hit. Gains 1 stack of \[Pain's Recompense\] after attacking.

If the target has at least 8 stacks of Burn, this attack deals an additional 100% Reality DMG.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 300% Reality DMG. Inflicts 5 stacks of \[Burn\] on the target hit. Gains 1 stack of \[Pain's Recompense\] after attacking.

If the target has at least 8 stacks of Burn, this attack deals an additional 150% Reality DMG.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 500% Reality DMG. Inflicts 8 stacks of \[Burn\] on the target hit. Gains 1 stack of \[Pain's Recompense\] after attacking.

If the target has at least 8 stacks of Burn, this attack deals an additional 250% Reality DMG.

### Burden Unspoken

**Rank 1**: Mass buff. Grants a Shield with (Marsha's ATK x150%) HP to all allies for 3 rounds. When actively cast, consumes 1 stack of \[Frontline Care\] to increase the Shield Multiplier by (Marsha's Critical Rate x40%) and dispels 1 type of \[Stats Down\] or \[Neg Status\] from all allies. If a target is already holding a Shield, the value of this Shield is stacked on top of that target's existing Shield, up to a maximum Shield value of (Marsha's ATK x650%) HP, and their Shield's duration is extended to 3 rounds. While holding this Shield, allies gain DMG Taken Reduction +30%.

**Rank 2**: Mass buff. Grants a Shield with (Marsha's ATK x225%) HP to all allies for 3 rounds. When actively cast, consumes 1 stack of \[Frontline Care\] to increase the Shield Multiplier by (Marsha's Critical Rate x60%) and dispels 1 type of \[Stats Down\] or \[Neg Status\] from all allies. If a target is already holding a Shield, the value of this Shield is stacked on top of that target's existing Shield, up to a maximum Shield value of (Marsha's ATK x650%) HP, and their Shield's duration is extended to 3 rounds. While holding this Shield, allies gain DMG Taken Reduction +30%.

**Rank 3**: Mass buff. Grants a Shield with (Marsha's ATK x375%) HP to all allies for 3 rounds. When actively cast, consumes 1 stack of \[Frontline Care\] to increase the Shield Multiplier by (Marsha's Critical Rate x100%) and dispels 1 type of \[Stats Down\] or \[Neg Status\] from all allies. If a target is already holding a Shield, the value of this Shield is stacked on top of that target's existing Shield, up to a maximum Shield value of (Marsha's ATK x650%) HP, and their Shield's duration is extended to 3 rounds. While holding this Shield, allies gain DMG Taken Reduction +30%.

## Ultimate: Violence Begets Violence

Mass attack. Gains 1 stack of \[Pain's Recompense\] and deals 750% Reality DMG to all enemies. Inflicts 4 stacks of \[Burn\] on the targets hit. After attacking, consumes up to 5 stacks of \[Pain's Recompense\] and grants the same number of \[Discipline Forged\] stacks to all allies for 3 rounds.

## Inheritance

**Insight 0 · Precognition**: Accumulates \[Lingering Glow\] (mutually exclusive with \[Bloodtithe\]) whenever an enemy is inflicted with \[Burn\] or \[Halo\].

**Insight 1 · Humanitarian Aid**: When Marsha enters battle, grants a Shield with (Marsha's ATK x150%) HP to all allies for 3 rounds (while holding this Shield, allies gain DMG Taken Reduction +30%) and gains a number of \[Morning Prayer\] stacks equal to the number of \[Lingering Glow\] characters on the field.

At the start of the round, Marsha gains 2 stacks of \[Frontline Care\], dispels 1 type of \[Control\] from any allies under \[Control\] effects, and grants \[Pardon\] to any other allies for 1 round.

**Insight 2 · Humanitarian Aid II**: Critical Rate +10% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Humanitarian Aid III**: Whenever 1 stack of \[Frontline Care\] is consumed, Marsha gains 1 stack of \[Pain's Recompense\].

At the start of the round, Marsha inflicts 3 stacks of \[Burn\] on all enemies.

After entering battle, Marsha gains Moxie +1. After Marsha scores a critical hit or consumes \[Frontline Care\], Marsha gains Moxie +1, up to +2 per round.

Marsha can reserve up to 2 excess Moxie (replenished at the end of the round if applicable).

## Statuses

- **Burn**: Healing Taken -15% (unstackable). At the end of the round, takes (the holder's ATK x4%) Genesis DMG (stackable up to 30 times, multiple stacks of Burn is regarded as 1 \[Neg Status\], removes 50% of the stacks when triggered).
- **Pain's Recompense**: Critical Rate +2.5%, Critical DMG +2.5% (stackable up to 20 times, undispellable).
- **Frontline Care**: After any ally is attacked, Marsha consumes 1 stack of \[Frontline Care\] (stackable up to 2 times) to cast a rank 1 \[Burden Unspoken\].
- **Discipline Forged**: Critical Rate +20%. When dealing DMG, 50% excess Critical Rate is converted into Critical DMG. Each stack grants Incantation Might +1% and Critical DMG +2.5% (each stack timed independently, undispellable).
- **Lingering Glow**: Accumulates \[Lingering Glow\] whenever an enemy is inflicted with \[Burn\] or \[Halo\]. The fewer enemies there are, the more \[Lingering Glow\] is accumulated.
- **Bloodtithe**: Whenever allies lose 3000 HP combined, accumulate 1 point of \[Bloodtithe\]; HP lost from being attacked accumulates \[Bloodtithe\] at only 30% efficiency.
- **Halo**: Healing Taken -25% (unstackable). At the end of the round, takes (the inflicter's ATK x4%) Genesis DMG (stackable up to 30 times, multiple stacks of Halo are regarded as 1 \[Neg Status\], removes 50% of the stacks when triggered).
- **Morning Prayer**: When 1/2/3 \[Lingering Glow\] allies are present, increases \[Lingering Glow\] accumulation by 3/5/8%.

  When 3 Lingering Glow allies are present: all enemies' \[Burn\] stack limits increase by 5. At the start of the round, for every 10 points of \[Lingering Glow\], grants Critical DMG +1% to all allies for the round, counting up to 100 points.
- **Control**: Statuses that limit characters' actions: Daze, Petrify, Nightmare, Freeze, Disarm, Silence, Seal.
- **Pardon**: While active, prevents the first \[Control\] effect inflicted on the carrier (removed after trigger, unstackable).
- **Shield**: Blocks certain DMG for a certain duration.
