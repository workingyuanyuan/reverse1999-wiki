## Incantations

### Old Idea

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 120% Reality DMG plus (the caster's Reality DEF ×80%) Genesis DMG.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 180% Reality DMG plus (the caster's Reality DEF ×120%) Genesis DMG. If the caster has an active \[Shield\] after the attack, Moxie +1 to the caster.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 300% Reality DMG plus (the caster's Reality DEF ×200%) Genesis DMG. If the caster has an active \[Shield\] after the attack, Moxie +1 to the caster.

### A New Wave

**Rank 1**: Mass buff. Grants a Shield with (the user's Reality DEF x150%) HP to all allies for 2 rounds.

**Rank 2**: Mass buff. Grants a Shield with (the user's Reality DEF x225%) HP to all allies for 2 rounds.

**Rank 3**: Mass buff. Grants a Shield with (the user's Reality DEF x375%) HP to all allies for 2 rounds.

## Ultimate: The Future Is Near

Self counter. Enters \[Taunt\] and \[Fanged Partner\] statuses and gains Control Immunity and Riposte: when attacked, deals 100% Reality DMG and (Reality DEF ×100%) Genesis DMG. Lasts 2 rounds.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · Visionary**: When the caster is attacked, gives the caster 1 stack of \[Guarding Instinct\] for 2 rounds.

**Insight 2 · Visionary Ⅱ**: DMG Taken -8% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Visionary Ⅲ**: After gaining a total of 15 \[Guarding Instinct\] stacks, enters \[Loyal Partner\] status and the caster no longer gains \[Guarding Instinct\].

## Portrait

**Portrait 1**: **The Future Is Near**'s effect changes to: when \[Fanged Partner\] ripostes, deals 110% Reality DMG plus (Reality DEF ×110%) Genesis DMG.

**Portrait 2**: **Old Idea**'s effect changes to: at 1/2/3 stars, deals (the caster's Reality DEF ×100/150/250%) Genesis DMG.

**Portrait 3**: **The Future Is Near**'s effect changes to: when \[Fanged Partner\] ripostes, deals 120% Reality DMG plus (Reality DEF ×120%) Genesis DMG.

**Portrait 4**: **The Future Is Near**'s effect changes to: when \[Fanged Partner\] ripostes, deals 130% Reality DMG plus (Reality DEF ×130%) Genesis DMG.

**Portrait 5**: **A New Wave**'s effect changes to: at 1/2/3 stars, grants a \[Shield\] with (Ms. NewBabel's Reality DEF ×170/250/410%) HP on all allies.

## Euphoria

**Epiphany 1, level 1**: When casting **A New Wave**, if the target already has a Shield, **A New Wave**'s Shield is stacked onto that existing Shield; this stacking effect cannot exceed 200% of the original Shield's maximum value.

**Epiphany 1, level 2**: When an ally attacks, the ally gains Critical Rate +1% for every 300 Shield value the ally has.

**Epiphany 1, level 3**: **A New Wave** gains an additional total Shield value +40%, and all allies gain Riposte DMG +25%.

**Epiphany 1, level 4**: When an ally is being attacked, immediately cast a rank 1 **A New Wave** as a follow-up attack, triggers up to once per round; after allies riposte a total of 3 times combined, cast a rank 1 **A New Wave** as a follow-up attack, triggers up to twice per round.

## Statuses

- **Shield**: Blocks certain DMG for a certain duration.
- **Taunt**: Draws the enemy's attention. When taking certain attacks, becomes the priority of the attacks.
- **Fanged Partner**: Immune to \[Control\]. Ripostes when the caster is attacked. The Riposte is regarded as 1 extra action.
- **Guarding Instinct**: Reality DEF +5% (stacks up to 5 times; each stack is timed independently).
- **Loyal Partner**: Reality DEF +25%. Immune to Moxie reduction effects (cannot be dispelled).
- **Control**: Statuses that limit characters' actions: Daze, Petrify, Nightmare, Freeze, Disarm, Silence, Seal.
