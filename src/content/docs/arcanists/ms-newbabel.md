Ms. NewBabel scales on her Reality DEF: her attack adds Genesis DMG based on it, and A New Wave shields every ally for a multiple of it. Her Ultimate makes her taunt and riposte every attack for 2 rounds, and her passives keep raising her Reality DEF each time she is hit.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old Idea (single-target attack) | 120% Reality DMG + Genesis DMG of 80% of her Reality DEF | 180% + 120%; Moxie +1 | 300% + 200%; Moxie +1 |
| A New Wave (mass buff) | Shield of 150% of her Reality DEF on all allies, 2 rounds | 225% | 375% |

At ranks 2 and 3, Old Idea gives Ms. NewBabel 1 Moxie if she has a [Shield] after the attack.

[Shield]: absorbs damage while it lasts.

## Ultimate: The Future Is Near

For 2 rounds, Ms. NewBabel enters [Taunt] and [Fanged Partner], ignores Control effects, and ripostes each attack on her for 100% Reality DMG plus Genesis DMG equal to 100% of her Reality DEF.

[Taunt]: draws enemy attention; certain attacks target the holder first.

[Fanged Partner]: the holder is immune to Control effects and ripostes when attacked; each riposte counts as an extra action.

## Euphoria

Euphoria opens at Insight III level 30. Ms. NewBabel has one Epiphany of four levels; it builds on A New Wave and on ripostes.

| Epiphany | Level | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | A New Wave on an ally who already has a Shield adds to that Shield, up to 200% of its original maximum. |
| 1 | 2 | When an ally attacks, it gains Critical Rate +1% for every 300 Shield value it holds. |
| 1 | 3 | A New Wave's total Shield value +40%; all allies gain Riposte DMG +25%. |
| 1 | 4 | When an ally is attacked, Ms. NewBabel casts a rank 1 A New Wave at once, at most once a round. Each time allies have riposted 3 times in all, she casts a rank 1 A New Wave, at most twice a round. |
