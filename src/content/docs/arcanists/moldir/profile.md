## Cover

A Mixed work. Exhibited in the 1990s for 24 years. Completed in Autumn, on September 13. Originally exhibited in Kostanay, Republic of Kazakhstan, before being relocated to Zeno Arms Academy, the exhibit was later toured across Siberia and Latin America.

Dimensions: 70 15/32 x 21 17/64 x 15 3/4 inches.

Medium: Majority

Fragrance Note: Woody - Guaiac Wood, White Lily, Vetiver

Afflatus: Whisper of the Cornered Beast \[Beast\], Sentinel Unit Lieutenant

## Item

### "World's #1 Sister"

Arcane carving, sharpened on whetstone, polished with alchemy. Entirely handmade, with zero industrial processing. Even the knife's etched lines are one-of-a-kind.

On the day she was promoted to Sentinel Unit Lieutenant, someone else pinned this medal on her.

### "Nanny's Blessings"

A silver belt adorned with yellow agate symbolizing her roots and the blessings she received. Whether she wears it depends on the mission. Better to leave her "Nanny's Blessings" at home than lose it on the battlefield.

### Toolkit

A plain, no-frills toolkit—you could buy one just like it at any industrial store. Before turning to her arcane skills, the interviewee always tries the "old way" first.

She sewed the storage pouch herself, as we learned during the interview.

## Story

### The "Storm" Orphans

Anyone who survived it will tell you: this might be the harshest conflict in recorded history.

But unlike a traditional war, it didn't create more orphans. The rain that flowed back up into the sky showed no mercy, surging like a tide and washing away everything on the beach—equally.

Memories of that day ... or those days ... have blurred. Maybe it was because she stood somewhere high enough. Maybe because she found shelter just in time under the right rooftop. Either way, she crossed a tide of time.

While the world reeled from upheaval, it became vast, strange, and scary. No one cared whether the girl in the corner had also lost her home.

Another letter returned to sender. She accepted it blankly.

Letters with no recipients became a sea of disorientation.

### Idyll of the Luberon

> Perhaps this moment of tranquility is what she's been yearning for.

In Provence's summer, lavender blooms into a purple sea.

Not far from the lavender fields stands Gordes Castle. Built in the 11th century, the fortress remains formidable even now, offering shelter to those displaced by war.

Moldir and her siblings have come here. After leaving the battlefield behind, they've finally crossed through the smoke and seen the world as it truly is. The shrieking in the air no longer comes from bombs, but from fireworks bursting overhead.

Yet war's shadow still clings to them. When you've grown accustomed to something, it's hard to simply walk away. Every night, Moldir inspects the castle grounds to ensure it can withstand any attack.

Again and again. Endlessly.

#### "Soaring Over the Steppe"

A silver circlet. It remains attached to her no matter how she moves, as if it were a part of her body. The engraved patterns likely originate from the steppe of her homeland. She once mentioned: "When something moves me, it must be connected to a place I've lived."

#### "Stainless Heart"

A set of repair tools. When the writer arrived, the interviewee was polishing them. Judging from their gentle gleam, they must see frequent use.

While demonstrating them for us, her clothing accidentally knocked one tool loose. Clearly, with her military background, she has yet to adapt to such decorative attire.

#### "A Reminder of Home"

A handcrafted medal remade into a brooch. The emblem is etched with traces of windblown sand, in stark contrast with the silk ribbon beneath.

When we brought it up, the interviewee's gaze turned firm—"It's just an old thing." The writer understood the subject was closed.

### Repose Amidst the Roses

> Folded in the linen rests the scent of roses and the memory of summer.

"When the full grain gilds the baking bread, grapes will turn to wine and wake again, and the roses will hold a grand parade ..."

The harvest wind has already crossed the mountains, and the celebration is making itself heard, just around the corner. On the eve of a festival, keeping the younger siblings in line is no easy task, but the eldest sister has a story, and it rouses the "reserve soldiers" to real enthusiasm for laundry duty. Mind the cuffs. Keep the uniform collars crisp. Use the right technique so fewer wrinkles set in ... She makes sure the cloth in every pair of hands comes out "banner-level clean."

At last, every "banner" passes inspection. Riding on sunlight and a kind breeze, they settle a patch of serene drowsiness onto the children's lashes.

"... And so, on this special holiday, every soldier who performed well received a Bulgarian rose. In praise of diligence, of wisdom, of the fragrance of life ..."

The sunlight gazing down at a napping rose must feel exactly as she feels now.

#### "The Scent of Home"

A rose plucked beneath the evening star over Saryarka, bearing the fragrance of distant lands and treated with an unknown arcane skill to keep it from wilting. The interviewee paired it with baksy silverwork to create this elegant earring. She revealed that wearing it as an earring was an aromatherapy technique recommended by a perfumer to calm the nerves.

#### "Sun-Blessed"

A black linen belt, prepared to match her Harvest Festival outfit. According to the interviewee, dark belts absorb light and heat more readily than pale clothes. As the garments dry, warmth reaches her waist first, as though the gift-givers of her childhood were still gathered around her.

#### "Her Cherished Duty"

A small, exquisite waist pouch embroidered in gold. The interviewee once showed what she keeps inside—clothespins, needle and thread, and several buttons. Though skilled at mending, she would rather use her warm hands than an arcane skill, sewing the afternoon sunlight into her younger siblings' hems one stitch at a time, year after year.
