## Incantations

### Empty Gums

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 150% Mental DMG. After attacking, inflicts 1 stack of \[Confusion\] on the target for 1 round.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 250% Mental DMG. After attacking, inflicts 1 stack of \[Confusion\] on the target for 1 round.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 450% Mental DMG. After attacking, inflicts 1 stack of \[Confusion\] on the target for 1 round.

### Lullaby

**Rank 1**: Mass healing. HP +(the caster's ATK x80%) for all allies. This healing effect gains an additional 30% Critical Rate.

**Rank 2**: Mass healing. HP +(the caster's ATK x120%) for all allies. This healing effect gains an additional 30% Critical Rate.

**Rank 3**: Mass healing. HP +(the caster's ATK x200%) for all allies. This healing effect gains an additional 30% Critical Rate.

## Ultimate: Song for the Bad Tooth

1-target attack. Deals 500% Mental DMG. This attack gains Critical Rate +30%. After this attack scores a critical hit, dispels \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], and \[Control\] effects from all allies.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · Baby Tooth Collector**: When entering battle, instantly gains 3 \[Baby Tooth\]. When any team member actively uses a character incantation, collects 1 \[Baby Tooth\]. At the start of the round, consumes 5 \[Baby Tooth\] to reduce the enemy team's Critical Resist and Critical DEF by 15%. Lasts 1 round. (Cannot trigger if Baby Tooth are insufficient.)

**Insight 2 · Baby Tooth Collector II**: Healing Done +10% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Baby Tooth Collector III**: When triggering the \[Baby Tooth\] Stats Down effect, all allies recover (Missing HP x10%) HP. When entering battle, gain additional \[Baby Tooth\] +2.

## Euphoria

**Epiphany 1, level 1**: Gains Max Eureka +5. At the start of the round, gains Eureka +2. Whenever an ally consumes Eureka, accumulate points of \[Inspiration\]. When entering battle, accumulates 5 points of \[Inspiration\].

\[Baby Tooth Collector\]: Actively casting incantations no longer grants \[Baby Tooth\]; instead, Tooth Fairy gains 1 \[Baby Tooth\] for every 4 Eureka consumed by allies. At the start of the round, Tooth Fairy now consumes up to 5 \[Baby Tooth\]; for each Baby Tooth consumed, grants DMG Dealt +4% to the first 3 attack instances of \[Impromptu Incantation\] for the round and inflicts 1 stack of \[Decayed Tooth\] on all enemies.

\[Baby Tooth Collector III\]: Whenever a \[Baby Tooth\] is consumed, Tooth Fairy now restores (Missing HP x5%) HP for all allies and additionally grants 2 points of \[Inspiration\] to Impromptu Incantation for the round.

**Epiphany 1, level 2**: **Empty Gums** New Effects: No longer inflicts \[Confusion\] on the target. Grants \[Continuous Action I\] for 1 round. After attacking, Tooth Fairy consumes 2 Eureka to grant attack instance +1 to Impromptu Incantation for the round.

**Epiphany 1, level 3**: **Lullaby** New Effects: Consumes 2 Eureka to grant DMG Dealt +20% to Impromptu Incantation for the round.

**Epiphany 1, level 4**: **Song for the Bad Tooth** New Effects: After attacking, grants 8 points of \[Inspiration\] to Impromptu Incantation for the round and inflicts \[Neuralgia\] on the target for 1 round.

## Statuses

- **Confusion**: Critical Resist -25% (stackable, each stack timed independently).
- **Control**: Statuses that limit characters' actions: Daze, Petrify, Nightmare, Freeze, Disarm, Silence, Seal.
- **Baby Tooth**: At 5 stacks, is consumed at the start of the round and triggers special effect.
- **Inspiration**: For every 1 Eureka consumed, accumulate 1 point of \[Inspiration\].

  At the start of each round, randomly distribute Inspiration points onto basic incantations (each incantation may receive up to 5 points of Inspiration, and at the end of the round, the points of Inspiration on unused incantations are cleared).

  When actively casting an incantation with \[Inspiration\] points on it, add an \[Impromptu Incantation\] to the end of the spell queue, and all Inspiration points on these casted incantations will be granted to the \[Impromptu Incantation\].
- **Impromptu Incantation**: 1-target attack. Deals 200% Mental DMG to a random enemy and an additional (the total points of Inspiration granted to Impromptu Incantation x15%) Mental DMG. If Impromptu Incantation has at least 3/6/12/20 points of Inspiration, it gains attack instances +1/2/3/5 (Impromptu Incantation's DMG calculation is based on the average stats of all allies).
- **Decayed Tooth**: Critical Resist Rate -10%, Critical DEF -10% (stackable up to 5 times, each stack timed independently).
- **Continuous Action I**: AP +1.
- **Neuralgia**: After being attacked by Impromptu Incantation, takes (the corresponding attack instance's total DMG x30%) \[Static Genesis DMG\]. Only 1 instance of \[Neuralgia\] can exist at a time.
- **Static Genesis DMG**: This type of Genesis DMG is not affected by relevant stat bonuses.
