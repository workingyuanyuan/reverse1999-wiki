## Story

### What Remains Unchanged in Ever-Changing Times

Two paths lie before her: an internship offer from a research institution, and the lemonade and cookies she had always dreamed of.

From then on, the "Storm" comes again and again, bringing new trends, new rules, and new opportunities. But it passes just as many times, taking with it entire eras, memories, and possibilities. Some eras go faster than others—fast enough that busy people never have time to look beyond what's right in front of them.

In this world, the era of the Scouts comes and goes. The children in gray-green uniforms shine at the top of their moment, then the tide of the times washes them away.

But sincerity is a fire that cannot be put out.

Boys and girls, children and teenagers—regardless of gender or age, these gray-green figures ignite the air around them with honesty, courage, and dedication.

And this time, she will burn in the silver kingdom of science, brighter than anyone.
