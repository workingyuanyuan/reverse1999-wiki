## Incantations

### Blaze Through the Lens

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 200% Mental DMG. When attacking, if the target has less than 20 stacks of \[Halo\] or there is only 1 target present, inflicts 4 stacks of \[Halo\] on the target; otherwise, inflicts \[Halo\] on every other enemy equal to 50% of the target's stacks of \[Halo\].

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 300% Mental DMG. When attacking, if the target has less than 20 stacks of \[Halo\] or there is only 1 target present, inflicts 6 stacks of \[Halo\] on the target; otherwise, inflicts \[Halo\] on another enemy equal to 50% of the target's stacks of \[Halo\].

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 500% Mental DMG. When attacking, if the target has less than 20 stacks of \[Halo\] or there is only 1 target present, inflicts 10 stacks of \[Halo\] on the target; otherwise, inflicts \[Halo\] on another enemy equal to 50% of the target's stacks of \[Halo\].

### Nothing Unexpected

**Rank 1**: Self buff. Gains 3 stacks of \[Vision\].

**Rank 2**: Self buff. Gains 5 stacks of \[Vision\].

**Rank 3**: Self buff. Gains 8 stacks of \[Vision\].

## Ultimate: A Shimmering Night

Mass attack. Deals 600% Mental DMG to all enemies. Before attacking, redistributes all enemies' \[Halo\] stacks based on their Current HP (up to \[Halo\]'s stack limit).

## Inheritance

**Insight 0 · Precognition**: Accumulates \[Lingering Glow\] (mutually exclusive with \[Bloodtithe\]) whenever an enemy is inflicted with \[Burn\] or \[Halo\].

When entering battle, if Vessel of Radiance is present, Beryl selects up to 2 Emanation crystals and up to 2 of each type.

For every 150 points of \[Lingering Glow\] accumulated, after any allied action, Beryl casts \[Destined Doom\] 1 time.

**Insight 1 · Prism Mirror**: When entering battle, Beryl gains \[Mirrored from Above\].

Starting from round 2, at the start of the round, Beryl generates 1 Precast Incantation \[Clairvoyant Prism\].

**Insight 2 · Prism Mirror II**: Critical Rate +10% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Prism Mirror III**: When entering battle, Beryl gains Moxie +1 and accumulates 20 points of \[Lingering Glow\].

At the end of the round, if there are at least 60 points of \[Lingering Glow\], Beryl gains Moxie +1.

## Statuses

- **Halo**: Healing Taken -25% (unstackable). At the end of the round, takes (the inflicter's ATK x4%) Genesis DMG (stackable up to 30 times, multiple stacks of Halo are regarded as 1 \[Neg Status\], removes 50% of the stacks when triggered).
- **Vision**: The accumulation count of \[Lingering Glow\] required to cast \[Destined Doom\] is reduced by 50 (unstackable). -1 stack upon casting \[Destined Doom\]. Undispellable.
- **Lingering Glow**: Accumulates \[Lingering Glow\] whenever an enemy is inflicted with \[Burn\] or \[Halo\]. The fewer enemies there are, the more \[Lingering Glow\] is accumulated.
- **Bloodtithe**: Whenever allies lose 3000 HP combined, accumulate 1 point of \[Bloodtithe\]; HP lost from being attacked accumulates \[Bloodtithe\] at only 30% efficiency.
- **Burn**: Healing Taken -15% (unstackable). At the end of the round, takes (the holder's ATK x4%) Genesis DMG (stackable up to 30 times, multiple stacks of Burn is regarded as 1 \[Neg Status\], removes 50% of the stacks when triggered).
- **Destined Doom**: Mass attack. Deals 500% Mental DMG to all enemies; deals an additional 500% Mental DMG to the enemy with the highest \[Halo\] stacks. For every stack of \[Vision\] held, this follow-up attack gains Incantation Might +1% (counts up to 20 stacks). Enhancements from each Emanation crystal:

  \[Emanation of Blue\]: +33% chance to cast \[Destined Doom · Emanation of Blue\] 1 additional time.

  \[Emanation of Purple\]: Additionally inflicts 2 stacks of \[Halo\]; for every point of \[Lingering Glow\], deals an additional 0.4% Mental DMG (+0.4% Mental DMG to the enemy with the highest \[Halo\] stacks).

  \[Emanation of Green\]: Rank +1 to this attack; at rank 1/2/3, deals an additional 80%/120%/150% Mental DMG (additionally +80%/120%/150% Mental DMG to the enemy with the highest \[Halo\] stacks).
- **Mirrored from Above**: \[Force Field\] Enhancements from each Emanation crystal:

  \[Emanation of Blue\]: Extra action attacks inflict 1 stack of \[Halo\] on the target.

  \[Emanation of Purple\]: Actively cast attacks inflict 2 stacks of \[Halo\] on the target.

  \[Emanation of Green\]: Rank 2/3 incantations inflict 1/2 stacks of \[Halo\] on the target.
- **Clairvoyant Prism**: Randomly generates a rank 1/2/3 incantation at a 40%/50%/10% chance; for every selected Emanation crystal of 1 type, -10% chance to generate a rank 1 incantation and +10% chance to generate a rank 3 incantation.

  The type of generated incantation is based on the distribution of Emanation crystals.

  \[Emanation of Blue\]: Increases \[Destined Doom\]'s accumulation count by 30/45/75.

  \[Emanation of Purple\]: Grants 2/3/5 stacks of \[Vision\] to Beryl.

  \[Emanation of Green\]: Right before the AP Area resolves, grants rank +1 to 1/2/3 incantations behind this incantation.

  The generated incantation is a \[Sovereign Incantation\].
- **Destined Doom · Emanation of Blue**: Mass attack. Deals 100% Mental DMG to all enemies; deals an additional 100% Mental DMG to the enemy with the highest \[Halo\] stacks. Only subject to \[Emanation of Blue\]. For every \[Emanation of Blue\] selected, deals an additional 120% Mental DMG (additionally +120% Mental DMG to the enemy with the highest \[Halo\] stacks). For every stack of \[Vision\] held, this follow-up attack gains Incantation Might +1% (counts up to 20 stacks).
- **Force Field**: The holder is granting effects to the relevant characters. Disappears when defeated.
- **Sovereign Incantation**: Casting this incantation counts as the character actively taking an action.
