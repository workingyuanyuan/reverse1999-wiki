## Story

### West Coast Streets

The second millennium of the Gregorian calendar draws to a close amid prosperity and revelry. Mass media, electronic entertainment, and worldwide disco culture have finally made this vast world smaller. My dear, here we have the Internet, globalization, and the new gospel of the Information Age!

Under the frenzy of disco lights, skyscrapers rise across the West Coast, just as this era that will mark the end of history reaches its peak. The city's chasms tear the two societies on the same land asunder—an economic boom, the rich get richer, and the poor get poorer. Beyond the glossy world governed by laws and regulations lies an underground society created by the people themselves.

After all, this city never lacks immigrants and wanderers who are shut out by the mainstream.

"If you're homeless, go to Tang's Restaurant, J and his pals will find you a job."

How do I know? Duh! He personally saved me from that ship!

### Metal Partners

Pandora Wilson: You mentioned metal and your friends on Haight Street, but most people don't see how they are related.

J: That's true, but they both have a common trait: reliability.

Pandora Wilson: What I'm asking is if you personally see any connection between them?

J: You can think them as my partners, lady.

Pandora Wilson: Partners?

J: They have consistent temperaments, distinct personalities, and they'll always help you when you're in need.

Pandora Wilson: I see. So, metals and grains did play a crucial role in the development of our civilization ...

Pandora Wilson: In that sense, I think I understand what you mean ...

J: If you ever need a knife, you know where to find me. I've got plenty of good stuff for you to choose from—

Pandora Wilson: Thank you, but I don't think I'll be needing any of those sharp and dangerous tools.

### The Lone Wolf's Howl

> His past quest has ended, yet hope still beats deep within his chest.

This armor is designed to make a knight unstoppable on the field, forged by the Foundation's newest smith. When telling A Knight about the gear, J pulls out a thick stack of denied supply requests. "That last piece took 80 mithril bars! Hope our pals in 'Razor' Squad ain't too jealous—this set here is one-of-a-kind." Though someone notices J lacks even a hint of remorse.

Thanks to the infusion of powerful arcane metal, this armored shell provides enough protection for his high-risk solo missions. Though J always insists he prefers working with a team, when danger strikes, he consistently chooses to take the brunt of it himself, ensuring safer paths for his fellow teammates.

When A Knight asks about his reasons for making this armor, J simply smooths out the wrinkles on a blue ribbon, grips an old badge tightly, and suddenly poses an unexpected question: "Do you believe a new age is coming?"

#### "Fluffy-Headed Tough Guy"

A helmet crafted from arcane materials offering protection, style, and warmth.​ When questioned about the furry attachments on either side, the smith and interviewee share a recurring dream featuring a solitary wolf of unknown origins.

#### "My Raddest Bud"

A newly forged greatsword born from countless hammer strikes. It embodies the Foundation's latest techniques and innovations.​

After a moment of admiration, the smith's expression hardens as he reveals its true purpose.

"It was made to protect."

#### "Scarlet Tempering"

A weathered red cloak, pocked with scorch marks and frayed edges, flares behind him. The interviewee loves it for the thunderous roar it makes when it catches the wind.

"Is this fast enough for you? Watch this! The dial's about to explode!"

With a snap of fabric, the interviewee accelerates towards the horizon in a blur.
