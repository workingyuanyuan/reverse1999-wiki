Every incantation of 37 adds Genesis DMG based on her ATK on top of its Mental DMG. She gathers Eureka from her own incantations and from allies' extra actions, and at 5 Eureka casts [Supplementary Formula] on all enemies as a follow-up attack. Her Ultimate raises the team's ATK and the extra Genesis DMG of her next incantations.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Triangulation (single-target attack) | 140% Mental DMG + Genesis DMG of 40% of ATK | 210% + 60% | 350% + 100% |
| Curve Mapping (attack on 2 enemies) | 70% Mental DMG + Genesis DMG of 40% of ATK; 1 stack of [Sturdiness] | 105% + 60%; 1 stack | 175% + 100%; 2 stacks |

Curve Mapping puts Sturdiness on 37 herself.

## Ultimate: Kingdom of Geometry

Gives all allies 2 stacks of [Numeral Perception], gives 37 2 stacks of [Enlightenment] and Eureka +2.

| Status | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Supplementary Formula | Attack on all enemies: 120% Mental DMG plus Genesis DMG of 40% of 37's ATK. |
| Numeral Perception | ATK +10%; one stack is used per attack. |
| Enlightenment | The extra Genesis DMG of an incantation +100%; one stack is used each time. |
| Sturdiness | DMG Taken Reduction +25% when attacked; one stack is used each time. |

## Euphoria

37's Euphoria is out only on the mainland China service. It opens at Insight III level 30, and she has one Epiphany of four levels.

| Epiphany | Level | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 37 gains the Pursuit set [Reductio ad Absurdum]. On entering battle she gains the Pursuit Scope and fills 2 Star Pursuit Points; Supplementary Formula fills 1 Star Pursuit Point, at most once a round. |
| 1 | 2 | She gains Eureka after any ally's action, instead of after her own incantations and allies' extra actions. |
| 1 | 3 | Triangulation deals 100% of its base Mental and Genesis DMG again and gives her 1, 1 or 2 stacks of Enlightenment by rank; Curve Mapping deals 100% of its base again and gives all allies Sturdiness; Supplementary Formula deals 150% of its base again. |
| 1 | 4 | Numeral Perception no longer stacks and gives ATK equal to 20% of 37's ATK, at most 500; the Ultimate gives it for 2 rounds and gives 37 [Continuous Action I] (AP +1) for 2 rounds. Her Critical Rate bonus on entering battle goes to all allies and grows by 50% of its base; she also gains Moxie +3 and Eureka cap +5, and the Ultimates of allied Pursuit arcanists do not count toward the Incantation Draw Limit. |

Reductio ad Absurdum, her Pursuit incantation, deals Mental DMG to all enemies, then gives 37 a stack of [Dichotomy Paradox]: the next Pursuit incantation of any ally adds Steady Genesis DMG equal to 40% of the damage of that attack, using one stack.
