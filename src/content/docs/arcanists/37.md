## Incantations

### Triangulation

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 140% Mental DMG plus (ATK x40%) Genesis DMG.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 210% Mental DMG plus (ATK x60%) Genesis DMG.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 350% Mental DMG plus (ATK x100%) Genesis DMG.

### Curve Mapping

**Rank 1**: Mass attack. Deals 70% Mental DMG plus (ATK x40%) Genesis DMG to 2 enemies. Gains 1 additional stack of \[Sturdiness\].

**Rank 2**: Mass attack. Deals 105% Mental DMG plus (ATK x60%) Genesis DMG to 2 enemies. Gains 1 additional stack of \[Sturdiness\].

**Rank 3**: Mass attack. Deals 175% Mental DMG plus (ATK x100%) Genesis DMG to 2 enemies. Gains 2 additional stacks of \[Sturdiness\].

## Ultimate: Kingdom of Geometry

Mass buff. Grants 2 stacks of \[Numeral Perception\] to all allies, gains 2 stacks of \[Enlightenment\], and gains Eureka +2.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · Calculation of Genesis**: After using an Incantation, gains Eureka +1.

After another ally takes an extra action, gains Eureka +1.

When at 5 Eureka, consumes 5 Eureka to cast \[Supplementary Formula\]. This attack is regarded as 1 follow-up attack and does not grant any Eureka.

**Insight 2 · Calculation of Genesis II**: Critical DMG +15% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Calculation of Genesis III**: When entering battle, Critical Rate +20%. All Genesis DMG applied through Incantations can critically hit.

## Portrait

**Portrait 1**: **Calculation of Genesis**: When entering battle, Critical Rate boost increased to 40%.

**Portrait 2**: **Calculation of Genesis**: \[Supplementary Formula\]'s Mental DMG is increased to 180%; additional Genesis DMG is increased to (own ATK x60%).

**Portrait 3**: **Kingdom of Geometry**: \[Enlightenment\] increased to 3 stacks; Eureka gained increased to 3.

**Portrait 4**: **Calculation of Genesis**: \[Supplementary Formula\]'s Mental DMG is increased to 240%; additional Genesis DMG is increased to (own ATK x80%).

**Portrait 5**: **Kingdom of Geometry**: \[Numeral Perception\] increased to 3 stacks; Eureka gained increased to 4.

## Statuses

- **Sturdiness**: When attacked, DMG Taken Reduction +25% (-1 stack after trigger).
- **Numeral Perception**: ATK +10% (-1 stack after attacking).
- **Enlightenment**: The Incantation's additional Genesis DMG Dealt +100% (-1 stack after trigger).
- **Supplementary Formula**: Mass attack. Deals 120% Mental DMG plus (ATK x40%) Genesis DMG to all enemies.
