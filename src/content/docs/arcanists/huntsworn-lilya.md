Huntsworn Lilya builds speed. Her channel Cobra Drive puts her in [Thunderous Rev], which stacks [Supercharger] each round until, at 10 stacks, it turns into [Hyper Velocity] for 2 rounds; every state raises her Incantation Might and the extra damage of her dive attack. Repeat casts of Cobra Drive load [Ammo Loading], which her Ultimate spends for a large hit, and she belongs to the Pursuit group of arcanists, whose shared Pursuit Points open a phase in which their Pursuit incantations can be cast. She is a separate arcanist from [Lilya](/arcanists/lilya/).

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum Dive (single-target attack) | 200% Reality DMG; in Thunderous Rev +400% and DMG Dealt +10%; in Hyper Velocity +800% and +20% | 400%; +800%, +20%; +1600%, +40% | 600%; +1200%, +30%; +2400%, +60% |
| Cobra Drive (channel) | enters Thunderous Rev; in it, 1 Ammo Loading; in Hyper Velocity, 2 | 2; 4 | 3; 6 |

- **Maximum Dive** can be cast once per round.
- **Cobra Drive** enters Thunderous Rev; cast again while she is already in Thunderous Rev or in Hyper Velocity, it gives Ammo Loading instead.

## Ultimate: Try This Move!

Deals 900% Reality DMG to all enemies and 900% more to the main target. If Huntsworn Lilya spends 6 stacks of Ammo Loading, the attack deals 1800% more Reality DMG with Ultimate Might +100%, Critical DMG +100% and Penetration Rate +20%. Cast in Hyper Velocity, it fills 4 [Star Pursuit Points], at most once per Hyper Velocity.

## Pursuit: Shoot Down

Huntsworn Lilya enters battle with the [Pursuit Scope]. Before an incantation, a team holding at least 6 Pursuit Points (15 at most) can enter [Full Pursuit], taking up to 10 points into it, and spend them on Pursuit incantations of the same Afflatus. A team cannot hold the Pursuit Scope together with Lingering Glow or Bloodtithe.

- **Shoot Down**, her Pursuit incantation: 900% Reality DMG to one enemy, with Critical DMG +60%.
- **Pursuit ability 1**: inside Full Pursuit, every third action of hers becomes an attack on all enemies for 2000% Reality DMG, with Critical DMG +80%.
- **Pursuit ability 2**: before Full Pursuit ends, she can make one extra finishing attack: 3000% Reality DMG to all enemies, Critical DMG +100%, and 150% more Reality DMG for each action the team took in the phase. Full Pursuit ends after it.
