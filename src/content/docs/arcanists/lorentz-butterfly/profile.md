## Item

### Additive Manufacturing Device

A Demonic Scale-based additive manufacturing machine, powered by tens of thousands of arcane skills operating simultaneously.

Every arcane skill within the printer is interconnected. Even the slightest deviation in initial conditions can lead to drastically different results.

According to the interviewee, the device can provide whatever help is needed—whether it's a sniper rifle or a toaster.

### Liquid Metal-Coated "Glove"

A pair of gloves with a metallic sheen, shimmering like the scales on a butterfly's wings.

Though the interviewee described them as the arms of an Awakened, we know they are simply the latest product of the LSCC—refined, of course, by Institutum Lorentz.

### "ID"

She can be Ms. Jones, Special Investigator of the St. Pavlov Foundation, or Marguerite, researcher of the Laplace Scientific Computing Center. Any identity will do, as long as it opens the right doors.

## Story

### The Beginning of Chaos

Eras bounce like marbles—backward, forward, backward again. Mostly backward, right?

She learned what 1999's Cambridge had to teach her. Her gift allows her to pick up on patterns intuitively, but her nature ensures she will always be a skeptic. And here lies the paradox that the rise and fall of eras brings—one that traps everyone who still remembers, who still thinks: Are we moving forward or backward? What can we hold on to?

Yes, the storms of an era are always more interesting than still water. Rather than clinging to a single foothold, she would rather leap with the times, joining in the dangerous game of takeoffs and landings.

So where is the butterfly that first fluttered its wings?

If you want to hide a leaf, the best place is a forest. If you want to hide a brilliant mind, the best place is Laplace. Yes, it is the temple of science—but the science it commands cannot answer the great questions of its era. That alone can trigger a collapse of faith. Fortunately, she was never attached to any of it.

When neat theories crumble with the era, a butterfly stirs its wings among the rubble, flying into endless chaos.

### Finale in Silver

> When the death knell tolls at the crescendo, a dying wail is but a single discordant note.

The cellist slipped into the orchestra quietly, arriving a little later than the others, though not so late as to draw notice. She smiled at the musicians, murmuring an explanation about finishing her last bit of tuning. After all, this was their first performance in this concert hall, and her cello was still adjusting to the humidity and to the layered resonance beneath the bare concrete dome overhead.

The lights dimmed, and the orchestra took the stage. The hem of her dress rustled as she walked, the sound quickly swallowed by welcoming applause. She looked up and gave a small nod toward the second box.

Layer upon layer, the concrete dome soared overhead. The cello came in, anchoring the orchestra's harmony, its sound echoing evenly off the bare walls—a tune that a certain arcane plant seemed to "love." Its powder would soon trigger a reaction with a silver spoon made to detect poison. That spoon belonged to a target unusually careful with his own life. Fifteen days ago, his trusted apothecary had brought him a new one.

With the music in full swing, no one caught the last breath from that box—the final sound that throat would ever make.

A satisfying performance and a flawless infiltration ended just like that.

#### "Antenna"

A weighty metal ornament, set with gemstones, its tip trembling faintly like an insect's antenna. The interviewer learned that the interviewee helped design this hairpiece herself.

"Sensing its surroundings, relaying information, even tracking a target? No—it's simply a decorative piece inspired by a tuning peg."

#### Deadly Cello

A cello and bow of distinctive design. Like any musician who cherishes their instrument, the interviewee holds them dear.

The interviewer accepted the interviewee's offer of an impromptu performance—the deep, resonant tone was enchanting. "Just a bit of technique," the interviewee said with a smile, setting the bow aside. "It's a beautiful instrument. Would you like to try it?"

#### "A String of Notes"

A string of notes, a fragment of melody. Made from the same metal as her hairpiece, it's the perfect finishing touch on the interviewee's gown.

Smooth, cold, and as light as the flutter of a butterfly's wings. When her target's life fades along with the final note, that is all they will ever feel.
