## Cover

An arcanist's work exhibited in the 20th century for 16 years. Completed in autumn, on September 23rd. First exhibited in Hawaii, USA.

Dimensions: 62 13/64 x 40 7/32 x 24 13/32 in.

Medium: Common Hibiscus

Fragrance note: Sea-breeze - Sea Water, Salt, Seaweed, Lemon, Leaf

Afflatus: Prey of the Waves \[Beast\] Solo Combat

## Item

### Longbow

A longbow which perfectly fits the word "plain." Its structure and design are simple, but its power is unexpectedly high in her hands. Arcanists are supposed to be extraordinary, and she has done a good job.

### Monocular

A monocular distributed by Zeno Arms Academy. It's portable, easy to maintain and suitable for all kinds of complicated battlefield environments, making it a reliable choice. Nevertheless, Leilani is not a fan of the monocular, so it is more like a decoration than a piece of equipment.

### Hibiscus

A common hibiscus which seems to carry the impression of the sea breeze and sunshine from a distant island. It is one of the few decorations she is wearing, so you can see how much she likes this flower.

## Story

### Fast Like Turtle

Zeno Arms Academy, the dream school of every soldier.

This is a place where modern technology and arcane skill—two systems believed to be mutually exclusive—work closely together towards one common goal.

Arcanists and soldiers from all over the world have gathered here to study, forming a force with considerable power.

In fact, Leilani doesn't belong here. She can never be a qualified soldier—not even a qualified student.

Her carelessness and innocence are not qualities that a soldier should possess.

But so what?

She would rather be a enthusiast of life than a soldier.

## Skins

### Default Skin

> A hot little sun and a sharp arrow.

### The Adventure on the Mountains

> The wrong path up the mountain, the right time for admiring the sunset.

#### "EasyGo" Alpenstock

A pair of lightweight but sturdy four-part trekking poles.

A backup pair amongst the interviewee's items for climbing mountains. The interviewee waxes lyrical about its uses and insists I admire her hiking shoes, surfboard, bow, and arrows... I had to request that we table that politely.

#### "Clawbing" 10mm Climbing Rope

The bestselling climbing rope woven with hopping Porcupines' mane, reasonably priced, sturdy and durable. Every mountain climber knows that a climbing rope is the only thing between them and death. When checking climbing ropes, the interviewee displayed a serious side of her rarely seen.

#### Bandhani Kerchief

A tie-dye cloth, purchased by the interviewee at the foot of the mountain. Before climbing the mountain, she spent a whole afternoon at the dyer's, showing off her skills. Finally she chose to buy a finished product, but this did not dampen her spirits.
