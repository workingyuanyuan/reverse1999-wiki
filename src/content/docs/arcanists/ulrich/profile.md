## Item

### Sound Strap

The sound strap​​, connected to an all-in-one toolbox, primarily assists in record playing by amplifying sound wave radiation to achieve optimal coverage. After a week of testing this strap, a warning note is left on the interviewee's desk.

### All-in-One Portable Toolbox

A multi-functional toolbox designed for "Storm" sampling, which also serves as a record player. For "Artificial Storm" experiments and Antarctic research, it's a key piece of equipment. But for the interviewee, it tends to see most use as a weight for fitness training.

### Just a Tank ... Or Is It?

A sealed spherical silica container. Equipped with an audio system and yellow oil-based solution, it satisfies the awakened's desire for communication while ensuring his mobility within the "tank."

During interviews, we noted that a minor accident seemed to have occurred during the device's production, though the interviewee cautiously avoided this topic.

Then one day—whether it was the discovery of electromagnetic induction, or the moment the first computer gave off its low hum, or when Magnetohydrodynamics became its own discipline—the first rhythm greeted the world for the very first time from within a physics lab.

### Considerate Confirmation

Pandora Wilson: As an Awakened, how did you come to discover your abilities in codebreaking? Not to sound accusatory—I only mean to ask whether you had formally studied the subject before joining Laplace.

Ulrich: I didn't study it in any official manner, but my interest stemmed from my arcane skills. Though they're not directly related to codebreaking, they were what sparked my interest in the pursuit.

Pandora Wilson: Would you mind sharing just how it is your skills assist you?

Ulrich: Of course. The simple explanation is that I can harness the power of nearby electronic devices to help me perform calculations.

Pandora Wilson: So, any device can act like a computer for you?

Ulrich: Close, but not quite. I can't alter their circuitry—only use them as "auxiliary brains" to assist me in processing. That said, being an Awakened doesn't mean I can directly understand the voices of machines.

Ulrich: I have to spend time deciphering their ultrasonic waves, spin-dry frequencies, blink counts ... Eventually, I realized that the steps I take to compute things have a lot in common with human cryptography.

Pandora Wilson: And now you're the head of the cryptography team. Working under an Awakened must be quite a unique experience.

Ulrich: I can't speak for my team, but I find **cracking their expressions is often harder than cracking any code.** Though I think I can say with confidence that they're not thrilled about how often I burn through our electronics.

Ulrich: But I always ensure my target isn't another Awakened. After all, we should all stand up for Awakened's rights!

Pandora Wilson: That's very noble of you.

### A Glimpse of the Cosmos

> The wise man declares, "A circle is perfection." The world laughs, "So is an ellipse."

Throughout cosmic evolution, how many quests for truth have unfolded?

The geocentric model believed Earth stood motionless at the universe's heart, with all celestial bodies moving according to the theory of epicycles and deferents, each following its own path.

When Copernicus introduced his heliocentric model in 1543, its elegant simplicity challenged established powers who rejected it despite later historical vindication.

Generations of thinkers have knocked on the world's door with questions. They pursued answers through struggle, only to find each solution birthing new mysteries. Science and civilization have interlocked, evolving together to this day.

Inside the glass tank, the ferrofluid swirls in configurations that mimic possible cosmic structures.

Every question ultimately requires an answer, even if obtaining it means enduring endless self-torment—someone must shoulder this burden.

#### Astral Sanctuary

A device used to record and simulate planetary motion, it also serves as a living space for the Awakened inside.

The interviewee added: "Just to be clear, this isn't my job. Don't assume I'm working 24/7 inside a celestial globe."

#### "Portent of Partnership"

A compact astrological aid device, offering stellar positioning and projection—designed to aid teamwork.

The interviewee admits his decoding project in astrology requires further collaboration. "Luckily, I have a few colleagues with the right background. I had to do something to attract partners. This instrument was one of my preparations."

#### "Watcher of Cycles"

A robe more symbolic than it is functional. Its style departs significantly from common Laplace uniforms, which the interviewee says he's pleased with. He claims it "resonates with the cosmos but defies convention." Clearly, he sees his clothing as an expression of belief: "Witnessing the cycle isn't the point. Progress never comes from staying stuck in it."
