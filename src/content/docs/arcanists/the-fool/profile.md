## Item

### Flower

A wildflower picked by the Fool at the roadside. It is not watered manually, maintained in a greenhouse or placed in an expensive vase, yet it still possesses a unique kind of beauty. The same goes for plays: even if they cannot be performed in the most luxurious theaters, their quality will remain the same.

### Ruff

During the Fool's time, the ruff was a decorative item worn by the nobles. The thick and hard ruff was incredibly inconvenient, yet was seen as a symbol of wealth and status. Hence, from the Fool's point of view, wearing a ruff was enough to poke fun at the "nobles" and the "king."

### Mask

Unlike the other actors in the troupe, the Fool never removes his mask. All of his emotions are reflected on this mask and even his colleagues do not know what he really looks like.

## Story

### Lord Protector

The Lord Protector was averse to all pleasures. This included music, banquets, festivals ... plays were no exception.

These were all sins and not permitted. Everybody should live like puritans.

The pipe organ was dismantled, along with other decorations. Singers were beheaded, writers' pens were broken, liquor was poured down the drain and delicacies were thrown into the bins.

"It's a sin and should not be allowed."

That's what they said.

"It is indeed a sin and should not be allowed."

The people followed the tyrant and repeated it woodenly.

To be or not to be? That is a question.

To die with a smile or to live in tears? That is another question.

### The Shadow Play Master

> A dance of silhouettes, where shadows intertwine in perfect duet.

During every festival season, a shadow-puppetry troupe would visit the town. Their craft was extraordinary—every puppet danced as if alive. Crowds swarmed the streets, blocking the roads; every show met with thundering applause and laughter that filled the air.

Over the years, villagers grew curious about the troupe master's origins. Despite many inquiries, no answers surfaced. One day, a mischievous child quietly lifted the master's sleeve and found his body was thin as a shadow puppet.

Suddenly, a great wind swept across the land. Every shadow puppet flew off its frame and rose into the air, clashing and spinning with metallic chimes. As the crowd watched in awe, a booming laugh echoed from above—the troupe master cast a long rope skyward. It soared into the clouds. He climbed it with ease, and both he and the rope vanished into thin air.

#### Lantern Shadow Puppet

A shadow puppet shaped like a lantern, crafted entirely by the interviewee—from material selection and coloring to assembly and calibration. Its intricate surface and harmonious colors reveal her meticulousness, patience, and passion for drama.

#### A "Small" Purse

No one knows what the interviewee's purse contains.

"The world is filled with countless stages, each performing countless dramas. Surely, you can see this purse couldn't possibly hold all the stories I've seen."

#### "Inside and Out of the Play"

"What did you see, what did you hear? Here lies a play crafted just for you, and as long as you're here, the curtain will never fall."

The interviewee always wears a mask, regardless of the genre of the play, yet he understands better than anyone how the drama will end.
