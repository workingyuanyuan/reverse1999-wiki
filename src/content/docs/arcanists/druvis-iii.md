## Incantations

### Wind into the Woods

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 200% Mental DMG.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 200% Mental DMG. Inflicts \[Petrify\] for 1 round on the target hit.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 300% Mental DMG. Inflicts \[Petrify\] for 2 rounds on the target hit.

### Early Dawn

**Rank 1**: Mass attack. Deals 120% Mental DMG to 2 enemies. This attack enjoys Penetration Rate +30%.

**Rank 2**: Mass attack. Deals 180% Mental DMG to 2 enemies. This attack enjoys Penetration Rate +30%.

**Rank 3**: Mass attack. Deals 300% Mental DMG to 2 enemies. This attack enjoys Penetration Rate +30%.

## Ultimate: Silence in the Woods

Mass attack. Deals 400% Mental DMG to all enemies. On hitting the main target, inflicts \[Petrify\] on the main target for 1 round.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · Among the Woods**: When the caster is in \[Stats Up\] or \[Pos Status\] statuses, DMG Dealt +20%.

**Insight 2 · Among the Woods Ⅱ**: DMG Dealt +8% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Among the Woods Ⅲ**: When entering battle, all \[Plant\] allies enter \[Circle of Life\] status (triggers 1 time).

## Portrait

**Portrait 1**: **Wind into the Woods**'s effect changes to: at 2/3 stars, deals 300/400% Mental DMG.

**Portrait 2**: **Silence in the Woods**: Mental DMG increased to 450%.

**Portrait 3**: **Early Dawn**'s effect changes to: at 1/2/3 stars, deals 135/200/325% Mental DMG.

**Portrait 4**: **Silence in the Woods**: Mental DMG increased to 500%.

**Portrait 5**: **Early Dawn**'s effect changes to: Penetration Rate +40%.

## Euphoria

**Epiphany 1, level 1**: Enhances \[Circle of Life\] with extra effects: gain DMG Bonus +15%, Penetration Rate +15%, and the effect becomes undispellable.

**Epiphany 1, level 2**: Enhances \[Circle of Life\] with an extra effect: for every 200 ATK Druvis III has, gain an additional DMG Bonus +1%.

**Epiphany 1, level 3**: Enhances \[Circle of Life\] with extra effects: attacking enemies in the \[Petrify\] status does not remove the \[Petrify\] status, and gain an additional Penetration Rate +25% against targets in the \[Petrify\] status.

**Epiphany 1, level 4**: Enhances \[Circle of Life\] with an extra effect: Gain ATK equal to 7.5% of Druvis III's Mental DEF.

**Epiphany 2, level 1**: \[Circle of Life\] new effect: When attacking, inflicts 1/2/3 stacks of \[Firebud\] (undispellable) on the target hit based on incantation rank.

**Epiphany 2, level 2**: \[Firebud\] new effect: Critical Resist Rate -15%. Critical DEF -30%. For every additional stack, gets an additional Critical Resist Rate -1.5% and Critical DEF -3%.

**Epiphany 2, level 3**: When entering battle, if there are at least 3 \[Plant\] allies on the team, enhances \[Circle of Life\]:

The \[Circle of Life\] on the adjacent ally in front grants an additional Penetration Rate +40%.

The \[Circle of Life\] on the adjacent ally behind grants an additional DMG Taken Reduction +30%.

**Epiphany 2, level 4**: When entering battle, if there are at least 3 \[Plant\] allies on the team, enhances \[Circle of Life\]:

When \[Firebud\] explodes, for every stack, all allies gain DMG Bonus +6% for 2 rounds.

## Statuses

- **Petrify**: Cannot act. Dispels the effect after taking Reality DMG attack.
- **Circle of Life**: DMG Heal +10%, Leech Rate +10%, no duration limit.
- **Firebud**: Explodes at the end of the round. Stackable up to 9 times. Each stack deals (average ATK of all allies x400%) Genesis DMG to the target before removing all stacks.
