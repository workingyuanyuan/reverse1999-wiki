Druvis III is one of the launch 6-star arcanists. Her damage rises while she holds a buff, her first incantation and her Ultimate petrify, and her Insight III passive gives every Plant ally [Circle of Life], which her Euphoria builds on.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wind into the Woods (single-target attack) | 200% Mental DMG | 200%; Petrify 1 round | 300%; Petrify 2 rounds |
| Early Dawn (attack on 2 enemies) | 120% Mental DMG | 180% | 300% |

Early Dawn always has Penetration Rate +30%.

## Ultimate: Silence in the Woods

Deals 400% Mental DMG to all enemies and petrifies the main target for 1 round.

[Petrify]: the target cannot act; the effect ends when it takes a Reality DMG attack.

## Euphoria

Euphoria opens at Insight III level 30. Druvis III has two Epiphanies of four levels each; both change Circle of Life.

| Epiphany | Level | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | Circle of Life also gives DMG Bonus +15% and Penetration Rate +15%, and cannot be dispelled. |
| 1 | 2 | Circle of Life also gives DMG Bonus +1% for every 200 ATK of Druvis III. |
| 1 | 3 | Attacks on a petrified enemy no longer end Petrify and gain Penetration Rate +25%. |
| 1 | 4 | Druvis III gains ATK equal to 7.5% of her Mental DEF. |
| 2 | 1 | An ally with Circle of Life puts 1, 2 or 3 stacks of [Firebud] on the target it hits, by incantation rank; Firebud cannot be dispelled. |
| 2 | 2 | Firebud lowers Critical Resist Rate by 15% and Critical DEF by 30%, and by 1.5% and 3% more for each further stack. |
| 2 | 3 | With 3 or more Plant allies on entering battle, Circle of Life gives the ally in front of its holder Penetration Rate +40% and the ally behind DMG Taken Reduction +30%. |
| 2 | 4 | With 3 or more Plant allies on entering battle, each stack of Firebud that explodes gives all allies DMG Bonus +6% for 2 rounds. |
