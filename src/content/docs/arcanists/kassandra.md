## Incantations

### Fury of Ares

**Rank 1**: Mass attack. Deals 120% Reality DMG to 2 enemies and gains Adrenaline +1.

**Rank 2**: Mass attack. Deals 180% Reality DMG to 2 enemies and gains Adrenaline +2.

**Rank 3**: Mass attack. Deals 300% Reality DMG to 2 enemies and gains Adrenaline +4.

### Advent of Artemis

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 150% Reality DMG. Inflicts 1 stack of \[Lethal Injury\] on the target hit.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 225% Reality DMG. Inflicts 2 stack of \[Lethal Injury\] on the target hit.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 375% Reality DMG. Inflicts 4 stacks of \[Lethal Injury\] on the target hit.

## Ultimate: Rush Assassination

1-target attack. Deals 750% Reality DMG. This attack counts as an \[Assassination\].

## Inheritance

**Insight 0 · Precognition**: Replace Moxie with Adrenaline. At the start of the round, during one Adrenaline cycle (0-10): when Adrenaline reaches 2 for the first time, generate 1 Precast Incantation \[Sparta Kick\]; when 6, generate 1 Precast Incantation \[Rain of Destruction\]; when 10, generate 1 \[Rush Assassination\] and then reset Adrenaline to 0.

**Insight 1 · Falconer's Instinct**: When entering battle, gain Adrenaline +1. At the start of the round, gain Adrenaline +1.

**Insight 2 · Eagle-Bearer's Instinct II**: DMG Dealt +8% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Eagle-Bearer's Instinct III**: When any ally triggers an \[Assassination\], gains 1 stack of \[Guidance of Three Gods\]; if there are other \[Assassination\] characters on the team, gains 1 extra stack of \[Guidance of Three Gods\].

## Statuses

- **Lethal Injury**: When being attacked, trigger \[Assassination\] and remove 1 stack. When taking an \[Assassination\] attack, for every 100 points of Critical Technique the attacker has more than the target, this attack gains Final DMG Dealt +1%; this effect does not remove stacks.
- **Assassination**: For every 100 points of Critical Technique the attacker has more than the target, the attack gains an additional Final DMG Dealt +5%.
- **Sparta Kick**: 1-target attack. Deals 200% Reality DMG. Inflicts \[Daze\] on the target hit for 1 round. If the target is already afflicted with \[Daze\], deals an additional 100% Reality DMG.

  This incantation is a \[Sovereign Incantation\].
- **Rain of Destruction**: Mass attack. Deals 500% Reality DMG to all enemies.

  This incantation is a \[Sovereign Incantation\].
- **Rush Assassination**: 1-target attack. Deals 750% Reality DMG. This attack counts as an \[Assassination\].
- **Guidance of Three Gods**: Provides Stats Up in \[Way of Ares\], \[Way of Artemis\], and \[Way of Nyx\], stackable up to 5 times. Undispellable.
- **Way of Artemis**: \[Force Field\] effect: All allies gain Critical DMG +(27% + number of \[Guidance of Three Gods\] stacks x5.4%). \[Assassination\] allies gain an additional Critical DMG +(number of \[Guidance of Three Gods\] stacks x5.4%). Grant Critical Rate +15% to all allies and an additional Critical Rate +10% to \[Assassination\] allies.
- **Way of Ares**: \[Force Field\] effect: All allies gain DMG Bonus +(14% + number of \[Guidance of Three Gods\] stacks x2.8%). \[Assassination\] allies gain an additional DMG Bonus +(number of \[Guidance of Three Gods\] stacks x2.8%). Grant Incantation Might +18% to all allies and an additional Incantation Might +12% to \[Assassination\] allies.
- **Way of Nyx**: \[Force Field\] effect: All allies gain ATK +(7% + number of \[Guidance of Three Gods\] stacks x1.4%). \[Assassination\] allies gain an additional ATK +(number of \[Guidance of Three Gods\] stacks x1.4%). Grant Penetration Rate +24% to all allies and an additional Penetration Rate +16% to \[Assassination\] allies.
- **Rush Assassination - Combo**: 1-target attack. Deal 750% Reality DMG. This attack counts as an \[Assassination\] and an Ultimate.
- **Sixth Sense**: When \[Fury of Ares\], \[Advent of Artemis\], or \[Rain of Destruction\] hits the targets, inflict 1 additional stack of \[Lethal Injury\] on them.

  Current \[Force Field\] New Effect Added: When an allied \[Assassination\] is triggered, deal an additional 50% DMG of the attacker's type.
- **Weapons Master**: \[Sparta Kick\] inflicts \[Kick Gash\] on the target hit for 3 rounds.

  \[Rain of Destruction\] inflicts \[Arrow Gash\] on the targets hit for 3 rounds.

  \[Rush Assassination\] inflicts \[Assassination Gash\] on the target hit for 3 rounds.

  \[Rush Assassination - Combo\] inflicts \[Assassination Gash\] on the target hit for 3 rounds.
- **Kick Gash**: When being attacked, take an additional (the user's ATK x10%) Genesis DMG.
- **Arrow Gash**: When being attacked, take an additional (the user's ATK x10%) Genesis DMG.
- **Assassination Gash**: When being attacked, take an additional (the user's ATK x10%) Genesis DMG.
- **Shadow Assassin**: When Kassandra performs an \[Assassination\], for every 100 points of Critical Technique the attacker has more than the target, the attack gains an additional Final DMG Dealt +1%.
- **Daze**: Cannot act.
- **Sovereign Incantation**: Casting this incantation counts as the character actively taking an action.
- **Force Field**: The holder is granting effects to the relevant characters. Disappears when defeated.
- **Fury of Ares**: Mass attack. Deals Reality DMG to 2 enemies; gains Adrenaline for self.
- **Advent of Artemis**: 1-target attack. Deals Reality DMG; inflicts \[Lethal Injury\] on the target hit.
