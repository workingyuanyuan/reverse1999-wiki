## Incantations

### A Must-See!

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 200% Mental DMG to the target; inflicts 2 random effects from \[Collection of Neg Status\] on the target hit.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 230% Mental DMG to the target; inflicts 2 random effects from \[Collection of Neg Status\] on the target hit.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 400% Mental DMG to the target; inflicts 2 random effects from \[Collection of Neg Status\] on the target hit.

### A Mind-Blower!

**Rank 1**: 1-target counter. Grants \[Memory Replay\] to the target and to 1 unit behind the target for 2 rounds.

**Rank 2**: 1-target counter. Grants \[Memory Replay\] to the target and to 2 units behind the target for 2 rounds.

**Rank 3**: 1-target counter. Grants \[Memory Replay\] to the target and to 3 units behind the target for 2 rounds.

## Ultimate: Cinema Binge

Mass attack. Deals 400% Mental DMG to all enemies. After attacking, inflicts 2 random effects from \[Collection of Neg Status\] on the targets.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · "Keeper of Memories"**: Whenever \[Memory Replay\] is triggered on an ally, Loggerhead gains 1 stack of \["Clear Headed"\] for 2 rounds. At the end of the round, for every stack of \["Clear Headed"\] on self, recovers (the ally's Max HP x1.2%) HP for all allies carrying \[Memory Replay\].

**Insight 2 · "Keeper of Memories" II**: DMG Heal +10% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · "Keeper of Memories" III**: Whenever an ally actively casts an incantation, for every 1 \[Neg Status\] inflicted on an enemy through that action, Loggerhead gains 1 stack of \["Clear Headed"\] for 2 rounds.

## Portrait

**Portrait 1**: **Cinema Binge**: Mental DMG increased to 450%.

**Portrait 2**: **A Must-See!**: At incantation rank 1/2/3, deals 220%/260%/450% Mental DMG.

**Portrait 3**: **"Keeper of Memories"**: The HP recovered for allies in the \[Memory Replay\] status is increased to 1.7% of their Max HP.

**Portrait 4**: **A Must-See!**: At incantation rank 1/2/3, the number of \[Collection of Neg Status\] effects inflicted on the target hit is increased to 3/3/3.

**Portrait 5**: **A Mind-Blower!**: The DMG Taken Reduction granted by \[Memory Replay\] is increased to 25%.

## Statuses

- **Collection of Neg Status**: There are 3 effects: 1 stack of \[Rigidity\], 1 instance of \[Poison\], and 5 stacks of \[Burn\]. Prioritizes effects not yet applied.
- **Memory Replay**: DMG Taken Reduction +15%; after being attacked, inflicts 1 effect from \[Collection of Neg Status\] on the attacker for 2 rounds.
- **"Clear Headed"**: Healing Done +3% for 2 rounds (stacks up to 20 times, each stack is timed independently).
- **Rigidity**: When there are 2 stacks, turns into Petrify status or extends the duration of existing Petrify status for 1 round.
- **Poison**: At the end of the round, takes (the user's ATK x30%) Genesis DMG (stackable).
- **Burn**: Healing Taken -15% (unstackable). At the end of the round, takes (the holder's ATK x4%) Genesis DMG (stackable up to 30 times, multiple stacks of Burn is regarded as 1 \[Neg Status\], removes 50% of the stacks when triggered).
