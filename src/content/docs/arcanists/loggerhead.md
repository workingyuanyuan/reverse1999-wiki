Loggerhead spreads negative statuses and heals the allies she guards. A Mind-Blower! puts Memory Replay on an ally and the allies behind it; each time an enemy hits one of them, the attacker takes a random negative status and Loggerhead gains Clear Headed. At the end of the round she heals every ally in Memory Replay for each Clear Headed stack she holds.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A Must-See! (single-target attack) | 200% Mental DMG | 230% | 400% |
| A Mind-Blower! (counter on an ally) | [Memory Replay] on the target and 1 unit behind it | target and 2 units behind | target and 3 units behind |

A Must-See! inflicts 2 random effects from [Collection of Neg Status] on its target at every rank. Memory Replay lasts 2 rounds at every rank.

## Ultimate: Cinema Binge

Deals 400% Mental DMG to all enemies, then inflicts 2 random effects from Collection of Neg Status on them.

## Statuses

| Status | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Memory Replay | DMG Taken Reduction +15%. When the holder is attacked, the attacker receives 1 random effect from Collection of Neg Status for 2 rounds. |
| Collection of Neg Status | One of three effects, an effect the target lacks first: 1 stack of Rigidity, 1 Poison, or 5 stacks of Burn. |
| Clear Headed | Healing Done +3% for 2 rounds; up to 20 stacks, each timed on its own. |
| Rigidity | At 2 stacks it becomes Petrify, or adds 1 round to an existing Petrify. |
| Poison | At the end of the round, Genesis DMG equal to 30% of the inflicting unit's ATK; it stacks. |
| Burn | Healing Taken −15%. At the end of the round, Genesis DMG based on 4% of the holder's ATK, then half the stacks are removed. Up to 30 stacks, which count as one Neg Status. |
