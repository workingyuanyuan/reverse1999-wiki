## Item

### Liquor Ladle

It is said to have been used in the East to scoop wine from barrels, but in reality the long handle is difficult to wield and is thought to have been used primarily for entertainment. Judging by its appearance, it would be most practical for removing unwanted guests from the tavern.

### Cherished Pouch

A simple purse made special because its owner carefully attached all the poems she received to it. There were kind words from neighbors, words of praise, and sincere thanks from others. Strangely enough, every interviewee who is familiar with this language seems a little embarrassed when shown this purse.

### "Friend"craft

Despite their appearance, these puppets are surprisingly flexible. They like to sing together, and the song is unheard of in the West. Of course, not every wooden puppet can sing and dance—it takes some freshly cut wood, a pair of skilled hands, and a stroke of luck to create such wonders.

## Story

### A City at Its Best

The city stood still in its heyday.

"There was gold and silver and no restrictions; there was silk and satin and no criticism. There were kings and common men and no hierarchy." It was the golden heyday of a great country, and its people believed their future was as bright as the noonday sun.

It remained vibrant, passionate, and colorful.

As the hem of the young girl's six-fabric dress brushed gently against the street, a wisp of sweetness and refreshing spice lingered in the air. With her satin shoes softly padding the ground and her golden hair ornaments tinkling, she hurried along, delivering jars of wine to each household. A young guard on horseback slowly made his way down the street. When he caught sight of her dangerous pace, he tried to catch up and stop her, but the girl was long gone, leaving nothing behind but the bright sun above and a fleeting smile in her eyes.

There was a time even greater than this.

It was before the broken bridge, when the Xiangrui still roamed the earth. Travelers once flocked to its gates, and adventurers ventured beyond its walls.

The people were nostalgic for days gone by, so the future was forgotten. The arrival of the capital carriage became a distant memory. In this self-sufficient land, only the moon shone softly and calmly, as it always had.

### Blossom Spring Fairy

> In a painting without time, sharing a drink so sublime.

One night during a festival, a weary wanderer came by a tavern that kept its doors open long past midnight. Drawn in by the sweet aroma of wine, he sat down to rest and drank cup after cup. As his vision blurred, he noticed a mural on the wall of a fairy, with a silk-flower lantern balanced on her shoulder, smiling gently. With a flick of her pale wrist, a wine jug shimmered into view, and she looked straight at him as if beckoning him to enter.

His heart stirred, and the wanderer staggered forward and fell straight into the painting. When he opened his eyes again, he had arrived in a paradise. There was sweet wine to melt away all bitterness, breathtaking sights to soothe the soul, and immortal fairies who laughed and drank with him. Shedding the troubles of the mortal world, the man wished only to remain in this enchanted valley forever.

But after three cups, dawn had broken. He roused from his slumber, lying atop an empty jar. Everything from the night before had been nothing but a dream.

He looked up. The mural still showed the peach-blossom paradise, but the Blossom Spring Fairy was nowhere to be found. Only the tavern girl's voice remained beside him: "Shall I open another jar for you?"

#### Flower Basket Lantern

The frame is made of slender bamboo ribs and decorated with peonies and orchids. Draped in silk and illustrated with scenes of the four seasons, it is designed to be carried on the shoulder.

After the shoot, the interviewee made a request: "May I take this lantern back with me? If it's hung under the tavern's eaves, I'm sure it will brighten the day of every customer who sees it."

#### Gold Yingluo Neck Ring

Gold threads run through jade beads, hammered into an accessory for a goddess in a painting, imbued in order to warm the shoulder and neck. When asked about the ruby's origin, the interviewee removed it for closer inspection—it turned out to be just a drop of red paint caught by her wooden puppets.

#### "Journey Into the Painting"

A chased copper ewer with a slender neck and circular base, gilded all over, reserved for entertaining esteemed guests during festivals. The interviewee often shares drinks with visitors until dawn, waking up as if from a dream.

As for what they see in their dreams, accounts differ, but all claim they've wandered into a painted scroll.
