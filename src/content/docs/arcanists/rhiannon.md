## Incantations

### Babble-Gabble

**Rank 1**: Mass attack. Deals 200% Mental DMG to all enemies. Before attacking, consumes 1 stack of \[Attunement\] to gain ATK +12% for 3 rounds.

**Rank 2**: Mass attack. Deals 300% Mental DMG to all enemies. Before attacking, consumes 1 stack of \[Attunement\] to gain ATK +13% for 3 rounds.

**Rank 3**: Mass attack. Deals 500% Mental DMG to all enemies. Before attacking, consumes 1 stack of \[Attunement\] to gain ATK +14% for 3 rounds.

### Sneaky-Peeky

**Rank 1**: Mass buff. Grants \[Flocksong\] to all allies for 3 rounds. Consumes 1 stack of \[Attunement\] to gain Max HP +12% for 3 rounds.

**Rank 2**: Mass buff. Grants \[Flocksong\] to all allies for 3 rounds. Consumes 1 stack of \[Attunement\] to gain Max HP +13% for 3 rounds.

**Rank 3**: Mass buff. Grants \[Flocksong\] to all allies for 3 rounds. Consumes 1 stack of \[Attunement\] to gain Max HP +14% for 3 rounds.

## Ultimate: Chirping Chorus

Mass buff. Grants \[Adar Llwch Gwin\] to all other allies for 3 rounds. Gains \[Bendith\] for 4 rounds.

## Inheritance

**Insight 0 · Precognition**: Before casting incantations, Rhiannon can activate \[Raptor Kinship\] and enter the \[Sensory Bond\] status. When entering battle, \[Raptor Kinship\] starts at 0% charge and has a max charge of 150%.

At the start of the round, gains 2 stacks of \[Attunement\].

Rhiannon is an \[All-Rounder\] character.

**Insight 1 · Bird Bonder**: At the start of the round, gains 1 stack of \[Rapport\] for 3 rounds.

**Insight 2 · Bird Bonder II**: ATK +5% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Bird Bonder III**: When entering battle, gains Moxie +3 and charges \[Raptor Kinship\] by 70%.

At the end of the round, if holding \[Attunement\], consumes 1 stack of \[Attunement\] to gain Moxie +1 (consumes up to 1 stack).

**Portrait 1**: **Precognition**: Rhiannon now gains Moxie +5 when entering battle.**Sneaky-Peeky**: \[Flocksong\] now grants Incantation Might +45% and Ultimate Might +45%.

**Portrait 2**: **Precognition**: When entering battle, now charges \[Raptor Kinship\] by 85%. Consuming \[Attunement\] now charges \[Raptor Kinship\] by 10% and grants 3 stacks of \[Rapport\] to Rhiannon. \[Rapport\] now stacks up to 7 times, and each stack now grants Stronger Afflatus DMG Multiplier +5%. \[Feast Call\] now deals 3000% Mental DMG plus 300% Mental DMG for each stack of \[Rapport\] held.

**Portrait 3**: \[Bendith\]-reinforced **Babble-Gabble** now inflicts Critical DEF -20% and up to an additional Critical DEF -21% based on the number of \[Rapport\] stacks. \[Bendith\]-reinforced **Sneaky-Peeky** now grants DMG Bonus +20% and up to an additional DMG Bonus +21% based on the number of \[Rapport\] stacks.

**Portrait 4**: **Chirping Chorus**: \[Adar Llwch Gwin\] now grants (Rhiannon's ATK x10%) ATK, up to 310, and (Rhiannon's Max HP x10%) Max HP, up to 1550.

When consuming \[Attunement\], \[Raptor Kinship\] now gains 12.5% recharge, and Rhiannon gains 4 stacks of \[Rapport\], up to 10 stacks. \[Feast Call\] now deals 5000% Mental DMG plus 500% Mental DMG for each stack of \[Rapport\] owned.

## Statuses

- **Attunement**: When consumed, charges \[Raptor Kinship\] by 8% and gains 2 stacks of \[Rapport\] for 3 rounds.

  (\[Attunement\] is stackable up to 4 times.)
- **Flocksong**: Incantation Might +30%, Ultimate Might +30%.
- **Adar Llwch Gwin**: ATK +(Rhiannon's ATK x8%), up to 250. Max HP +(Rhiannon's Max HP x8%), up to 1250.
- **Bendith**: Actively casting own incantations does not consume AP. Basic incantations are reinforced.
- **Raptor Kinship**: When charged up to 100%, this can be activated before casting incantations. When activated, consumes 100% charge and enters \[Sensory Bond\].
- **Sensory Bond**: While in this status, allies gain an extra round of actions, while enemies cannot act. Right before the AP Area resolves, adds 1 rankless \[Feast Call\] to the spell queue.
- **All-Rounder**: Counts as a character belonging to the \[Lingering Glow\], \[Bloodtithe\], \[Dynamo\], \[Inspiration\], \[Conduit\], \[Burn\], \[HP Sacrifice\], \[Poison\], \[Ritual\], \[Rank Up\], \[Extra Action\], and \[Shift\] archetypes.
- **Rapport**: \[Force Field\]: Allied Stronger Afflatus DMG Multiplier +4% (stackable up to 5 times, each stack timed independently, undispellable).
- **Feast Call**: Mass attack. Deals 1500% Mental DMG to all enemies. For each stack of \[Rapport\] held, deals an additional 150% Mental DMG. This attack is always considered to be of a Stronger Afflatus.
- **Lingering Glow**: Accumulates \[Lingering Glow\] whenever an enemy is inflicted with \[Burn\] or \[Halo\]. The fewer enemies there are, the more \[Lingering Glow\] is accumulated.
- **Bloodtithe**: Whenever allies lose 3000 HP combined, accumulate 1 point of \[Bloodtithe\]; HP lost from being attacked accumulates \[Bloodtithe\] at only 30% efficiency.
- **Dynamo**: A product of technology used for meticulous machinery (up to 30 stacks, undispellable).
- **Inspiration**: For every 1 Eureka consumed, accumulate 1 point of \[Inspiration\].

  At the start of each round, randomly distribute Inspiration points onto basic incantations (each incantation may receive up to 5 points of Inspiration, and at the end of the round, the points of Inspiration on unused incantations are cleared).

  When actively casting an incantation with \[Inspiration\] points on it, add an \[Impromptu Incantation\] to the end of the spell queue, and all Inspiration points on these casted incantations will be granted to the \[Impromptu Incantation\].
- **Conduit**: Use Energy to power Conduits. Conduits activate at the end of the AP Area and repeatedly cast incantations by consuming Energy.
- **Burn**: Healing Taken -15% (unstackable). At the end of the round, takes (the holder's ATK x4%) Genesis DMG (stackable up to 30 times, multiple stacks of Burn is regarded as 1 \[Neg Status\], removes 50% of the stacks when triggered).
- **Poison**: At the end of the round, takes Genesis DMG based on the user's ATK (stackable).
- **Rank Up**: When the incantation is subject to rank calculations, its rank is increased by an extra 1 (the incantation's inherent effects are unaffected).
- **Force Field**: The holder is granting effects to the relevant characters. Disappears when defeated.
- **Halo**: Healing Taken -25% (unstackable). At the end of the round, takes (the inflicter's ATK x4%) Genesis DMG (stackable up to 30 times, multiple stacks of Halo are regarded as 1 \[Neg Status\], removes 50% of the stacks when triggered).
- **Impromptu Incantation**: 1-target attack. Deals 200% Mental DMG to a random enemy and an additional (the total points of Inspiration granted to Impromptu Incantation x15%) Mental DMG. If Impromptu Incantation has at least 3/6/12/20 points of Inspiration, it gains attack instances +1/2/3/5 (Impromptu Incantation's DMG calculation is based on the average stats of all allies).
