Each of Ezra's incantations costs him a share of his current HP and plants a mushroom: the attack plants [Honey Fungus], the shield plants [Ghost Fungus]. At most 2 mushrooms stand at once; each grows at the start of every round and matures after 3 growths, Honey Fungus weakening all enemies and Ghost Fungus raising all allies' Critical DMG. His Ultimate gives the other allies Moxie and Eureka and matures every mushroom at once.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Protection to the Body (attack on 2 enemies) | costs 15% of current HP; 30% Mental DMG plus Genesis DMG of 15% of his Max HP; plants 1 Honey Fungus | 15%; 40% plus 20%; 1 | 10%; 50% plus 25%; 2 |
| Stimulus to the Mind (team shield) | costs 15% of current HP; shield of 15% of his Max HP for 1 round; plants 1 Ghost Fungus | 15%; 20%; 1 | 10%; 25%; 2 |

The Genesis DMG of Protection to the Body can crit. The field holds at most 2 mushrooms.

## Ultimate: Compassion All-Round

The other allies gain Moxie +1 and Eureka +1. Ezra fills every empty mushroom space with Honey Fungus, then every mushroom matures and triggers at once.

## Euphoria

Euphoria opens at Insight III level 30. Ezra has two Epiphanies of four levels each: the first builds an electric field that boosts the team, the second turns the HP the team loses into heals.

| Epiphany | Level | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | At the start of a round with no Pulsing Field of any level, Ezra deploys [Pulsing Field Lv.1]. Every action adds 10 [Dynamo], and at the end of a round up to 20 Dynamo are fed into the field. |
| 1 | 2 | Ezra plants 1 Honey Fungus and 1 Ghost Fungus at the start of each round. |
| 1 | 3 | 10 Dynamo on entering battle. |
| 1 | 4 | Both incantations cost 10% less of current HP at every rank. Stimulus to the Mind's shield lasts 1 round longer and adds onto an existing shield, up to 60% of Max HP. Ezra gains [Amplified Surge], and every mushroom that matures triggers [Stacked Shield]. |
| 2 | 1 | HP lost by the team builds [Bloodtithe]. The Ultimate no longer gives the other allies Eureka; it adds 6 Bloodtithe instead. At the end of a round with at least 5 Bloodtithe, each mushroom heals the other allies by 8% of their lost HP; this heal can crit. |
| 2 | 2 | Basic incantations cost 15% more of Ezra's current HP and plant 2 mushrooms of their kind. Stimulus to the Mind becomes a heal: allies recover HP equal to the shield it would have given. Casting it spends 2 Bloodtithe for Healing Done +30% on that cast, and +30% more if Ezra's HP is below 50%. |
| 2 | 3 | A basic incantation cast while the mushrooms are full also takes 10% of current HP from all allies and grows every mushroom once. Ezra gains Healing Taken +20%. |
| 2 | 4 | Each mushroom that matures adds 1 Bloodtithe and heals all allies by 20% of their lost HP; this heal can crit. At the start of a round with at least 15 Bloodtithe, 1 is spent to heal all allies by 20% of their lost HP. |

- **Pulsing Field**: at the start of each round it rises with the Dynamo fed into it: 50 or more makes it Lv.2, 120 or more Lv.3. The Dynamo in it is cleared when the field ends. Lv.1 gives all allies DMG Bonus +15% for 3 rounds; Lv.2 Incantation Might +25% and DMG Bonus +15% for 3 rounds; Lv.3 Ultimate Might +35%, Incantation Might +25% and DMG Bonus +15% for 2 rounds.
- **Dynamo**: up to 30 stacks; cannot be dispelled.
- **Amplified Surge**: a mushroom under Pulsing Field Lv.2 or Lv.3 grows to maturity at the start of the round.
- **Stacked Shield**: all allies gain another shield equal to that of a rank 2 Stimulus to the Mind, added onto an existing shield up to 60% of Max HP.
- **Bloodtithe**: 1 point for every 3000 HP the team loses in total; HP lost to attacks counts at 30%. On the mainland service it cannot be held together with Lingering Glow or the Pursuit Scope, a clause the global client does not have.
