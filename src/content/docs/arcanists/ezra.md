## Incantations

### Protection to the Body

**Rank 1**: Mass attack. Loses (Current HP x15%) and deals 30% Mental DMG plus (Own Max HP x15%) Genesis DMG to 2 enemies. The inflicted Genesis DMG can critically hit. Plants 1 \[Honey Fungus\]. Fungus cap: 2.

**Rank 2**: Mass attack. Loses (Current HP x15%) and deals 40% Mental DMG plus (Own Max HP x20%) Genesis DMG to 2 enemies. The inflicted Genesis DMG can critically hit. Plants 1 \[Honey Fungus\]. Fungus cap: 2.

**Rank 3**: Mass attack. Loses (Current HP x10%) and deals 50% Mental DMG plus (Own Max HP x25%) Genesis DMG to 2 enemies. The inflicted Genesis DMG can critically hit. Plants 2 \[Honey Fungus\]. Fungus cap: 2.

### Stimulus to the Mind

**Rank 1**: Mass buff. Loses (Current HP x15%) and grants all allies a Shield with (the user's Max HP x15%) for 1 round. Plants 1 \[Ghost Fungus\]. Fungus cap: 2.

**Rank 2**: Mass buff. Loses (Current HP x15%) and grants all allies a Shield with (the user's Max HP x20%) for 1 round. Plants 1 \[Ghost Fungus\]. Fungus cap: 2.

**Rank 3**: Mass buff. Loses (Current HP x10%) and grants all allies a Shield with (the user's Max HP x25%) for 1 round. Plants 2 \[Ghost Fungus\]. Fungus cap: 2.

## Ultimate: Compassion All-Round

Mass buff. Moxie +1 and Eureka +1 for other allies. Plant the max number of \[Honey Fungus\] on any available empty spaces; all mushrooms then mature and immediately trigger their effects.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · Fungus Ecosystem Machine**: When entering battle, gain Eureka +2. After casting an Ultimate, gain Eureka +4. When casting a normal incantation, if mushrooms are maxed out and match the incantation type, spend 2 Eureka to grow the matching mushrooms 1 time and trigger them if they are mature.

**Insight 2 · Fungus Ecosystem Machine II**: DMG Taken -8% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Fungus Ecosystem Machine III**: At the end of the round, for every 1 mushroom on the field, recover (own Missing HP x8%) HP.

This effect can critically hit. After casting a basic incantation, if the mushrooms are maxed out but don't match the incantation type, spend 2 Eureka to gain Moxie +1

(only triggers when Moxie is below 5).

## Portrait

**Portrait 1**: **Fungus Ecosystem Machine**: When entering battle, additional Eureka +2; Each mushroom's recovery amount is increased to 12% of own Lost HP.

**Portrait 2**: **Protection to the Body**: At incantation rank 1/2, the HP loss effect is reduced to 10% of current HP. The \[Vulnerability\] inflicted by \[Honey Fungus\] increases to 3 stacks.

**Portrait 3**: **Stimulus to the Mind**: At Incantation rank 1/2/3, the \[Shield\] granted to all allies is increased to (Own HP x20%/25%/35%).

**Portrait 4**: **Protection to the Body**: At Incantation rank 1/2/3, The inflicted Genesis DMG is increased to (Own HP x20%/25%/35%).

**Portrait 5**: **Stimulus to the Mind**: At incantation rank 1/2, the HP loss effect is reduced to 10% of current HP. The \[Discernment\] granted by \[Ghost Fungus\] is increased to 3 stacks.

## Euphoria

**Epiphany 1, level 1**: At the start of the round, if not under a \[Pulsing Field\] of any level, deploy \[Pulsing Field Lv.1\]. After an ally takes an action, they gain 10 stacks of \[Dynamo\]. At the end of the round, transfer up to 20 stacks of \[Dynamo\] into the \[Pulsing Field\].

**Epiphany 1, level 2**: At the start of each round, plant 1 \[Honey Fungus\] and 1 \[Ghost Fungus\].

**Epiphany 1, level 3**: When entering battle, gain 10 stacks of \[Dynamo\].

**Epiphany 1, level 4**: When casting a rank 1/2/3 **Protection to the Body** or **Stimulus to the Mind**, the Current HP loss effect is reduced by a flat 10%.

**Stimulus to the Mind**: \[Shield\] duration +1 round. If a target already has a Shield, the new Shield value is stacked on top of the existing Shield, up to a maximum Shield value of (Max HP x60%) HP.

Gain \[Amplified Surge\]. When a mushroom matures, a \[Stacked Shield\] is triggered.

**Epiphany 2, level 1**: Whenever an ally loses HP, accumulate points of \[Bloodtithe\].

The ultimate no longer grants Eureka to other allies, and instead grants 6 points of \[Bloodtithe\].

At the end of the round, if \[Bloodtithe\] is at 5 or higher, for every 1 mushroom, recover (the target's Missing HP x8%) HP to other allies, this effect can critically hit.

**Epiphany 2, level 2**: When casting a basic incantation, lose an additional (own current HP x15%) HP, and the corresponding planted mushrooms is increased to 2.

**Stimulus to the Mind** is now changed to a healing incantation, changing its effect from granting Shields to all allies to recovering HP equal to the Shield's value to all allies.

When casting **Stimulus to the Mind**, consume 2 points of \[Bloodtithe\] to grant Healing Done +30% to that action; and if own HP is below 50%, grant additional Healing Done +30% to that action.

**Epiphany 2, level 3**: When casting a basic incantation, if the mushrooms are maxed out, consume an additional 10% Current HP from all allies to grow all mushrooms 1 time.

This unit gains Healing Taken +20%.

**Epiphany 2, level 4**: When a mushroom matures, accumulate 1 point of \[Bloodtithe\] and recover (the target's Missing HP x20%) HP to all allies, this effect can critically hit.

At the start of the round, if \[Bloodtithe\] is at 15 or higher, consume 1 point of \[Bloodtithe\] and recover (the target's Missing HP x20%) HP to all allies.

## Statuses

- **Honey Fungus**: Mushroom. Grows 1 time at the start of the round. Matures after growing 3 times, inflicting 2 stacks of \[Vulnerability\] on all enemies.
- **Ghost Fungus**: Mushroom. Grows 1 time at the start of the round. Matures after growing 3 times, granting 2 stacks of \[Discernment\] on all allies.
- **Vulnerability**: When attacked, DMG Taken Reduction -25% (-1 stack after trigger).
- **Shield**: Blocks certain DMG for a certain duration.
- **Discernment**: Critical DMG +40% (-1 stack when scoring a critical hit with an incantation).
- **Pulsing Field**: At the start of the round, Pulsing Field upgrades based on the \[Dynamo\] transferred into it. At 50+, upgrades into \[Pulsing Field Lv.2\]. At 120+, upgrades into \[Pulsing Field Lv.3\]. When Pulsing Field disappears, all \[Dynamo\] transferred into it are also cleared.
- **Pulsing Field Lv.1**: All allies gain DMG Bonus +15% for 3 rounds.
- **Dynamo**: A product of technology used for meticulous machinery (up to 30 stacks, undispellable).
- **Amplified Surge**: When a mushroom is under a \[Pulsing Field Lv.2\] or \[Pulsing Field Lv.3\], the mushroom instantly matures at the start of the round.
- **Stacked Shield**: Additionally grants a Shield equal to a rank 2 \[Stimulus to the Mind\] to all allies; and if a target is already holding a Shield, the value of this Shield is stacked on top of that target's existing Shield, up to a maximum Shield value of (own Max HP x60%) HP.
- **Bloodtithe**: Whenever allies lose 3000 HP combined, accumulate 1 point of \[Bloodtithe\]; HP lost from being attacked accumulates \[Bloodtithe\] at only 30% efficiency.
- **Pulsing Field Lv.2**: All allies gain Incantation Might +25% and DMG Bonus +15% for 3 rounds.
- **Pulsing Field Lv.3**: All allies gain Ultimate Might +35%, Incantation Might +25%, and DMG Bonus +15% for 2 rounds.
