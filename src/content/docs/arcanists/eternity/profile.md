## Item

### Eurybia The Ocean Series

Released in 1893 by Eurybia, the Ocean series immediately became the upper class's favorite for its artful design and the unique color which is slightly brighter than the Prussian blue. As time passed, this color has gradually become the color of Eurybia.

### Eurybia Century Collection 1893 Series Sling Bag

In 1993, the collection made its debut at New York's Spring/Summer Fashion Week in Bryant Park. At the time, Eurybia, known for its jewel series, was already one of the leading brands in the fashion industry.

Until today, the demand for this purse remains extremely high among luxury collectors.

### Early Victorian Period Antique Jewelry

A classic piece of jewelry from the Romantic Period (early Victorian era). Unlike stamped jewelry, the nature-themed patterns on it were hand-carved by skillful jewelers.

What's more, its current owner's glamor is a good complement to its beauty.

## Story

### Sealed Candy Jar

At the end of the 19th century, torches constantly lit up the night sky of Exeter, a frontier town in the USA.

The door of her store was closed. In a dimly lit room, on the shelves stood many candy jars. The last young buyer of these candies had been sent to that crowded, shabby cemetery (if you prefer to call it that) three months ago.

Too many farms had been destroyed in the civil war, and plague was not the only terrible thing to develop from the ashes: fear of an unknown future also gradually turned into madness. The candies were taken down. Instead, the store owners now stock their shelves with garlic necklaces and bottles of holy water.

More and more vampires were brought to trial, and the seed of suspicion grew in everyone's mind. The once-friendly residents became ruthless intruders and broke into the woman's house, despite the help she had provided them.

But they found nothing in her house. It seemed to have been long abandoned. The floor was covered with dead shellfish, under which there were marks of dried blue blood.

Peace didn't come back to the town of Exeter until the day when everyone heard about a disease called Tuberculosis.

### A Visit to the Arctic

> Those who brave the cold, reap treasures untold.

The research vessel is about to set sail for the North Pole!

By nightfall, almost everyone is talking about the news. What will the scientists uncover this time in that distant, frozen land? Its ecosystems, its indigenous peoples and their cultures, or the truths of ancient myths?

Even the arcanist celebrity Eternity cannot resist spending a small fortune and joining the voyage.

For a woman who has seen as much as she has, the journey is more of a rare indulgence than an investment. The world has long failed to surprise her, but the North still holds its secrets.

For better or worse, she is on the move again—drawn not only by profit, but by the thrill of stepping into the unknown.

#### "Morning Star"

The necklace is reminiscent of a compass used to navigate the seas, adorned with a multitude of gemstones that glow brilliantly under the dancing lights of the aurora borealis. Rumor has it that only the stones surrounding the center are real and valuable, while the centerpiece is just a standard cubic zirconia. However, when asked about this speculation, the interviewee remained silent.

The latest backpack from the Eurybia brand, based on a classic design and decorated with seasonal fur elements. The interviewee herself had a hand in its design. "I just told some interesting seafaring stories," she said.

### The Golden Nurturer

> A thousand golden grains fall into a warm embrace.

This is the season of abundance.

Ears of golden wheat bend low, soon to meet the farmer's scythe. Across the fields, a river of browning husks flows toward the horizon where it meets the soft blue of the evening sky.

The earth is the first mother of us all. She feeds everything that lives, her rivers marking lines across her surface like gentle wrinkles, and her hills forming the graceful curves of her lips. Pomegranate trees sink their roots deep into her soil and grow strong with life.

Eternity follows this golden trail. She bends to collect brightly colored stones from the sandy soil, astonished to discover such breathtaking beauty. This kind land offers more than gold and minerals. It gives every creature a place of calm and belonging.

#### "Cornucopia" Pouch

A treasure from the field, holding golden grains and plumes in radiant bloom.

"Everything in fashion is cyclical, just like the seasons," says the interviewee. Her horn-shaped pouch holds seasonal harvest, captured forever in solidified precious metal.

This sparks rumors—is she expanding her bizarre product line? She answers only with a smile.

#### "Iris of the Plume" Earrings

The color-shifting gemstone earrings are said to come from a gem workshop in southern Greece.

The dazzling light of these gems shifts with every angle, like the never-closing eyes on the tail feathers of a goddess's favorite bird.

"Oh, darling, when we talk, you can look into the pair of eyes you like."

#### "Empress of the Seasons" Tiara

Every queen should have her own crown, and this one is no exception.

Gold, turquoise, and malachite are carefully carved and set onto a headband, like twelve stars. Work takes its own time—no matter how the myths and years pass, the laws of the seasons do not bend to human will. This is one unchanging thing in a hundred years.
