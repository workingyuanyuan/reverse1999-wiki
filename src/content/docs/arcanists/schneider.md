## Incantations

### One with Guns

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 150% Mental DMG. Inflicts DMG Taken +20% for 1 round on the target hit.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 230% Mental DMG. Inflicts DMG Taken +25% for 1 round on the target hit.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 400% Mental DMG. Inflicts DMG Taken +35% for 1 round on the target hit.

### Sinner

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 150% Mental DMG. Inflicts DMG Dealt -20% for 1 round on the target hit.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 230% Mental DMG. Inflicts DMG Dealt -25% for 1 round on the target hit.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 400% Mental DMG. Inflicts DMG Dealt -35% for 1 round on the target hit.

## Ultimate: Darkness United

Mass attack. Deals 360% Mental DMG to all enemies. Moxie +1 for the caster.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · For Survival's Sake**: After the caster casts the Ultimate, Moxie +1.

**Insight 2 · For Survival's Sake Ⅱ**: Leech Rate +10% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · For Survival's Sake Ⅲ**: When an ally is defeated, Moxie +2.
