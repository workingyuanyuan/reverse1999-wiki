## Incantations

### Undying Light

**Rank 1**: Mass attack. Deals 200% Reality DMG to 2 enemies. Gains Moxie +1.

After attacking, inflicts 5 stacks of \[Shrapnel\] on the main target and inflicts \[Lock On\] on them for 1 round.

**Rank 2**: Mass attack. Deals 300% Reality DMG to 2 enemies. Gains Moxie +1.

After attacking, inflicts 7 stacks of \[Shrapnel\] on the main target and inflicts \[Lock On\] on them for 1 round.

**Rank 3**: Mass attack. Deals 500% Reality DMG to 2 enemies. Gains Moxie +1.

After attacking, inflicts 9 stacks of \[Shrapnel\] on the main target and inflicts \[Lock On\] on them for 1 round.

### Night's Command

**Rank 1**: Gains 2 random types of \[Bullet\] from \[Arsenal\] (4 stacks for each type), prioritizing \[Bullet\] types not yet obtained. For each type of \[Bullet\] held, gains 1 stack of \[War Simulation\].

**Rank 2**: Gains 2 random types of \[Bullet\] from \[Arsenal\] (5 stacks for each type), prioritizing \[Bullet\] types not yet obtained. For each type of \[Bullet\] held, gains 1 stack of \[War Simulation\].

**Rank 3**: Gains 2 random types of \[Bullet\] from \[Arsenal\] (6 stacks for each type), prioritizing \[Bullet\] types not yet obtained. For each type of \[Bullet\] held, gains 1 stack of \[War Simulation\].

## Ultimate: Galloping Across the Underworld

Mass attack. Deals 900% Reality DMG to all enemies and deals an additional 900% Reality DMG to the main target.

For each type of \[Bullet\] held, this attack benefits from Ultimate Might at a +8% rate.

For each stack of \[Shrapnel\] on the target, deals an additional 25% Reality DMG. Removes all \[Shrapnel\] from the target after attacking.

This attack ignores dodge effects.

## Inheritance

**Insight 0 · Precognition**: Increases Max Moxie to 12. Increases Ultimate Moxie consumption to 8.

Igor cannot gain his Ultimate. Instead, at the end of the round, he consumes as much Moxie as possible to cast his Ultimate; this ignores \[Control\] effects.

**Insight 1 · The Gryphon Legion**: When an ally casts an Ultimate, Igor gains Moxie +1.

If there are 1/2 or more other Beast allies present, \[Undying Light\] inflicts 3/5 additional stacks of \[Shrapnel\] on the main target. When an ally performs a 1-target attack, inflicts 1/2 additional stacks of \[Shrapnel\] on the target. When casting an Ultimate, inflicts 3/5 stacks of \[Shrapnel\] on all enemies.

If Lopera is on the team, she gains an additional Critical DMG +50% and inflicts 6 stacks of Burn on the target before attacking. When Lopera casts her Ultimate, she distributes 1 stack of \[Ritual Bullet\]/\[Incantation Bullet\] to adjacent allies in front/behind.

If Moldir is on the team, Moldir and Igor can reserve up to 5 excess Moxie each (replenished at the end of the round if applicable). After Moldir casts an Ultimate, grants Moxie +1 to Moldir, grants Moxie +2 to Igor, and inflicts 12 additional stacks of \[Shrapnel\] on all enemies.

**Insight 2 · The Gryphon Legion II**: ATK +5% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · The Gryphon Legion III**: When entering battle, gains Moxie +2. For every other Beast ally present, gains Moxie +1, up to Moxie +2.

## Portrait

**Portrait 1**: Additional Reality DMG for each stack of \[Shrapnel\] when hit by Igor's Ultimate increased to 45%. **Precognition**: Igor now gains Moxie +2 when an ally casts an Ultimate.

**Portrait 2**: The percentage of additional \[Shrapnel\] stacks inflicted on the main target by \[War Simulation\] increased to 14%. \[Shrapnel\] stack limit increased to 120.

**Portrait 3**: **Night's Command**: \[Bullet\] types gained from \[Arsenal\] increased to 3. **Galloping Across the Underworld**'s Reality DMG dealt increased to 1150%, and the additional Reality DMG dealt to the main target increased to 1150%.

**Portrait 4**: **Undying Light**: At rank 1/2/3, \[Shrapnel\] stacks inflicted on the main target increased to 8/10/12. \[Shrapnel\] stack limit increased to 140. When casting **Galloping Across the Underworld**, the attack benefits from Ultimate Might at a +11% rate for every \[Bullet\] type held.

**Portrait 5**: \[Precognition\]: Moxie cost for casting Ultimate at the end of the round reduced to 6. **Galloping Across the Underworld**'s Reality DMG dealt increased to 1200%, and the additional Reality DMG dealt to the main target increased to 1200%.

## Statuses

- **Shrapnel**: When attacked by Igor's Ultimate, takes an additional 25% Reality DMG per stack (stackable up to 100 times).
- **Lock On**: Igor's Ultimate prioritizes the enemy with \[Lock On\]. Only 1 \[Lock On\] can exist on the field at a time.
- **Arsenal**: There are 6 types of bullets in total: \[Piercing Bullet\], \[Penetrating Bullet\], \[Critical Bullet\], \[Rupturing Bullet\], \[Power Bullet\], and \[Crippling Bullet\].
- **War Simulation**: When casting an Ultimate, for each stack of \[War Simulation\], inflicts (total enemy \[Shrapnel\] stacks x8%) stacks of \[Shrapnel\] on the main target (\[War Simulation\] can stack up to 10 times, removed after casting Ultimate).
- **Control**: Statuses that limit characters' actions: Daze, Petrify, Nightmare, Freeze, Disarm, Silence, Seal.
- **Ritual Bullet**: When attacking, Ultimate Might +25%.
- **Incantation Bullet**: When attacking, Incantation Might +25%.
- **Piercing Bullet**: When attacking, Penetration Rate +30% (-1 stack after trigger).
- **Penetrating Bullet**: When attacking, Penetration Rate +20% (-1 stack after trigger).
- **Critical Bullet**: When attacking, Critical Rate +20% (-1 stack after trigger).
- **Rupturing Bullet**: When attacking, Critical DMG +20% (-1 stack after trigger).
- **Power Bullet**: When attacking, ATK +10% (-1 stack after trigger).
- **Crippling Bullet**: After attacking, inflicts Critical DEF -20% on the main target for 2 rounds (-1 stack after trigger).
