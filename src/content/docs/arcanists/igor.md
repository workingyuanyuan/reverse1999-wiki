Igor's Moxie cap is 12, and at the end of each round he spends Moxie to cast his Ultimate on his own, where Control effects cannot stop it. His first incantation loads the main target with [Shrapnel], which adds damage to that Ultimate, and his second draws [Bullet] types from the [Arsenal] that strengthen his attacks and make the Ultimate draw more on Ultimate Might. He works best beside other Beast allies, and his Insight 1 passive adds effects for Lopera and Moldir.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Undying Light (attack on 2 enemies) | 200% Reality DMG; 5 Shrapnel | 300%; 7 | 500%; 9 |
| Night's Command (self buff) | 2 Bullet types, 4 stacks each | 2 types, 5 stacks each | 2 types, 6 stacks each |

- **Undying Light** gives Igor 1 Moxie at every rank. After the attack, the main target gets the Shrapnel in the table and [Lock On] for 1 round.
- **Night's Command** prefers Bullet types Igor does not hold yet. For each Bullet type he holds, he gains 1 stack of [War Simulation].

## Ultimate: Galloping Across the Underworld

Deals 900% Reality DMG to all enemies and another 900% Reality DMG to the main target. Each stack of Shrapnel on a target adds 25% Reality DMG, and all Shrapnel is removed from the targets after the attack. For each Bullet type Igor holds, the attack is affected by Ultimate Might 8% more. The attack cannot be dodged.
