## Incantations

### Discipline

**Rank 1**: Mass attack. Deals 120% Mental DMG to 2 enemies. After attacking, inflicts 2 random effects from \[Collection of Debuffs\] on the targets for 2 rounds. Gain Eureka +1 for self when at 4 or less Eureka.

**Rank 2**: Mass attack. Deals 175% Mental DMG to 2 enemies. After attacking, inflicts 2 random effects from \[Collection of Debuffs\] on the targets for 2 rounds. Gain Eureka +2 for self when at 4 or less Eureka.

**Rank 3**: Mass attack. Deals 305% Mental DMG to 2 enemies. After attacking, inflicts 2 random effects from \[Collection of Debuffs\] on the targets for 2 rounds. Gain Eureka +3 for self when at 4 or less Eureka.

### Duty

**Rank 1**: 1-target buff. Dispels 2 random types of \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] effects from the target and grants 2 random effects from \[Collection of Buffs\] to the target for 2 rounds. If at 5 or more Eureka, spends 5 Eureka to grant \[Empower Incantation I\] to the target for 2 rounds.

**Rank 2**: 1-target buff. Dispels 4 random types of \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] effects from the target and grants 2 random effects from \[Collection of Buffs\] to the target for 2 rounds. If at 5 or more Eureka, spends 5 Eureka to grant \[Empower Incantation I\] to the target for 2 rounds.

**Rank 3**: 1-target buff. Dispels 6 random types of \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] effects from the target and grants 2 random effects from \[Collection of Buffs\] to the target for 2 rounds. If at 5 or more Eureka, spends 5 Eureka to grant \[Empower Incantation I\] to the target for 3 rounds.

## Ultimate: The Revelation

1-target attack. Deals 700% Mental DMG. If at 6 or more Eureka, spend 6 Eureka to deal 75% increased Mental DMG to the target for every 1 type of \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\] and \[Control\] status they have. Increases up to 4 times.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · Advocator of Knowledge**: Max Eureka +1. At the end of the round, if any 1 ally has 4 or more \[Stats Up\], \[Pos Status\], or \[Counter\] statuses, gains Eureka +1. When attacking a target with \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\] or \[Control\] statuses, DMG Dealt +16%.

**Insight 2 · Advocator of Knowledge II**: DMG Dealt +8% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Advocator of Knowledge III**: Before the Ultimate takes effect, applies 2 random \[Collection of Debuffs\] effects to all enemies for 2 rounds. At the start of the round, applies 1 random \[Collection of Buffs\] effect to all allies for 2 rounds.

## Portrait

**Portrait 1**: **The Revelation**: If at 5 or more Eureka, spend 5 Eureka to deal 100% increased Mental DMG to the target for every 1 type of \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] status they have.

**Portrait 2**: **Advocator of Knowledge**: Gain 1 additional Eureka. When attacking targets with \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] statuses, the DMG Dealt increases to 24%.

**Portrait 3**: At rank 1/2/3, **Duty** now dispels 3/5/7 random types of \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] effects from a single ally.

**Portrait 4**: **The Revelation**: Increased Mental DMG Dealt now at 125%.

**Portrait 5**: **The Revelation**: If at 4 or more Eureka, spend 4 Eureka to deal increased Mental DMG to the target for every 1 type of \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] status they have. Increases up to 6 times.

## Euphoria

**Epiphany 1, level 1**: **Advocator of Knowledge**: **Discipline**'s Collection of Debuffs now includes Critical DEF -30%, Critical Resist Rate -20%, DMG Taken Reduction -20%, Reality DEF -20%, Mental DEF -20%, and \[Fracture II\]. **Discipline** now inflicts 2/4/6 effects for 2/3/4 rounds at rank 1/2/3; if the target has every effect included in the Collection of Debuffs, additionally inflicts 2/4/6 instances of \[Poison\] on them for 2 rounds and 3/6/9 stacks of \[Burn\]. Effects previously included in the Collection of Debuffs (\[Blind\] and \[Mis-aim\]) are now inflicted before casting Ultimate and last 2 rounds.

**Epiphany 1, level 2**: **Advocator of Knowledge**: **Duty**'s Collection of Buffs now includes DMG Bonus +20%, Extra Action DMG Bonus +20%, Penetration Rate +20%, Incantation Might +30%, Ultimate Might +30%, and Genesis DMG Dealt +30%. **Duty** now grants 2/4/6 effects for 2/3/4 rounds at rank 1/2/3; Empower Incantation (no longer consumes Eureka) is now granted after the target has every effect included in the Collection of Buffs, and the target gains Eureka +10 afterward. Effects previously included in the Collection of Buffs (Mental DEF +15%, Reality DEF +15%, \[Vitalize I\], and \[Ricochet I\]) are now granted at the start of every round and last 2 rounds.

**Epiphany 1, level 3**: **The Revelation** now consumes 6 Moxie to cast. New Effects: When entering battle, 6 gains Max Moxie +1. At the end of the round, 6 gains Moxie +2.

**Epiphany 1, level 4**: **The Revelation** now deals an additional (base Mental DMG x100%) increased Mental DMG for each type of debuff on the target, and the number of times this DMG can be increased is doubled.

## Statuses

- **Collection of Debuffs**: There are 6 effects: Critical DEF -30%, Reality DEF -20%, Mental DEF -20%, \[Blind\], \[Mis-aim\], and \[Fracture I\]. Prioritizes effects not yet applied.
- **Control**: Statuses that limit characters' actions: Daze, Petrify, Nightmare, Freeze, Disarm, Silence, Seal.
- **Collection of Buffs**: There are 6 effects: Penetration Rate +20%, Incantation Might +15%, Mental DEF +15%, Reality DEF +15%, \[Vitalize I\], and \[Ricochet I\]. Prioritizes effects not yet applied.
- **Empower Incantation I**: At the start of the round, a random incantation gains rank +1.
- **Counter**: Grants continuous Counter. Effect is triggered When the caster is attacked.
- **Fracture II**: When being attacked, take an additional (the user's ATK x50%) Genesis DMG.
- **Poison**: At the end of the round, takes (the user's ATK x30%) Genesis DMG (stackable).
- **Burn**: Healing Taken -15% (unstackable). At the end of the round, takes (the holder's ATK x4%) Genesis DMG (stackable up to 30 times, multiple stacks of Burn is regarded as 1 \[Neg Status\], removes 50% of the stacks when triggered).
- **Blind**: DMG Bonus -30% when attacking 1 target.
- **Mis-aim**: DMG Bonus -30% when attacking multiple targets.
- **Vitalize I**: When the round starts, HP +(Max HP x10%).
- **Ricochet I**: When attacked, reflects (the user's ATK x30%) Genesis DMG.
- **Fracture I**: When attacked, takes additional (the user's ATK x20%) Genesis DMG.
