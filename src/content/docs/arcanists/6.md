6 runs on Eureka, a resource of his own. Discipline hits two enemies with random debuffs and gives him Eureka while he holds 4 or less; Duty cleanses one ally, gives it random buffs and, at 5 Eureka, spends them to raise the rank of the ally's incantations. His Ultimate spends Eureka to deal more damage for each kind of debuff on its target.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discipline (attack on 2 enemies) | 120% Mental DMG; Eureka +1 | 175%; Eureka +2 | 305%; Eureka +3 |
| Duty (single-ally buff) | removes 2 types of debuff; Empower Incantation I 2 rounds | 4 types; 2 rounds | 6 types; 3 rounds |

- **Discipline** puts 2 random effects of [Collection of Debuffs] on each target for 2 rounds. 6 gains the Eureka only while he has 4 or less.
- **Duty** removes random types of Stats Down, Neg Status or Control effects from one ally and gives it 2 random effects of [Collection of Buffs] for 2 rounds. If 6 has 5 or more Eureka, he spends 5 to give the ally [Empower Incantation I].

## Ultimate: The Revelation

Deals 700% Mental DMG to one enemy. If 6 has 6 or more Eureka, he spends 6, and the hit deals 75% more Mental DMG for each type of Stats Down, Neg Status or Control effect on the target, counted up to 4 times.
