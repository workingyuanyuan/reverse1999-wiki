## Cover

An arcanist's work. Exhibited in the 1980s for 15 years. Completed in Spring, on April 19. First exhibited in Murmansk, USSR, later transferred to Leningrad. After several years of touring within the country, it was archived at the LSCC.

Dimensions: 61 1/32 x 20 5/16 x 18 7/64 in.

Medium: Aurora

Fragrance Note: Woody and Aromatic, Red Apple, Hearth Smoke, Sandalwood, Sage, Honey

## Story

### Higher than the White Birch

This is the vibrant eighties.

New things are everywhere. Volga drivers roll down their windows to grab a Pepsi from roadside vendors. A little bear plush sits quietly on a shop counter, watching the crowds wave their goodbyes. Inside the circus tents, the shows go on as scheduled. Troupe members deliver just the right amount of thrill and excitement to the audience, then relieve the lingering tension with hot meals after the curtain falls. She rests safely inside this eggshell of a world—beyond it, chaos and the human condition churn.

Her troupe loves to share stories with her during their breaks. Night school classmates eagerly bring her news and gossip. Myths, legends, and ongoing events in the real world tangle together like the warm, rough knots in a bear cub's fur. Not every story actually happens, but every single one overflows with hope for the future, enough to make you forget the frostbite in your fingers, the endless lines, the long journeys and exhaustion.

There is an ideal here. A yearning for a steadier, safer life. If you can reach the cosmos, what pain or death could ever defeat you?

That's why she keeps stepping onto the narrow wire again and again, chasing a higher vantage point.

Higher than that taut wire. Higher than the waving flags.

High enough to look down on this glass sphere of a world—until everything on the ground blurs together, and nothing seems separate anymore.

Pandora Wilson: There are Arcane Skills that can preserve images and memories. But they need to be used on-site, in the moment. Have you tried artificial somnambulism? Does it meet your expectations?

Determining one's future isn't easy. That's why a heart that longs to gather light and find the way matters. This device, though rudimentary, reflects that hope.

### A Utopia in Painted Glass

> Her ideal world lies on the other side of the stained glass.

It's a fine afternoon. Sunlight spills across the palette, catching Tyrian purple with a faint glow. Centuries ago, this color could only be extracted from murex snails. The color's rarity made it the emblem of royalty and nobility. Now, it sits on a shelf in an ordinary shop.

When she first started learning to paint, she was always too caught up in proportion and accuracy. Afraid of making mistakes, she hesitated before every stroke. Then one day, a hand reached from behind, grasped her brush, and drew the first line across the canvas.

"Ms. Vila?" She turned to find her art teacher.

"There's no rush, Comrade Kiperina," came a gentle but firm voice. "Be bold. Precise measurement isn't always the answer in art and aesthetics."

"Then again ..." her teacher's eyes flickered. "Your measuring method is very unique. I've seen another comrade use it before, and I believe it can be quite a useful aid."

#### "Fresh Vision" Omni-Easel

Crafted from stained glass with exceptional light transmission and brass commonly used in ley line surveying instruments.

It is said to be a popular sketching aid among scholars of ley lines. It grants the artist a unique perspective—one that reaches "deeper than reality."

#### The Pigments' Sanctum

A well-worn sketchbook—the perfect sanctum for arcane pigments.

Having fed on her thoughts and inspiration, the images these pigments produce are startlingly vivid. They leap off the page and beckon.

#### The Other Canvas

It allows the interviewee to cast aside her usual caution, leaving spontaneous splashes of color along her hem.

After all, this is how stars and clouds are scattered across the canvas of the cosmos—just as nostalgia and longing settle into her blue eyes.
