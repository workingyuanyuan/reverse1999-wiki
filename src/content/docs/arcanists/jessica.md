Jessica poisons enemies and hits harder the more Poison they carry. Her Insight 1 passive poisons all enemies on entering battle and every third round, her Insight 3 passive poisons any unpoisoned enemy she attacks, and her Ultimate poisons the whole field. Her two Euphoria Epiphanies add Petrify and make Poison settle more often.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| White Blankie (single-target attack) | 180% Reality DMG; 40% more on a poisoned target; 20% more per further Poison, at most 40% | 270%; 60%; 30% per Poison, at most 60% | 450%; 100%; 50% per Poison, at most 100% |
| Good Friends (attack on 2 enemies) | 135% Reality DMG; 30% more | 200%; 45% more | 335%; 75% more |

Good Friends deals its extra damage to a target with a Stats Down, Neg Status or Control effect.

## Ultimate: Gaze From the Forest

Deals 350% Reality DMG to all enemies, then poisons all of them for 2 rounds; the Poison deals Genesis DMG equal to 30% of her ATK at the end of each round.

- [Poison]: at the end of the round, the holder takes Genesis DMG based on the inflicter's ATK; several Poisons can stack.
- [Petrify]: the target cannot act; the effect ends when it takes a Reality DMG attack.

## Euphoria

Euphoria opens at Insight III level 30. Jessica has two Epiphanies of four levels each: the first builds on Petrify, the second on Poison.

| Epiphany | Level | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | She gains 2 stacks of [Moss on Stone] on entering battle and at the end of each round, and 1 more when an ally's incantation attack hits a poisoned main target. With 10 stacks (the cap) at the start of a round, the first incantation attack she casts that round petrifies the main target for 1 round before it lands, and all stacks are removed at round end. Attacks no longer end Petrify. |
| 1 | 2 | White Blankie's bonus for further Poisons caps at 80, 120 or 200% by rank, and Petrify counts as 2 further Poisons. |
| 1 | 3 | Her attacks on a petrified main target gain Incantation Might +30%. |
| 1 | 4 | Her attacks on a petrified main target are always critical hits. |
| 2 | 1 | On entering battle and at the start of rounds 1, 3, 6, 9 and so on, all enemies gain [Mycelium Mayhem] for 3 rounds: at the start of each round it settles 1 round of the holder's Poison without shortening it. |
| 2 | 2 | After the Ultimate hits, every enemy's Poison settles at once. |
| 2 | 3 | White Blankie and Good Friends put 2 Poisons on each target hit for 2 rounds (Genesis DMG equal to 30% of her ATK each round end). At round end, if the enemy with the most Poisons has 5 or more, she casts a rank 1 White Blankie on the enemy with the highest HP percentage and gains 1 Moxie. |
| 2 | 4 | Mycelium Mayhem also gives its holder Poison DMG Taken +10%, and each time its Poison settles, Jessica gives it 1 stack of [Fungal Culture] for 3 rounds at round end (up to 4 stacks). When Mycelium Mayhem is applied, each Fungal Culture stack on the target becomes a 2-round Poison (30% of her ATK), and the stacks are removed. |
