## Incantations

### White Blankie

**Rank 1**: 1-target attack. Deals 180% Reality DMG. If the target is afflicted with \[Poison\], deals an additional 40% Reality DMG. For each additional stack of \[Poison\] afterwards, deals 20% more Reality DMG, up to a max of Reality DMG +40%.

**Rank 2**: 1-target attack. Deals 270% Reality DMG. If the target is afflicted with \[Poison\], deals an additional 60% Reality DMG. For each additional stack of \[Poison\] afterwards, deals 30% more Reality DMG, up to a max of Reality DMG +60%.

**Rank 3**: 1-target attack. Deals 450% Reality DMG. If the target is afflicted with \[Poison\], deals an additional 100% Reality DMG. For each additional stack of \[Poison\] afterwards, deals 50% more Reality DMG, up to a max of Reality DMG +100%.

### Good Friends

**Rank 1**: Mass attack. Deals 135% Reality DMG to 2 enemies. If the target is in \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] statuses, this attack deals 30% more Reality DMG.

**Rank 2**: Mass attack. Deals 200% Reality DMG to 2 enemies. If the target is in \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] statuses, this attack deals 45% more Reality DMG.

**Rank 3**: Mass attack. Deals 335% Reality DMG to 2 enemies. If the target is in \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\] statuses, this attack deals 75% more Reality DMG.

## Ultimate: Gaze From the Forest

Mass attack. Deals 350% Reality DMG to all enemies. After attacking, inflicts \[Poison\] on all enemies for 2 rounds: at the end of the round, deals (Jessica's ATK x30%) Genesis DMG to the target.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · Changeling**: When attacking an enemy afflicted with \[Poison\], DMG Dealt +20%. When entering battle and at the start of every 3rd round (Round 1, 3, 6, 9 ...), inflicts \[Poison\] on all enemies for 2 rounds: at the end of the round, takes (the user's ATK x30%) Genesis DMG.

**Insight 2 · Changeling II**: DMG Dealt +8% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Changeling III**: When attacking an enemy not afflicted with \[Poison\], inflicts \[Poison\] for 2 rounds: at the end of the round, takes (the user's ATK x30%) Genesis DMG.

## Portrait

**Portrait 1**: **Gaze From the Forest**: Reality DMG increased to 425%.

**Portrait 2**: **Changeling**: When entering battle and at the start of every 3rd round, \[Poison\] inflicted on enemies +1 round.

**Portrait 3**: **White Blankie**: At Incantation rank 1/2/3, Reality DMG increased to 200/300/500%.

**Portrait 4**: **Good Friends**: At Incantation Rank 1/2/3, if the target is afflicted with \[Stats Down\], \[Neg Status\], or \[Control\], Reality DMG increased to 50/75/125%.

**Portrait 5**: **Gaze From the Forest**: Reality DMG increased to 500%.

## Euphoria

**Epiphany 1, level 1**: When entering battle and at the end of the round, gain 2 stacks of \[Moss on Stone\] for self; after an ally actively casts and attacks with an incantation, if the main target is in a \[Poison\] status, gain 1 stack of \[Moss on Stone\] for self.

At the start of the round, if holding 10 stacks of \[Moss on Stone\], then right before this unit actively casts and attacks with an incantation for the first time in the round, inflict an additional \[Petrify\] status on the main target for 1 round, and remove all \[Moss on Stone\] statuses on self at the end of the round.

Attacking an enemy in the \[Petrify\] status does not remove the \[Petrify\] status.

**Epiphany 1, level 2**: **White Blankie**: At incantation rank 1/2/3, the Reality DMG bonus from each additional instance \[Poison\] is increased to 80%/120%/200%; and the \[Petrify\] status is regarded as 2 additional instances of \[Poison\].

**Epiphany 1, level 3**: When actively attacking, if the main target is in the \[Petrify\] status, the attack gains Incantation Might +30%.

**Epiphany 1, level 4**: When actively attacking, if the main target is in the \[Petrify\] status, the attack is always a critical hit.

**Epiphany 2, level 1**: When entering battle and at the start of every 3 rounds (Round 1, 3, 6, 9 ...), inflict \[Mycelium Mayhem\] on all enemies for 3 rounds.

**Epiphany 2, level 2**: **Gaze From the Forest**: After hitting the target, immediately resolve \[Poison\] effects on all enemies.

**Epiphany 2, level 3**: When **White Blankie** or **Good Friends** lands a hit, inflict 2 instances of \[Poison\] on the target hit for 2 rounds: at the end of the round, take (the user's ATK x30%) Genesis DMG. At the end of the round, if the enemy with the most \[Poison\] instances has at least 5 instances of \[Poison\], cast a rank 1 **White Blankie** on the enemy with the highest HP percentage and gain Moxie +1.

**Epiphany 2, level 4**: \[Mycelium Mayhem\]'s effect: Poison DMG Taken +10%. Each time \[Poison\] is resolved on the holder, the user inflicts 1 stack of \[Fungal Culture\] on the holder for 3 rounds at the end of the round. When the target is inflicted with \[Mycelium Mayhem\], for every stack of \[Fungal Culture\] the target has, inflict 1 instance of \[Poison\] on the target for 2 rounds, deal (the user's ATK x30%) Genesis DMG at the end of the round, then remove \[Fungal Culture\] from the target.

## Statuses

- **Poison**: At the end of the round, takes Genesis DMG based on the user's ATK (stackable).
- **Control**: Statuses that limit characters' actions: Daze, Petrify, Nightmare, Freeze, Disarm, Silence, Seal.
- **Moss on Stone**: Triggers special effects (stacks up to 10 times).
- **Petrify**: Cannot act. Dispels the effect after taking Reality DMG attack.
- **Mycelium Mayhem**: At the start of the round, resolve 1 round of \[Poison\] effects for the holder. \[Poison\] statuses do not decrease in duration on resolve.
- **Fungal Culture**: Jessica will convert it into \[Poison\] (stackable up to 4 times).
