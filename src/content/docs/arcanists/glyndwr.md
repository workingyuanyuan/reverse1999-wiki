Glyndŵr fights beside a summoned eagle, [Rhyfelwr Adar Llwch Gwin]. The first Ultimate of a fight only calls the eagle in; while it is on the field, each incantation gains a second effect from it, and later Ultimates cast both incantations again as extra actions. The Ultimate costs no action point from Insight 1, and Glyndŵr starts every battle with 3 Moxie.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shadowless Hunter (attack on 2 enemies) | 160% Reality DMG; eagle follow-up 80% | 240%; 120% | 400%; 200% |
| Annwn's Blessing (team buff) | allies DMG Bonus +30%; with the eagle, Extra Action DMG Bonus +15% | +40%; +20% | +50%; +25% |

- **Shadowless Hunter**: with the eagle on the field, it strikes the main target and 1 random other enemy for the follow-up share of Reality DMG in the table; the follow-up counts as an extra action.
- **Annwn's Blessing**: both bonuses go to all allies and last 2 rounds; the Extra Action DMG Bonus needs the eagle on the field.

## Ultimate: Fight for Extra Treats

Glyndŵr heals by 50% of their own ATK. If the eagle is not on the field, it is summoned. If it is, Glyndŵr casts rank 1 Annwn's Blessing and then rank 1 Shadowless Hunter, each counted as an extra action.

[Rhyfelwr Adar Llwch Gwin]: a unit tied to Glyndŵr, not a separate fighter. Skills cannot target it, and it leaves when Glyndŵr leaves the field. Its damage and effects count as Glyndŵr's and are calculated from Glyndŵr's stats.
