## Item

### Fine Accessories

An exquisite yet surprisingly cheap accessory on the interviewee's simple outfit. Judging from her behavior, she must care about this little thing greatly and there must be a story hidden behind it.

### A Pair of Peculiar Handcuffs

A pair of handcuffs designed to control her arcane skill instead of constraining her freedom. These are costly items, she could live a decent life for quite some time on the money if the handcuffs could be sold.

### A Cherry Blossom Falling in the Peak Bloom Period

One of the cherry blossoms that fall from the tree in May, the last flowering month in the year. Florets like this one are born tenacious: they try hard to grow until they finally bloom, even if such effort is unnoticeable and deemed valueless.

## Story

### A Certain Girl

Some may think it's an honor to clear the uncultivated land for the country, yet the truth is poles apart: they were merely slaves driven to do hard labor on a wasteland.

On this hopeless land, the orphan girl could only make a living by theft, which had earned her the title "Little Thief Using Arcane Skill" from the local government. To put her behind bars, the government hired an arcanist from Honshu who eventually captured her and put her in a pair of unbreakable handcuffs.

The arcanist said to the girl, "You will have to wear these for the rest of your life, unless you cut off your arms, but you'd be dead then."

Many years later, she ran away and found a place for herself in the prosperous city of Tokyo. There she worked as a maid in a teahouse under the name Satsuki and started a new life.

Maybe, just maybe, this will be a story of a young girl who lives happily ever after.

### Silver Rose

> She has accepted and is waiting.

A girl stands in the sitting room under a gleaming crystal chandelier. Layers upon layers of lace form the skirt of her dress, each pleat perfectly ironed flat. She clenches her hands, rubbing her fingers along the seams.

At this moment, a girl named Sophie is waiting for the Rosenkavalier to arrive.

The door opens. A young man holding a silver rose hurries inside. His light footsteps strike against the polished floor, echoing throughout the silent room.

Sophie lifts her head. She sees his hand tremble as it holds onto the flower and his eyes glisten in the glittering light. The silver rose at his chest shines like a drop of moonlight plucked from the night sky.

"I ..."

They both begin to speak, then stop at the same time.

Sophie looks down and holds the rose closer. She allows a smile to slip onto her face, then she starts to sing the words of a song that everyone knows.

Sophie the maiden and the Rosenkavalier create a beautiful duet, forming a shining moment in the play.

#### Sakura Bonnet

A deep blue cotton bonnet with lace trim. The first choice for a young lady's strolling attire. Before going on stage, the prop master decorated it with a sakura blossom to calm the performer's nerves.

#### Chains of Pearl

A necklace made of special metals and rhinestones that preserves the actress's unique style while matching the era of her performance. Hand strung by the performer backstage.

#### "Silver Rose"

A neck accessory made of organza, ribbons, and plastic pearls, with cotton padding on the inside for added comfort when worn by the performer. The performer was very concerned about its proper placement, and was often distracted during early rehearsals, constantly worrying about its aesthetic incongruity.
