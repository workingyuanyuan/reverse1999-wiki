## Item

### Pendant

A small accessory on Regulus' backpack. She knows nothing about its origin or purpose but hangs it on her backpack purely as decoration. Mr. APPLe must know something about it,

but has always been tight-lipped.

### Sunglasses

Sunglasses purchased at Harrods, London during a seasonal sale. The UV rays of the high seas are so strong that a pair of sunglasses is necessary for protection. Evidently, Regulus' reason for buying it isn't that, but it looks "soooooo cool!"

### Backpack

The backpack that Regulus uses to carry her records. Generally speaking, Regulus, who takes great care of her records, does not carry them around. However, when danger came knocking, her backpack became useful for escaping with her beloved records.

## Story

### Pirate、DJ、Souls

In London's high seas, pirate ships modified from fishing boats cruise the waters. They are pirates, pirates who stray from the rules. They are DJs, DJs who put rock music on the radio. They are nodes, nodes where soundwaves, EM waves and notes are linked together. The government keeps a close watch on them and is ready to chase them to the shore at any point. But they will never yield. Blues rock is their soul, rockabilly their bones, folk rock their skin and pop rock their armor. They are fully armed and fearless. Eventually, they will spread rock to every corner of the world. R-O-C-K!

### The Fierce Fan

> Cheer, London, for the glorious victory, for the invincible team ... Cheer louder!

England!

After all these years, the World Cup returns to the birthplace of modern football. The Rimet Cup has been miraculously recovered. World Cup Willie wins hearts the moment he debuts. The lads sing their way to the finals. Now, "football is more important than life and death." People paint their faces, wave flags, and cheer for unexpected hat tricks—arguing and shouting over every goal in extra time. And when it's all over, they hurl fireworks and beer bottles at each other in defense of their teams' honor.

... Wait. A fight on the pitch?

The rockin' pirate in the Saint George's Cross leaps to her feet and declares a new title for herself:

"The Football Pirate is here! Nobody causes trouble at a celebration like this—not on my watch!"

#### Pirate Hat

A special hat said to be "a crossover of the trend among the fans and the irresistible rock pirate aesthetic style." The design is packed with all kinds of elements—even the accessory on her backpack is used to decorate the necklace—but at least it doesn't look like a mess.

#### Jersey of the Home Team

A fan-edition home jersey bought from a sports store near Wembley Stadium.

Claimed to be a "one and only" treasure by the salesman, it naturally has no other sizes and is inevitably too large for Regulus. Luckily she doesn't care if it fits at all.

#### Flag of England

A home flag which Regulus uses as a wrap occasionally. It seems longer than the usual satin flags, with a smoother texture. One will understand, however, the appeal of the scene doesn't lie in the flag if one has the chance to see that joyful wielder.

### Take Off! To the Future

> Ahoy, me listeners! Welcome to the return of Pirate Radio!

Faster, higher, further, rock climbs into the stratosphere!

"The days of braving open seas to dodge capture are over! The great Captain Regulus declares a new golden age of music! World tour routes, 3,000 feet in the air. We will soar to new heights! Our one and only mission; to share the glory of rock'n'roll with the world ... A music festival in the sky AND on land ... dig it! The highest-altitude concert in history!"

\*ahem\* "APPLe completely understands how the Captain feels. Still, it may be best to keep the music down at night. Even if there's no police presence in suitcase, the folks here are sometimes more dangerous than any old copper."

#### "Conqueror" Aeronautic Set - Headgear

A functional suit designed for low-intensity flight needs.

Unique in style, the craftsmanship comes from the same production line as many official military goods, serving multiple purposes from reducing wind resistance to providing warmth. "Most importantly, the price is incredibly reliable!" she says candidly.

#### "Freedom APPLe" Startup Key

One of the key components of the experimental flying vessel developed by the Laplace Scientific Computing Center. It bears an official code composed of Latin letters and Roman numerals, and a concise temporary name.

The latter is mostly used in secret circumstances outside certain official flight routes that cannot be disclosed.

#### "Conqueror" Aeronautic Set - Special Toolkit

Another masterpiece from the "Conqueror" manufacturer. "Providing a considerate experience for every flyer." Clearly, this is not an overstatement.
