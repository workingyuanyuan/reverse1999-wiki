## Incantations

### Lethal Melody

**Rank 1**: Mass attack. Deals 70% Reality DMG to 2 enemies and an additional (own ATK x60%) Genesis DMG to the enemy if it is below 50% HP.

**Rank 2**: Mass attack. Deals 105% Reality DMG to 2 enemies and an additional (own ATK x90%) Genesis DMG to the enemy if it is below 50% HP.

**Rank 3**: Mass attack. Deals 175% Reality DMG to 2 enemies and an additional (own ATK x150%) Genesis DMG to the enemy if it is below 50% HP.

### Makeshift Inventions

**Rank 1**: 1-target counter. Gains 2 bullets from \[Veteran's Cartridge\] and enters the \[Quick Draw\] status for 2 rounds.

**Rank 2**: 1-target counter. Gains 3 bullets from \[Veteran's Cartridge\] and enters the \[Quick Draw\] status for 2 rounds.

**Rank 3**: 1-target counter. Gains 5 bullets from \[Veteran's Cartridge\] and enters the \[Quick Draw\] status for 2 rounds.

## Ultimate: A Veteran's Regards

1-target attack. Deals 500% Reality DMG to an enemy. For each type of \[Bullet\] held on self, this attack gains an additional Critical Rate +10%.

## Inheritance

**Insight 1 · Military Experience**: Whenever an ally triggers \[Bullet\] 2 times, gain 1 stack of \[Ceremonious\] for self.

**Insight 2 · Military Experience II**: Critical Rate +10% when entering battle.

**Insight 3 · Military Experience III**: After scoring a critical hit or defeating an enemy, all allies gain 1 type of Bullet from \[Veteran's Cartridge\].

Whenever this unit scores 3 critical hits, this unit gains Moxie +1.

## Portrait

**Portrait 1**: **Lethal Melody**: At incantation rank 1/2/3, the Genesis DMG is increased to 100/150/250%.

**Portrait 2**: **A Veteran's Regards**: For each type of \[Bullet\] held, the Critical Rate gained is increased to 20%.

**Portrait 3**: **Lethal Melody**: At incantation rank 1/2/3, the Reality DMG is increased to 120/180/300%.

**Portrait 4**: **Makeshift Inventions**: \[Swift Riposte\]'s Reality DMG dealt is increased to 300%.

**Portrait 5**: **Military Experience**: After defeating an enemy, the types of bullets gained from \[Veteran's Cartridge\] by all allies is increased to 2.

## Statuses

- **Veteran's Cartridge**: A backup cartridge containing \[Acidic Bullet\] and \[Fragmented Bullet\].
- **Quick Draw**: When holding a \[Bullet\], right before being attacked, preemptively strike the attacker with \[Swift Riposte\].
- **Ceremonious**: Ultimate Might +3% (up to 100 stacks, undispellable, disappears after casting an Ultimate).
- **Swift Riposte**: 1-target attack. Deals 200% Reality DMG to the unit attacking this unit.
- **Acidic Bullet**: After attacking, inflict Reality DEF -5% on the target for 2 rounds, stacks up to 5 times.
- **Fragmented Bullet**: Deals an additional (ATK x50%) Genesis DMG.
