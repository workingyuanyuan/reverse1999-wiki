Mr. Duncan loads himself and his allies with two kinds of bullet from his Veteran's Cartridge. His counter incantation gives him bullets and Quick Draw, which shoots an attacker first; his attack on two enemies adds Genesis DMG to targets below half HP, and his Ultimate gains Critical Rate for each kind of bullet he holds.

## Incantations

| Incantation | Rank 1 | Rank 2 | Rank 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lethal Melody (attack on 2 enemies) | 70% Reality DMG; Genesis DMG 60% of ATK | 105%; 90% | 175%; 150% |
| Makeshift Inventions (counter) | 2 bullets; [Quick Draw] 2 rounds | 3 bullets | 5 bullets |

- **Lethal Melody** adds its Genesis DMG only to a target below 50% HP.
- **Makeshift Inventions** takes its bullets from [Veteran's Cartridge]; Quick Draw lasts 2 rounds at every rank.

## Ultimate: A Veteran's Regards

Deals 500% Reality DMG to one enemy. The attack gains Critical Rate +10% for each kind of bullet Mr. Duncan holds.
