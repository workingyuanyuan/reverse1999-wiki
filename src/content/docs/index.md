## Summoning

A 6-star has a 1.5% base rate; the rate rises after 60 summons without one and a 6-star is certain by the 70th. Only Time-limited Banners carry the count over to the next one; limited, collaboration and most event banners clear it when they end: [Summoning and pity](/systems/summoning/).

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Afflatus | An arcanist's type: Star, Mineral, Beast, Plant, Spirit or Intellect. |
| Insight | Ascension in three steps (I, II, III); each raises the level cap and opens an Inheritance passive. |
| Portrait | Five upgrades, one per duplicate copy of the arcanist. |
| Euphoria | A later upgrade for some arcanists, opened at Insight III level 30, with two Epiphanies of four levels. |
| Moxie | Points that fill toward an arcanist's Ultimate. |
