| Rarity | Base rate |
| --- | --- |
| 6 stars | 1.5% (2.36% overall, counting the pity) |
| 5 stars | 8.5% |
| 4 stars | 40% |
| 3 stars | 45% |
| 2 stars | 5% |

Every banner uses these rates except Covenant of the Water, and on every banner 10 summons give at least one arcanist of 4 stars or more.

## Pity

- After 60 summons in a row without a 6-star, the 61st summon has a 4% chance of a 6-star.
- Each further summon without a 6-star adds 2.5% to that chance.
- A 6-star resets the chance to 1.5% on banners of the same type, and the count of summons without a 6-star starts again.

## Rate-up and the 50/50

On a banner with one rate-up 6-star, a 6-star has a 50% chance to be the rate-up arcanist. When the 6-star is another arcanist, the next 6-star is the banner's rate-up arcanist. On a banner with two rate-up 5-stars, a 5-star has a 50% chance to be one of them, each equally likely.

Banners where the player picks the rate-up arcanist work the same way with the picked arcanist. Some event banners change the share: 70% on Revelation of the Water (two picked 6-stars), 100% on Abundance of the Water (four rate-up 6-stars) and Boon of the Water (three picked 6-stars).

## Carry-over by banner type

| Banner type | Pity count | When the banner ends | Rate-up 6-star |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Time-limited Banner (限时角色征集 / 限時角色徵集) | shared by all Time-limited Banners | the count and the raised rate carry over to the next Time-limited Banner | 50%, then guaranteed |
| Ripples on the Water, Yearning of the Water (pick one 6-star) | shared by banners of the same name | carries over to the next banner of the same name | the first 6-star is the picked one; then 50%, then guaranteed |
| Regular Banner, Amongst the Lake (常驻角色征集) | its own | permanent banner | no rate-up |
| Dushuo, Anniversary and Carnival Limited Banners | its own, not shared with the next one of the same kind | cleared | 50%, then guaranteed |
| Limited rerun banner where the player picks the 6-star (限定复刻自选征集) | its own | cleared | 50%, then guaranteed |
| Collaboration Banners | its own | cleared | 50%, then guaranteed |
| Other Time-Limited Event Banners (Abundance, Covenant, Revelation, Boon, Promise of the Water, The Snow of Dushuo) | its own | cleared | as the banner states |

Arcanists from the Dushuo, Anniversary and Carnival Limited Banners are not added to other banner types; they come back in later banners of the same kind or in limited rerun banners. Each summon on these banners also gives one Cassette of the Lost, exchanged in the banner's shop for the limited arcanist, its rate-up 5-stars or materials; the cassettes expire when the shop closes.

A banner belongs to the type that its rules name in brackets, such as 【限时角色征集】. Some banners titled Special Event Banner (限时活动征集) are of the Time-limited type and share its count; other event banners are not.

The carry-over rule of the Time-limited Banner names the summon count and the raised 6-star rate. The guarantee after a lost 50/50 names the rate-up arcanist of the current banner (本期, "this period's" in the Chinese text).

## Banners with their own rules

| Banner | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| The First Drop of Rain (第一滴雨), the beginner banner | at most 30 summons; a 6-star within 30 |
| Covenant of the Water (湖的许诺 / 湖的許諾) | a Golden Skein within 30 summons, at most 60 summons; open the skein to pick a 6-star before the banner ends |
| Golden Spindle (金纺轮 / 金紡輪) | an item for simulated ten-summons from the version 2.2 Amongst the Lake pool; each has at least one 6-star, and none counts toward any pity |
| Collaboration Banners | an extra reward at the 100th, 160th, 220th and 280th summon: a copy of the collaboration arcanist, or a choice between the arcanist and another reward |
