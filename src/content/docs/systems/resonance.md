Resonance raises an arcanist's stats with Ideas: pieces of several shapes placed on a grid. Each level enlarges the grid, adds Ideas or raises their level, and raises the main Idea, the piece in the shape of the arcanist's own set. Resonance opens at Insight I. Some arcanists show the same system as Reasoning, with Reasoning Patterns.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Opens at | Insight I |
| Max level | 15 for 5- and 6-star arcanists, 10 for 2-, 3- and 4-star |
| Resonance Patterns | from Resonance level 10 |
| Main Idea, max level | 10 (5 and 6 stars), 6 (2 to 4 stars) |

## Resonance Patterns

Patterns open at Resonance level 10. Each arcanist has one of four sets, told apart by the shape of their main Idea; every set has the default pattern Placidity and five more. A pattern changes the stats of the main Idea, and changing pattern keeps the Idea level. Compared with Placidity:

| Set | Pattern | Raises | Lowers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Z-shaped | Elucidation | DMG Bonus, Ultimate Might | HP, Reality DEF, Mental DEF, Critical Resist Rate, DMG Taken Reduction |
| Z-shaped | Genuinity | DMG Bonus, Incantation Might | HP, Reality DEF, Mental DEF, Critical Resist Rate, DMG Taken Reduction |
| Z-shaped | Mercy | DMG Taken Reduction, DMG Heal | ATK, Critical DMG, DMG Bonus |
| Z-shaped | Equanimity | HP, Reality DEF, Mental DEF, DMG Bonus | ATK, Critical Resist Rate, DMG Taken Reduction |
| Z-shaped | Equibalance | HP, ATK, Reality DEF, Mental DEF, DMG Bonus, DMG Taken Reduction | Critical Resist Rate |
| Cross-shaped | Elucidation | ATK, Critical Rate, Ultimate Might | HP, Critical Resist Rate |
| Cross-shaped | Genuinity | ATK, Critical Rate, Incantation Might | HP, Critical Resist Rate |
| Cross-shaped | Stupefaction | HP, ATK, Critical Rate | Reality DEF, Mental DEF, Critical DMG |
| Cross-shaped | Aspirational | ATK, Critical Rate, Critical DMG | HP, Critical Resist Rate |
| Cross-shaped | Hyperphrenia | HP, ATK, Reality DEF, Mental DEF, Critical Rate, Critical DMG | Critical Resist Rate |
| T-shaped | Elucidation | Ultimate Might, Penetration Rate | Reality DEF, Mental DEF, Critical Resist Rate, Critical DEF |
| T-shaped | Genuinity | Penetration Rate, Incantation Might | Reality DEF, Mental DEF, Critical Resist Rate, Critical DEF |
| T-shaped | Hyper | ATK | Reality DEF, Mental DEF, Critical Resist Rate, Critical DEF |
| T-shaped | Inspire | DMG Bonus | Reality DEF, Mental DEF, Critical Resist Rate, Critical DEF |
| T-shaped | Delirament | HP, ATK, Reality DEF, Mental DEF, DMG Bonus | Critical Resist Rate, Critical DMG, Critical DEF |
| U-shaped | Hyper | ATK | Reality DEF, Critical Rate, Critical Resist Rate, DMG Bonus, DMG Taken Reduction |
| U-shaped | Overindulgence | HP | Reality DEF, Critical Rate, Critical Resist Rate, DMG Bonus, DMG Taken Reduction |
| U-shaped | Quiescence | Reality DEF | Mental DEF, Critical Rate, Critical Resist Rate, DMG Bonus, DMG Taken Reduction |
| U-shaped | Prudentiality | Mental DEF | Reality DEF, Critical Rate, Critical Resist Rate, DMG Bonus, DMG Taken Reduction |
| U-shaped | Equanimity | HP, ATK, Reality DEF, Mental DEF, DMG Taken Reduction | Critical Rate, DMG Bonus |

For 2-, 3- and 4-star arcanists the same, except: Delirament does not lower Critical DMG.
