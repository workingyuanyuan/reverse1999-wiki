Insight raises an arcanist's level cap once the arcanist reaches it. Each Insight costs Sharpodonty and materials that depend on the arcanist (listed on each arcanist's page), and returns the arcanist to level 1 of the new cap.

## Level caps

| Rarity | Insight 0 | Insight I | Insight II | Insight III |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 stars | level 30 | level 40 | level 50 | level 60 |
| 5 stars | level 30 | level 40 | level 50 | level 60 |
| 4 stars | level 30 | level 40 | level 50 | — |
| 2 and 3 stars | level 30 | level 40 | level 50 | — |

## Cost of each Insight

| Rarity | Insight I | Insight II | Insight III |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 stars | 18,000 Sharpodonty | 40,000 Sharpodonty | 200,000 Sharpodonty |
| 5 stars | 12,000 Sharpodonty | 30,000 Sharpodonty | 120,000 Sharpodonty |
| 4 stars | 8,000 Sharpodonty | 25,000 Sharpodonty | — |
| 2 and 3 stars | 5,000 Sharpodonty | 20,000 Sharpodonty | — |

Insight III also takes a Tome of the arcanist's Afflatus and higher-rarity materials; each arcanist's page gives the full list.

## What each Insight unlocks

| Insight | Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Insight I | level cap 40; the first Inheritance passive |
| Insight II | level cap 50; the second Inheritance passive; for 4-star and higher arcanists, a garment (all but Sonetto) |
| Insight III | level cap 60; the third Inheritance passive (5 and 6 stars only); opens Euphoria for arcanists that have it |

Euphoria opens at Insight III level 30 for a 6-star arcanist and at Insight III level 1 for a 5-star.

## Portrait

A duplicate of an arcanist raises its Portrait by one level, up to Portrait 5; each level changes one skill, given on the arcanist's page. What later duplicates turn into is on [Summoning and pity](/systems/summoning/).
