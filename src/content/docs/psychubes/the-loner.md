## Effect

- A Plant carrier has DMG Dealt +6% when attacking.
- When the carrier attacks an enemy in [Nightmare] status, the carrier gains Penetration Rate +4/6.5/9/11.5/14%, up to 3 stacks (Amplification 1 to 5).
