## Effect

When the carrier attacks with an incantation, it gains Ultimate Might by the incantation's rank (Amplification 1 to 5):

- rank 1: +8/10/12/14/16%
- rank 2: +12/15/18/21/24%
- rank 3: +18/22.5/27/31.5/36%
